This is the Master Equation. Nine factors inside the integral. $C_W$ wraps the result as $\chi$ — coherence is the *functional*, the sovereign output, not a tenth ingredient sitting alongside the others. This correction retires the old ten-factor product form everywhere it appeared. The equation was not designed — it was *forced*. Each axiom in the spine (Page 02) demands a variable. The variables compose into a single functional. The composition is multiplicative, not additive, because the structural veto requires it: if any single factor goes to zero, the whole goes to zero.

That veto is not a design choice. It is a mathematical non-negotiable. Grace without motion is inert. Motion without knowledge is blind. Knowledge without faith-network is isolated. Every factor is load-bearing. Remove one and the structure collapses — not partially, not gracefully, but to zero.

The equation has four forms, each revealing a different face of the same object:

1. **Product form (veto):** The multiplicative integral above. Lean-proven.
2. **Ratio form (combat):** $\chi$ as the ratio of ordering to disorder — makes the war visible.
3. **Dynamic form (engine):** $d\chi/dt = G - S + \Gamma$ — the differential engine, BC2 quantified. Lean-proven.
4. **Field form (wave):** $\chi$ as a field evolving on spacetime — connects to the Lagrangian (Page 06).

These are not four different equations. They are four *representations* of one object, related by exact transformations. The product form gives the veto structure. The ratio form gives the combat picture. The dynamic form gives the time evolution. The field form gives the spacetime geometry. Together they close the descriptive space.
