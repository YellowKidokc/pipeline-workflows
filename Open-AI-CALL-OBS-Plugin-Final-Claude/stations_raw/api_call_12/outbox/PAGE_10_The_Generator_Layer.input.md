## The Derivation

**The Hamiltonian as generator:**

In Hamiltonian mechanics, the Hamiltonian $H$ is not just the total energy. It is the generator of time evolution:

$$\frac{dq_i}{dt} = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i}, \quad \frac{dp_i}{dt} = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_i}$$

Every trajectory in phase space is generated by $H$ through these equations. $H$ does not live in phase space the way $q_i$ and $p_i$ do — it is the function that tells phase space how to move.

**The identification:**

$$E \longleftrightarrow H \longleftrightarrow \text{Noether's theorem (the generator of all charges)}$$
