### Physical Gravity {#physical-gravity .font-oswald .text-xl .font-semibold .text-sky}
:::

Physical gravity follows the inverse square law, one of the most fundamental principles in physics.

::: {.eq-box .text-center}
\$\$F = G \\frac{m_1 m_2}{r\^2}\$\$
:::

- [F]{.font-mono .text-sky .text-xs} --- gravitational force
- [G]{.font-mono .text-sky .text-xs} --- gravitational constant
- [m₁, m₂]{.font-mono .text-sky .text-xs} --- masses
- [r]{.font-mono .text-sky .text-xs} --- distance between objects

::: {.mt-4 .text-xs .text-gray-500 .space-y-1}
Increases with mass of both objects

Decreases with the square of the distance

Acts universally across all massive objects

Cannot be shielded or eliminated
:::

::: {.eq-box .text-center}
\$\$F\_{\\text{spiritual}} = G_s \\frac{m\_{\\text{sin}} \\cdot m\_{\\text{soul}}}{d\^2}\$\$

- [G~s~]{.font-mono .text-teal .text-xs} --- spiritual gravity constant
- [m~sin~]{.font-mono .text-teal .text-xs} --- magnitude of sin
- [m~soul~]{.font-mono .text-teal .text-xs} --- soul\'s capacity for separation
- [d]{.font-mono .text-teal .text-xs} --- spiritual distance from divine alignment

Universal across all conscious beings

::: {.flex .gap-3 .text-sm .text-gray-400}

**Statistically improbable but not impossible.** The miracle occurs within the space of possibilities that physical law permits.
