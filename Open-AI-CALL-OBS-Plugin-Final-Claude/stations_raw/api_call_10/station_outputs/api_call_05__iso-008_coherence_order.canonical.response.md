## 1. THREE MOST CRITICAL FAILURES

**Failure 1: Equivocation on "coherence" without resolution.** The paper explicitly identifies the risk that "coherence" is a floating signifier meaning Shannon entropy in physics and "divine order" in theology, but never resolves this. The Strain Analysis states: "The risk is that 'coherence' becomes a floating signifier—it means Shannon entropy when the framework needs physics credibility, and it means 'divine order' when the framework needs theological credibility, without a rigorous bridge between the two." This is a fatal admission that the paper does not overcome.

**Failure 2: No operational definition for theological coherence.** The paper admits: "'Divine harmony' is NOT precisely defined. It has no units. It is not independently measurable. It does not make quantitative predictions." Without an independent measurement procedure for the theological side, the claimed isomorphism collapses into analogy—the mapping cannot be tested, falsified, or refined.

**Failure 3: Asymmetric prediction transfer undermines isomorphism claim.** Test 1 shows that physics predictions are strong and confirmed while theological predictions are "weak and hard to test independently." Test 2 (symmetry breaking) further shows that the mapping is not clean—disorder plays constructive roles in both domains. The paper's own tests demonstrate that the mapping fails the criteria for genuine isomorphism.
