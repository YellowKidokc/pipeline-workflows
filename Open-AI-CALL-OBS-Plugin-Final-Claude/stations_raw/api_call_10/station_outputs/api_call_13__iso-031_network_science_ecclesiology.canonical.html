Falsification Criteria

Kill conditions — any one breaks the isomorphism
  1. In Network Science: Demonstrate a scale-free network robust to targeted hub removal (unaffected). Breaks “vulnerability to hub attack” correspondence.
  2. In Network Science: Demonstrate that preferential attachment does NOT produce power-law degree distributions under standard conditions. Breaks Matthew 25:29 correspondence.
  3. In Theology: Demonstrate that the early church grew through a non-scale-free mechanism — uniform degree distributions, centrally planned growth, long path lengths. SNA of Pauline epistles reveals random/regular network.
  4. In Theology: Demonstrate that institutionally structured (non-scale-free) churches are equally or more resilient to targeted persecution than organic (scale-free) churches.
  5. Break the topology: Show that structural features (preferential attachment, small-world, hub vulnerability, bridge-tie diffusion, cascading failure) do not actually match between network science and Acts narrative.
