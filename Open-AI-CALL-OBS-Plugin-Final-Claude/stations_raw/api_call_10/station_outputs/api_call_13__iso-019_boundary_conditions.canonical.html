Falsification criteria

  • Show that the 8 BCs are not independent — if some BC can be derived from others, the uniqueness claim weakens.
  • Show that a non‑Christian worldview satisfies all 8 BCs — directly refutes T7.1.
  • Show that the 8 BCs are not sufficient — if the chi‑field equations with all 8 BCs have multiple solutions, uniqueness fails.
  • Show that the BCs were selected to guarantee the desired outcome — circularity would invalidate the entire exercise.
Honest assessment: 4 BCs independently derived, 3 Christian‑presuppositional, 1 mixed. Uniqueness claim currently circular.
