Domain Specifications

Domain A · Physics

Developmental Psychology — Attachment Theory (Bowlby, Ainsworth, Main, Bartholomew)

Early relational experience with a primary caregiver produces internal working models (IWMs) that shape all subsequent relational behavior. Four attachment styles (secure, anxious-preoccupied, dismissive-avoidant, fearful-avoidant/disorganized) map onto a 2D space of anxiety and avoidance. Earned security is possible through corrective relational experience.

ECR-R · Strange Situation · AAI · 2D: Anxiety × Avoidance

Domain B · Theology

Christian Theology — Prayer, Worship, and the Divine-Human Relationship

Biblical theology describes prayer and worship as the primary mode of divine-human relationship. The believer's image of God (theology proper) shapes all worship behavior. Four worship postures (secure trust, anxious legalism, avoidant deism, disorganized trauma-theology) map onto a 2D space of spiritual anxiety and spiritual avoidance. Sanctification is corrective relational experience.

AGI (Attachment to God Inventory) · Psalm 131 · Psalm 22 · James 4:8

Test A · Structural Preservation

Do relations survive under mapping?

Secure base, proximity seeking, separation protest, IWM, earned security, hierarchy — all map with preserved relational structure. The 2D anxiety/avoidance grid is identical.

Is the mapping forced or could you swap targets?

Attachment behavior cannot be placed in the theology slot (Strange Situation not applicable to God). But AGI measures anxiety/avoidance in the God-relationship on the same 2D space. Topological mapping is forced; metric mapping is partially supported.

Test C · Constraint

Does the mapping rule things out?

Yes: worship models that ignore the attachment system (pure cognitive assent) are ruled out. Pastoral care must provide corrective relational experience. Different attachment styles require different pastoral approaches.

Does each side predict the other?

Psychology → Theology: attachment constrains viable worship models. Theology → Psychology: Scripture predicts secure base, proximity seeking, separation protest, earned security — discovered 2500+ years later. Bidirectional.

Falsification Criteria

  • 1. Demonstrate that the 2D anxiety/avoidance model does NOT capture the primary dimensions of variation in close relationships — breaking the 2D correspondence.
  • 2. Demonstrate that earned security occurs without corrective relational experience (purely cognitive or pharmacological) — breaking the sanctification parallel.
  • 3. Demonstrate that worship postures do NOT distribute across a 2D anxiety/avoidance space (AGI fails to replicate the structure).
  • 4. Demonstrate that attachment style does NOT predict theological orientation (zero correlation between ECR-R and theological orientation).
  • 5. Break the topology: show that a structural feature present in one domain is absent in the other (secure base, proximity seeking, separation protest, IWM, earned security, hierarchy).
If any one of these conditions is met, the isomorphism is falsified.
