# Peer Review: "Ten Laws: A Canonical Treatment of Physical-Spiritual Isomorphism"

## A. CITATION QUALITY

**A1: 1 — Source Existence & Verifiability** | Comment: The paper contains zero formal citations to peer-reviewed sources. It references "Wolfram Verified" and "Computational Verification v3" but provides no DOIs, arXiv IDs, journal references, or verifiable publication data for any claim.

**A2: 1 — Source Authority & Relevance** | Comment: No authoritative sources are cited. The paper invokes Einstein, Maxwell, Shannon, and Feynman by name but provides no specific citations to their original works or to any secondary literature that would establish scholarly grounding.

**A3: 0 — Citation Formatting** | Comment: There is no citation formatting whatsoever. No bibliography, no footnotes, no inline citations, no reference list. The paper violates every standard of academic citation convention.

**A4: 0 — Citation Density & Distribution** | Comment: The paper has zero citations across its entire length. There is no distribution of citations because there are no citations. This is a complete absence of scholarly apparatus.

## B. FACTUAL ACCURACY

**B1: 4 — Empirical Claim Accuracy** | Comment: The physical equations are correctly stated (Newton's law, Einstein's field equations, Maxwell's equations, QCD potential, Shannon entropy, Schrödinger equation, Fermi decay rate). However, the paper makes unverified empirical claims about "0/1000 violations" in a "coherent truth network" test with no methodological documentation.

**B2: 3 — Historical/Contextual Accuracy** | Comment: The historical framing of gravity as "the weakest force" is technically correct but misleadingly presented. The claim that "the Second Law has never been observed to fail" is accurate but the paper's extension to moral domains lacks historical grounding.

**B3: 6 — Mathematical/Formal Accuracy** | Comment: The physical equations are correctly transcribed. However, the spiritual analogs introduce variables ($\psi$, $G_s$, $\lambda$, $R$, $I$, $A$, $B$, $P_{will}$) that are never operationally defined, making mathematical verification impossible.

**B4: 2 — Claim-Evidence Alignment** | Comment: The central claim—that physical laws and spiritual realities share isomorphic mathematical structure—is asserted but never demonstrated through evidence. The paper provides equations but no empirical data, no experimental results, and no formal proofs connecting the domains.

## C. LOGICAL RIGOR

**C1: 3 — Deductive Validity** | Comment: The paper claims that because physical equations and spiritual equations "look the same," they are structurally identical. This is a category error: syntactic similarity does not entail semantic or ontological identity. The deduction from "same equation form" to "same underlying reality" is invalid.

**C2: 2 — Inductive Strength** | Comment: The inductive argument rests on nine analogies. Even if all nine held, the inference to a unified framework would be weak without independent evidence. The paper provides no statistical or probabilistic reasoning to support the inductive leap.

**C3: 1 — Absence of Fallacies** | Comment: The paper commits multiple fallacies: false analogy (physical equations and spiritual concepts are not comparable domains), equivocation (using "entropy" to mean both thermodynamic disorder and moral corruption), and argument from personal incredulity ("Love that can't be refused isn't love").

**C4: 2 — Argument Chain Integrity** | Comment: The argument chain is: (1) physical laws have mathematical form X, (2) spiritual concepts can be written in form X', (3) therefore they are the same thing. Step 2 to step 3 is a non sequitur. The chain breaks at every link.

## D. EVIDENCE SUFFICIENCY

**D1: 1 — Claim Strength vs Evidence Weight** | Comment: The paper makes extraordinary claims (mathematical isomorphism between physics and theology, "0/1000 violations" of a truth network test, Hessian matrix rankings) but provides zero evidence. The claims are maximal; the evidence is absent.

**D2: 0 — Counter-Evidence Engagement** | Comment: The paper acknowledges no counter-evidence, no alternative interpretations, no criticisms from physics, theology, or philosophy. It mentions "kill conditions" but provides no engagement with actual scholarly objections.

**D3: 0 — Replication & Corroboration** | Comment: No replication attempts are cited. No corroborating studies are referenced. The "Computational Verification v3" is mentioned but not provided, not published, and not verifiable.

**D4: 0 — Statistical Rigor** | Comment: The paper makes statistical claims ("0/1000 violations," "Hessian matrix rankings") with no description of methodology, sample size, significance testing, or any statistical apparatus whatsoever.

## E. SEMANTIC PRECISION

**E1: 2 — Term Definition Consistency** | Comment: Key terms are defined circularly or not at all. "Soul mass $\psi$" is never defined. "Relational distance $d$" is never operationalized. "Moral entropy $S_{sin}$" is asserted but not defined in measurable terms.

**E2: 1 — Equivocation Detection** | Comment: The paper systematically equivocates on "entropy" (thermodynamic vs. informational vs. moral), "force" (physical vs. spiritual), "energy" (physical vs. moral), and "information" (Shannon vs. theological Logos). These are not the same concepts.

**E3: 2 — Domain Translation Fidelity** | Comment: The translation from physics to theology is asserted without justification. The paper assumes that mathematical form transfers meaning across domains, which is a fundamental error in domain translation.

**E4: 3 — Hedging Calibration** | Comment: The paper occasionally hedges ("This is not a proof—it is a structural identification") but overwhelmingly presents claims as established fact ("The equation says so," "The Hessian found it independently"). Hedging is inconsistent and insufficient.

**F2: 0 — Methodology Transparency** | Comment: No methodology is described. How were the spiritual equations derived? How were the "computational verifications" conducted? What data was used? The paper provides zero methodological information.

**H3: 1 — Mapping Rigor** | Comment: The mapping between physical and spiritual terms is arbitrary. Why is grace mapped to gravity and not electromagnetism? Why is love mapped to the strong force and not gravity? No justification is given for any mapping.

**H4: 0 — Prediction Transfer** | Comment: The paper makes no testable predictions that could be verified in either domain. The "kill conditions" are not specific enough to constitute genuine predictions.
