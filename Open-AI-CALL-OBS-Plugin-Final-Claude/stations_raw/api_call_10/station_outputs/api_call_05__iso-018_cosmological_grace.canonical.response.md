## B. FACTUAL ACCURACY

**B1: 5 — Empirical Claim Accuracy | Comment:** The empirical claims about current measurements (w = -1.03 ± 0.03, H_0 = 73.04 ± 1.04, H_0 = 67.4 ± 0.5) are factually correct as of 2024. However, the claim that the Grace Function "resolves" the Hubble tension is unsubstantiated—no numerical computation is provided to demonstrate this.

**B2: 6 — Historical/Contextual Accuracy | Comment:** The historical framing of the cosmological constant problem (120 orders of magnitude discrepancy) and the Hubble tension (5 sigma) is accurate. The theological claim about "continuous creation" is a legitimate position in Christian theology, though presented without nuance.

**B3: 4 — Mathematical/Formal Accuracy | Comment:** The Friedmann equation is correctly written, and the exponential decay function G(t) = G_0 e^(-λt) + G_inf(1 - e^(-λt)) is mathematically well-formed. However, the consciousness coupling term Ψ_collective is never mathematically defined—it appears as an argument to G(t, Ψ) but has no formal specification.

**B4: 3 — Claim-Evidence Alignment | Comment:** The central claim that "cosmic expansion rate depends on consciousness" has zero empirical evidence. The paper explicitly admits the coupling is "far below current measurement precision" and provides no experimental protocol. The evidence cited (modified Friedmann equation) is a mathematical proposal, not empirical evidence.

## C. LOGICAL RIGOR

**C1: 4 — Deductive Validity | Comment:** The deductive chain from "dynamic dark energy" to "consciousness coupling" is invalid. There is no logical necessity that dynamic dark energy implies consciousness dependence—this is a non sequitur. The paper attempts to motivate it theologically but provides no deductive bridge.

**C2: 3 — Inductive Strength | Comment:** The inductive argument from "dark energy has properties X, Y, Z" to "dark energy is divine sustenance" is weak. The properties listed (universal, dominant, mysterious, sustaining, positive) are shared by many concepts (e.g., the Higgs field, quantum vacuum, inflation). No inductive strength calculation or Bayesian reasoning is provided.

**C3: 2 — Absence of Fallacies | Comment:** Multiple fallacies are present: (1) Argument from ignorance—"dark energy is mysterious, therefore it could be divine grace"; (2) False analogy—structural parallel is asserted without demonstrating causal or ontological connection; (3) Special pleading—consciousness coupling is exempted from empirical requirements.

**C4: 3 — Argument Chain Integrity | Comment:** The argument chain is: Axiom (dynamic dark energy) → Claim (consciousness coupling) → Evidence (modified equation) → Bridge (theological parallel). The chain breaks at multiple points: the axiom is unproven, the claim is unfalsifiable, the evidence is a mathematical proposal, and the bridge conflates analogy with identity.

## D. EVIDENCE SUFFICIENCY

**D1: 2 — Claim Strength vs Evidence Weight | Comment:** The claim that "cosmic expansion rate depends on consciousness" is extraordinarily strong (it would revolutionize physics), yet the evidence is a single modified equation with an undefined term. **D2: 1 — Counter-Evidence Engagement | Comment:** The paper acknowledges that current measurements are consistent with constant Λ (w = -1.03 ± 0.03) but does not engage with the substantial body of evidence supporting ΛCDM. **D4: 2 — Statistical Rigor | Comment:** No statistical analysis is performed. The paper mentions confidence intervals for H_0 and w but provides no statistical framework for evaluating the Grace Function against data. No Bayesian evidence, chi-squared values, or likelihood ratios are computed.
