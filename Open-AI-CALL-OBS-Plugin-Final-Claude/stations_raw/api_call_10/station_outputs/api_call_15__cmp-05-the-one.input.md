---

Ten assumptions. Zero provable by experiment. Every one required before the experiment can be designed.

The Münchhausen Trilemma names why this is structural, not incidental. Remove logic and no argument holds. Remove mathematical effectiveness and no equation reaches matter. Remove moral honesty and no data is trustworthy.

The ten presuppositions are not just assumptions. They are the minimum coherence requirements for rational investigation. And every one of them describes a universe that looks like the product of a rational mind.

Science has been borrowing the coherence conditions of theology for four centuries without acknowledgment.

## II. The Reverse Audit

*Coherence thread: The six leak points are decoherence events — places where naturalism's own coherence collapses from inside its own standards.*

---

The standard move in science-religion discourse is one-directional. Hold theology accountable to science's standards. Test religious claims against evidence, falsifiability, predictive power, internal consistency. That is half an audit.

The Reverse Audit runs the other half. Hold science accountable to the same standards science applies to everything else. Not to attack science. Science is extraordinary at what it does. But to ask: does naturalism, evaluated by its own criteria, close its own circle?

It does not. It leaks at six specific points.

**Consciousness.** Naturalism can map neural correlates. It cannot explain why there is something it is like to be a brain processing information. Eliminate the explanandum and you use consciousness to deny consciousness. Reduce it to neurons and you cannot specify what turns information processing into felt awareness without smuggling in the thing you were reducing. Appeal to brute emergence and you invoke the one move naturalism refuses everywhere else. The hard problem is not a gap waiting to be filled. It is a decoherence point — the place where the materialist account loses internal coherence.

**Mathematical effectiveness.** Physics describes reality with mathematics to twelve decimal places. Why? Mathematics is abstract. Reality is physical. There is no naturalist mechanism for why an abstract human invention should map the deep structure of the universe. The effectiveness is not just unexplained. It is structurally incoherent under closed naturalism — because the explanation would require something outside the system that contains both math and matter.

**Morality.** Naturalism describes moral behavior brilliantly. It cannot derive binding obligation from any description. Hume's wall holds. "Humans tend to judge this wrong" does not produce "this IS wrong." Deny that morality binds and the Holocaust becomes a preference disagreement. Ground it in flourishing and you need to explain why flourishing binds. Treat moral order as brute fact and you have conceded something prior to naturalism exists.

**Information origin.** Information theory handles processing, transmission, encoding. It does not explain where information came from. DNA is information — 3.2 billion base pairs of error-correcting code. The laws of physics are information. The initial conditions are information. Naturalism quantifies information beautifully and has no account of its origin.

**Fine-tuning.** Thirty cosmological constants calibrated to precisions that make the lottery look like a coin flip. Penrose calculated the initial entropy condition at one part in 10^(10^123). The multiverse response relocates the question without answering it and relies on an unobservable postulate. The anthropic response is true and evasive — of course we wouldn't be here otherwise, but why is anything here in a form that supports observers?

**Initial conditions.** The universe began in the lowest-entropy state we have direct evidence of. The Second Law says entropy increases. Working backwards, extraordinary order was present at the beginning. Why? Naturalism quantifies the improbability and offers no mechanism. The low-entropy initial condition is treated as brute fact — the same move naturalism rejects when theology makes it.

Six points. Same pattern at each: naturalism either denies the explanandum, reduces it without remainder, or appeals to brute fact. The same three moves. The same structural failure. The coherence of the naturalist account breaks at exactly the points where the questions reach below mechanism into foundation.

The Reverse Audit does not prove theology. It proves that naturalism cannot close its own circle. The leaks are named. The diagnostic is reproducible. And the question the leaks open — what *can* ground these six conditions? — is the question the rest of the framework spends its strength answering.

## IV. The Method

*Coherence thread: Isomorphism IS coherence across translation. The method is a coherence detection system — it finds structures that hold under pressure across domains and discards the ones that don't.*

---

A substrate without a method is philosophy. The method is what makes it testable.

Four movements. Every researcher thinks they do this. They do not.

**Audit.** Start where nobody starts — at the borrowed foundations. Do not accept the permission any field grants its practitioners to begin. Ask what was assumed before the first claim was made. **Map.** Take a physics process — a phase transition, a symmetry breaking, a decoherence event — and decompose it into its structural stages. Not its vocabulary. Its stages. The actual sequence of what happens, in what order, under what constraints, with what failure modes. Then find the correspondent in another domain. Not a metaphor. Not "this reminds me of that." **Model.** Turn mapped structure into dynamics. Most cross-domain work stays static — "here is a parallel." The method asks: how does it behave over time? What grows? What decays? Under what conditions? The Coherence Equation emerged not because someone went looking for it but because the same mathematical form kept appearing across thermodynamics, population biology, information theory, and moral restoration. Independent convergence on the same differential equation across domains that have no business sharing mathematics is not analogy. It is signal.

**Verify.** Most research stops when the pattern is found. The method begins there. Every claim is subjected to pressure — compression, strongest objection, time, translation, counterevidence, cross-domain transfer, hostile misuse. The framework uses Lean 4 formal verification not because mathematics is the highest truth but because mathematics is the hardest to fool. It uses adversarial AI review not to polish conclusions but to attack them. It classifies every claim: load-bearing, suggestive, or overreached. It demotes its own favorite claims when they fail under pressure. This is not humility as virtue. It is humility as method.

The difference between this method and ordinary interdisciplinary work is the difference between collecting parallels and testing isomorphisms. A parallel says "this looks like that." Parallels are decorative. Isomorphisms are load-bearing.

The method does not claim that physics proves theology or that theology explains physics. It claims that if both describe reality, their deepest structures should not be permanently alien. And it provides a way to test that claim that is not analogy, not metaphor, not dialogue. It is structural correspondence, verified or falsified.

## VII. The Demonstration

*Coherence thread: Superposition is unresolved possibility — pre-coherent. Actualization is coherence — one outcome made real. The measurement problem is the question of how coherence emerges from incoherence. The triadic composition is the minimal operation that produces coherence without regress.*

---

A method that cannot solve a problem is a method that cannot be trusted. This section takes one problem — the deepest unsolved problem in quantum foundations — and solves it with the framework's method.

Since John von Neumann formalized the measurement problem in 1932, it has been open. The question is deceptively simple. Before measurement, a quantum system exists in superposition — multiple possibilities, all real, none definite. After measurement, one outcome becomes actual. What causes the transition?

If a device measures the particle, the device becomes part of the system. What measures the device? A person. The person becomes part of the system. What measures the person? Another observer. The chain never terminates.

Ninety-three years. No consensus solution. Every major interpretation fails at the same structural point.

Copenhagen says measurement causes collapse but cannot define measurement. Many-Worlds removes collapse but cannot explain why any observer experiences one outcome. Decoherence explains why systems appear classical but not why one result becomes actual. Objective collapse introduces mechanisms by hand without deriving them from anything deeper.

The framework's method asks: what do you actually need to make something real? Not observed. Actual.

Three things. Not two. Not four. Three.

You need **possibility** — something must provide the field of what could happen. You need **structure** — possibility alone is noise; something must order possibilities into intelligible form. You need **actualization** — even with possibility and structure, something must make one outcome definite, here, now.

Can one thing do all three? No. Generation and collapse are opposite operations. You cannot hold all possibilities open and simultaneously collapse them. Can two things do it? No. The collapsing agent enters the system and needs its own collapse. Regress returns. Can three separate things do it? No. Independent agents need coordination. The coordinator becomes a fourth element. Regress again.

What is left is composition. Not three watchers in a chain. Three functions in a single operation:

$$T = A \circ L \circ G$$

G generates possibility. L structures it into intelligible form — the eigenbasis, the ordering, the logic. A actualizes one outcome. The composition is closed. No element stands outside the operation. No element requires an external verifier. The operation generates, structures, and actualizes in a single act.

The chain fails because observation is external — each observer watches from outside the prior observer. Composition succeeds because the functions are internal — A applies L to G from within. That is the formal difference between a chain and a closed operation. The chain extends indefinitely. The composition terminates.

The triadic composition is the minimal self-contained actualization operation. Remove any function and the regress reopens. Add a function and you add redundancy without structural necessity. The operation is irreducibly three-in-one.

The framework did not begin with the Trinity. It began with a camera, a question, and a chain that would not close. It followed the logic. It eliminated every alternative. It wrote the mathematics. The structure that survived is three distinct functions in one unified act.
