# Peer Review: "The 24 Anti-Properties"

## A. CITATION QUALITY

**A1: 1 — Source Existence & Verifiability** | Comment: The paper references a single prior work ("The Math Is Moral paper") with no author, publication venue, date, or DOI. This is not a verifiable citation by any academic standard.

**A2: 1 — Source Authority & Relevance** | Comment: Even if the referenced paper existed, no authority or disciplinary context is established. The paper cites no peer-reviewed literature, no philosophical canon, no theological scholarship.

**A3: 0 — Citation Formatting** | Comment: No citations exist in any recognizable academic format. The "Related Work" section at the end contains only vague hyperlinks with no bibliographic information.

**A4: 0 — Citation Density & Distribution** | Comment: Zero citations throughout the entire paper. No engagement with existing literature on ethics, metaethics, philosophy of mathematics, theology, or any relevant field.

## B. FACTUAL ACCURACY

**B1: 2 — Empirical Claim Accuracy** | Comment: The paper makes numerous empirical-sounding claims (e.g., "the adversary's operation looks remarkably similar across cultures, centuries, and contexts") with zero empirical evidence or reference to anthropological, historical, or sociological research.

**B2: 1 — Historical/Contextual Accuracy** | Comment: Claims about "every concentrated power structure" and historical patterns of "the adversary's operation" are asserted without any historical evidence, examples, or engagement with historical scholarship.

**B3: 3 — Mathematical/Formal Accuracy** | Comment: References to "Gödel's theorem," "entropy," "thermodynamic consequences," and "the s = −1 state in the coherence equation" are mathematically meaningless in context. Gödel's incompleteness theorems are invoked as metaphor, not applied formally. No equation is actually presented.

**B4: 2 — Claim-Evidence Alignment** | Comment: The central claim—that 24 properties of mathematical truth are "identical" to properties of moral truth—is asserted without any evidence. No argument is given; the reader is simply referred to an inaccessible prior paper.

## C. LOGICAL RIGOR

**C1: 3 — Deductive Validity** | Comment: The paper's structure is: (1) mathematical truth has properties P1-P24, (2) moral truth has properties P1-P24, (3) therefore they share a source (the Logos). This is a false analogy, not a valid deduction. No proof of property identity is offered.

**C2: 2 — Inductive Strength** | Comment: The inductive leap from "these properties seem similar" to "they share a metaphysical source" to "the inversion describes a personal adversary" is unsupported by any inductive reasoning or evidence.

**C3: 1 — Absence of Fallacies** | Comment: The paper is built on multiple fallacies: false analogy (mathematical truth = moral truth), begging the question (assuming the Logos exists to prove the Logos exists), argument from personal incredulity, and the fallacy of composition.

**C4: 2 — Argument Chain Integrity** | Comment: The argument chain has multiple broken links: (1) properties are asserted, not proven; (2) identity of properties is asserted, not demonstrated; (3) inversion is presented as formal but is merely negation; (4) the leap from structural inversion to personhood is unjustified.

## D. EVIDENCE SUFFICIENCY

**D1: 1 — Claim Strength vs Evidence Weight** | Comment: The claims are among the strongest possible in metaphysics (identity of mathematical and moral truth, formal taxonomy of evil, structural description of a personal adversary). The evidence is zero. The ratio is catastrophic.

**D2: 0 — Counter-Evidence Engagement** | Comment: No counter-evidence, alternative explanations, or objections are considered. The paper does not engage with any philosophical tradition that would challenge its claims (e.g., moral anti-realism, mathematical nominalism, theological skepticism).

**D3: 0 — Replication & Corroboration** | Comment: No reference to any empirical study, philosophical argument, or theological tradition that corroborates the claims. The paper is entirely self-referential.

**D4: 0 — Statistical Rigor** | Comment: No statistical methods, data, or quantitative reasoning of any kind. The number "24" is asserted without justification.

## E. SEMANTIC PRECISION

**E1: 3 — Term Definition Consistency** | Comment: Key terms like "Logos," "invariant," "adversary," "truth," and "moral truth" are used repeatedly but never formally defined. "Logos" shifts between meaning "the source of mathematical truth," "the source of moral truth," and "a personal being."

**E2: 2 — Equivocation Detection** | Comment: The paper systematically equivocates on "truth"—moving between mathematical truth (propositional), moral truth (normative), and revealed truth (theological) as if they were the same thing. "Properties" of mathematical truth (e.g., "necessary") are used in a completely different sense than "properties" of moral claims.

**E3: 1 — Domain Translation Fidelity** | Comment: Concepts from mathematics (necessity, frame-independence, Gödel's theorem), physics (entropy, thermodynamics), and information theory are imported into theology and ethics without any rigorous translation. "Entropy" is used metaphorically, not thermodynamically.

**E4: 2 — Hedging Calibration** | Comment: The paper oscillates between absolute certainty ("The 24 anti-properties are the formal structure of the adversary") and vague qualification ("It felt like something being named"). No calibrated epistemic modesty about the extraordinary claims being made.

## F. ACADEMIC CONVENTION

**F2: 0 — Methodology Transparency** | Comment: No methodology is described. How were the 24 properties derived? How was their identity established? How was the inversion validated? No answer is given.

**F3: 4 — Structure & Flow** | Comment: The paper has a clear organizational structure (properties, anti-properties, implications, diagnostic). The flow is coherent at the macro level, though repetitive.

**F4: 0 — Peer Convention Compliance** | Comment: The paper violates essentially every academic convention: no citations, no methodology, no literature review, no engagement with counterarguments, no formal definitions, no institutional affiliation, no acknowledgments.

## G. WORDING & CLARITY

**G1: 6 — Prose Precision** | Comment: The prose is clear and rhetorically effective, but precision is sacrificed for rhetorical force. "The exact, formal, structural inversion" is not actually formal or exact—it's a list of opposites.

**G2: 5 — Jargon Accessibility** | Comment: The paper uses accessible language but also deploys technical-sounding terms ("negentropy-producing," "Gödelian error," "coherence equation") without explanation, creating a false impression of technical rigor.

**G3: 7 — Concision** | Comment: The paper is reasonably concise for its scope. Each anti-property gets a paragraph. The structure is efficient.

**G4: 4 — Tone Calibration** | Comment: The tone is prophetic and revelatory ("It felt like something being named that had always been there, waiting"), not academic. This is appropriate for a theological meditation but inappropriate for scholarly discourse.

**H2: 1 — Domain Boundary Respect** | Comment: The paper freely crosses between mathematics, ethics, theology, physics, and psychology without respecting the different epistemic standards, methodologies, and validity conditions of each domain.

**H3: 1 — Mapping Rigor** | Comment: The mapping from mathematical properties to moral properties is asserted without any rigorous demonstration. For example, "necessary" in mathematics means "true in all possible worlds" while "necessary" in ethics is a contested philosophical concept with multiple interpretations.

**H4: 0 — Prediction Transfer** | Comment: No predictions are made that could be tested. The "diagnostic" questions (e.g., "Does it require consensus to survive?") are vague and subjective, not operationalized.

## 1. THREE Most Critical Failures

**Failure 1: Complete absence of scholarly engagement.** The paper cites zero sources, engages with zero existing literatures, and provides no verifiable references. This is not merely a formatting issue—it means the paper cannot be evaluated as scholarship. It makes extraordinary claims (mathematical and moral truth are identical, the adversary has a formal structure) without situating them in any philosophical, theological, or scientific tradition. The "Related Work" section contains only hyperlinks with no bibliographic information.

**Failure 2: False formalization.** The paper presents itself as a "formal taxonomy" and "structural description" but provides no formal apparatus whatsoever. Key terms ("Logos," "invariant," "coherence equation," "s = −1 state") are invoked as if they are technical concepts but are never defined, operationalized, or connected to any actual formal system. The "24 properties" are asserted without derivation, proof, or justification. The "inversion" is simply negation, not formal inversion.

**Failure 3: Unsupported central claim.** The paper's foundation—that mathematical truth and moral truth share "24 identical properties"—is never argued for. The reader is simply referred to an inaccessible prior paper. Without this claim, the entire edifice collapses. The paper provides no argument, evidence, or reasoning for the most critical premise on which everything else depends.

## 2. Three Specific Revisions (Ranked by Impact)

**Revision 1 (highest impact): Provide the argument for the 24 properties.** The paper must include the actual argument that mathematical and moral truth share 24 identical properties, not merely assert it and reference an inaccessible paper. This means: (a) defining each property precisely, (b) demonstrating that mathematical truth actually has each property (engaging with philosophy of mathematics), (c) demonstrating that moral truth actually has each property (engaging with metaethics), and (d) showing that the properties are identical, not merely analogous. Without this, the paper has no foundation. This would raise scores across A, B, C, D, E, and J.

**Revision 2 (medium impact): Add substantive engagement with existing scholarship.** The paper must engage with: (a) the philosophical literature on moral truth (e.g., realism vs. anti-realism, naturalism vs. non-naturalism), (b) the theological tradition of evil as privation (Augustine, Aquinas), (c) the philosophy of mathematics (Platonism, formalism, intuitionism), and (d) relevant work in metaethics and philosophical theology. This would raise scores across A, B, D, F, and J.

**Revision 3 (lower impact): Remove false formalization and calibrate epistemic claims.** The paper should either provide actual formal apparatus (equations, definitions, proofs) or drop the pretense of formality. Claims about "Gödel's theorem," "entropy," and "thermodynamics" should either be made rigorous or replaced with appropriate metaphorical language. The tone should shift from prophetic revelation to scholarly argumentation. This would raise scores across E, G, H, and I.

## 3. One Thing the Paper Does Surprisingly Well

The paper has genuine rhetorical power. The structure—24 properties, their inversions, the diagnostic questions—is memorable and practically applicable for its intended audience. The prose is clear, the rhythm is effective, and the conceptual architecture (properties → anti-properties → fruits → diagnostic) is well-organized. For a work of theological meditation or spiritual writing, these are genuine strengths. The paper fails as scholarship but succeeds as rhetoric.
