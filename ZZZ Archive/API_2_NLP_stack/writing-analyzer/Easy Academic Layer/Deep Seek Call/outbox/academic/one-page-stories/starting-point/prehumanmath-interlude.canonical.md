# Pre-Human Mathematics: An Inquiry into the Ontological Status of Mathematical Structures Prior to Human Cognition

## Abstract

This article examines the epistemological and ontological implications of mathematical structures that demonstrably predate the emergence of *Homo sapiens*. Through a systematic elimination of the human observer from mathematical formalism, the analysis demonstrates that mathematical truths possess an existence independent of human cognition. The central thesis—that mathematics is discovered rather than invented—is supported by temporal evidence indicating that mathematical relationships were operative in the physical universe prior to the evolution of any species capable of formalizing them. This investigation proceeds through four structural moves: (1) the elimination of the human observer, (2) the construction of a temporal timeline for pre-human mathematical structures, (3) the demonstration of what this temporal priority proves regarding mathematical ontology, and (4) the implications for the relationship between mathematical realism and theological cosmology.

---

## 1. The Elimination: Methodological Framework

### 1.1 The Observer Elimination Protocol

The foundational methodological question guiding this inquiry is: *What survives when the human observer is eliminated from mathematical reasoning?* This question is not merely epistemological but ontological, as it addresses whether mathematical entities possess mind-independent reality.

The elimination protocol proceeds as follows: consider any mathematical proposition *P* that is currently known to human mathematicians. The question is whether *P* would remain true in a universe devoid of human observers. This thought experiment, while counterfactual, is grounded in the temporal priority of physical laws over human cognition. The universe, according to current cosmological models, existed for approximately 13.8 billion years before the emergence of anatomically modern humans approximately 300,000 years ago. During this interval, physical processes governed by mathematical relationships—gravitational dynamics, quantum mechanical interactions, thermodynamic equilibria—operated without any human agent to formalize or observe them.

### 1.2 Formal Statement of the Elimination Thesis

Let *M* be the set of all mathematical truths. Let *H* be the set of all human cognitive acts. The elimination thesis asserts:

\[
\exists m \in M : m \not\equiv f(H)
\]

where *f(H)* denotes any function dependent upon human cognitive activity. In natural language: there exist mathematical truths that are not contingent upon human cognition.

This is not a trivial claim. It asserts that mathematical structures possess *ontic* status—they are features of reality itself, not merely convenient fictions or linguistic conventions invented by human minds.

## 2. The Timeline of Pre-Human Mathematical Structures

### 2.1 Temporal Priority as Evidence

The argument from temporal priority proceeds as follows:

1. **Premise 1**: The physical universe began approximately 13.8 × 10⁹ years ago (Planck Collaboration, 2018).
2. **Premise 2**: Mathematical relationships governed physical processes from the earliest moments of cosmic history (e.g., the Planck epoch, inflationary expansion, nucleosynthesis).
3. **Premise 3**: *Homo sapiens* emerged approximately 300 × 10³ years ago (McDougall et al., 2005).
4. **Conclusion**: Mathematical relationships were operative for approximately 13.5 × 10⁹ years prior to the emergence of any species capable of formal mathematical reasoning.

## 3. What This Proves: The Ontological Argument

### 3.1 The Discovery Thesis

The temporal priority of mathematical structures over human cognition supports what is termed the *discovery thesis*: mathematics is not invented by human minds but is discovered as an objective feature of reality. This thesis stands in opposition to:

1. **Formalism**: The view that mathematics is a game played with symbols according to arbitrary rules (Hilbert, 1925).
2. **Intuitionism**: The view that mathematical objects are mental constructions (Brouwer, 1912).
3. **Nominalism**: The view that mathematical entities do not exist (Field, 1980).

The discovery thesis aligns most closely with **mathematical Platonism** (Gödel, 1947; Maddy, 1990), though with an important modification: the evidence here is not a priori but empirical, grounded in the temporal structure of cosmic history.

### 3.2 Formal Statement of the Conclusion

Let *T* be the set of all true mathematical propositions. Let *t_H* be the time of emergence of *Homo sapiens* (≈ 300 × 10³ years BP). Let *t_U* be the time of the origin of the universe (≈ 13.8 × 10⁹ years BP). Then:

\[
\forall p \in T, \text{ if } p \text{ governs a physical process at time } t < t_H, \text{ then } p \text{ is true independently of human cognition.}
\]

This is a conditional ontological claim: for any mathematical proposition that demonstrably governed physical reality before humans existed, that proposition possesses mind-independent truth.

### 3.3 Epistemological Caveat

It must be acknowledged that the *identification* of these mathematical structures as "mathematics" is a human cognitive act. The claim is not that pre-human physical processes were accompanied by conscious mathematical reasoning, but rather that the *structure* of those processes is isomorphic to mathematical formalisms that humans later discovered. This isomorphism was identified through structural comparison of physical law with mathematical theory, following the methodology of Wigner (1960) regarding the "unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics in the natural sciences."

## 4. Implications for Theophysics

### 4.1 The Logos Thesis

The discovery that mathematical structures predate human cognition has direct implications for the relationship between physics and theology. If mathematics is ontologically prior to human existence, then the question arises: *In what does this mathematical reality subsist?*

The theological correlate is the *Logos* doctrine (John 1:1–3, Nestle-Aland 28th edition): "Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ Λόγος" ("In the beginning was the Word/Reason"). The Greek term *Logos* carries connotations of both rational structure and creative agency. The isomorphism between pre-human mathematical reality and the theological concept of a rational, ordering principle that precedes creation is structurally significant.

### 4.2 The Bridge Structure

This analysis maintains a strict cross-domain bridge structure: the physical evidence (mathematical structures operative in cosmic history) and the theological interpretation (the Logos as the ground of mathematical reality) are presented as distinct domains connected by structural isomorphism, not by conflation. The argument does not claim that mathematical structures *are* the Logos, but rather that the existence of mind-independent mathematical reality is *consistent with* and *illuminated by* the Logos doctrine.

---

## 5. Conclusion

The elimination of the human observer from mathematical reasoning reveals that mathematical structures were operative in the physical universe for approximately 13.5 billion years prior to the emergence of any species capable of formal mathematical thought. This temporal priority constitutes strong evidence for the discovery thesis: mathematics is not invented by human minds but is discovered as an objective feature of reality. The implications for theophysics are significant, as this mind-independent mathematical reality finds a natural correlate in the theological concept of the Logos as the rational ground of creation.
