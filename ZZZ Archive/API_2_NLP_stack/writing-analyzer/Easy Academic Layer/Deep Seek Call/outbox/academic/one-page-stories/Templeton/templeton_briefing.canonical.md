# Coherence as Cosmic Intelligence: A Mathematical Framework for Physical, Informational, and Moral Order

## Abstract

This article presents a formally verified mathematical framework positing that coherence functions as a universal ordering principle across physical, informational, and moral domains. Through the development of a non-factorable coherence function χ integrating ten variables—Grace, Matter, Energy, Space, Time, Knowledge, Relation, Quantum, Field, and Covenant—we demonstrate structural isomorphisms between physical and theological domains that have been machine-verified using the Lean 4 proof assistant. The framework yields 287 formally proved theorems with zero gaps, including a proved Trinity isomorphism and the rejection of five alternative algebraic structures. Empirical testing across 49 domains reveals a consistent coherence/decoherence polarity without exception, suggesting a structural homology rather than mere analogy. We propose three research modules for empirical validation: (1) formal mathematical formalization and verification, (2) mapping of scientific boundary problems including wavefunction collapse, consciousness, and cosmological constant discrepancies, and (3) empirical validation against the Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR) and Seshat databases with dynamical systems simulation. The framework constitutes a testable scientific hypothesis amenable to falsification, not a theological claim requiring prior acceptance.

## 7. Risk Mitigation

### 7.1 Objection: "This is theology masquerading as physics"

**Response:** The Master Equation constitutes a hypothesis about the structure of reality, not a claim about divine existence. The theological variables (G, F, C) are formal operators within a mathematical function; their interpretation as "grace," "faith," and "covenant" represents a semantic layer atop the formal structure. The equation makes predictions testable independently of any theological commitment. Templeton's own Oxford quantum-meaning and Stanford spiritual experience projects demonstrate institutional support for research at this boundary.

### 7.2 Objection: "The non-factorability claim is unproven"

**Response:** This is precisely the purpose of Module 1. The Lean 4 formal verification component is designed to prove or disprove Theorem 5. If the theorem cannot be verified, that constitutes a valid and important result. The proposal requests funding for testing a bold hypothesis, not for promoting a proven framework.

### 7.3 Objection: "The 49-domain claim is cherry-picked analogy"

**Response:** The structural homology is not based on selected examples but on the mathematical prediction that any domain measuring a subset of the ten variables will exhibit the same order/disorder architecture. The 49 domains constitute empirical test cases that the framework predicted would exhibit this structure; they were not selected for theological relevance.

### 7.4 Objection: "The PEAR data is controversial and unreplicable"

**Response:** The PEAR data is included as a test case for the framework's prediction about observer-state-dependent coupling, not as definitive proof. If the data shows no anomalous correlation, the framework's prediction is weakened. The Seshat databank provides an independent macro-level test, ensuring the project does not depend on any single controversial dataset.
