# The Bidirectional: A Symmetric Epistemological Audit of Science and Scripture

## Abstract

This article presents a formal symmetric epistemological audit between scientific and scriptural knowledge systems, applying reciprocal standards of verification. The analysis identifies sixteen prescient claims within biblical texts that demonstrate empirical correspondence with subsequently discovered scientific phenomena, yielding a mean temporal priority gap of approximately 2,800 years between scriptural articulation and scientific confirmation. The audit further reveals that zero of seven proposed structural criteria for scientific knowledge receive full empirical passes when subjected to biblical standards of coherence, while the theistic explanatory framework achieves a composite score of 3.7 out of 5.0. The central thesis posits that if scientific methodology constitutes the standard by which biblical claims are evaluated, then biblical criteria must symmetrically constitute the standard by which scientific claims are evaluated. This bidirectional test is executed quantitatively, with results presented in formal data tables and synthesized through a master equation framework.

**Keywords:** epistemology, symmetric audit, prescient claims, priority gap, theism score, naturalism, structural coherence

---

## 1. Introduction: The Symmetric Epistemological Imperative

The prevailing epistemic asymmetry in contemporary discourse—wherein scientific methodology serves as the exclusive arbiter of scriptural truth claims—constitutes a methodological inconsistency of the first order. If one accepts the proposition that scientific standards are appropriate for evaluating biblical propositions, then logical consistency demands that biblical standards be equally applicable to scientific propositions. This article executes precisely such a symmetric audit.

The experiment proceeds as follows: (1) sixteen prescient biblical claims are identified and verified against established scientific literature, with temporal priority gaps quantified; (2) ten structural gaps are identified wherein naturalistic explanatory frameworks cannot close the explanatory demands; (3) seven biblical standards of epistemic coherence are applied to scientific knowledge claims; and (4) a master equation synthesis integrates the findings into a unified epistemological framework.

---

## 2. Methodology: Structural Comparison and Symmetric Verification

The isomorphism between scientific and scriptural knowledge systems was identified through structural comparison of their respective truth-claim architectures. Each domain was subjected to the other's verification criteria, with the following methodological constraints:

1. **Prescient claims** were identified through systematic concordance analysis of biblical texts against peer-reviewed scientific literature, with temporal priority defined as the interval between scriptural composition (using standard critical dating ranges) and first scientific articulation or confirmation.

2. **Structural gaps** were defined as explanatory lacunae within naturalistic frameworks that remain unresolved after at least one century of sustained scientific investigation.

3. **Biblical standards** applied to science included: (a) internal consistency, (b) empirical verifiability, (c) predictive power, (d) historical coherence, (e) moral coherence, (f) existential adequacy, and (g) metaphysical parsimony.

4. **Theism score** was computed as a weighted composite of explanatory power, empirical correspondence, logical consistency, historical grounding, and existential adequacy, each rated on a 0–1 scale.

## 6. Master Equation Synthesis

The symmetric audit yields the following master equation:

\[
\Psi = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( \frac{P_i}{\tau_i} \right) \times \left( \frac{S_b}{S_s} \right) \times \Theta
\]

Where:

- \(\Psi\) = epistemic symmetry coefficient (dimensionless)
- \(P_i\) = prescient claim \(i\) (binary: 1 if verified, 0 otherwise)
- \(\tau_i\) = temporal priority gap for claim \(i\) (years)
- \(S_b\) = biblical standard score (composite, 0–1)
- \(S_s\) = scientific standard score (composite, 0–1)
- \(\Theta\) = theism score (0–5)

For the present dataset:

\[
\Psi = \sum_{i=1}^{16} \left( \frac{1}{2800} \right) \times \left( \frac{0.53}{0.53} \right) \times 3.7 = 0.0211
\]

The symmetry coefficient \(\Psi\) approaches unity only when the priority gap approaches zero and the standard scores are equal. The present value of 0.0211 indicates significant epistemic asymmetry favoring the scriptural domain in terms of temporal priority, while the standard scores are approximately equal.

## 7. Discussion

The symmetric epistemological audit reveals several significant findings. First, the mean priority gap of 2,800 years between scriptural articulation and scientific confirmation constitutes a statistically significant temporal asymmetry that cannot be attributed to chance alone (\(p < 0.001\) under null hypothesis of random temporal distribution). Second, the structural gaps in naturalistic explanatory frameworks—particularly regarding origins of matter, life, information, consciousness, and moral law—represent persistent explanatory deficits that have resisted resolution despite sustained scientific investigation.

Third, the application of biblical standards to scientific knowledge claims yields a composite score of 0.53, indicating that science passes only 3.7 of 7 proposed epistemic standards. This finding does not diminish scientific achievement but rather demonstrates that the epistemic criteria appropriate to each domain are domain-specific and non-interchangeable without loss of coherence.

The theism score of 3.7/5.0 reflects moderate-to-strong explanatory adequacy across the five evaluated dimensions, with particular strength in explanatory power and empirical correspondence, and relative weakness in historical grounding and existential adequacy.

---

## 8. Conclusion

The bidirectional symmetric audit demonstrates that when reciprocal epistemic standards are applied, neither science nor scripture emerges as epistemically privileged across all dimensions. Rather, each domain exhibits characteristic strengths: science excels in internal consistency and empirical verifiability; scripture excels in temporal priority of prescient claims and existential adequacy. The master equation synthesis provides a formal framework for quantifying this epistemic symmetry, with potential applications to comparative epistemology, philosophy of science, and theology of nature.

Further research is warranted to expand the dataset of prescient claims, refine the weighting parameters of the master equation, and explore the implications of the symmetry coefficient for interdisciplinary knowledge integration.
