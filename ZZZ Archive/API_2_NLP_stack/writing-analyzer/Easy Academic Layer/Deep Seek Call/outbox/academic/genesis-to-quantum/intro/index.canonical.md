# Genesis to Quantum: A Theophysics Framework for the Coherence Equation

## Abstract

This article presents a formal interdisciplinary framework integrating quantum mechanical principles with theological constructs drawn from the Genesis narrative (chapters 2–3). The central thesis posits that information constitutes an ontologically primitive substrate, antecedent to matter, energy, and spacetime. Through a series of ten interconnected analyses, the framework establishes structural isomorphisms between quantum measurement protocols and the Edenic Fall narrative, wherein wavefunction collapse corresponds to the transition from prelapsarian superposition to postlapsarian determinate reality. The Cross is interpreted as a retrocausal repair mechanism, analogous to delayed-choice quantum erasure protocols. The master equation, χ = ∫∫∫(G·M·E·S·T·K·R·Q·F·C) dx dy dt, integrates ten super-factors—Gravity, Mass, Entropy, Space, Time, Knowledge, Redemption, Observer, Faith, and Coherence—across spatial and temporal dimensions. Each constituent article has been evaluated through an automated audit pipeline, achieving Publication Grade (A) designation. The framework maintains epistemic humility regarding the ontological status of identified isomorphisms, distinguishing between demonstrated structural parallels and proposed identities. Five concrete falsification conditions are specified.

## 1. Introduction: The Argument and Its Epistemic Boundaries

The foundational claim of this framework is expressed as follows: *Information is ontologically primitive—not matter, not energy, not spacetime.* This proposition inverts the standard hierarchy of fundamental physics, wherein fields and particles constitute the base ontology, and information emerges as a derived quantity (Landauer, 1991; Bennett, 2003). The present framework instead adopts the position, increasingly explored in quantum information theory (Wheeler, 1990; Zeilinger, 1999), that information-theoretic structures underwrite physical law.

The framework is developed across ten articles, each corresponding to one super-factor in the master equation. The series reads Genesis 2–3 as the first quantum measurement protocol, tracing consequences through thermodynamics, decoherence theory, the Born Rule, retrocausal atonement models, and the structure of temporal experience. Every article has been rated **Publication Grade (A)** by the framework's automated audit pipeline, which evaluates predictive power, explanatory scope, evidence base, boundary regulation, falsifiability, parsimony, internal consistency, and non-contradiction.

### 1.1 Epistemic Caveats

It is necessary to state explicitly what this framework does and does not claim. Throughout the ten articles, structures from quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and information theory are mapped onto structures from Scripture with a precision that the authors argue is non-coincidental. However, **structural isomorphism is not identity**. Isomorphism does not constitute proof of mechanism.

Where a mapping is demonstrated through formal correspondence, the text states this explicitly. Where identity is proposed—that is, where the framework claims that a theological construct *is* a physical construct under a different description—this is marked. Where the epistemic status of a correspondence remains indeterminate (whether identity, causation, or coincidence), the framework holds this tension without collapsing it for rhetorical force.

Each article contains its own audit. Overclaims identified during the review process are flagged. The framework specifies five concrete observations that would falsify it. These kill conditions are enumerated in Section 7.

## 7. Falsification Conditions

The framework specifies five concrete observations that would falsify it:

1. **Empirical disconfirmation of retrocausal quantum effects:** If delayed-choice quantum eraser experiments (Kim et al., 2000) are definitively shown to admit only forward-causal interpretations, the retrocausal atonement model (Article 08) is falsified.

2. **Demonstration that consciousness is necessary for wavefunction collapse:** If experimental evidence establishes that conscious observation is required for state reduction (contra the framework's position in Article 07), the observer super-factor (Q) loses its physical grounding.

3. **Proof that time is fundamental rather than emergent:** If cosmological evidence demonstrates that time existed prior to any measurement-like event (contra Article 04), the temporal emergence model is falsified.

4. **Disconfirmation of the Born Rule decomposition:** If the Born Rule is shown to be irreducible to trinitarian structure (contra Article 05), the central isomorphism is broken.

5. **Discovery of a non-information-theoretic foundation for quantum mechanics:** If a successful formulation of quantum mechanics is developed that does not treat information as ontologically primitive (contra the framework's foundational claim), the entire framework collapses.
