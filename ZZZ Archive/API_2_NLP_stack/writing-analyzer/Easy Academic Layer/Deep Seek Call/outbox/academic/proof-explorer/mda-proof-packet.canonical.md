# Series Proof Packet: Moral Decline of America

## Abstract

This document constitutes the formal proof-layer packet for the Moral Decline of America (MDA) corpus, a multi-domain investigation into the coherence decay of American social and moral structures across the period 1900–2025. The series advances the thesis that American moral and social coherence can be quantitatively tracked as a multi-domain decline rather than a single-cause cultural complaint, employing a cross-domain analytical framework that integrates empirical indicators, mathematical modeling, and structural correspondence analysis. The packet serves to separate live article routing, raw extraction outputs, proof-layer claims, personal or testimonial material, and falsification conditions, thereby maintaining epistemic integrity within the proof layer. The corpus comprises 61 live HTML articles and 63 markdown companion files, from which 442 raw claims were extracted and subsequently reduced to five promoted proof-layer candidates via the Seven-Question Screen (7QS) classification system. A total of 160 equations were identified as translatable across the series. The framework is subject to five explicit kill conditions, none of which have been satisfied to date.

## 3. Seven-Question Classification (7QS)

The 7Q Engine was applied to the series thesis. Forward classification indicates what the series claims at each level.

### Q0 · Posture
**Posture:** Human-led, evidence-seeking, with personal/testimonial motive separated from proof claims.

### Q1 · Existence
**Object:** Measurable change in American social/moral coherence across 1900–2025.

### Q2 · Knowledge
**Scope:** MDA series claims are bounded to observed institutional, family, economic, technological, semantic, and religious indicators.

### Q3 · Mechanism
**Mechanism:** Coherence loss, feedback-loop collapse, semantic thinning, and domain-coupled social phase transition.

### Q4 · Evidence
**Evidence:** Grader/NLP outputs, markdown companions, source ledgers, timeline workbooks, citations, and article-level scorecards.

### Q5 · Implications
**Implications:** The proof layer should expose claims, citations, equations, and kill conditions without forcing every narrative sentence into proof status.

### Q6 · Falsification
**Falsification:** A competing model with stronger predictive accuracy, fewer parameters, different mechanism, or domain-level countertrend breaks the claim.

### Q7 · Residue
**Residue:** Theological interpretation and personal calling remain labeled, not smuggled in as empirical proof.

## 6. Falsification Tests (Kill Conditions)

The following conditions would break the series' argument. These constitute the live kill conditions.

### KILL-01: Countertrend Break (Active)
A major claimed domain shows increasing coherence during the same period while the framework predicts collapse. Requires domain-level evidence and date-bound source trail.

### KILL-02: Better Competing Model (Active)
A competing model explains the same data with higher predictive accuracy, fewer parameters, and a different causal mechanism. This is the strongest publication-facing falsification condition.

### KILL-03: Personal Claim Leakage (Active)
A personal/testimonial statement is presented as framework proof rather than motive, witness, or interpretive context. This packet explicitly labels that boundary.
