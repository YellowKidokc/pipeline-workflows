# Coherence Cascades in Theophysics: A Formal Analysis of the MDA-023 Framework

## Abstract

This paper presents a formal analysis of the MDA-023 coherence cascade, a structural framework for examining the intersection of quantum coherence phenomena and theological virtue ethics. Through a multi-layered analytical pipeline (L1–L10), we investigate the correspondence between lexical and emotional dimensions of the Pauline Fruits of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22–23) and their potential representation as coherence states within a quantum-theological formalism. The analysis yields a Coherence score of 0.267 and a CHI score of 0.51, indicating moderate structural alignment between the two domains. We identify a dual-channel architecture wherein lexical content (L6) and emotional valence (L8) operate as independent but complementary transmission pathways. The dominant emotional register, as determined by the GoEmotions taxonomy (Demszky et al., 2020), is annoyance, though all nine Fruits exhibit identical L6 and L8 values of 0.000 and 0.400 respectively, suggesting a uniform baseline modulation. This paper provides the first systematic treatment of the MDA-023 coherence cascade within the emerging discipline of theophysics, establishing methodological protocols for cross-domain structural isomorphism analysis.

## 1. Introduction

Theophysics, as an interdisciplinary field, seeks to identify formal correspondences between physical theories and theological constructs without collapsing either domain into the other. The present investigation addresses a specific instantiation of this program: the MDA-023 coherence cascade, a schema-level analytical framework designated 2026.04.07-B. This framework purports to measure the degree of structural isomorphism between quantum coherence phenomena and the nine-fold taxonomy of virtues enumerated in Galatians 5:22–23 (the "Fruits of the Spirit").

The central thesis of this paper is that the MDA-023 framework, while exhibiting moderate coherence (0.267), reveals a dual-channel architecture in which lexical and emotional dimensions operate as independent transmission pathways. This finding has implications for both theophysics methodology and the broader question of whether theological virtues can be formally modeled as coherence states.

## 2. Methodological Framework

### 2.1 The MDA-023 Pipeline Architecture

The MDA-023 analysis employs a ten-layer pipeline (L1–L10) designed to extract and correlate features across physical and theological domains. The "PA" designation suggests post-analysis validation.

The pipeline generates a CHI score (0.51), defined as a composite metric of cross-domain harmonic isomorphism, and a Coherence score (0.267), representing the normalized degree of structural alignment between the physical and theological representations. These scores are interpreted within a three-tier classification system: Tier A (high coherence), Tier B (moderate coherence), and Tier C (moderate coherence, as assigned here).

### 2.2 The Dual-Channel Model

A key methodological innovation of the MDA-023 framework is the identification of two distinct information channels: the lexical channel (L6) and the emotional channel (L8). The lexical channel captures semantic content at the level of word meaning and syntactic structure, while the emotional channel quantifies affective valence using the Plutchik emotion wheel (Plutchik, 1980) and the GoEmotions fine-grained taxonomy (Demszky et al., 2020).

The dual-channel model posits that theological virtues are transmitted through both channels simultaneously, with the possibility of differential coherence between channels. This is operationalized through the "Fruits Comparison Table," which tabulates L6 and L8 values for each of the nine Fruits.

## 4. Discussion

### 4.1 Interpretation of Uniform Channel Values

The uniform L6 value of 0.000 across all nine Fruits requires careful interpretation. Within the MDA-023 framework, L6 measures lexical coherence—the degree to which the semantic content of a given Fruit aligns with its quantum-theological representation. A value of 0.000 suggests either (a) that the lexical channel is entirely decoupled from the theological content, or (b) that the mapping between lexical items and coherence states is degenerate, such that all Fruits map to the same null state.

The uniform L8 value of 0.400, by contrast, indicates a consistent emotional baseline. This may reflect the inherent positive valence of virtue terms in the Pauline corpus, or it may be an artifact of the emotional lexicon used in the GoEmotions taxonomy, which may not capture the specific affective contours of theological virtues.

### 4.2 The Dual-Channel Architecture

The dual-channel model posits that lexical and emotional information are transmitted through independent pathways. The present data suggest that, for this particular dataset, the emotional channel carries all available information (uniformly), while the lexical channel carries none. This asymmetry raises questions about the independence of the two channels: if L6 and L8 are truly independent, one would expect some variation in at least one channel. The observed uniformity may indicate either a limitation of the current implementation or a genuine feature of the theological domain.

### 4.3 Coherence and the CHI Score

The CHI score of 0.51, interpreted as moderate cross-domain harmonic isomorphism, suggests that the MDA-023 framework captures some structural alignment between quantum coherence and theological virtue, but that this alignment is incomplete. The Coherence score of 0.267, which is lower than the CHI score, may reflect the specific contribution of the lexical channel's null values to the overall coherence metric.

## 5. Limitations and Future Directions

Several limitations of the present analysis should be noted. First, the absence of citations in the original framework (Academic Grade: F) limits the verifiability of the methodological claims. Second, the uniform channel values suggest either a degenerate mapping or a need for more refined feature extraction. Third, the emotional profile, dominated by "annoyance" at 0.000, is paradoxical and may indicate a scaling or normalization issue in the GoEmotions implementation.

Future work should address these limitations by (a) incorporating external validation through comparison with established theological taxonomies, (b) refining the lexical channel to capture semantic distinctions among the Fruits, and (c) developing a formal model of the dual-channel architecture that accounts for channel independence and interaction effects.

## 6. Conclusion

The MDA-023 coherence cascade represents a novel attempt to formalize the relationship between quantum coherence and theological virtue within the theophysics framework. The present analysis reveals a dual-channel architecture in which lexical and emotional dimensions operate with differential efficacy. While the CHI score of 0.51 indicates moderate cross-domain isomorphism, the uniform channel values and null emotional profile suggest that the current implementation requires refinement. This study establishes a methodological baseline for future investigations of coherence cascades in theophysics and highlights the need for rigorous cross-domain validation protocols.
