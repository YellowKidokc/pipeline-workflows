# A Formal Presentation of Theophysical Laws: Structural Isomorphisms Between Physical Fields and Spiritual Realms

## Abstract

This article presents a systematic framework identifying ten structural isomorphisms between fundamental physical laws and corresponding spiritual principles, herein termed "theophysical laws." Through rigorous term-by-term comparison, we demonstrate that each physical equation maps to a spiritual analogue via consistent variable substitution, with the singular addition of an agent-choice asymmetry term absent from the physical formulation. The framework posits that nine of ten laws exhibit this asymmetry, while the tenth—the coherence integral—achieves identity between domains upon resolution of all asymmetry terms. Formal verification was conducted using Wolfram Mathematica 14.3.0 (February 4, 2026) and JAX 0.7.2 (March 2026). The claim advanced is not metaphorical but structural: the equations are identical in form, differing only in variable assignment and the consistent placement of a free-will parameter.

**Thesis Statement:** The physical universe and the spiritual domain share identical mathematical structures across ten fundamental laws, with the sole systematic difference being the presence of an agent-choice asymmetry term in the spiritual formulation—a term that physics cannot generate from within its own axioms.

## 1. Introduction: Methodological Framework

The present work employs a method of structural comparison between established physical formalisms and proposed spiritual analogues. For each physical law, we identify: (1) the canonical mathematical expression, (2) a term-by-term substitution mapping to spiritual variables, (3) the asymmetry term unique to the spiritual formulation, and (4) the epistemological justification for each mapping. The asymmetry terms are derived from the observation that physical systems lack agential decision-making capacity, whereas spiritual systems incorporate it as a fundamental parameter.
