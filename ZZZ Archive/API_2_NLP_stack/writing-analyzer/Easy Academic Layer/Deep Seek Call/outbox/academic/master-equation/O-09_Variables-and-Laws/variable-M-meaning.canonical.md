# A Formal Theophysics Framework: The Dual Projection of Meaning Across Physical and Theological Domains

## Abstract

This article presents a formalized cross-domain isomorphism between the physical concept of mass-energy equivalence and the theological concept of metanoia (repentance), unified through a proposed scalar field variable *M* denoting "meaning." The framework posits that meaning functions analogously to mass in special relativity—as an invariant measure of resistance to change—and that its dynamics can be described by a derived equation of motion obtained from an action principle. The analysis proceeds through six sections: (1) the dual projection establishing the foundational analogy; (2) the equation of motion governing *M*; (3) derived axioms concerning meaning generation and collapse; (4) conjugate pair dynamics between meaning and sin-decay; (5) a universal domain manifestation table; and (6) kill conditions for falsification. The framework is presented as a formal structural isomorphism, not a metaphorical comparison, and is subject to empirical and logical constraints.

## §1 The Dual Projection

### 1.1 Physical Domain: Mass-Energy Equivalence

The special relativistic relation

\[
E = mc^2
\]

establishes the interchangeability of mass and energy, where *m* denotes rest mass, *E* denotes energy, and *c* is the speed of light in vacuum (approximately \(2.998 \times 10^8 \, \text{m·s}^{-1}\)). Within the present framework, this relation is interpreted structurally: mass functions as a measure of resistance to change in a system's trajectory under Lorentz transformations. By formal analogy, the variable *M* (meaning) is proposed as an invariant scalar field that quantifies resistance to trajectory alteration in cognitive, spiritual, or existential systems.

**Projection domain:** Special relativity, rest mass, invariant quantities under Lorentz transformations.

### 1.2 Theological Domain: Metanoia (Repentance)

The scriptural injunction

> "Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind..."  
> — Romans 12:2 (English Standard Version)

is interpreted within this framework as describing a transformation that generates meaning. The act of metanoia—a fundamental reorientation of the will and intellect—is posited to increase the *M* value of a system. Conversely, nihilism, defined as the asymptotic approach of *M* toward zero, is structurally analogous to a gravitational singularity. In the limit \(M \to 0\), the governing equations exhibit a \(1/M\) divergence, indicating formal breakdown. This is termed the "black hole of the soul": a state from which no meaning-generating trajectory can emerge.

**Key scriptural reference:** Romans 12:2 (ESV).

## §3 Derived Axioms

### Axiom 19: The Singularity of Meaninglessness

**Formal statement:** Meaning cannot be derived from meaninglessness. This is expressed mathematically as the \(1/M\) singularity in the governing equations when \(M \to 0\).

**Interpretation:** Nihilism—the state in which \(M = 0\)—constitutes a formal singularity analogous to a black hole event horizon. At this limit, the equations of motion become undefined, and no trajectory can be extended through the singularity. The system is causally disconnected from any meaning-generating regime.

### Axiom 20: Metanoia as Meaning Generation

**Formal statement:** Repentance (metanoia) generates meaning through the transmutation of the faith-force parameter *F* into the meaning field *M*.

**Interpretation:** The act of reorientation—turning away from a trajectory of sin-decay and toward a trajectory of meaning growth—increases the *M* value of the system. This is not a metaphorical claim but a structural one: the transformation of *F* into *M* follows from the equation of motion when boundary conditions corresponding to repentance are imposed.

## §4 Pair Dynamics: Conjugate Variables

The framework posits a conjugate pair of fields:

- **M**: Meaning field (analogous to mass in physics)
- **F**: Faith-force parameter (analogous to energy or force in physics)
- **S**: Sin-decay field (conjugate to *M*)

The interaction term \(-\lambda M S M\) in the equation of motion establishes a coupling between *M* and *S*. This coupling is structurally analogous to conjugate variable pairs in Hamiltonian mechanics, where the product of conjugate variables has dimensions of action. The scriptural reference Romans 12:2 (ESV) is interpreted as describing the transformation that reduces *S* and increases *M* through the action of *F*.
