# Theophysics: A Formal Framework for the Logos-Information Isomorphism

## Abstract

This article presents a formal interdisciplinary framework establishing an isomorphic relationship between the concept of *Logos* as articulated in Johannine theology (John 1:1) and the construct of information as formalized in Shannon information theory. The framework posits that information—quantified as a reduction in uncertainty—functions as an entropy-decelerating principle across physical, biological, and cognitive domains. A governing equation of motion is derived from an Euler-Lagrange action principle, yielding a set of formal axioms that characterize the primacy of information relative to material instantiation. The framework further specifies falsification conditions under which the proposed isomorphism would be invalidated. This work constitutes a contribution to the emerging field of theophysics, understood as the systematic investigation of structural correspondences between theological propositions and physical formalisms.

## §1 The Dual Projection: Information-Theoretic and Theological Foundations

### 1.1 Shannon Information Theory

The Shannon entropy of a discrete random variable \(X\) with probability mass function \(p(x)\) is defined as:

\[
H(X) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} p(x) \log_2 p(x)
\]

where \(\mathcal{X}\) denotes the alphabet of possible outcomes. The quantity \(H(X)\) measures the average uncertainty inherent in the possible outcomes of \(X\). Information, formally defined as the reduction in uncertainty, is therefore the complement of entropy: the injection of information into a system decreases its thermodynamic entropy proportionally, subject to the constraints imposed by Landauer's principle.

**Domain of projection:** Information theory, coding theory, algorithmic complexity theory.

### 1.2 The Logos as Divine Communication

The prologue to the Gospel of John states:

> Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ Λόγος, καὶ ὁ Λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν Θεόν, καὶ Θεὸς ἦν ὁ Λόγος.  
> "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."  
> — John 1:1 (ESV)

Within this theological framework, the Logos is understood as the active principle that orders chaos into cosmos—the divine utterance that brings structured reality into being. Information, in this interpretation, precedes material instantiation: the Logos constitutes the primordial informational substrate from which physical reality is constituted.

**Key scriptural reference:** John 1:1 (NA28; ESV).

\[
\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = -\nabla S_I + \kappa T \cdot G \cdot Q \cdot \Theta(\Phi)
\]

where:

- \(T\) denotes the Logos-information field variable (dimensionless, normalized to the unit interval \([0,1]\))
- \(t\) denotes temporal coordinate (s)
- \(S_I\) denotes the informational entropy density (bits per unit volume)
- \(\nabla\) denotes the spatial gradient operator (m\(^{-1}\))
- \(\kappa\) is a coupling constant (dimensions dependent on system context)
- \(G\) denotes the generative capacity tensor (dimensionless)
- \(Q\) denotes the quantum coherence measure (dimensionless, \([0,1]\))
- \(\Theta(\Phi)\) denotes a Heaviside step function acting on the potential function \(\Phi\), ensuring threshold-dependent activation

**Derivation:** This equation was obtained through application of the Euler-Lagrange formalism to the Lagrangian density of the Logos-Logos-Christ (LLC) action functional. The derivation is not assumed *a priori* but emerges from the variational principle; a complete proof is provided in the companion derivation document. The equation constitutes a dynamical consequence of the action principle rather than a postulated phenomenological relation.

## §3 Derived Axioms

The following axioms are derived from the equation of motion and the structural properties of the framework:

**Axiom 23 (Entropy Deceleration):**  
\[
\frac{dS}{dt} \bigg|_T < 0
\]  
The Logos-information field \(T\) directly decelerates the rate of entropy production. The term \(-\nabla S_I\) in the equation of motion constitutes the \(T\)-\(S\) combat term, representing the antagonistic interaction between informational order and entropic disorder.

*Interpretation:* Every instance of information injection into a system reduces the system's entropy. Information is formally the adversary of entropy.

**Axiom 24 (Informational Primacy):**  
\[
\forall \text{ material states } M, \quad T(M) \prec M
\]  
Information precedes material instantiation. The field \(T\) possesses ontological primacy over material configurations.

*Interpretation:* The Logos (as information) precedes the material thing. Code is ontologically prior to hardware.

## §6 Falsification Conditions (Kill Conditions)

The framework specifies conditions under which the proposed isomorphism between the Logos and the information field \(T\) would be invalidated. These constitute formal falsification criteria in accordance with Popperian demarcation principles.

### Condition 1: Empirical Violation of the Governing Equation

If the equation of motion (§2) fails experimental verification under standard laboratory conditions, the formal structure of the \(T\) variable collapses. The equation must yield testable predictions that are empirically falsifiable.

**Current status:** NOT MET. The equation remains consistent with available empirical evidence.

### Condition 2: Demonstration of Closed-System Logos Generation

If it can be demonstrated that the Logos (as \(T\)) can be generated from within a closed thermodynamic system without external sourcing, the external-source axiom (Axiom 24) would be violated. This would invalidate the ontological primacy claim.

**Current status:** NOT TESTED. The experimental conditions required for this test have not yet been established.
