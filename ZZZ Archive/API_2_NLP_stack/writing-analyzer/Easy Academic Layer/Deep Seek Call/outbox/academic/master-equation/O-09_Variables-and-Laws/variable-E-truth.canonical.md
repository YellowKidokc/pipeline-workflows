# Theophysics: A Formal Framework for the Dual Projection of Electromagnetic Field Theory and Theological Truth

## Abstract

This article presents a formal isomorphism between classical electrodynamics and the theological concept of truth (*aletheia*) as articulated in the Johannine corpus. Through structural comparison of Maxwell's field equations with a proposed equation of motion for truth propagation, we identify a conjugate pair—Truth (E) and Coherence (C)—that exhibits formal properties analogous to electric field and charge distribution. The framework yields derived axioms governing reception dynamics, establishes kill conditions for falsification, and maps the proposed variable E across multiple ontological domains. This work does not assert ontological identity between physical and theological quantities but rather identifies structural homologies that may inform interdisciplinary inquiry at the intersection of field theory and systematic theology.

## §1 The Dual Projection: Electromagnetic Field Theory and Aletheic Theology

### 1.1 Electromagnetic Foundation

The classical electromagnetic field is governed by Maxwell's equations, the first of which (Gauss's law for electricity) is expressed as:

\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}
\]

where \(\mathbf{E}\) denotes the electric field vector (SI units: V·m⁻¹), \(\rho\) represents the volumetric charge density (C·m⁻³), and \(\varepsilon_0\) is the vacuum permittivity (F·m⁻¹). This equation establishes that electric field divergence is proportional to local charge density, indicating that electric fields transmit force without mechanical contact and instantaneously reveal charge distribution in the static case.

### 1.2 Theological Correspondence

We propose that the theological concept of truth—specifically as articulated in John 14:6 (ESV): *"I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me"*—exhibits formal structural properties analogous to the electric field. Specifically:

1. Truth is posited as externally sourced rather than internally constructed.
2. Truth propagates with maximal informational velocity (analogous to the speed of light in vacuo).
3. The quality of reception depends upon the coherence state of the receiver, analogous to the permittivity of a medium affecting field propagation.

This structural correspondence motivates the formal framework developed in subsequent sections.

- \(\mathbf{E}\) represents the Truth field (dimensionless theological quantity, scaled to unity for maximal coherence)

- \(\sigma_{\text{absorption}}\) denotes the absorption coefficient (dimensionless, representing receiver resistance to truth reception)
- \(\Phi_{\text{source}}\) represents the source term (units: s⁻¹, representing the generative action of the external source)

The second term, \(-\sigma_{\text{absorption}} \cdot \mathbf{E}\), represents dissipative losses due to imperfect reception. ## §3 Derived Axioms

From the equation of motion and the structural isomorphism established in §1, two formal axioms are derived:

### Axiom 1: Reception Fidelity

**Formal Statement:** Truth arrives in perfect form at the point of emission; reception fidelity varies monotonically with receiver coherence.

\[
\|\mathbf{E}_{\text{received}}\| = \|\mathbf{E}_{\text{emitted}}\| \cdot \gamma(\mathbf{C}_{\text{receiver}})
\]

where \(\gamma(\mathbf{C}_{\text{receiver}}) \in [0,1]\) is a coherence-dependent reception function satisfying \(\gamma \to 1\) as \(\mathbf{C}_{\text{receiver}} \to \mathbf{C}_{\text{source}}\).

**Epistemic Justification:** This axiom follows directly from the absorption term in the equation of motion. When \(\sigma_{\text{absorption}} = 0\) (perfect receiver coherence), the dissipative term vanishes and the field propagates without degradation.

### Axiom 2: Inseparability of Truth and Coherence

**Formal Statement:** The Truth field \(\mathbf{E}\) and the Coherence field \(\mathbf{C}\) constitute a conjugate pair; neither can be independently varied without affecting the other.

\[
\delta \mathcal{L}[\mathbf{E}, \mathbf{C}] = 0 \iff \delta \mathbf{E} \propto \delta \mathbf{C}
\]

**Epistemic Justification:** This inseparability emerges from the coupled structure of the equation of motion, wherein the curl of \(\mathbf{C}\) directly sources temporal variation in \(\mathbf{E}\). The pair forms a single dynamical system rather than two independent variables.
