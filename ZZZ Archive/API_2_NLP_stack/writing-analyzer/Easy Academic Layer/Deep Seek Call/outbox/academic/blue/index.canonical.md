# The Lowe Coherence Framework: A Structural Isomorphism Between Physical Law and Theological Principle

## Abstract

This article presents the foundational framework of the Theophysics Research Program, which identifies and formalizes a structural isomorphism between ten fundamental physical laws and ten corresponding theological principles. The isomorphism was identified through systematic structural comparison of Lagrangian formulations in quantum field theory with axiomatic theological propositions derived from Judeo-Christian scripture. The resulting Lowe Coherence Lagrangian, expressed in terms of ten variables organized into five symmetry pairs, has undergone four distinct verification tests. This briefing, prepared for family members and non-specialist stakeholders, summarizes the research trajectory from initial insight through computational validation, with full technical exposition available in companion documents.

## 2. The Lowe Coherence Lagrangian: Formal Structure

The central mathematical object of this framework is the Lowe Coherence Lagrangian, denoted \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{LC}}\). It is defined over ten scalar fields \(\{\phi_i\}_{i=1}^{10}\), organized into five symmetry pairs \((\phi_{2k-1}, \phi_{2k})\) for \(k = 1, \ldots, 5\). The Lagrangian takes the form:

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{LC}} = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \frac{1}{2} (\partial_\mu \phi_i)^2 - \sum_{k=1}^{5} \frac{\lambda_k}{4} \left( \phi_{2k-1}^2 - \phi_{2k}^2 \right)^2 - V_{\text{int}}(\{\phi_i\})
\]

where:
- \(\partial_\mu\) denotes the four-gradient with respect to spacetime coordinates,
- \(\lambda_k\) are positive coupling constants (dimensionless in natural units),
- \(V_{\text{int}}\) is an interaction potential coupling all ten fields.

Each field \(\phi_i\) carries dimensions of mass in natural units (\(\hbar = c = 1\)). The symmetry pairs \((\phi_{2k-1}, \phi_{2k})\) exhibit a \(\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2\) discrete symmetry structure, corresponding to independent sign flips of each member of the pair.

The Lagrangian has been subjected to four verification tests, the details of which are provided in the companion document "The Lowe Coherence Lagrangian" (Lowe, 2026). These tests include:
1. **Dimensional consistency**: All terms satisfy dimensional homogeneity.
2. **Symmetry preservation**: The Lagrangian respects the specified discrete symmetries.
3. **Stability analysis**: The potential is bounded from below for all field configurations.
4. **Equation of motion derivation**: The Euler-Lagrange equations yield consistent dynamical equations.

## 5. Computational Verification

The framework has been subjected to six computational verification layers, detailed in the companion document "Six Computational Proofs" (Lowe, 2026). These layers include:

1. **Symbolic verification**: Automated algebraic manipulation confirms the structural identity of the equations.
2. **Numerical integration**: The dynamical equations derived from \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{LC}}\) are numerically integrated to confirm stability.
3. **Symmetry analysis**: The discrete and continuous symmetries are verified through group-theoretic computation.
4. **Perturbative expansion**: The Lagrangian is expanded to second order to confirm the absence of tachyonic modes.
5. **Consistency with known limits**: In appropriate limits, the framework reproduces known results from quantum electrodynamics and general relativity.
6. **Adversarial testing**: Independent language model instances (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok) are employed to identify potential inconsistencies or hidden assumptions.

Each verification layer includes explicit kill conditions—criteria under which the framework would be considered falsified. To date, no kill condition has been triggered.

**Layer 2 (Structural)** : The invariance group of Maxwell's equations under gauge transformations is isomorphic to the invariance group of truth conditions under contextual variation. The mapping is bijective and preserves the group operation.

Preliminary analysis suggests consistency with this prediction.
