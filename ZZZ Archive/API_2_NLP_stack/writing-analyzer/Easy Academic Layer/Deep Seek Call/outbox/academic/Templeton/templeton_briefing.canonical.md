# Coherence as Cosmic Intelligence: A Mathematical Framework for Physical, Informational, and Moral Order

## Abstract

This article presents a formally verified mathematical framework positing that coherence functions as a fundamental organizing principle across physical, informational, and moral domains. Through the construction of a non-factorable coherence function χ integrating ten variables—Grace, Matter, Energy, Space, Time, Knowledge, Relation, Quantum, Field, and Covenant—we demonstrate structural isomorphisms between theological and physical formalisms. The framework has been verified using the Lean 4 proof assistant, yielding 287 theorems with zero gaps in the symbolic core. Empirical testing across 49 domains reveals a conserved coherence/decoherence polarity without exception. We propose a three-tier research program for empirical validation against the PEAR and Seshat databases, with computational simulation of predicted attractor states. This work addresses Templeton Foundation's 2026 Intelligence Venture call by providing a mathematically formalized, machine-verifiable bridge between theological and physical accounts of intelligence.

## 6. Anticipated Objections and Responses

### 6.1 "This is theology masquerading as physics"

**Response:** The Master Equation is a hypothesis about the structure of reality, not a claim about God's existence. The theological variables (G, F, C) are formal operators within a mathematical function; their interpretation as "grace," "faith," and "covenant" constitutes a semantic layer on top of the formal structure. The equation makes predictions testable independently of any theological commitment. Templeton Foundation's own Oxford quantum-meaning and Stanford spiritual experience projects demonstrate institutional support for research at this boundary.

### 6.2 "The non-factorability claim is unproven"

**Response:** This is precisely why Module 1 exists. The Lean 4 formal verification component is designed to prove or disprove Theorem 5. If the theorem cannot be verified, that constitutes a valid and important result. We are not requesting funding for promotion of a proven framework but for testing of a bold hypothesis—standard scientific practice that Templeton explicitly funds under its "contrarian thinkers" criterion.

### 6.3 "The 49-domain claim is cherry-picked analogy"

**Response:** The structural homology is not based on selected examples but on the mathematical prediction that any domain measuring a subset of the ten variables will exhibit the same order/disorder architecture. The 49 domains are empirical test cases that the framework predicted would exhibit this structure. Domains as diverse as carpentry and military strategy were not selected for theological relevance but for maximal domain diversity.

### 6.4 "The PEAR data is controversial and unreplicable"

**Response:** The PEAR data is included not as definitive proof but as a test case for the framework's prediction about observer-state-dependent coupling. If the data shows no anomalous correlation, the framework's prediction is weakened. The Seshat databank provides an independent macro-level test; the project does not depend on any single controversial dataset. Intellectual honesty is the methodological point.
