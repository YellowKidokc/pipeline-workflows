# Theophysics Media Library: A Formal Catalog of Visual Assets for the Interdisciplinary Physics-Theology Research Program

## Abstract

This document presents a systematically organized catalog of visual assets developed within the Theophysics research program, an interdisciplinary framework that investigates structural isomorphisms between physical law and theological doctrine. The catalog encompasses twelve chapters of the *Logos Story* series, fifteen slides of the *Substrate Fractured* presentation, twenty-four images from the *Genesis to Quantum* series, eighteen images from the *Moral Decline of America* series, eight core diagrams including the Master Equation and Ten Laws Map, and eleven architectural infographics. Each asset is identified by its filename, series designation, and chapter or slide number, with descriptive titles that indicate the conceptual domain addressed. This catalog serves as a reference index for researchers seeking visual representations of the formal mappings between quantum mechanics, information theory, and theological constructs such as the Logos, grace, and eschatological completion.

The Theophysics research program employs a structural comparison methodology to identify formal correspondences between the mathematical frameworks of theoretical physics and the doctrinal structures of Christian theology. Asset filenames follow a standardized convention that includes the series abbreviation, chapter or slide number, and a descriptive label.
