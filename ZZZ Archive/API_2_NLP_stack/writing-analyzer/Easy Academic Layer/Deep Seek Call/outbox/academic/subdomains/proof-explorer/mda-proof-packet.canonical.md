# Series Proof Packet: Moral Decline of America

## Abstract

This document constitutes the formal proof-layer packet for the Moral Decline of America (MDA) corpus, a multi-domain investigation into the coherence decay of American social and moral structures across the period 1900–2025. The packet integrates live article routing, raw extraction outputs, proof-layer claims, personal and testimonial materials, and explicit falsification conditions. The series advances the thesis that American moral and social coherence can be operationalized as a measurable, multi-domain decline rather than a univariate cultural complaint. Evidence is drawn from 61 HTML articles and 63 markdown companion documents, yielding 442 raw claims subsequently reduced to 5 promoted proof-layer candidates via the Seven-Question Screen (7QS) methodology. The corpus contains 160 translatable equations spanning statistical, thermodynamic, and information-theoretic formalisms. Ten universal law mappings are assessed for coverage, with substantiated mappings identified for Laws 1–8 and 10. Five explicit kill conditions are specified, any one of which, if satisfied, would constitute falsification of the series' central claims.

---

## 1. Series Thesis and Methodological Framework

### 1.1 Central Thesis

The MDA series posits that American moral and social coherence—defined as the degree of structural alignment among institutional, familial, economic, technological, semantic, and religious indicators—has undergone a measurable decline across the period under investigation. This decline is characterized as a multi-domain phenomenon rather than a single-cause cultural complaint, and is operationalized through the construct of *coherence decay*, defined as the progressive loss of cross-domain synchronization and feedback-loop integrity.

### 1.2 Methodological Approach

The series employs a mixed-methods framework combining:

1. **Empirical indicator analysis**: Longitudinal tracking of institutional, family, economic, technological, semantic, and religious indicators (1900–2025)
2. **Formal modeling**: Thermodynamic and information-theoretic formalisms applied to social coherence metrics
3. **Control group analysis**: The Amish community as a quasi-experimental control group for coherence maintenance
4. **Falsification protocol**: Explicit kill conditions with specified evidentiary thresholds

The proof layer is structured to maintain separation between empirical claims, formal models, and interpretive or testimonial materials, following the 7QS classification protocol (Q0–Q7).

- **Q2 (Knowledge)**: Scope bounded to observed institutional, family, economic, technological, semantic, and religious indicators

## 5. Falsification Protocol

### 5.1 Active Kill Conditions

Three kill conditions remain active and are tracked in the series falsification framework:

**KILL-01: Countertrend Break** (Active)
- A major claimed domain shows increasing coherence during the same period while the framework predicts collapse.
- Requires domain-level evidence and date-bound source trail.

**KILL-02: Better Competing Model** (Active)
- A competing model explains the same data with higher predictive accuracy, fewer parameters, and a different causal mechanism.
- This is the strongest publication-facing falsification condition.

**KILL-03: Personal Claim Leakage** (Active)
- A personal/testimonial statement is presented as framework proof rather than motive, witness, or interpretive context.
- This packet explicitly labels that boundary.

### 5.2 Falsification Invitation

The series explicitly invites replication and falsification attempts. The authors state: "If you can satisfy any kill condition, this paper is falsified." This invitation is extended to independent researchers with access to alternative datasets or competing theoretical frameworks.
