\[

\]

where \(G\) denotes the gravitational subsystem, \(R\) denotes the resurrection subsystem, and \(\text{Closure}(\mathcal{S})\) is a predicate indicating that system \(\mathcal{S}\) satisfies all closure constraints. The disjunction indicates that removal of either \(G\) or \(R\) is sufficient to violate closure.

\[
\text{Matter}(M) \land \neg \text{Entropy}(S) \implies \neg \text{Complete Description}

### 3.3 Interpretive Significance

### 5.1 Structural Correspondence

Quantum behavior is paired with coherence conservation. The framework posits that a closed system cannot spontaneously generate increasing coherence without external intervention or violation of closure constraints.

\[
\frac{d\mathcal{C}}{dt} \leq 0 \quad \text{(for closed quantum systems)}
