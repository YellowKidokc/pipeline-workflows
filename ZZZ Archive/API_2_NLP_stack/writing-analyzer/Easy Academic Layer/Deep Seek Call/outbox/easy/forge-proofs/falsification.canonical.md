# Falsification Court

This is where we inspect what breaks. We look at what happens when you deny the core claims. This is an adversarial (opposing) check — we try to break the system on purpose.

### Break condition: Reject external grace

If you say there is no grace coming from outside the system, the works-based sign-flip becomes impossible. The framework requires a non-unitary (not self-contained) input. Without it, the math doesn't work. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet. Reject that tilt, and nothing moves downhill.

---

### Break condition: Reject information primacy

If you say information is not the most basic thing, the system falls back into circular definitions or infinite regress (an endless chain of "what caused that?"). Why? Because rival starting points secretly borrow informational structure to make their own arguments work. Like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal. If you deny the signal exists, you still need it to explain why you can hear anything at all.

---

### Break condition: Reject Time Wall

If you say the system is not intentionally incomplete (the Time Wall), the unresolved boundary problem does not disappear. It is merely hidden. The problem is still there — you just stopped looking at it. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside.

---

### Break condition: Reject terminal observer

If you say there is no final observer who closes the system, the measurement/interpretation chain remains open forever. The closure claim is never completed. Like two people watching the same car crash from different street corners — what you see depends on where you're standing, but the crash still happened. Without a final observer, nobody ever agrees on what they saw.

---

### Break condition: Reject coherence conservation

If you say coherence (the property of things being in harmony) is not conserved, the proof loses its reason for requiring external negentropic (order-creating) intervention in the first place. Like a tuning fork — when you strike it near other objects, they start vibrating at the same frequency. One source of perfect pitch that brings everything else into harmony. If you deny that harmony needs a source, you can't explain why things don't just fall into noise.

---

### Court function

This page is adversarial by design. It asks what happens when core claims are denied. The answer is not "nothing." The answer is specific structural damage. Each denial breaks a specific part of the framework in a predictable way.
