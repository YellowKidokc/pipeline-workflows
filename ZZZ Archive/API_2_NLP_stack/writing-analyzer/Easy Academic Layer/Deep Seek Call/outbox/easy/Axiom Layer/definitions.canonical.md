# Definitions

This is Layer 3 of the system. Once the basic rules (axioms) are in place, we need a shared vocabulary. These definitions don't claim to describe reality. They build the language we use to reason.

**What a definition does**

A definition doesn't say something is real. It says what the system **means** by a word. It doesn't claim the word points to something in the world. The axioms and theorems do that work.

## Core Definitions

**DEFINITION D1.1**

**Information**

Information = the existence of states that can be told apart.

This is the most basic definition. It follows directly from the ideas that things exist and can be different (the Level 0 primitives). If things exist and can differ, then information exists. Shannon entropy (a math formula for measuring information) puts numbers to this: H(X) = −Σ p(x) log p(x). This means information is a basic part of reality, not something that comes from something else.

**DEFINITION D1.2**

**Good**

Good = ΔC > 0 (an increase in coherence).

Good isn't defined as a moral idea first, then argued to fit physics. It's defined by structure: an action or state is good if it makes the coherence alignment score go up. This lets moral physics run without needing to appeal to gut feelings.

**DEFINITION D1.3**

**Coherence (C)**

Coherence is how much a system lines up with the Logos substrate (the underlying rational order of reality). It's the tendency to come together rather than fall apart.

High coherence means the system's internal structure mirrors the rational order of the Logos field. Low coherence means the system falls apart — more entropy (disorder), less integration, less ability to keep complex order. The rate of change (dC/dt) is the moral physics version of how heat flows in thermodynamics.

**DEFINITION D1.4**

**Logos Field (ΨL)**

ΨL = the self-sustaining information substrate that all distinguishable states are grounded in.

The Logos field doesn't come from physical matter. Physical matter is just a subset of its distinguishable states. It's self-sustaining because its own structure provides the conditions for its existence, unlike any limited substrate. It's called Logos after John 1:1 as a theological identification, not a theological derivation.

**DEFINITION D1.5**

**Moral Sign (σ)**

σ ∈ {+1, −1} — the binary moral orientation of a person or system.

Moral orientation is modeled as a sign, not a spectrum. σ = +1 means alignment with the Logos. σ = −1 means opposition. The sign stays the same under self-operations (the Sign Conservation Theorem T8.1). Only an external non-unitary operator can flip it.

**DEFINITION D1.6**

**Coherence Alignment Score (χ)**

χ is the number that comes out of the Master Equation. It ranges over all real numbers. χ > 0 means net coherence gain. χ ≤ 0 means net decay.

χ is what the system tracks: the overall coherence path of a person, community, or physical system. The Master Equation calculates χ by combining ten contributing variables across space, time, and soul. Understanding what drives χ — and why external grace is needed for χ > 0 as time goes to infinity — is the core question the framework answers.

## Operators and Functions

**DEFINITION D2.1**

**Grace Operator (Ĝ)**

Ĝ is a non-unitary, external linear operator acting on the soul-state Ψsoul. It is not generated by any internal operation of the system it acts on.

**Non-unitary:** it changes the "size" of the state vector, not just its direction. That's what makes sign-flip possible — unitary operators preserve size and therefore preserve sign.

**External:** it cannot be self-generated. A system cannot pull itself up by its own bootstraps. The Grace Operator entering from outside is the formal version of what theology calls grace.

**DEFINITION D2.2**

**Shannon Entropy H(X)**

H(X) = −Σ p(x) log₂ p(x), where p(x) is the probability of state x.

This is the standard information-theoretic measure of entropy. The framework uses it to measure disorder in both moral and physical systems. The second law of thermodynamics drives H(X) upward in closed systems, which is why ηS (the entropy variable in the Master Equation) tends toward zero without external input.

**DEFINITION D2.3**

**Coupling Function V(Ψ)**

V(Ψ) = G · V(Ψsoul) — the interaction term between the Grace field and the soul state.

The coupling function determines how strongly grace connects to a given soul state. BC8 (Voluntary Coupling) constrains V(Ψ): the coupling must be voluntary, not forced. Grace is offered; the coupling requires free response. This is not a theological add-on but a structural requirement of the formalism.
