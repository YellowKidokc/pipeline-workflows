# The Physics of Truth: How Light and Revelation Work Together

## Introduction

People often say truth is like "light." But the "No-Drift" framework takes this idea way beyond a simple comparison. When we look at how electromagnetic waves actually work, we find a perfect match for how information gets sent, received, and twisted in the spiritual world.

## The Physics of Light

Maxwell's equations describe how electric fields and magnetic fields create each other. They work together to make a self-sustaining wave that travels through empty space at the speed of light (c). These waves don't need anything to travel through (like air or water). They carry energy and information across huge distances.

Think of it like a flashlight in a dark room — it doesn't create what's there, it reveals what was always there. You can't have half-light. It either reaches the corner or it doesn't.

## The Constructive Regime: Truth and Revelation

In the spiritual world, electromagnetic radiation maps directly to **Truth** and **Revelation**. Truth is a self-sustaining signal. Once it gets launched, it keeps going forever. It shows the structure of reality to anyone who can receive the signal.

Here's a key thing about light: it doesn't "fight" darkness. It just takes up space. Darkness is what happens when the signal is absent — it's not an opposing wave. This matches what the Bible says in *John 1:5*: *"The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it."*

In this framework, the universal constant c (the speed of light) isn't just a speed limit. It's the "Speed of Christ" — the rate at which Truth fills all of empty space.

## The Destructive Regime: Deception as Interference

Deception isn't a separate thing from truth. It's a specific arrangement of truth. In wave physics, **destructive interference** happens when two waves of the same frequency are exactly 180° out of phase:

E_total = A sin(ωt) + A sin(ωt + π) = 0

This equation means: when you take two identical waves and flip one completely upside down, they cancel each other out. The result is zero — nothing.

This maps to **Deception**. To make an effective lie, you have to take Truth and flip its phase. Half-truths are waves that are only partially flipped. They cancel out the real signal while still looking like a wave. Deception is the act of arranging "truths" so that they destroy the clear signal of Revelation.

## Free Will and Signal Reception

Humans are the "receivers" in this system. The **Free Will variable (W)** controls how the receiver is tuned:

E_received = W · E_truth + (1 - W) · E_distortion

This equation means: what you receive depends on your free will setting.

- **W = 1:** Faithful reception. You are perfectly tuned to the Truth signal.
- **W = 0:** You receive only the flipped, distorted version.
- **W > 1:** Amplification and retransmission. You don't just receive the light — you become a secondary source. This is Testimony or Prophecy.

## Information Theory and the Shannon Limit

How much revelation you can receive follows a math formula called the Shannon-Hartley theorem:

C = B · log₂(1 + S/N)

This equation means: the "revelation capacity" of a soul depends on two things. First, the bandwidth (B) of your spiritual perception — how wide your channel is. Second, the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) of your environment and your internal state. If the "noise" of deception or the "distortion" of your flesh is too high, you can't pick up the truth, no matter how strong the signal is.

Like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal.

## Conclusion

Truth is not just what you feel is right. It's a mathematical fact built into the universe. When we understand Revelation through Maxwell's equations, we see that the battle between Truth and Deception is really a battle of wave coherence. To live in the Truth means to stay in phase with the self-sustaining signal of Christ.
