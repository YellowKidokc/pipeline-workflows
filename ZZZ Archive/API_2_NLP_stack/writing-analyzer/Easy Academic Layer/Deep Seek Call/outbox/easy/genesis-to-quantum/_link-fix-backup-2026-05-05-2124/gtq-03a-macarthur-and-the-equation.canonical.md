# The One Equation That Ends a 1600-Year Fight

For sixteen hundred years, Christians have argued over one question: Who decides who gets saved? Is it God alone, like John Calvin said, using grace you can't resist? Or is it the human will, like Jacob Arminius said, responding to grace you can say no to?

Calvinism and Arminianism have filled libraries with books. They've split churches apart. The best Bible experts on both sides were sure they had the truth.

That whole argument disappears the moment you describe salvation with one math equation.

## MacArthur's Position

John MacArthur speaks for the Calvinist side very clearly: Before grace, humans are *dead in trespasses and sins* (Ephesians 2:1). Not sick. Not hurt. Dead. A dead body can't choose anything. So God has to act first—He *wills*, *draws*, *predestines*, and *grants* the ability to believe (John 6:44, Philippians 1:29). This is called effectual calling (a call that actually works). It is, by definition, irresistible (you can't fight it off).

But MacArthur also says humans are truly responsible. You must repent. You must believe. You must obey. This looks like a perfect contradiction: God does everything, but you still have to do it.

## The Other Side

Arminians point to different Bible verses. God doesn't want anyone to die (2 Peter 3:9). Christ died for everyone (1 Timothy 2:4–6). The Spirit calls and invites; humans can say no (Acts 7:51). The image of God in humans is never erased—even fallen humans have genuine free will. Salvation needs both grace and that free choice working together.

Both sides read the same Bible. Both are serious. Both have been right for 1600 years—because they were describing different parts of the same process.

## The Resolution: Two Phases, One Equation

**The coherence equation:**
$$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

This equation means: The rate at which your coherence (closeness) with God changes over time equals your openness times grace times the room you have left to grow, minus sin (which acts like decay) times your current coherence.

$C$ = coherence with God, $O$ = openness, $G$ = grace, $S$ = sin/entropy (decay pressure).

Salvation has two phases, described by one equation with one free parameter: **the surrender parameter** $s$, which runs from $-1$ (total defiance, fighting God completely) to $+1$ (total surrender, giving in completely).

### Phase 1: From Death to Openness ($s = -1 \to 0$)

When $s = -1$, the coupling function $\alpha(s) = \frac{1+s}{2}$ equals zero. This function controls how much human openness connects with divine grace. At -1, there's zero connection.

The equation becomes pure decay: $\frac{dC}{dt} = -S \cdot C$. This means your coherence just falls apart over time, like a sandcastle washing away.

The system is dead. No human input ($O$) can help. Only divine grace $G$ is strong enough to move the parameter from $s=-1$ to $s=0$. This transition is *monergistic*—God alone starts it. There is no human "yes" at this step because the human is dead. This is MacArthur's whole case, and it is correct.

### Phase 2: From Openness to Coherence ($s = 0 \to +1$)

Once $s=0$, the coupling wakes up. Now human openness $O$ and divine grace $G$ together build coherence. The person genuinely chooses. The equation becomes synergistic—both sides contribute. This is the Arminian case, and it is correct.

**The contradiction disappears because it was describing two steps in a row of one process.** MacArthur is right that the dead need God's call you can't resist. Arminius is right that once awakened, humans genuinely work together with God in their own salvation.

## Augustine's Four States: Historical Confirmation

**Before the Fall:** Humans have $s \approx 0$—real choice, full freedom, no decay pressure.

**After the Fall:** Humans locked at $s = -1$—can't help but sin, dead, no ability to respond to grace.

**Under Grace:** Humans can move $s: -1 \to +1$—grace starts it, humans cooperate, both contribute.

**In Glory:** Humans at $s = +1$—can't sin, maximum freedom, perfect closeness with God.

Augustine mapped the same two-phase structure 1600 years ago. MacArthur made it formal. The equation just puts it in math.

## The One-Verse Equation

Philippians 2:12–13 contains the whole structure in one sentence:

*"Work out your salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you both to will and to work for his good pleasure."*

Work (human action, Phase 2). It is God who works (God's action, Phase 1). Both. One sentence. One equation. The verse assumes you already understand that Phase 1 (God's call that actually works) has already happened. Now Phase 2 (human cooperation in becoming holy) begins. This is the whole Calvinist-Arminian map resolved.

### The Verdict

The 1600-year argument was a category error (mixing up two different things as if they were the same). Both sides were right, describing different phases of one process. MacArthur's theology is not criticized here—it is made formal. The equation makes Augustine formal, validates MacArthur, and backs up the Arminian insistence on genuine human freedom. Neither was wrong. They were describing sequential steps of the same coherence journey.

## The Coherence Equation

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

### Variable Definitions

*   **$C$** = coherence with God (0 to 1, no units)
*   **$O$** = openness / receptivity (human capacity, 0 to 1)
*   **$G$** = grace input (God's sovereign action, no units, $G > 0$)
*   **$S$** = sin / entropy (decay pressure, $S > 0$)
*   **$s$** = surrender parameter ($-1$ to $+1$, controls coupling)

## The Coupling Function

$$\alpha(s) = \frac{1+s}{2}$$

This function controls the connection between human openness and divine grace.

**Key Properties:**

*   When $s = -1$: $\alpha(-1) = 0$ (dead, no connection)
*   When $s = 0$: $\alpha(0) = 0.5$ (waking up, half-open)
*   When $s = +1$: $\alpha(+1) = 1$ (fully alive, complete potential for closeness)

## Phase 1: Monergistic Transition ($s: -1 \to 0$)

At $s = -1$, $\alpha(s) = 0$: $$\frac{dC}{dt} = 0 \cdot O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C = -S \cdot C$$ Pure exponential decay: $C(t) = C_0 e^{-St}$

This means coherence drops off like a radioactive substance decaying. No human input ($O$) can help. Only divine grace $G$ is strong enough to start the transition $s: -1 \to 0$. This phase is entirely God's work. It is irresistible because there is no human agent to resist it.

## Phase 2: Synergistic Growth ($s: 0 \to +1$)

Once $s > 0$, $\alpha(s) > 0$: $$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$ Full equation active. Human openness $O$ and divine grace $G$ together increase coherence.

Equilibrium approaches $C_{eq} = \frac{O \cdot G}{O \cdot G + S}$. This means the final level of closeness depends on both grace and openness. The rate of growth depends on both grace $G$ and openness $O$. Both must be present. Neither alone decides the outcome. This is synergism (working together).

## Boundary Behavior

| Condition | Behavior |
|---|---|
| $s = -1, G = 0$ | System decays to $C = 0$ (death) |
| $s = -1, G > 0$ (large) | Transition to $s = 0$ (irresistible call) |
| $s = 0, O = 0$ | Stagnation at $C = C_{current}$ (no growth) |
| $s = +1, O = 1, G > S$ | Approach $C \to 1$ (glory state) |

## Evidence Markers

### Philippians 2:12–13

*"Work out your salvation... for it is God who works in you both to will and to work."* Contains both phases in one verse: human work ($O$) and God's work ($G$), together building coherence ($C$).

### MacArthur's 8 Verbs

Wills, draws, predestines, grants, calls, awakens, imputes, justifies. All describe the $s:-1 \to 0$ transition where $G$ alone moves the parameter. MacArthur is mapping Phase 1.

### Augustine's 4 States

Before Fall ($s=0$), After Fall ($s=-1$), Under Grace ($s:-1 \to +1$), In Glory ($s=+1$). Each maps to a region of the parameter space and the equation's behavior there.

### Romans 9:22–23 Grammar

*"Vessels of wrath... vessels of mercy prepared beforehand."* The Greek grammar supports passive reprobation (system left at $s=-1$) vs. active mercy (transition to $s=0$). No symmetry in divine action—one is active, one is passive.

## Kill Conditions: What Would Disprove This Model

1.  **Single-phase coherence model:** If a mathematically consistent single-phase model can explain all Reformed and Arminian Scripture without using two phases or a parameter transition.
2.  **Post-hoc fitting:** If the equation can be shown to be a curve-fit to theology rather than derived from first principles of coherence and grace dynamics.
3.  **MacArthur's preaching contradicts Phase 1 mapping:** If detailed textual analysis of MacArthur's actual teaching (not assumptions about Calvinism) shows he does not describe $s:-1 \to 0$ as purely God's work.
4.  **Coupling function boundary failure:** If the function $\alpha(s) = \frac{1+s}{2}$ produces incorrect Phase 1 or Phase 2 behavior at $s=-1$, $s=0$, or $s=+1$.
5.  **Augustine's states don't map:** If Augustine's four states cannot be assigned consistent $s$-values and equation behaviors that match his actual theology.
6.  **Scripture ignores the transition:** If no text in the New Testament describes or implies a $s:-1 \to 0$ transition as a distinct monergistic event.

**[overreach 1]** We asserted that the equation "derives from first principles" when really it's a plausible model that fits the data. It's not derived from anything more fundamental.

**[overreach 2]** We implied the equation proves the theology is correct. It doesn't. It shows the theology is mathematically consistent. That's different from being true.
