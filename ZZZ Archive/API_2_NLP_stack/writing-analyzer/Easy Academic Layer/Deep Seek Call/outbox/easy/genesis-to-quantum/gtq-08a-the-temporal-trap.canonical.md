## Two Kinds of Fall

Satan's fall was **spatial** (a change in location). He was cast out. It was a permanent change of position, with no time involved. There is no "before and after" in a spatial move. This is why there's no offer of redemption for angels. The mechanism of redemption needs time, and spatial beings don't have it.

Humanity's fall was **temporal** (a change over time). Humans were created inside time itself. Temporal beings have a "before and after." We have a past we can return to, and a future that hasn't happened yet.
