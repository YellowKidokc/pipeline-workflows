# System Audit

This paper challenges the idea that consciousness directly influences quantum systems. It argues for a more limited view of what measurement really means. The direction of the correction is strong, but some conclusions go beyond what the evidence actually shows.

## What Holds

- The point that measurement does not need human consciousness matches what mainstream physics says.
- Separating observation from intention is an important and necessary distinction.
- Criticizing exaggerated claims about consciousness affecting quantum systems is justified.

## What Needs Proof

- The interpretation of PEAR and Global Consciousness Project data needs strict statistical analysis to be trusted.
- The boundary between physical measurement and informational interpretation needs a clearer definition.
- The role of consciousness in broader system dynamics remains unresolved.

## Where We Overreached

- Statements that imply consciousness has no role at all in physical systems go beyond what most scientists agree on.
- Dismissing all strange data as non-physical influence may be too quick.
- Some conclusions rely on interpretation rather than controlled experimental validation.
