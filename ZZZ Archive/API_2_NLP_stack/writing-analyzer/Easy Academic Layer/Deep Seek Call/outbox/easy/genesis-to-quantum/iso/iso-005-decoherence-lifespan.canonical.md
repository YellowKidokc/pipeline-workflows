## Domain Specifications

### Domain A — Physics: Quantum Decoherence

**Quantum decoherence** is what happens when a quantum system loses its perfect order by bumping into its surroundings. Think of it like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal. The off-diagonal elements of the density matrix (the parts that show quantum connections) decay exponentially as the system gets tangled up with its environment. This is described by exponential decay with an asymptotic floor (a bottom limit it never falls below). The floor exists because a persistent external coherence source (grace) prevents total collapse. The decoherence timescale τ sets the rate at which coherence is lost.
