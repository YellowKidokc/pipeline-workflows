### What holds up the floor that physics calls final?

Conservation laws feel final because they never seem to break. Energy is conserved. Momentum is conserved. Charge is conserved. The equations hold, and the world behaves as if something deeper is keeping score.

If a conservation law stands on symmetry, then symmetry is the new floor. But a floor still needs something to hold it up.
