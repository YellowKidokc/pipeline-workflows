## What the Calculator Measures

The Coherence Score is a combination of six stress factors that any text must survive. Each factor adds entropy — noise, breakdown, information loss. A higher score means a text kept more of its original message while facing more destructive pressure.

**The six factors:** age (time = entropy), number of independent authors (harder to keep stories straight), languages translated into (translation loss), composition timespan (harder to stay consistent), manuscript gap (more copies = more degradation), and textual agreement (actual measured coherence).

The Bible's numbers: 3,400 years old, 40+ authors, 700+ languages, 1,500-year composition span, 25,000+ manuscripts, 25-year gap (New Testament), 99.5% textual agreement.

By information theory, a text with those transmission numbers should be garbled noise. The Bible isn't. That gap between what should happen and what actually happened is the measurement.
