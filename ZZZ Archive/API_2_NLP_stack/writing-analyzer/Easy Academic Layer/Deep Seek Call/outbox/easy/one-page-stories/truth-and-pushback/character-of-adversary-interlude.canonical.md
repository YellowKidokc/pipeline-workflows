# The Character of the Adversary

## Strategic decay is a signature, not a coincidence

If evil has a physics signature, it will look like a system that breaks apart coherence (the state of things holding together in a meaningful way) everywhere it touches.

David Lowe — FP-020 — POF 2828

## 01 — The Question

### What does corruption look like when you stop pretending it is abstract?

Entropy (the natural tendency of things to move from order to disorder) is not a personality. But the pattern it serves can be read. Decay separates what belonged together, scrambles signal (like a radio message breaking up in static), isolates systems, weakens memory, and turns form into noise.

Evil is not the opposite of creation. It is the parasitic inversion of coherence.

— Faith Through Physics

The adversary does not need to create a world. He only needs to fracture one.

## 02 — Proof by Signature

### The same pattern keeps appearing

**Vector 1 — Decay**  
Structure fails first. What was ordered begins to fall apart. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system. (Thermodynamics / Judgment)

**Vector 2 — Decoherence**  
Signal becomes unstable and the system can no longer hold a shared meaning. Like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal. (Information / Logos)

**Vector 3 — Isolation**  
Relation weakens until distance feels normal and communion feels impossible. Like a parent holding a child's hand — loose grip when you're walking together, iron grip when you try to run into traffic. The closer you stay, the more freedom you feel. (Strong Nuclear Force / Love)

**Vector 4 — Identity theft**  
Naming is attacked so the person forgets who was spoken over them. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside. (Weak Force / Moral Conservation)

Those are not random temptations. They are attack vectors against coherence channels (the pathways that hold meaning together). The signature keeps recurring in physics, language, identity, community, and worship.
