# Math Before Matter

## Declared Position and Ground

### What problem is everyone skipping?

This page argues that math comes before matter in the order of explanation. Think of it like this: you don't explain the blueprint by looking at the building. You explain the building by looking at the blueprint. Math is the blueprint. Matter is the building.

The clue is in the asymmetry — the fact that math describes the physical world with perfect precision, but the physical world doesn't describe math. That lopsided relationship is the giveaway. And if you disagree — if you think matter comes first — the burden of proof is on you to explain why math works so perfectly on something it supposedly just made up.

— Faith Through Physics
