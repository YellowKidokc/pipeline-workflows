# ISO-011: Superposition and Collapse

## Isomorphism Record

**ID:** ISO-011
**Date:** 2026-03-10

## Domains

**Domain A: Physics** — Quantum Superposition, Wavefunction Collapse, Irreversibility, Landauer Bound
**Domain B: Christian Theology** — Mystery (Pre-Revelation), Revelation (Divine Disclosure), Salvation History as Irreversible

**Concept A:** A quantum system can exist in superposition. That means it's in multiple states at once — like a coin spinning in the air that is both heads and tails at the same time. We write this as |ψ⟩ = Σᵢ cᵢ|aᵢ⟩. The system has definite probabilities (the cᵢ values) but no definite outcome until someone measures it. When measured, the superposition "collapses" to a single state. In the Copenhagen interpretation, this collapse is irreversible — you can't go back. The Landauer bound says that erasing one bit of information costs at least kT ln 2 in energy. That's a real, measured cost. The framework's modified Schrödinger equation (E6.2) says the collapse rate depends on something called Φ (the coherence or integrated information of the system doing the measuring).

**Concept B:** Theological mystery (the Greek word is *mysterion*) means divine truth that exists in a hidden state before it gets revealed. Revelation (the Greek word is *apokalypsis*, meaning "uncovering") collapses that mystery into definite, shareable truth. Salvation history — the story of God saving humanity — is irreversible. The Incarnation (God becoming human), the Crucifixion (Jesus' death), and the Resurrection (Jesus rising from the dead) are "once for all" events. The Greek word is *ephapax*, used in Hebrews 7:27, 9:12, and 10:10. These events cannot be undone.

## The Mapping

### Mathematical Form A

**Superposition:**
|ψ⟩ = Σᵢ cᵢ|aᵢ⟩, where Σᵢ|cᵢ|² = 1

This equation says the system is in a mix of all possible states at once. It is NOT like "we don't know which state it's in" (that's a classical mixture, like a coin under a cup). The system is genuinely in all states at the same time. The numbers cᵢ (called amplitudes) tell you the probability and phase information.

**Collapse (measurement):**
|ψ⟩ → |aₖ⟩ with probability |cₖ|²

When you measure the system, the superposition shrinks down to just one state. The other states are gone — they don't exist anymore (in the Copenhagen interpretation). The information changes from a full set of possibilities to one definite answer.

**Landauer Bound:**
ΔQ ≥ kT ln 2 per bit of information erased

This means that getting rid of uncertainty (collapsing a superposition) always costs a minimum amount of energy. You cannot get definite information for free. This has been proven in experiments.

**Modified Schrödinger Equation (Framework Element E6.2):**
iℏ ∂|ψ⟩/∂t = Ĥ|ψ⟩ − iγΦ(|ψ⟩⟨ψ| − ρ_classical)|ψ⟩

Here γ is the collapse rate and Φ is the coherence/integrated information parameter. This equation says that collapse is not instant or random — it's a physical process that happens faster or slower depending on how coherent the observing system is. This is a TESTABLE prediction of the framework.

### Mathematical Form B

**Mystery (*mysterion*):**

In Paul's letters (Ephesians 3:3-6, Colossians 1:26-27, Romans 16:25-26), mystery doesn't mean something you can never know. It means truth that was hidden for a while before being revealed. "The mystery hidden for ages and generations but now revealed to his saints" (Colossians 1:26).

The mystery is like superposition: the truth EXISTS but hasn't been RESOLVED into a definite, shareable form yet. Multiple possible interpretations coexist — like how Old Testament prophecies pointed to Christ without anyone being sure exactly how.

**Revelation (*apokalypsis*):**

Revelation collapses mystery into definite truth. "When the fullness of time had come, God sent forth his Son" (Galatians 4:4). The Incarnation is the collapse event: the eternal Logos (God's Word) takes definite, particular, historical form — born of a woman, born under the law. The multiple possibilities (which prophecies? what form would the Messiah take?) resolve into one definite person: Jesus of Nazareth.

**Irreversibility:**

*Ephapax* — "once for all." Hebrews uses this term repeatedly:

- Hebrews 7:27: Christ offered himself "once for all" (not repeatable like the old animal sacrifices)
- Hebrews 9:12: He entered the Most Holy Place "once for all"
- Hebrews 10:10: "We have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all"

The Incarnation, death, and resurrection of Christ are irreversible historical events. They cannot be undone, repeated, or reversed. This is structural irreversibility — it's built into the nature of the events, not just a fact about history.

### Shared Structure

1. **Pre-resolution multiplicity** — Superposition: multiple states coexist with definite probabilities. Mystery: multiple interpretive possibilities coexist with partial validity. Both describe genuine multiplicity, not just ignorance.

2. **Resolution event** — Measurement collapses superposition to one state. Revelation collapses mystery to definite truth. Both are events that resolve multiplicity into definiteness.

3. **Irreversibility** — Collapse is irreversible (in Copenhagen). Salvation-historical events are *ephapax*. Both describe one-way processes: once resolved, you cannot go back to the prior state.

4. **Thermodynamic cost** — Landauer: resolving information costs at least kT ln 2 per bit. Theology: revelation costs something — ultimately, the Incarnation and Crucifixion are the "cost" of collapsing the mystery of salvation into definite, historical form. "It cost God something to reveal himself" is a theological claim with a formal structural parallel in Landauer's principle.

5. **Temporal structure** — Superposition comes before collapse. Mystery comes before revelation. Both have a before/after structure that is NOT reversible.

### What Is NOT Claimed

- NOT claiming revelation IS wavefunction collapse — revelation is a divine act of self-disclosure to persons. Wavefunction collapse is a physical process (or mathematical formalism, depending on interpretation). These happen in radically different domains.

- NOT claiming theological mystery means "God doesn't know" — God knows the resolution; the mystery is from the CREATURELY perspective (our side). Superposition (in some interpretations) is genuine real-world indeterminacy; theological mystery is epistemological hiddenness (hidden from our knowledge). This is a significant disanalogy — see Strain Analysis.

- NOT claiming the Incarnation has a thermodynamic cost in joules — the Landauer parallel is structural, not about actual energy measured in physics.

- NOT claiming quantum mechanics proves Christianity — the structural parallel is suggestive, not proof.

- NOT claiming the modified Schrödinger equation (E6.2) is established physics — it is a PROPOSED modification that is testable but not yet tested.

## Tests

### The Four-Test Protocol

**Test 1 — Prediction Constraint:** Does the mapping constrain predictions in both domains?

In Domain A:

- The modified Schrödinger equation (E6.2) predicts that collapse rate depends on Φ (integrated information / coherence of the observing system). This is testable: systems with higher Φ should collapse superpositions faster than systems with lower Φ. Specific prediction: biological neural systems (high Φ) should be more effective at inducing decoherence (loss of quantum behavior) than equivalent-mass thermodynamic systems (low Φ). This is a genuine, falsifiable prediction.
- Landauer's bound is already confirmed. The mapping predicts it should be a structural minimum, not just a practical limit — resolving information NECESSARILY costs energy.
- Collapse should be genuinely irreversible (not just apparently so). This distinguishes Copenhagen from many-worlds (where all branches persist).

In Domain B:

- If mystery maps to superposition, then pre-revelation theology should exhibit genuine multiplicity — multiple valid typological readings of the Old Testament that genuinely resolve into Christ. This is consistent with the New Testament's own way of reading Scripture (Matthew's "fulfillment" formulas, Hebrews' typology).
- If revelation is irreversible, then "unrevelation" should be impossible — God should not retract genuine revelation. This is consistent with the doctrine that God's gifts are irrevocable (Romans 11:29).
- If there is a "cost" to revelation, the most costly revelation (full incarnation, crucifixion) should correspond to the most complete resolution of mystery. This is consistent with Hebrews' argument that Christ's once-for-all sacrifice is MORE effective than repeated Levitical sacrifices precisely because it costs MORE.

Verdict: The physics prediction (E6.2, Φ-dependent collapse rate) is genuinely testable and novel. The theological predictions are consistent but not independently generated by the mapping. **Strong pass for E6.2; moderate pass otherwise.**

**Test 2 — Symmetry Breaking:** Can you swap the roles?

Can collapse happen before superposition? In quantum mechanics: no. Collapse is defined as the transition FROM superposition. You cannot collapse a state that is already definite — that's not collapse, that's just identity.

Can revelation happen before mystery? In theology: no. Revelation is the disclosure of what was previously hidden. If it was never hidden, there's nothing to reveal. "The mystery hidden for ages... NOW revealed" (Colossians 1:26) — the temporal order is fixed.

Can the irreversibility be reversed? In quantum mechanics (Copenhagen): no — collapse is a projection, and projections cannot be inverted (they map a higher-dimensional space to a lower-dimensional subspace; you cannot reverse that without information you no longer have). In theology: *ephapax* is definitional — "once for all" means non-repeatable.

**Symmetry breaking result: PASSED.** The temporal ordering (multiplicity → resolution → irreversibility) is fixed in both domains and cannot be reversed.

### Strain Analysis — The Honest Part

**The core objection: Is "mystery" in theology really like superposition? Superposition means the system is LITERALLY in multiple states. Theological mystery means WE don't know, not that God is in multiple states. This might be analogy dressed up as isomorphism.**

This objection identifies the deepest strain in this ISO, and it is correct that the two kinds of "multiplicity" are fundamentally different:

**Quantum superposition (real-world multiplicity):**
The quantum system IS in multiple states simultaneously. This is not ignorance — it is real. The electron IS in spin-up AND spin-down (with specific probabilities). There is no hidden "real state" underneath.

**Theological mystery (hidden from our knowledge):**
God KNOWS the resolution. The mystery is hidden from creatures, not from God. "Known to God from eternity are all his works" (Acts 15:18). God is not in superposition. God has a definite plan. The "multiplicity" is in human understanding, not in divine reality.

**This is a genuine disanalogy, and it matters.** If superposition is real and mystery is about knowledge, then the mapping fails at the deepest level: the multiplicity is of a different kind.

**Possible responses (each with its own problems):**

1. **Accept the knowledge-based reading:** Accept that the mapping is between real multiplicity (physics) and knowledge-based multiplicity (theology). The structural parallel still holds: in both cases, from the perspective of the finite observer, there is genuine multiplicity that resolves irreversibly. The fact that GOD knows the resolution is like the fact that the UNIVERSAL WAVEFUNCTION (in Everett) contains all outcomes — the collapse is from a perspective, not absolute. This salvages the mapping but weakens it to a perspectival isomorphism, which is less impressive.

2. **Push back on the knowledge-based reading:** Some theologians (Jürgen Moltmann, open theism) argue that the future is genuinely open even to God. If God truly doesn't know the future with certainty (which is outside mainstream classical theism but held by some), then mystery becomes real and the mapping strengthens. But this requires abandoning classical omniscience (the idea that God knows everything).

3. **Redefine the mapping level:** The isomorphism is not between "God's perspective" and "quantum reality" but between "the structure of how things unfold in time" and "quantum measurement." Both describe processes where the temporal structure is: indefinite → definite → irreversible. The mapping is about the PROCESS SHAPE, not about the real status of the pre-resolution state.

**Honest verdict:** Response 3 is the most defensible. The isomorphism is about PROCESS TOPOLOGY (indefinite → definite → irreversible) rather than about the real status of the pre-resolution state. At this level, the mapping is genuinely structural. At the deeper level of "what kind of multiplicity is it?" the mapping strains significantly.

**Where it holds:**

- The process shape (multiplicity → resolution → irreversibility) is identical in both domains.
- The thermodynamic cost structure (Landauer bound / cost of revelation) is a non-trivial parallel.
- The modified Schrödinger equation (E6.2) is a genuine, testable prediction that flows from the mapping.
- The *ephapax* / irreversibility parallel is strong and specific.

**Where it strains:**

- Real vs. knowledge-based multiplicity — the pre-resolution states are DIFFERENT KINDS of "multiple."
- God's knowledge vs. quantum indeterminacy — God knows the outcome; the quantum system (in Copenhagen) genuinely doesn't "have" an outcome until measurement.
- If many-worlds is correct, there is no collapse, and the entire mapping loses its physics anchor.

**The E6.2 modified Schrödinger equation is the saving grace of this ISO.** If it is experimentally confirmed, the mapping goes beyond analogy into genuine cross-domain prediction. If it is falsified, the mapping retreats to analogy. The ISO's status hinges on E6.2.
