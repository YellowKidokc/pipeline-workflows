## Domain A — Physics: The Born Rule

**The Born Rule:** P(a) = |⟨φ|ψ⟩|². This is the basic equation that turns quantum amplitudes (wave sizes) into measurable probabilities (chances of something happening). It contains three mathematical operations that cannot be broken down further:

**|ψ⟩ — The State Vector:** This is the possibility space. Think of it like a hill's slope — the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. All potential outcomes are packed into one mathematical object. It contains everything that could happen but says nothing about what will happen.

**⟨φ| — The Measurement Basis:** This is the projection operator. It selects which observable (thing you're measuring) gets measured. It gives structure and definition to the raw possibility space. Without it, |ψ⟩ stays as undefined potential — like a radio signal through static, but nobody has tuned the channel yet.

**|·|² — The Modulus Squared:** This is the actualization operator. It converts complex amplitude (a fancy number with two parts) into real probability (a plain number you can use). **Remove any one and the equation fails.** These are not three descriptions of one operation — they are three distinct operations that cannot be reduced to fewer.

## Domain B — Theology: The Trinity

**Three Persons, one God.** Each Person performs an irreducible function that cannot be absorbed by the others:

**The Father — Possibility Space:** The ground of all being, the source from which all reality comes. Outside time, containing all potential. "In the beginning God..." (Gen 1:1). The Father holds all that could be — the unbounded possibility from which everything is drawn. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom.

**The Son / Logos — Measurement Basis:** The Word through whom all things were made (John 1:3). The one who gives structure and definition to reality. "Upholding all things by the word of his power" (Heb 1:3). The Logos selects, defines, and structures the raw potential of the Father into specific forms. Like a flashlight in a dark room — it doesn't create what's there, it reveals what was always there.

**The Spirit — Actualization Operator:** The one who "hovered over the waters" (Gen 1:2), who actualizes potential into reality. Operates without temporal constraint: "The wind blows where it wishes" (John 3:8). The Spirit takes the Logos-structured possibility and makes it real. Like a parent holding a child's hand — loose grip when you're walking together, iron grip when you try to run into traffic.

**Remove any one Person and the Godhead fails to function.** This is not Modalism — it is irreducible threeness.

## ⚠ Falsification Criteria — 7 Kill Conditions

This claim carries its own kill conditions. If any of the following are demonstrated, the isomorphism is demoted to Correspondence (Level 2) or Analogy (Level 1):

- **Kill 1 — Wrong Born Rule operation:** If any biblical event shows a Trinitarian Person performing the wrong Born Rule operation (e.g., the Father structuring instead of holding potential), the mapping fails for that event.
- **Kill 2 — Born Rule reduces to fewer operations:** If the Born Rule can be mathematically reduced to fewer than three operations, the Trinitarian parallel collapses. The threeness must be irreducible.
- **Kill 3 — Non-Trinitarian theology produces same mapping:** If a non-Trinitarian theology (e.g., Unitarian, Binitarian) can produce the same structural three-operation mapping, this is not unique to Christianity.
- **Kill 4 — Spirit shown to NOT actualize:** If the Spirit's biblical role is shown to be mere influence rather than actualization, the |·|² mapping fails. The Spirit must make things real, not just nudge them.
- **Kill 5 — Father's role is active selection:** If the Father's role is shown to be active selection (choosing specific outcomes) rather than possibility-holding, the mapping inverts. The Father must be |ψ⟩, not ⟨φ|.
- **Kill 6 — Son's role is NOT structuring/defining:** If the Son/Logos is shown to not structure or define reality, the ⟨φ| mapping fails. "All things were made through Him" must mean structural mediation, not mere participation.
- **Kill 7 — Any of the six tested events fails:** If any of the six events (Creation, Fall, Incarnation, Cross, Resurrection, Pentecost) shows a Person in the wrong role, that event's mapping fails and the overall confidence drops.
