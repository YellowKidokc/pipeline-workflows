## The Seven Attack-Counter Pairs

Each pair is shown below. The attack (from Genesis 3) is on the left. The counter-move (from the Cross) is on the right. Notice how the structural level matches in every case.

### Pair 1: Information Distortion → Truth Incarnation

**Genesis 3 Attack**
The serpent twists God's words. "Did God really say?" (Genesis 3:1). The attack targets the information layer — like a radio signal getting scrambled before the listener can hear it. (Think of the Information / Logos analogy: sin is the static, truth is the signal. The serpent adds static to God's message.)

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
"The Word became flesh" (John 1:14). The information source itself enters the channel. You cannot distort the message when the message IS the messenger. The counter works at the informational level.

### Pair 2: Boundary Violation → Voluntary Boundary Submission

**Genesis 3 Attack**
Eating from the forbidden tree breaks the one boundary condition. The single rule God placed on the system is violated. The boundary layer is broken.

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
Christ submits to every boundary — the law, suffering, death — voluntarily (Philippians 2:8). Where the first humans broke the boundary, the counter-move is total submission to every boundary. The counter works at the boundary-condition level.

### Pair 3: Autonomous Self-Exaltation → Self-Emptying (Kenosis)

**Genesis 3 Attack**
"You will be like God" (Genesis 3:5). This is an attempt to raise your status through your own independent action. The direction points upward — grabbing for equality with the Creator by doing it yourself.

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
Christ "did not consider equality with God something to be grasped" (Philippians 2:6). This is the exact reversal of the direction. Where the attack grabs upward, the counter empties downward. Kenosis (emptying yourself) is the structural opposite of trying to exalt yourself.

### Pair 4: Knowledge Gained, Innocence Lost → Innocence Voluntarily Laid Down

**Genesis 3 Attack**
Illegal knowledge gained through forbidden measurement. "Their eyes were opened" (Genesis 3:7). Knowledge comes at the cost of innocence. The measurement changes the state forever — like the Quantum / Faith analogy: once you choose a door, the other future disappears.

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
Christ, knowing all things (John 13:3), voluntarily submits to not knowing the hour (Mark 13:32) and experiences being forsaken by God (Matthew 27:46). Where the first humans grabbed for knowledge, the counter voluntarily gives it up.

### Pair 5: Physical Death Enters → Physical Death Absorbed and Reversed

**Genesis 3 Attack**
"You shall surely die" activates. Death enters the physical world. Entropy (the natural tendency toward disorder) becomes the ruling law of the material world. This is damage at the physical level itself. (Think of the Thermodynamics / Judgment analogy: heat only flows one direction, and you can't reverse it without outside energy.)

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
Physical death on the Cross, physical resurrection. The physical level gets entropy reversed. The counter is not spiritual only — it is physical. The empty tomb is the load-bearing wall. If the resurrection is not physical, this pair fails and the entire framework collapses.

### Pair 6: Separation from God → God Experiences Separation

**Genesis 3 Attack**
Adam hides from God (Genesis 3:8). The relationship between Creator and creature is broken. Humanity experiences being cut off from the source.

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (Matthew 27:46). God experiences the separation FROM THE INSIDE. The counter is not watching separation from the outside — it is entering separation itself. The relationship is repaired by the one party who did not cause it voluntarily experiencing it.

### Pair 7: Exile from Eden → Return Path Opened

**Genesis 3 Attack**
Cherubim (angelic guardians) block the way back (Genesis 3:24). Permanent exile. The flaming sword turns every direction. There is no return to the garden by any human path.

**→**

**Cross Counter-Move**
Temple veil torn (Matthew 27:51). Access is restored. The cherubim were embroidered on the veil — the same guardians that blocked the way in Genesis are the figures torn apart at the Cross. The exile is structurally reversed at the exact point of blockage.
