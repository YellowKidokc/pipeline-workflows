#### 1. Truth Travels at Maximum Speed

#### 3. Lies Are Like Electric Charges

Deception bends the field around it. One lie needs more lies to support it. The number of lies grows very fast.

#### 4. Truth Cannot Be Blocked Forever

No material perfectly blocks electromagnetic waves. No lie perfectly blocks truth. Some truth always leaks through.

#### 5. Truth and Coherence Go Together [E,C]

Truth without coherence is just noise. Coherence without truth is just a nice-sounding lie.
