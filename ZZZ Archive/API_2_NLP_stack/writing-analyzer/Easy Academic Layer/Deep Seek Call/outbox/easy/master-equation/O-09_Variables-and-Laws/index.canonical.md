# The Master Equation

ΛG + MΦ² + E + S + T + K + R + Q + F + C

Each letter stands for a piece of the equation. Together, they describe one unified field where physical reality and spiritual reality follow rules that work together.

## The Four Pairs + Center

G ⟷ Q

### Pair 1-8: Grace ↔ Faith

Grace comes from outside you. Faith comes from inside you. Neither one works without the other.

M ⟷ R

### Pair 2-7: Meaning ↔ Relationship

Meaning comes from relationship. And relationship is empty without meaning. Like a match — tiny object, but strike it and the energy stored inside can light a whole room on fire. Small actions carry enormous consequences.

E ⟷ C

### Pair 3-10: Truth ↔ Coherence

Truth without coherence is just noise. Coherence without truth is just delusion. Like a tuning fork — when you strike it near other objects, they start vibrating at the same frequency. One source of perfect pitch that brings everything else into harmony.

S ⟷ F

### Pair 4-9: Entropy ↔ Sin

Sin creates entropy. Entropy is the buildup of consequences. Cause and effect. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside.

T

### Center: Logos / Information

This one stands alone. It's the foundation under everything else. "In the beginning was the Word."

## All Ten Laws

**[ 1 G — Grace ](law-01-G-grace.html)** Negentropy as unmerited favor. The open system term. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet.

**[ 2 M — Meaning ](law-02-M-meaning.html)** Mass-energy as semantic density. E=mc² is also meaning. Like a match — tiny object, but strike it and the energy stored inside can light a whole room on fire. Small actions carry enormous consequences.

**[ 3 E — Truth ](law-03-E-truth.html)** Electromagnetism as aletheia (the Greek word for truth, meaning "unhidden"). Light reveals. Like a flashlight in a dark room — it doesn't create what's there, it reveals what was always there. You can't have half-light. It either reaches the corner or it doesn't.

**[ 4 S — Entropy ](law-04-S-entropy.html)** The second law as judgment. Disorder demands consequence. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system.

**[ 5 T — Logos ](law-05-T-logos.html)** Information as the Word. The center of everything. Like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal.

**[ 6 K — Love ](law-06-K-love.html)** Strong nuclear force as binding. Covenant holds against repulsion. Like a parent holding a child's hand — loose grip when you're walking together, iron grip when you try to run into traffic. The closer you stay, the more freedom you feel.

**[ 7 R — Relationship ](law-07-R-relationship.html)** Relativity as frame-dependence. Meaning requires reference. Like two people watching the same car crash from different street corners — what you see depends on where you're standing, but the crash still happened.

**[ 8 Q — Faith ](law-08-Q-faith.html)** Quantum superposition as commitment. Uncertainty requires choice. Like standing in front of two doors — until you choose one, both futures are possible. The moment you choose, reality locks in. Faith is the act of choosing before you can see what's behind the door.

**[ 9 F — Sin ](law-09-F-sin.html)** Weak force as decay. Symmetry breaking generates entropy. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside.

**[ 10 C — Coherence ](law-10-C-coherence.html)** Integration as Christ. "In him all things hold together." Like a tuning fork — when you strike it near other objects, they start vibrating at the same frequency. One source of perfect pitch that brings everything else into harmony.
