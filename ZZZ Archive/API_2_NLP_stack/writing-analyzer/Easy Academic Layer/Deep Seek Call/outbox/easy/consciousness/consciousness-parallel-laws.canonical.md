## Gravity & Sin's Pull

The universal force that pulls everything downward

### Physical Gravity

Physical gravity follows the inverse square law. This is one of the most basic rules in physics.

$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

This equation means the force of gravity (F) depends on the masses of two objects (m₁ and m₂) multiplied together, divided by the distance between them squared (r²). A constant (G) makes the numbers work out.

- Increases with the mass of both objects
- Decreases with the square of the distance
- Acts on all massive objects everywhere
- Cannot be blocked or removed

### Spiritual Gravity: Sin's Pull

Spiritual gravity — the pull of sin and separation from God — follows the exact same math pattern.

$$F_{\text{spiritual}} = G_s \frac{m_{\text{sin}} \cdot m_{\text{soul}}}{d^2}$$

This equation means the spiritual force pulling you away from God depends on the size of the sin (m_sin) times the soul's capacity for choice (m_soul), divided by the spiritual distance from God (d²). A spiritual constant (G_s) makes the numbers work out.

- Spiritual weight increases with how big the transgression is
- The pull gets stronger when the soul has more capacity to choose
- Being farther from God weakens the effect
- Getting closer to God dramatically reduces the force
- Works on all conscious beings

## Light & Truth

Radiant energy that fades with distance but never disappears

### Light Propagation

Light spreads out according to the inverse square law, radiating evenly in all directions.

$$I = \frac{P}{4\pi d^2}$$

This equation means the intensity of light (I) equals the power of the source (P) divided by the surface area of a sphere (4πd²). The farther you are from the source, the dimmer the light gets.

- Gets dimmer with distance but never fully disappears
- Cannot be destroyed, only changed into another form
- Travels at a constant, unchangeable speed
- Moves through all transparent materials

The 4π factor shows that truth reaches everyone who seeks it.

This equation means entropy (S) equals Boltzmann's constant (k_B) times the natural log of the number of possible arrangements (W). More possible arrangements means more disorder.

- Disorder naturally increases in isolated systems

- Establishes a universal arrow of time

## Miracles as Quantum-Temporal Anomalies

Within this framework, miracles represent moments where eternity touches time. They create localized probability collapses that produce outcomes that are statistically impossible under normal conditions.

$$M = \frac{|P_{\text{observed}} - P_{\text{expected}}|}{\sigma_{P}} > k$$

This equation means a miracle (M) is defined as the absolute difference between the observed probability (P_observed) and the expected probability (P_expected), divided by the standard deviation (σ_P). If this number is greater than a threshold (k, typically 3 to 5 sigma), it is a miracle.

### Miracles Do Not Violate Physical Law

Crucially, this definition preserves the integrity of physical law. A miracle is not something that breaks the laws of physics. Instead, it represents:

1. **Selection among quantum possibilities.** Physical law permits many possible outcomes through quantum superposition. A miracle represents the selection of an outcome that, while physically possible, is extraordinarily unlikely.

2. **Collapse of superposition.** Instead of random quantum collapse, a divine consciousness collapses the probability wave toward a specific outcome.

3. **Information-preserving.** The outcome respects all conservation laws and physical constraints.

4. **Statistically improbable but not impossible.** The miracle occurs within the space of possibilities that physical law permits.

At the quantum level, many so-called impossible events are actually possible with extremely tiny probability. Divine action, in this framework, does not override quantum law. It orchestrates quantum possibilities toward specific outcomes.
