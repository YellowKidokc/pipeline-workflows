## Four Equations That Run the Universe

Stay with me here — I'm going to give you Maxwell's equations in a way that doesn't require a physics degree.

Maxwell wrote four equations. That's it. Four. And between them, they govern every electromagnetic phenomenon you've ever encountered — radio waves, microwaves, X-rays, visible light, the signal on your phone, the MRI that scans your brain. Four equations run all of it.

Here's what each one says, in plain English:

**Equation 1 — The Source**

Electric charges are the source of electric fields. Where there's charge, there's a field radiating outward.

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$

This means: the amount of electric field spreading out from a point equals the charge density (how much charge is packed in) divided by a constant.

**Equation 2 — The Coupling**

A changing magnetic field creates a curling electric field. The two fields drive each other.

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$$

This means: a magnetic field that changes over time creates an electric field that swirls around it, like a whirlpool.

**Equation 3 — No Monopoles**

There are no magnetic monopoles. No isolated north without south. Magnetic field lines always close on themselves.

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$$

This means: magnetic fields never start or end at a single point — they always form complete loops.

**Equation 4 — Maxwell's Addition**

Electric currents *and* changing electric fields create curling magnetic fields. The last term is Maxwell's contribution — the one that changed everything.

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$$

This means: a magnetic field swirls around both electric currents and electric fields that change over time. The last part (∂E/∂t) is the displacement current — Maxwell's key insight.

That last term — $\mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$ — is called the displacement current. Three of these four equations were already known before Maxwell. His genius was adding that one term to the fourth equation. And when he did, waves emerged. Take equations 2 and 4, combine them, and out drops the wave equation:

**The Wave Equation — Light**

$$\frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{E}}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 \mathbf{E}$$

This means: the electric field changes over time in a way that creates a wave traveling at speed c — the speed of light.

Light. Traveling at speed $c$. Born from the coupling of two fields that everyone thought were separate.
