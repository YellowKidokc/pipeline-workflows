# Critical Thresholds

**1962 — Engel v. Vitale**

The Supreme Court banned prayer in public schools. This was the first domino. Within one generation, every single FACTS metric started going downhill.

**1973 — Roe v. Wade**

The Supreme Court redefined when life is sacred. This decision made it legal to separate moral consequences from personal choices. That separation sped up.

**1995 — Internet Mass Adoption**

Information started spreading without central control. Within 15 years, people's attention spans got cut in half. Algorithms started pushing people toward extreme views.[9]

**2015 — Social Media Saturation**

Words started losing their meaning. This is called **semantic collapse** — like a radio signal drowning in static. Words like "violence," "safety," and "justice" no longer meant the same thing to everyone. Public conversation broke into pieces.
