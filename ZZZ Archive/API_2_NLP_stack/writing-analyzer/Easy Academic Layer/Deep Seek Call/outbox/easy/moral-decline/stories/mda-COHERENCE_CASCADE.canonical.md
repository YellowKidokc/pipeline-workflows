# The Coherence Cascade

Tracking the Four-Domain Collapse Across Six Generations (1900–2025)

**The Hypothesis:** Social coherence (how well a society holds together) collapses in a predictable order. **Words fail first** — the meaning of language erodes before bigger structures fall apart. Then **Family**, then **Institutions**, then **Economy**. The data from 125 years backs this up.

Year | Story | SEMANTIC | FAMILIAL | INSTITUTIONAL | ECONOMIC | Status
---|---|---|---|---|---|---
1900 | Samuel | 98 | 95 | 92 | 90 | BASELINE
1926 | Henry | 90 | 93 | 90 | 85 | THINNING
1950 | William | 75 | 88 | 90 | 92 | FALSE PEAK
1974 | Thomas | 40 | 50 | 45 | 65 | PHASE TRANSITION
1998 | Jacob I | 25 | 35 | 35 | 45 | RUBBLE
2025 | Jacob II | 18 | 20 | 18 | 25 | BOTTOM

## THE PATTERN IS CLEAR

**SEMANTIC** (meaning of words) drops first: 98 → 90 → 75 → 40 → 25 → 18
**FAMILIAL** (family bonds) follows: 95 → 93 → 88 → 50 → 35 → 20
**INSTITUTIONAL** (trust in organizations) third: 92 → 90 → 90 → 45 → 35 → 18
**ECONOMIC** (financial stability) last: 90 → 85 → 92 → 65 → 45 → 25

## Causal Events by Domain

### SEMANTIC DRIFT (Words Lose Meaning)

  * 1926 Henry must EXPLAIN "duty" to sons — the word still exists, but the meaning is fading
  * 1950 Words become advertising slogans — used to sell things, not to tell truth
  * 1974 "Integrity" only used as accusation — nobody says "I have integrity," they say "you lack it"
  * 1998 "Friend" = screen name — the word now means a contact list, not a real relationship
  * 2025 Words require conscious CHOICE — you have to decide what words mean, they don't carry shared meaning anymore

### FAMILIAL COLLAPSE (Family Bonds Break)

  * 1950 "Ball and chain" jokes begin — marriage is seen as a trap, not a commitment
  * 1969 California no-fault divorce — you can leave without proving wrongdoing
  * 1974 Divorce rate 4.6/1000 — nearly one in five marriages ends each year
  * 1998 33% unmarried age 30-39 — a third of people in their prime years are not married
  * 2025 46% unmarried, family optional — almost half of adults don't marry, and family is a choice, not a given

### INSTITUTIONAL COLLAPSE (Trust in Organizations Dies)

  * 1958 Peak trust: 73% — most people trusted the government, church, and media
  * 1974 Watergate: trust drops to 36% — the scandal breaks faith in leadership
  * 1998 "They all lie" as baseline — people assume every institution is dishonest
  * 2025 Record lows: <20% trust — almost nobody believes institutions tell the truth

### ECONOMIC UNMOORING (Money and Jobs Lose Stability)

  * 1971 Nixon ends gold standard — money is no longer backed by gold, just government promise
  * 1974 Inflation hits 12.3% — prices rise fast, savings lose value
  * 1998 Two incomes = one 1950 income — both parents must work to match what one earner made in 1950
  * 2025 Gig economy, no pensions — jobs are temporary, no retirement security
