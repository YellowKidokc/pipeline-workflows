# Lane 05 · Amish & Case Studies

## The Control Group

Same country, same century, opposite outcome. The Amish as a living test of the coherence model.

9 chapters · David Lowe · Theophysics Research Initiative

## Chapters

9 pages

[ 01 The Amish Exception ](MDA-042-amish-exception-THE-CLAIM.html) — The Amish are a living control group. They rejected the things that cause social decoherence (the breakdown of social order). While America's coherence fell, theirs stayed steady.

[ 02 The Amish Control Case ](MDA-043-amish-proof-THE-PROOF.html) — We tracked the decline from chi = 0.85 in 1900 to chi = 0.27 today. (Chi is a measure of social coherence — how tightly a society holds together.) The Amish case asks the question that matters: was this decline inevitable?

[ 03 Amish vs. America ](MDA-044-amish-control-study-THE-METHOD.html) — Two populations. Same country. Same century. Same legal rights. Same access to modern medicine, modern law, modern infrastructure.

[ 04 The Amish Anomaly ](MDA-045-amish-control-group-THE-DATA.html) — Between 1960 and 2025, every measurable indicator of American social coherence declined: family structure collapsed, institutional trust evaporated, and community bonds frayed.

[ 05 Amish Demographics ](MDA-046-amish-demographics-THE-NUMBERS.html) — The earlier articles in this sub-series established why the Amish are the control group: the Coherence Factory in Holmes County (09a), the head-to-head comparison of outcomes, and the demographic data that proves the point.

[ 06 The Amish Tech Filter ](MDA-047-amish-tech-filter-THE-FILTER.html) — The Amish are not anti-technology. That's the first thing most people get wrong.

[ 07 The Ordnung Algorithm ](MDA-048-ordnung-algorithm-THE-MECHANISM.html) — This report presents a full structural analysis of the Old Order Amish Ordnung. It confirms the hypothesis that this unwritten code works like an algorithm — a set of rules that keeps the system running smoothly.

[ 08 The Coherence Factory ](MDA-049-coherence-factory-THE-SYNTHESIS.html) — Most Americans look at the Amish and see nostalgia. A people frozen in 1850, opting out of modern life because they cannot or will not keep up.

[ 09 The Korea Experiment ](MDA-051-korea-experiment.html) — The Korea experiment: a natural test of the coherence model. It compares two different moral and institutional paths that started from the same place.
