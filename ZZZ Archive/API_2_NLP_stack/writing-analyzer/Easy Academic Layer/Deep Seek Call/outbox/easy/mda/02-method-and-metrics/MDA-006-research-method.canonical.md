# The Five-Page System

Each inquiry in this project uses **five pages**. They are not optional and they are not just for show. Each page does a different mental job. You must create all five for every new question.

## Inquiry Seed

**Question & Orientation**

This page states the research question exactly. It also captures the researcher's first thought or belief *before* any AI input changes it. This matters: your pre-AI thinking is load-bearing. If you let AI frame the question, you've already lost control of the answer.

**File:** Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md

## AI Prompt Engine

**How AI Is Used**

This page records the exact prompts you used, the AI models you asked, and the outputs you got — without judging them yet. The AI output is pasted or summarized here raw. No evaluation at this stage. The engine maps the terrain: competing ideas, key names, major fault lines, suggested search areas. It does not decide what is true.

**File:** AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md

## Research Workspace

**Evidence & Links**

This is the living document where actual evidence piles up. Primary sources, data links, study citations, statistics with dates and sources. This is where AI suggestions get tested against real sources. Not every AI claim survives contact with the primary literature. The workspace is where that collision happens.

**File:** Research_Workspace_[Question].md

## Contradictions & Resolution

**Sense-Making**

This is the most important page in the system. Where do the sources disagree? Where does the AI output contradict the primary literature? Where does your gut feeling conflict with the data? This page forces you to sit with the confusion long enough to understand it — instead of only picking evidence that supports the idea you started with.

Truth is most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily.

**File:** Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md

## Quotes, Domains, & Links

**Shared Library**

A shared reference library built across all inquiries. Key quotes with who said them and when. Domain categories that came up during research. External links with short notes about what they contain. This page grows over time — it builds up across every inquiry and creates the raw material for publications.

**File:** Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md

# What AI Does — and Does Not Do

## AI Is Used To

- **Map intellectual terrain quickly across many different fields
- **Surface contradictions between competing ideas
- **Organize competing frameworks side by side
- **Suggest where deeper primary-source study is needed
- **Test early conclusions against counterarguments

## AI Is Not Used To

- **Decide what is true
- **Replace primary-source verification
- **Frame the initial question before the researcher does
- **Choose which evidence counts as important
- **Resolve contradictions that require human judgment

Some AI outputs in this project are incomplete. Some are wrong. That is expected and is not a failure of the method. The method treats AI error as a signal — it tells you where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested, where consensus has been manufactured instead of earned, and where you need to dig deeper into primary sources.

---

# How to Use This System

**Workflow Sequence**

1. Start with the **Inquiry Seed**. Write your question and your first thought *before* running any AI prompt.

2. Run the **AI Prompt Engine**. Paste the output. Do not judge it yet — just collect it.

3. Open the **Research Workspace**. Follow the AI's leads into primary sources. Cite everything. Record what the data actually says.

4. Build the **Contradictions page**. Force the disagreements into the open. Sit with them. Do not cover them up.

5. Maintain the **Quotes, Domains, & Links** library as you go. Every session adds to it.

Create all five pages for each new question. The structure is not bureaucracy — it is the mental framework that prevents AI-assisted research from turning into AI-generated opinion.
