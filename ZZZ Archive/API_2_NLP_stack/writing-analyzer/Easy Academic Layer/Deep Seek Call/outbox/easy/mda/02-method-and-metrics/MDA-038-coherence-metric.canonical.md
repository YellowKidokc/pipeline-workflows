# Can Physics Measure Moral Decay?

## Abstract

This paper introduces the **Coherence Metric** (χ, pronounced "kai"). It's a way to measure how well something holds together. The math comes from physics—specifically, how materials change from one state to another (like water turning to ice). We show that the same equation that describes when magnets lose their magnetism also describes when societies fall apart. We looked at data from 1900 to 2025 across nine different areas of life. All of them started to collapse at the same time—between 1968 and 1973. This suggests one single cause, not nine separate problems. The framework makes predictions you can test. It doesn't depend on your political views.

**Keywords:** coherence • phase transitions • critical phenomena • social physics • structural integrity

## 1. Introduction

Here's the simple question this paper asks: **Can physics measure moral decay?**

Not as a metaphor. Not as a comparison. As actual measurement.

Here's the claim: Things fall apart the same way—whether it's a bridge, a human body, or a civilization. When you remove the things that hold a system together, it doesn't slowly get worse. It looks stable for a while, then hits a tipping point and collapses fast. This isn't an opinion. This is how phase transitions work in physics.

Your politics don't matter. You can vote for gravity or against gravity. Gravity doesn't care. It still pulls.

### The Coherence Master Equation

\[ \chi \approx |P - P_c|^{\beta} \]

This equation says: Coherence (χ) equals how far the control parameter (P) is from its critical threshold (Pc), raised to a certain power (β). The same math that describes how superconductors work also describes how civilizations work.

## Framework Variables

**χ (Chi / Coherence)** — How well a system keeps its internal order and resists falling apart.

**P (Control Parameter)** — The current level of constraint in a domain. Think: law, covenant, standard, anchor.

**Pc (Critical Threshold)** — The value where the system tips. Cross this line, and collapse speeds up.

**β (Scaling Exponent)** — How fast the collapse happens near the tipping point. The average β across all domains is 0.41.

## 2. The Synchronization Problem

If coherence decay were random—if divorce rates, crime rates, obesity rates, trust numbers, and education results were all caused by different things—then their tipping points would be spread randomly across the 125 years we looked at.

They're not.

Between 1968 and 1973, nine separate measures all crossed their critical thresholds within a 5-year window.

The chance of this happening randomly is vanishingly small (p < 10⁻⁶). This is the signature of a system failure, not a bunch of unrelated problems.

**1970.0** — Mean Break Year (average across all nine domains)
**3.2 yrs** — Standard Deviation (tight clustering confirms synchronization)
**10⁻⁶** — p-value (vanishingly improbable by chance)

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Data Sources

We used long-term data sets from 1900 to 2025 for nine domains. Sources include CDC, Pew, Gallup, NORC GSS, BGSU NCFMR, and BLS. See Appendix A for the full list.

### 3.2 Normalization

We converted all data to z-scores (a standard way to compare different types of measurements) using 1940–1949 as the baseline. This lets us compare divorce rates and trust numbers on the same scale.

### 3.3 Curve-Fitting

We used a statistical method called nonlinear least squares regression with bootstrap confidence intervals (1,000 iterations) to fit the data to the power-law equation χ ≈ |P − Pc|β.

## 8. Discussion

### The 1968–1973 Constraint Removal Cluster

Synchronized inflection across nine domains demands a common cause. The 1968–1973 window produced an identifiable cluster of constraint-removal legislative and cultural events. These are not interpretations. They are dates.

**1965** — *Griswold v. Connecticut* — Decoupled contraception from the state's interest in marriage, weakening the familial constraint structure.

**1968** — Vietnam / Civil Unrest — Institutional authority collapsed publicly. Trust in government began its terminal decline from 62% toward 16%.

**1970** — No-Fault Divorce — California's Family Law Act removed permanence from the marriage covenant. Divorce rate began vertical climb.

**1971** — Gold Window Closure — Nixon severed the dollar's constraint anchor. Currency decoupled from physical reality. $1.00 became $0.14 over 53 years.

**1972** — *Eisenstadt v. Baird* — Extended contraceptive access to unmarried individuals, completing the legal decoupling of sexuality from covenant.

**1973** — *Roe v. Wade* — Removed the final institutional constraint on the consequences of decoupled sexuality. Nine domains show synchronized inflection within this window.

### Limitations

1. **Correlation is not causation.** Synchronization is statistically anomalous, but the causal mechanism requires further formal analysis beyond Granger causality.

2. **Measurement validity.** Domain operationalizations involve index construction choices that affect results. Alternative operationalizations should be tested.

3. **β universality requires replication.** The mean β = 0.41 is consistent with known universality classes but requires independent replication across additional longitudinal datasets.

### Control Group: The Amish Proof

Amish communities maintained the constraint set that the wider culture removed. They did not adopt no-fault divorce, gold window closure, or the semantic drift. The result is a natural experiment with an observable outcome.

**~0%** — Divorce Rate (vs. 45% national average)
**Fastest-growing demographic in North America**
**20 yr** — Population Doubling

Structural coherence = demographic survival. The equation works in reverse. Restore constraints, coherence returns. The Amish are not a curiosity. They are the experimental proof.
