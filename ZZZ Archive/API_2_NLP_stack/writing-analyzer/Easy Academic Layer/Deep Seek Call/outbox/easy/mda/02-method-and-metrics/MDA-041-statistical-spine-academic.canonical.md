# Moral Decline of America — Data Layer

## THE STATISTICAL SPINE

Eight measurements that work the same way every time. Six generations of Americans. One clear path. When you look at this much data all together, you're not interpreting it anymore — you're just reading what it says.

## Part I: Samuel — The Quiet Republic

**Coherence Factor (χ) = 0.85**

The Spanish-American War just ended. McKinley is president. There are 8,000 cars in America. Rural electrification doesn't exist yet. STD rates are high but hidden — syphilis is everywhere.

- **Divorce:** 0.7 per 1,000 people
- **Marriage:** 9.3 per 1,000 people
- **Church Weekly:** Almost everyone
- **Fertility:** Not measured the same way
- **Premarital Sex (by age 20):** About 10%
- **Violent Crime:** About 10 per 100,000 people
- **Life Expectancy:** 47 years

**Outside Voices Per Day: 0**

The information environment: looking at stars, hearing nature sounds, and talking to people who are physically in the same room. Mediated input from outside the home: zero per day.

## Part II: Henry — The First Crack

**Coherence Factor (χ) = 0.82**

Radios enter homes. Model T Fords are everywhere. Prohibition is failing. The Scopes trial shakes up religious America. The Roaring Twenties — the first mass consumer culture.

- **Divorce:** 1.6 ↑
- **Marriage:** 10.1 ↑
- **Church Weekly:** Almost everyone
- **Fertility:** 2.5 ↓
- **Trust Others:** Not measured yet
- **Premarital Sex (by age 20):** About 25% ↑
- **Violent Crime:** About 8 ↓
- **Life Expectancy:** 58 years ↑

**Outside Voices Per Day: ~5**

Broadcast radio: about one hour per day, listened to as a family in the home. This is the first time information from outside breaks into the house — but it's limited, shared, and controllable.

## Part III: William — Peak Signal

**Coherence Factor (χ) = 0.78**

The GI Bill builds the middle class. Television goes from 9% of homes to 87% of homes. The Korean War happens. The baby boom peaks. The Kinsey reports shock the nation.

- **Divorce:** 2.6 ↑
- **Marriage:** 11.1 ↑ (peak)
- **Church Weekly:** 49% ↑ (peak)
- **Fertility:** 3.5 ↑ (boom)
- **Trust Others:** About 55%
- **Premarital Sex (by age 20):** About 30% ↑
- **Violent Crime:** About 5 ↓ (low)
- **Life Expectancy:** 68 years ↑

**Outside Voices Per Day: ~50**

Broadcast television: about 4.5 hours per day of shared family watching. This is historically important: it's the first time national trauma events — Kennedy's assassination in 1963, Vietnam war footage — come directly into your living room in real time.

Nixon resigns. No-fault divorce spreads. Roe v. Wade (1973). The gold window closes (1971). Mainline Protestant churches start collapsing.

**Outside Voices Per Day: ~200**

Television: 6+ hours per day. Radio, print ads, direct mail. The total amount of information coming in has passed the point where quiet silence is something you can just have at home.

## Part V: Jacob — The Scattering

**Coherence Factor (χ) = 0.40**

Clinton impeachment. The internet goes mainstream. Columbine (1999). The dot-com bubble. Dating moves online.

- **Divorce:** 4.0 ↓
- **Marriage:** 8.2 ↓↓
- **Church Weekly:** 33% ↓

- **Premarital Sex (by age 20):** About 75% ↑↑

**Outside Voices Per Day: ~3,000**

Internet access, email, cable TV (500+ channels), pagers, and early cell phones. Now you don't just get pushed information — you can pull it yourself. You start scrolling. This behavior will define the next generation.

## Part VI: Jacob — The Drowning

**Coherence Factor (χ) = 0.27 — THRESHOLD CROSSED**

AI is everywhere. The birth rate is below replacement. The marriage rate is at a historic low. Trust is at 22%. Loneliness is officially an epidemic. People are having less sex even though it's easier to find.

- **Divorce:** 2.3* ↓
- **Marriage:** Less than 5.0 ↓↓↓
- **Church Weekly:** About 22% ↓↓
- **Fertility:** 1.6 ↓ (below replacement)
- **Trust Others:** About 22% ↓↓
- **Premarital Sex (by age 20):** About 85% ↑
- **Violent Crime:** About 380 ↓
- **Life Expectancy:** 79 years → (stalled)

**Outside Voices Per Day: 10,000+**

About 96 smartphone notifications per day on average. Algorithm-curated infinite-scroll content. AI-mediated social interaction. Quiet silence is no longer the normal state of being at home — you have to actively build it and actively defend it against technology.

**\* THE DIVORCE PARADOX**

The divorce rate looks like it's going down. But that's a trick of the numbers. Think of it like this: if fewer people get married in the first place, then fewer people can get divorced. The rate of marriages that end in divorce hasn't really dropped. The real collapse — people not forming permanent bonds at all — keeps going. The old measurement just can't see it anymore.

## The Long-Term Path

Same measurements, six points in time, one direction. The lines don't lie.

**Coherence Factor χ:** It goes from 0.85 (1900 starting point) to 0.27 (2025 estimate). It crossed the critical threshold (χ_c = 0.35) sometime between 2018 and 2022. For comparison, the Amish control group kept a Coherence Factor above 0.88 across the same time period.

**Marriage rate:** Rose to a peak in 1950 (11.1 per 1,000 people), then kept falling to a historic low (less than 5.0 per 1,000 in 2025). **Divorce rate:** Rose to a peak in 1981 (5.3 per 1,000), then looked like it dropped — but that's because the marriage rate shrank, not because people are more committed.

**Estimated daily outside information contacts per person:** 0 (1900) → 10,000+ (2025). You need a logarithmic scale (a graph that jumps by powers of 10 instead of by 1s) just to show the full range. The pattern isn't a slow spread — it's an explosion.

**Three specific measurements on the same percentage scale:**
- Weekly church attendance (dropping from about 90% to about 22%)
- Interpersonal trust — "most people can be trusted" (dropping from about 55% to about 22%)
- Premarital sex by age 20 (rising from about 10% to about 85%)

All three move in the same direction at the same time. This fits the idea that American society is going through a synchronized phase transition — like water suddenly turning to ice, but in reverse.
