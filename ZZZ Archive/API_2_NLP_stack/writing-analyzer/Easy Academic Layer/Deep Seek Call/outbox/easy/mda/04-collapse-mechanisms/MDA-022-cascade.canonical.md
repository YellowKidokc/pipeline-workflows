# The Coherence Factor: How Civilizations Fall Apart

**The Coherence Factor (χ)** measures how well a society's parts work together. These parts include families, schools, churches, government, trust between strangers, and shared beliefs.

Think of it like a radio signal through static (Information / Logos). When the signal is clear, the message gets through. When there's too much static, you hear nothing but noise. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal.

χ = 1.0 means everything is working together perfectly. χ = 0.0 means everything is shattered.

**Current U.S. χ: ≈ 0.27**

**Critical Threshold: 0.35**

**Status: Threshold Crossed**

The big shift happened by 1973.

What followed was fifty years of steady decline.

**χ dropped from 0.55 to 0.27.**

Every decade made things worse. Every "solution" actually sped up the fall.

Here's how we fell below the threshold.

---

## Decade by Decade

### 1973–1983: The Fracturing

**χ: 0.55 → 0.48**

**What happened**:

- Divorce rate peaked (1980-81)
- Inflation destroyed people's savings
- Iran hostage crisis
- Three Mile Island and Love Canal — people stopped trusting institutions
- Single-parent households doubled
- Cable TV broke up the shared information everyone used to get from just three channels

**Key metric**: Divorce rate hit 5.3 per 1,000 people (1981) — the highest in history.

**What it felt like**: Malaise. President Carter's "crisis of confidence" speech. A feeling that the future would be worse than the past.

---

### 1983–1993: The Substitution

**χ: 0.48 → 0.43**

**What happened**:

- Reagan's "Morning in America" — economic optimism came back
- Cocaine epidemic, then crack epidemic
- AIDS crisis
- Savings & Loan banks collapsed
- Crime peaked (1991)
- USSR fell — gave a temporary boost to coherence

**Key metric**: Out-of-wedlock births hit 30% (up from 5% in 1960).

**What it felt like**: Prosperity was hiding the rot. Money was flowing. But families kept breaking apart. Crime kept rising. The inner cities burned.

**The substitution**: People replaced real coherence with material stuff. "Things are good" meant "I have money and things," not "my community is healthy."

---

### 1993–2003: The Virtualization

**χ: 0.43 → 0.38**

**What happened**:

- Internet goes mainstream
- Clinton scandals made leadership corruption seem normal
- Columbine school shooting (1999)
- 9/11 — the last major spike in coherence
- Reality TV replaced shared culture
- First generation raised on digital screens

**Key metric**: "Most people can be trusted" drops below 35%.

**What it felt like**: Everything sped up. More options. More fragmentation. The internet promised connection but actually delivered isolation.

**The spike**: 9/11 briefly pushed χ up — shared enemy, shared grief, shared purpose. By 2004, that boost was gone. The last natural event that could have united everyone failed to reverse the trend.

---

### 2003–2013: The Fragmentation

**χ: 0.38 → 0.35** (THRESHOLD APPROACHED)

**What happened**:

- Iraq War lies were revealed
- 2008 financial crisis — trust in institutions collapsed
- Social media began (Facebook 2004, Twitter 2006, iPhone 2007)
- "Deaths of despair" (suicide, drug overdose, alcohol poisoning) began rising fast
- Religious "nones" (people with no religion) became the largest "religious" group
- Opioid epidemic began

**Key metric**: Suicide rate began a steep climb after decades of being stable.

**What it felt like**: The center cannot hold. Everyone retreated to their own tribes. Facts became contested. Reality itself started to fragment.

**Threshold approach**: By 2013, χ ≈ 0.35. Right at the edge.

## The Cascade Mechanics

Why did it keep getting worse?

Because the **feedback loops** reversed.

In a coherent society:

- Trust builds community, which builds more trust
- Families raise healthy kids, who raise healthy families
- Shared meaning enables cooperation, which creates more shared meaning

In an incoherent society:

- Distrust fragments community, which creates more distrust
- Broken families raise wounded kids, who struggle to form families
- Fragmented meaning prevents cooperation, which fragments meaning further

**Once you cross the threshold, the system speeds up its own decline.**

Every attempt to fix one area fails because the other areas undermine it.

- Try to strengthen families → but trust is gone, so commitments can't hold
- Try to rebuild trust → but shared meaning is gone, so there's no basis for trust
- Try to restore meaning → but institutions are corrupt, so no one believes them

The cascade feeds itself.
