# The Coherence Cascade

Tracking the Four-Domain Collapse Across Six Generations (1900–2025)

**The Hypothesis:** Social coherence (how well a society holds together) collapses in a predictable sequence. **Words fail first** — shared meaning in language breaks down before anything else. Then **Family** falls apart. Then **Institutions** (government, church, schools) lose trust. Then **Economy** unravels. The data across 125 years confirms this cascade happens in that exact order.

Year | Story | SEMANTIC (Words) | FAMILIAL (Family) | INSTITUTIONAL (Institutions) | ECONOMIC (Economy) | Status
---|---|---|---|---|---|---
1900 | Samuel | 98 | 95 | 92 | 90 | BASELINE (Starting point)
1926 | Henry | 90 | 93 | 90 | 85 | THINNING (Starting to weaken)
1950 | William | 75 | 88 | 90 | 92 | FALSE PEAK (Looks good, but isn't)
1974 | Thomas | 40 | 50 | 45 | 65 | PHASE TRANSITION (Rapid change)
1998 | Jacob I | 25 | 35 | 35 | 45 | RUBBLE (Broken down)
2025 | Jacob II | 18 | 20 | 18 | 25 | BOTTOM (Lowest point)

## THE PATTERN IS CLEAR

**SEMANTIC** drops first: 98 → 90 → 75 → 40 → 25 → 18
**FAMILIAL** follows: 95 → 93 → 88 → 50 → 35 → 20
**INSTITUTIONAL** third: 92 → 90 → 90 → 45 → 35 → 18
**ECONOMIC** last: 90 → 85 → 92 → 65 → 45 → 25

Think of it like a radio signal through static (Information / Logos analogy). The clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin (brokenness) is the static. Truth is the signal. In 1900, the signal was almost clear. By 2025, the static has nearly drowned everything out.

## Causal Events by Domain

### SEMANTIC DRIFT (Words lose their meaning)

  * 1926 Henry must EXPLAIN "duty" to sons — the word no longer carries automatic weight
  * 1950 Words become advertising slogans — "pure," "natural," "fresh" become sales tools, not real descriptions
  * 1974 "Integrity" only used as accusation — nobody says "he has integrity," only "he lacks integrity"
  * 1998 "Friend" = screen name — the word now means someone you've never met in person
  * 2025 Words require conscious CHOICE — you have to deliberately decide what words mean, because nothing is shared anymore

### FAMILIAL COLLAPSE (Family structure breaks down)

  * 1950 "Ball and chain" jokes begin — marriage starts being treated as a trap, not a commitment
  * 1969 California no-fault divorce — the first state to let people divorce without proving the other person did something wrong
  * 1974 Divorce rate 4.6/1000 — nearly double what it was in 1950
  * 1998 33% unmarried age 30-39 — a third of people in their prime adult years have never married
  * 2025 46% unmarried, family optional — nearly half of adults don't marry, and having a family is seen as a choice, not a norm

Like a parent holding a child's hand (Strong Nuclear Force / Love analogy) — loose grip when you're walking together, iron grip when you try to run into traffic. The closer you stay, the more freedom you feel. When families loosen their grip, everyone drifts.

### INSTITUTIONAL COLLAPSE (Trust in big organizations vanishes)

  * 1958 Peak trust: 73% — most Americans trusted the government to do what's right most of the time
  * 1974 Watergate: trust drops to 36% — after the scandal, trust in government was cut in half
  * 1998 "They all lie" as baseline — people assume every politician, news outlet, and leader is dishonest
  * 2025 Record lows: <20% trust — fewer than 1 in 5 people trust major institutions

Like heat flowing from hot to cold (Thermodynamics / Judgment analogy) — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system. Trust only flows one way: down.

### ECONOMIC UNMOORING (Money loses its anchor)

  * 1971 Nixon ends gold standard — the dollar is no longer backed by gold, just by government promise
  * 1974 Inflation hits 12.3% — prices rise so fast that savings lose value every month
  * 1998 Two incomes = one 1950 income — a couple both working full-time earns what one worker earned in 1950
  * 2025 Gig economy, no pensions — most jobs are temporary, no retirement plans, no stability

Like a match (Mass-Energy / Meaning analogy) — tiny object, but strike it and the energy stored inside can light a whole room on fire. Small actions carry enormous consequences. Ending the gold standard was a small match strike. The fire is still burning.
