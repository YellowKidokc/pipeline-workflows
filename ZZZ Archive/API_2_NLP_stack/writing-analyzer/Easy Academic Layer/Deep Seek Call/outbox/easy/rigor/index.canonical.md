# Measurement · Verification · Replication

## The Rigor Layer

Every claim in Theophysics and MDA is backed by a formal measurement system. This isn't metaphor or analogy. It uses the same math that powers information theory, statistical mechanics, and signal processing — but applied to coherence, morality, and how healthy a civilization is.

Here are the core equations:

Li = log( (Ci + ε) / (Di + ε) )

This equation measures the signal in one area. It compares constructive signals (good stuff) to destructive signals (bad stuff) using a log ratio. Think of it like a radio signal through static — the clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal.

χ = (1/N) Σ wi · Li

This equation combines all the individual signals into one overall score. It's like averaging all your grades to get a final GPA for a whole society.

dS/dt = σ − W/T

This equation tracks whether things are getting better or worse over time. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system.

## The Mathematics

The rigor layer is not an afterthought. It is the foundation. Every narrative claim in MDA — every drop in family stability, every collapse in shared meaning, every phase transition — can be traced back to a specific variable, a channel score, and an overall coherence calculation.

### Channel Score

Li = log( (Ci + ε) / (Di + ε) )

Each domain gets a constructive signal C and a destructive signal D. The log-ratio tells you which side is winning. This is structurally identical to Kullback-Leibler divergence and Shannon channel capacity — the same math used to measure how much information can pass through a noisy phone line.

### Aggregate Coherence

χ = (1/N) · Σ wi · Li

The aggregate coherence χ is the weighted average of all channel scores. It's a single number that tells you the health of a civilization: above 0.35 is sustainable, below is precarious, negative means collapse.

### Dynamics

dSm/dt = σ − W/T

This equation tracks whether coherence is growing (σ > W/T) or shrinking (σ < W/T). It uses the same entropy-production math from stochastic thermodynamics — the physics of how order and disorder change over time.
