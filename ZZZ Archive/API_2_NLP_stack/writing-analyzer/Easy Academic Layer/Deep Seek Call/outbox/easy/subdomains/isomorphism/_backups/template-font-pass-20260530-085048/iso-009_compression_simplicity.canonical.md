# ISO-009: Compression and Simplicity

## Isomorphism Record

**ID:** ISO-009
**Date:** 2026-03-10
**Status:** Candidate

---

## Domains

**Domain A:** Physics / Mathematics — The Principle of Least Action, Kolmogorov Complexity, Occam's Razor as a physical rule
**Domain B:** Christian Theology — Wisdom Theology, Divine Simplicity, Creation through Economy

**Concept A:** Nature prefers the simplest descriptions and the most efficient paths. The principle of least action (δS = 0, where S = ∫L dt) says that nature picks the path that uses the least "effort" — like water always finding the fastest route downhill. Kolmogorov complexity K(x) measures the shortest computer program that can produce a given string of data. Nature's laws are very compressible — the Standard Model of physics fits on a t-shirt, yet it explains every known particle. This suggests reality has low Kolmogorov complexity compared to how rich it looks. Occam's Razor (prefer simpler explanations) works incredibly well in physics.

**Concept B:** "The Lord by wisdom founded the earth; by understanding he established the heavens" (Proverbs 3:19). Wisdom (חָכְמָה, *sophia*) is the divine quality through which creation is ordered with elegant economy. Divine simplicity (*simplicitas Dei*) means God is not made of parts — God's essence is as simple as it gets. Matthew 6:22: "If your eye is single (ἁπλοῦς — simple, single-focused), your whole body will be full of light."

---

## The Mapping

**Mathematical Form A:**

Principle of Least Action:

δS = 0, where S = ∫₀ᵗ L(q, q̇, t) dt

This equation says: the path a physical system takes is the one that uses the least "action" (a measure of energy over time). It's not a step-by-step force calculation — it's a GLOBAL optimization. Nature "chooses" the path that satisfies this rule over the whole journey, not just at each point.

Kolmogorov Complexity:

K(x) = min{|p| : U(p) = x}

This says: the complexity of a piece of data is the length of the shortest computer program that can produce it. Nature's fundamental laws have very low K — the Standard Model Lagrangian fits on a t-shirt, yet it generates the behavior of all known particles. Reality is massively compressed.

In information terms: Nature's laws achieve near-perfect compression of physical phenomena. The laws are the compression algorithm; the phenomena are the output.

**Mathematical Form B:**

Proverbs 8:22-31: Wisdom (personified) speaks: "The Lord possessed me at the beginning of his work, the first of his acts of old. Ages ago I was set up, at the first, before the beginning of the earth." Wisdom is the FIRST principle — present before creation, the organizing intelligence through which creation is structured.

Proverbs 3:19: "The Lord by wisdom founded the earth" — wisdom is the METHOD of creation. Not brute force, not random decree, but wise ordering.

Divine Simplicity (Aquinas, ST I, Q.3): God is absolutely simple — not made of matter and form, not made of essence and existence (in God they are the same thing), not a category. God is the least complicated thing possible. The most fundamental reality is the simplest.

Matthew 6:22 (ἁπλοῦς — *haplous* — single, simple): Spiritual health is described as simplicity and single-focus. Complexity of motive ("double-mindedness" — δίψυχος, James 1:8) is a problem.

Formal parallel: Creation operates by an economy principle — maximum effect from minimal specification. This is exactly what the action principle does: it extracts maximum predictive power from a minimal rule.

**Shared Structure:**

1. **Economy of generation** — Both domains say the fundamental creative process uses extreme economy. Nature doesn't use more action than needed. God doesn't use more complexity than needed. Both optimize.

2. **Simplicity at the foundation** — Physics: the deepest laws are the simplest (symmetry rules, variational rules). Theology: the deepest reality (God) is the simplest (divine simplicity). In both, complexity comes later; simplicity is the starting point.

3. **Compression as a sign of intelligence** — Low Kolmogorov complexity means the data was generated by a short program, not by randomness. Theology says creation was generated by Wisdom — which is the theological name for the generative intelligence that compresses infinite possibility into elegant reality.

4. **Global optimization, not local tinkering** — The action principle is not a local force law; it's a global optimization over entire paths. Wisdom theology portrays God as operating from a global perspective ("declaring the end from the beginning" — Isaiah 46:10), not local tinkering.

**What Is NOT Claimed:**

- NOT claiming the action principle IS wisdom — the action principle is a math rule. Wisdom in Proverbs is a personified divine quality. These are different categories.
- NOT claiming Kolmogorov complexity proves God designed the universe — low complexity is consistent with design, but also with other explanations like anthropic selection, multiverse theories, or mathematical necessity.
- NOT claiming "simplicity" means the same thing in physics and theology — divine simplicity (not being made of parts) is a metaphysical claim about God's nature. Physical simplicity (low K, few parameters) is an observation about laws. They share the word "simplicity" but refer to different things.
- NOT claiming this is more than suggestive — see Strain Analysis below.

## Tests

### The Four-Test Protocol

**Test 1 — Prediction Constraint:** Does the mapping force predictions in both domains?

In Domain A:

- Future fundamental theories should be simpler than current ones (fewer free parameters, deeper symmetries, higher compression). History supports this: Newton → Maxwell → Einstein → Standard Model → (future unification). Each step reduces the number of independent rules.
- Any proposed fundamental law that is LESS compressible than current laws (more parameters, more exceptions) is suspicious. This is already standard practice in physics.

In Domain B:

- If wisdom-creation maps to least action, then creation should show variational structure — and it does (all of classical mechanics, general relativity, and quantum field theory come from action principles).
- Theology should predict that elegant, compressible explanations of reality are more likely correct than ugly, incompressible ones. This is just Occam's Razor restated theologically. It's not a new prediction; it's a restatement of existing practice.

Verdict: The mapping does not generate genuinely NEW predictions in either domain. It reframes existing principles (least action, Occam's Razor) in theological language and existing theology (wisdom) in mathematical language. This is intellectually interesting but predictively weak. **Marginal pass at best.**

**Test 2 — Symmetry Breaking:** Can you swap the roles?

Can complexity be fundamental and simplicity be derivative? In physics: this is possible — some complexity theorists argue that emergence generates simplicity from underlying complexity (renormalization group flow). If the fundamental level is NOT simple (e.g., the string landscape is enormously complex), then the mapping's physics side weakens.

In theology: divine simplicity is a strong doctrinal commitment in classical theism (Augustine, Aquinas, the Reformed tradition). But it is debated — some theologians (Plantinga, Wolterstorff) reject divine simplicity as incoherent. If God is NOT simple in the classical sense, the theological side weakens.

**Symmetry breaking result: WEAK.** Both sides of the mapping are internally debated. Simplicity-as-fundamental is a POSITION in both domains, not an established fact. This weakens the isomorphism to a conditional: IF simplicity is fundamental in physics AND in theology, THEN the mapping holds. But the "if" is doing a lot of work.

**Test 3 — Connection Density:**

- ISO-001 (Trinity) — The Logos as the "compression algorithm" of creation
- ISO-006 (Information Primacy) — Information compression and information-first reality
- ISO-008 (Coherence) — Coherent order as compressed, non-random structure

Connection count: 3 direct connections. **Adequate.**

**Test 4 — Falsifiability Invitation:**

1. Find a fundamental physical law that is NOT derivable from an action principle. If such a law exists and is truly fundamental, the universality of least action fails and Domain A narrows.
2. Show that the universe's Kolmogorov complexity is NOT low — that it requires a description as long as the universe itself (incompressible). This would mean reality is random noise, not compressed by laws.
3. Show that divine simplicity is incoherent (as Plantinga argues) — that God must be complex to be personal, knowing, and loving. If simplicity fails in theology, Domain B collapses.
4. Show that "both value simplicity" is trivially true of ANY two rational frameworks and therefore not informative. If every pair of rational systems shows this mapping, the isomorphism is meaningless.

### Strain Analysis — The Honest Part

**The core objection: Is this just "both value simplicity," which is trivially true?**

This objection is the most damaging one for this ISO. Let me assess it honestly.

**The trivial version:** "Physics likes simple laws. Theology says God is wise. Wise things are simple. Therefore physics and theology agree." This is empty. ANY rational enterprise prefers simple explanations (psychology, economics, history, cooking). The fact that "both value simplicity" doesn't create a structural link between physics and theology any more than it creates one between physics and French cuisine.

**The non-trivial version:** The claim is not just that "both value simplicity" but that the SPECIFIC FORM of simplicity is the same:

- Physics: variational/extremal (the path that uses the least action)
- Theology: economical/wise (the creation that gets maximum effect from minimal specification)
- Shared form: GLOBAL optimization with minimal specification

This is stronger than "both like simplicity" because it identifies the optimization structure. But is it strong enough?

**Honest answer: Probably not for full isomorphism.** The action principle is a precise mathematical framework with specific predictions. Wisdom theology is a literary tradition with broad thematic claims. The mathematical precision is entirely on the physics side. The theology side contributes themes, not equations.

**What saves this ISO from being purely trivial:**

1. The action principle IS remarkable — it's not obvious that nature should operate by global optimization rather than local rules. The fact that it does is surprising.
2. Wisdom theology IS specific — it doesn't just say God is smart; it says God creates with a particular ECONOMY that mirrors the action principle's structure (global perspective, minimal specification, elegant outcome).
3. The compression claim IS testable — the universe's low Kolmogorov complexity (simple laws generating complex phenomena) is a real, non-trivial observation that is consistent with the wisdom theology claim and inconsistent with some alternatives (e.g., a universe with no laws, or with arbitrarily complex laws).

**Where it holds:** Global optimization structure (least action / wise creation) is a genuine shared pattern, not a trivial similarity.

**Where it strains:** The mapping is suggestive, not constraining. It does not generate predictions that either domain does not already generate independently. The action principle does not need wisdom theology to work, and wisdom theology does not need the action principle to be meaningful. The mapping ILLUMINATES but does not CONSTRAIN.

**Classification consequence:** This ISO should remain at Candidate status, not advance to Testing, until it can be shown that the mapping generates at least one prediction that neither domain generates independently.
