# ISO-006: Information Primacy

## ISOMORPHISM RECORD

**ID:** ISO-006

## DOMAINS

**Domain A:** Physics / Information Theory
**Domain B:** Christian Theology — John's Prologue / Creation Theology
**Concept A:** Information is the most basic thing in reality. Physicist John Wheeler called this "It from Bit" — the idea that physical reality comes from information underneath. Matter and energy are second. What's truly fundamental is the ability to tell things apart (distinguishability), which is information.
**Concept B:** "In the beginning was the Word (Logos)" (John 1:1) — a rational, structuring message comes before and creates all material reality. "All things were made through him" (John 1:3). Creation happens by speaking (Genesis 1: "And God said...").

---

## THE MAPPING

**Mathematical Form A:**

Wheeler (1990): "It from Bit" — every physical thing gets its meaning from simple yes/no choices (bits of information).

- **Landauer's Principle:** Erasing one bit of information gives off at least kT ln(2) joules of energy. This means getting rid of information costs real energy. Information is not just a side effect — it has real physical weight.
- **Zeilinger's Foundational Principle:** Each basic system (qubit) holds exactly one bit of information. The information content IS the system itself.
- **Quantum information theory:** The quantum state |ψ⟩ is the most complete description you can have of a system. There is nothing "underneath" the information — no hidden gears or particles. The wavefunction IS what's really there (on the Everett/information-theoretic reading).

Formal expression: Physical reality R comes from an information base I:

R = f(I), where I cannot be broken down further and R is second.

**Mathematical Form B:**

John 1:1-3: "In the beginning was the Logos (λόγος), and the Logos was with God, and the Logos was God. He was in the beginning with God. All things were made through him, and without him was not any thing made that was made."

Genesis 1: Creation happens by God speaking: "And God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light." The speech act (sending information) comes before and causes the physical result.

Hebrews 11:3: "The universe was created by the word of God, so that what is seen was not made out of things that are visible." — visible (material) reality comes from invisible (informational/verbal) cause.

Formal parallel: Material reality M comes from Logos L:

M = f(L), where L comes first and M is second.

**Shared Structure:**

1. **Information comes before matter** — In both cases, the informational/verbal base is FIRST and matter/energy is DERIVED. The order is not: matter exists, then we describe it with information. The order is: information exists, then matter forms from it.
2. **Generative function** — Information doesn't just describe reality; it MAKES reality. Wheeler's bit makes the "it." God's word makes creation. Both produce things, not just describe them.
3. **Cannot be broken down further** — In both cases, the information base cannot be reduced. You cannot ask "what is the bit made of?" in Wheeler's framework. You cannot ask "what came before the Logos?" in John's framework. Both are the bottom level.
4. **Information has real physical cost** — Landauer's principle shows information has real physical effects. Biblical speech acts have real physical effects ("Let there be light" — and there WAS light). Information is not cheap talk in either domain.

**What Is NOT Claimed:**

- NOT claiming Shannon entropy (a measure of uncertainty) IS the Logos. Shannon entropy measures uncertainty in a probability distribution. The Logos is a self-aware, personal, creative agent. These are completely different types of things.
- NOT claiming John was doing information theory. John's Prologue uses Greek philosophy (Stoic logos, Philo's mediating Logos), not math formulas. The structural parallel exists even though the two ideas come from totally different times and places.
- NOT claiming physics proves John 1:1. The "It from Bit" idea is a philosophical way of reading quantum mechanics, not a proven experiment. Many physicists disagree with it.
- NOT claiming "Word" and "information" mean the same thing. Logos in Greek carries meanings of reason, proportion, speech, and cosmic order that "information" in the Shannon sense does not. See the Strain Analysis below.
- NOT claiming this is the strongest isomorphism (mapping) in the framework. It might be just an analogy with some matching features rather than a full structural match. This record is honest about that.

## TESTS

### The Four-Test Protocol

**Test 1 — Prediction Constraint:** Does the mapping limit what can happen in both domains?

In Domain A: If information is truly the most basic thing, then:

- No physical process can destroy information (unitarity). This can be tested, and it's exactly what the Black Hole Information Paradox is about. The resolution (Hawking's 2004 concession, Page curve calculations, recent replica wormhole results) supports that information is conserved.
- Every physical law must be expressible as an information-processing operation. This limits what kinds of physical theories are possible.

In Domain B: If the Logos truly creates all reality, then:

- Nothing created exists independently of the Logos. "Without him was not any thing made that was made" (John 1:3). This is a total claim: no exceptions.
- Destroying the Logos would destroy all reality. This matches Colossians 1:17: "in him all things hold together."

Verdict: The mapping does limit what can happen in both domains, but the limits are not the same type. The physics limits are quantitative and can be tested by experiments (unitarity, Landauer bound). The theology limits are qualitative and cannot be tested the same way. **Partial pass.**

**Test 2 — Symmetry Breaking:** Can you swap the elements?

Can matter come before information? In Wheeler's framework: no. In John's framework: no. Both domains agree on the order.

Can information be second? In Wheeler: that would break the framework — you'd be back to old-fashioned materialism. In John: that would mean the Logos was created, which is the Arian heresy directly contradicted by "the Logos WAS God."

**Symmetry breaking result: PASSED.** The order cannot be swapped in either domain.

**Test 3 — Connection Density:** How many other ISOs does this touch?

- ISO-001 (Trinity) — The Logos IS the second person of the Trinity. Information as structure maps to the Son/Logos as the structural principle through which all is made.
- ISO-003 (Entropy/Sin) — Information breaking down in closed systems parallels moral breaking down.
- ISO-011 (Superposition/Collapse) — Wavefunction as information, measurement as pulling information out.
- ISO-012 (Sign Operator) — The sign operator acts on an information base.

Connection count: 4+ direct connections. **Passed.**

**Test 4 — Falsifiability Invitation:** What would break this mapping?

1. Show that "It from Bit" is wrong — that information comes from some deeper physical base (hidden variables, superdeterminism, or a future theory that gets quantum information from something more basic). If information is NOT the most basic thing in physics, Domain A collapses and the mapping fails.
2. Show that John's logos is not about information being first but purely about God being a person with no claim about creating the universe. This is hard to defend given John 1:3 ("all things were made through him"), but a purely existentialist reading of the Prologue would weaken Domain B.
3. Find a physical system whose behavior cannot be described using information. If there exists a real physical process that carries zero information content, Wheeler's idea fails.

### STRAIN ANALYSIS — The Honest Part

**The core objection:** "Word" (logos) in John 1 and "information" (bit) in Wheeler mean DIFFERENT THINGS.

This objection is serious and must be addressed head-on:

- **Logos** (λόγος) in Greek philosophy means: rational principle, cosmic order, the understandable structure of reality, speech, proportion, reason. In Philo of Alexandria (who wrote around the same time as John), logos is the bridge between the distant God and the material world. In the Stoics, logos is the active rational principle that fills the cosmos. John takes this loaded term and identifies it with a specific person (Jesus Christ). The logos is therefore PERSONAL, CONSCIOUS, INTENTIONAL, and DIVINE.

- **Information** in Shannon's sense means: reduction of uncertainty, measured in bits, defined by the probability distribution over a message space. Shannon information is IMPERSONAL, UNCONSCIOUS, UNINTENTIONAL, and MATHEMATICAL.

These are not the same concept. The mapping holds at the structural level (both come before matter, both create physical reality) but breaks at the personal level (logos is a who; information is a what).

**Honest assessment:** This isomorphism is STRUCTURAL, not semantic. The word "logos" and the word "information" do not mean the same thing. What they share is a structural role: the most basic base from which physical reality comes. The mapping is real at that level but should not be pushed to identity. Logos includes information but is not just information. Information may be a shadow or aspect of logos but is not the whole thing.

**Where it holds:** The claim that reality is information-first (not matter-first) maps cleanly onto the claim that reality is logos-first (not matter-first). The generative direction, the irreducibility, and the ontological priority all match.

**Where it strains:** When you ask "is information conscious?" physics says no (or stays silent). When you ask "is the logos impersonal?" theology says absolutely not. The personal dimension of logos has no counterpart in Shannon information or Wheeler's bit. This is a significant gap.

**Possible resolution within the framework:** The Theophysics framework suggests that consciousness/personhood is itself an informational phenomenon (integrated information, per Tononi's IIT). If consciousness IS sufficiently integrated information, then the gap narrows: logos is maximally integrated information (infinite Φ), and impersonal information is logos at low integration (low Φ). This is speculative but structurally consistent.
