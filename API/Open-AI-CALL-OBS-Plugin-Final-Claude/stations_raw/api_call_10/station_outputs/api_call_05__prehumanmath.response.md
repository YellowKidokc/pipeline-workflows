**Thesis:** Mathematical laws existed and governed the universe for billions of years before any human mind existed to discover them.  
**Method:** The argument eliminates possible explanations for where math "lives" — in matter, in human minds, or nowhere — leaving only one logical option.  
**Key Finding:** Mathematical equations like E=mc² and the inverse-square law were fully operational for 13.8 billion years before humans wrote them down.  
**Significance:** This means the rational structure of the universe is eternal, immaterial, and independent of both physical matter and human consciousness.

---

The document argues that mathematics is not a human invention but a discovered reality that existed before humans. It presents a timeline showing that nuclear fusion in stars followed E=mc² for 13.4 billion years before Einstein was born. Gravity obeyed the inverse-square law for 13.8 billion years before Newton. The fine structure constant — a number that governs how atoms behave — has been fixed since the first atoms formed.

The argument works by eliminating alternatives. Math cannot be "in" matter because matter follows math, not the other way around. It cannot be "in" human minds because humans didn't exist for most of cosmic history. Saying math is "nowhere" fails because something that governs all physical reality must exist somewhere. The only remaining option is that math exists in a substrate that is immaterial (not made of matter), eternal (it predates the universe), and rational (it is logical structure itself).

The document concludes that this points toward a reality described in ancient texts as the "Logos" — a Greek word meaning rational order, structure, and logic. It claims that physics arrived at this conclusion through observation and logic, not through theology.
