05 Falsification Criteria

Kill Conditions — the mapping is destroyed if ANY of the following are demonstrated:

  1. Ruach/pneuma linguistic identity is accidental — homonyms with no semantic connection (like “bank” = riverbank vs. financial institution). Semitic linguistics shows the opposite.
  2. The Spirit operates independently of resistance — human opposition has no effect on the Spirit's manifestation. Empirical observation across traditions contradicts this.
  3. Weakness does not correlate with spiritual power — 2 Cor 12:10 is mere rhetoric; spiritual power correlates positively with human strength. Historical pattern of persecuted, marginal communities contradicts this.
  4. The Spirit IS depleted by distribution — zero-sum spiritual economy. Numbers 11:25 and Trinitarian theology explicitly deny this.
  5. Charismatic manifestation bears no relationship to institutional flexibility — rigid and flexible communities show identical rates of charismata. Strong inverse correlation observed.
  6. Navier-Stokes is solved completely — general smooth solutions always exist (no singularities). Mapping weakens; if blow-up is proven possible, mapping strengthens.
