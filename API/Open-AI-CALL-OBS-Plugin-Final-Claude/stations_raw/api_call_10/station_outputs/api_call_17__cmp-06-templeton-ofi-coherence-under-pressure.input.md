## 3. PROJECT DESCRIPTION — 4,000 character limit

> **Gap.** Science-and-religion research depends heavily on cross-domain analogies, but the field lacks a repeatable protocol for distinguishing metaphor, formal homology, and structural isomorphism. When someone says "entropy is like sin," the physicist dismisses and the theologian gets defensive — and neither has a method for testing whether the claim is poetry or architecture. AI makes the problem urgent: cross-domain pattern generation is now cheap, fast, and abundant, and the field has no shared filter for separating signal from sophisticated noise.
> 
> **Method.** Isomorphic Event Density operates at three levels. Level 1 checks point mapping: do parameters correspond one-to-one? Necessary but insufficient. Level 2 requires process isomorphism: do dynamic events, state transitions, constraints, and failure modes map across domains? Two confirmed process-level events constitute the threshold for a serious structural claim. Level 3 applies a density metric: the more sequential events that map without internal contradiction, the stronger the claim. The protocol converts the subjective question of metaphor-versus-architecture into a continuous, measurable variable. Two researchers examining the same mapping can independently count isomorphic events and arrive at comparable scores. In a pilot mapping between weak-force decay and moral-conservation structure, six process events preserved order, constraint, irreversibility, hidden remainder, spontaneous decay, and conservation structure without contradiction.
> 
> **Instrument.** The χ-Evaluator is a computational coherence-pressure engine built on a multiplicative architecture: ten coherence channels evaluated as a product, not a sum. If any single channel approaches zero, total coherence collapses. This is structurally different from every existing claim-evaluation framework in NLP, fact-checking, and media analysis, which use additive scoring permitting compensation. The evaluator subjects claims to pressure states: compression, strongest objection, counterevidence, time, translation, implementation, falsification, and moral output. A claim that scores well statically but decays under pressure exhibits a propaganda signature. A claim that scores modestly but strengthens under pressure is underdeveloped but structurally sound. The runtime logic is formalized in Lean 4 and compiles with 287 verified theorems and zero unproven gaps. Lean verifies the runtime logic once scores are assigned; it does not prove empirical or theological claims.
> 
> **Falsification.** The framework names what would break it. If a domain is found where stable order persists without fidelity, boundary, correction, or external input, the universality claim fails. If multiplicative coupling collapses into additive compensation, the architectural claim fails. If Lean 4 verification produces a sorry or admit declaration, the formal consistency claim fails. If independent replication fails to reproduce the preliminary diagnostic results, the empirical claim fails. If the Christian theological mapping requires forced or ad hoc parameter assignments that would not be accepted in a physics application, the theological claim fails.
> 
> **Preliminary evidence.** A coherence diagnostic derived from the framework's product structure was applied to nine U.S. government datasets. A preliminary analysis produced a high-significance synchronized structural break across all nine domains between 1968 and 1973. The diagnostic equation independently reproduces the mathematical form of Shannon channel capacity, KL divergence, and the Crooks fluctuation theorem. A separate analysis of biblical patriarchal lifespans fits an exponential decoherence curve with an independently predicted asymptotic floor. These analyses motivate the next phase but do not substitute for independent replication. The Seshat Global History Databank, previously supported by the Foundation, is a natural future validation target.
> 
> **Three-year plan.** Year 1: formalize the IED protocol for peer review; complete the χ-Evaluator including the two critical missing channels (coercion detection and urgency/propaganda detection); preregister the validation plan; draft the methodology paper. Year 2: independent pre-registered replication of the preliminary diagnostic, adversarial cross-domain mapping corpus, interdisciplinary workshops with mathematical physicists, systematic theologians, statisticians, and AI evaluators. Year 3: open-source release of the protocol, validation corpus, and Lean specification; peer-reviewed publications; public methodology guide.
