# YOUTUBE ARGUMENT CALL v0.1 — what is argued, and how
*David Lowe · POF 2828 · Oct 17, 2026 · base call for argument channels (apologetics, skeptic, debates)*

One call per video. Two layers out. Everything timestamped [MM:SS]. Lenses (theology, conspiracy-history...) ride on
this same call as channel focus lines; they never become extra calls.

## LAYER 1 — WHAT IS ARGUED  ->  _ARGUMENTS\<Q-ID>\04_SOURCES\YOUTUBE_FOR | YOUTUBE_AGAINST
1. POSITIONS on the table — every position voiced, not only two sides; who holds each.
2. TOP ARGUMENTS — the strongest 3-5 in the video, each stated at full strength, mapped to Q-IDs, side for/against.
3. EXCHANGE RECORD per argument (never a single "defeated" verdict — the model must not pick winners):
   - answered by: what reply, by whom, [MM:SS]
   - rebuttal: what came back, [MM:SS]
   - still standing on: the points no one answered
   - attack outline if unanswered: the premise an attack would have to target, and how
4. WITNESSES — who made this argument before (Augustine, Hume, Craig, Ehrman...). Witnesses, not sources.

## LAYER 2 — HOW IT IS ARGUED  ->  _MOVES_BANK\ (beside _STORY_BANK)
For each speaker:
- OPENING POSTURE — swing first, ask first, grant first? tone at the start.
- CONCESSIONS — what was granted, and what the concession bought later. [MM:SS]
- FRAME MOVES — reframes, burden shifts, sincerity moves, questions that do the arguing. [MM:SS]
- ANTICIPATION — where a speaker plainly expected the other's reply, and whether it came.
- PACING — absorbing hits vs matching beat for beat; where the momentum turned; when the strongest point was played.
- HELD GROUND — positions taken and never given back; positions overclaimed and later retreated from (the costly ones).
- TONE — calm / heated / warm / mocking, and what each did to the room.

## RULES
- Strong-voice, both sides: state each argument as its best advocate would.
- Posture over lines: the bank records WHY a move worked (it was true, it was patient, it conceded what was fair),
  not scripts to copy. Staged humility reads as a trick; the lesson is the posture.
- Short quotes only, for internal reference; these are other people's words.
- Global focus still applies (five things nobody asked about + NOTE: lines -> DEEPSEEK_NOTES).

## WITNESS for Layer 2
Greg Koukl, *Tactics* (ask questions, keep the burden where it belongs, never stand where you'll have to retreat).
