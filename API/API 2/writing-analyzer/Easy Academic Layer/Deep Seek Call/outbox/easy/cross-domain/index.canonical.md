## Framework Foundations

### An Introduction to Theophysics
The starting point — what this framework is and why it exists. **

Theophysics is the study of how physics and theology describe the same reality. Think of it like this: you can describe a mountain in two ways — by its height, rock type, and slope (physics), or by its history, meaning, and the stories people tell about it (theology). Both describe the same mountain. Theophysics says these two descriptions are not in conflict. They're two languages for the same thing.

The key idea is that the universe has a built-in design. When things line up with that design, they work well. When they don't, they break down. This isn't a metaphor — it's a measurable fact. We can measure how well anything — from a single atom to a whole civilization — lines up with its design. We call this measurement **coherence**.

### The Coherence Metric
The mathematical measure that connects every domain. **

The Coherence Metric is a number that tells you how well a system is working. The formula is:

**C = Σ(w_i × c_i)**

This means: take each part of the system, give it a weight (w_i) based on how important it is, multiply it by its coherence score (c_i), and add them all up. The result is a single number between 0 and 1. A score of 1 means the system is perfectly aligned with its purpose. A score of 0 means it's completely broken.

Think of it like a radio signal through static (Information / Logos). The clearer the channel, the more of the message gets through. Sin is the static. Truth is the signal. The Coherence Metric measures how much signal vs. static is in any system.

### The Only Top-Down Framework
Why starting from axioms changes everything. **

Most science works from the bottom up — start with small pieces and build up to big theories. Theophysics works from the top down. It starts with the big picture (the axioms, or self-evident truths) and works down to the details.

The axioms are:
1. God exists and created the universe with purpose.
2. That purpose is knowable through both scripture and science.
3. The universe is rational — it follows rules that we can discover.
4. Human beings are designed to find and follow truth.

Starting from these axioms changes everything. Instead of guessing and testing randomly, you have a framework that tells you where to look. It's like having a map instead of wandering in the dark.
