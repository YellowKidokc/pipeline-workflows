# Master Equation Term

**R = ∂t/∂τ · γ(v)**

This equation says: Relationship (R) equals the change in time (∂t) divided by the change in proper time (∂τ), multiplied by a factor (γ) that depends on velocity (v). In plain English: **Relationship is the reference frame.**

Think of it like the Gravity/Grace analogy — the slope of a hill. You don't get pushed; the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet. In the same way, just as time slows down when you move fast (time dilation), meaning gets bigger or smaller depending on how close you are to someone (meaning dilation). There is no absolute perspective — no single way to see everything from outside.
