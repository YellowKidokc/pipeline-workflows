# Theophysics: The Physics of Grace

## Master Equation Term

∂G/∂t = Φext(t) · Θ(Sc − S) − γG∇SC

**What this means in plain English:** The rate at which grace (G) changes over time depends on two things. First, an outside force (Φext) that kicks in when the system's brokenness (S) gets close to a critical breaking point (Sc). Second, a damping term (γG∇SC) that prevents grace from just piling up without effect.

**Grace flows from outside.** You cannot make it yourself. This is Boundary Condition 2 (BC2): no closed system generates grace internally. Grace responds to brokenness — the more a system falls apart (the closer S gets to Sc), the more grace flows in.

## Dual Projection

### Physics Side: Gravity / Negentropy

F = Gm₁m₂/r²

**What this means:** The force of gravity between two objects equals a constant (G) times the mass of each object, divided by the distance between them squared.

- Always pulls things together, never pushes them apart
- You need escape velocity (a certain minimum speed) to break free from it
- It shapes the geometry of space and time itself

## The Story

"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God..."

— Ephesians 2:8-9

Grace is the **external rescue**. No matter how hard you try, you cannot generate grace from within — just like a falling object cannot generate upward thrust. This is **Boundary Condition BC2**: no closed system generates grace internally.

The physics is precise: gravity is never repulsive. Grace never pushes away. Both determine the geometry of their domains — gravity curves spacetime; grace curves the soul toward God.

Think of it like the slope of a hill. You don't get pushed — the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet.

The G in the Master Equation is **multiplicative**. If G = 0, then χ (the overall outcome) = 0 regardless of other variables. This is the veto power of grace — without it, nothing else matters.

## Five Axioms

#### 1. External Source Only (BC2)

No closed system generates grace internally. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system. Grace must flow from outside.

#### 2. Responds to Need

∂G/∂t increases as S → Sc. The more broken a system is, the more grace flows into it. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside.

#### 3. Idempotent Source

Giving grace away doesn't diminish it. It's drawn from an infinite reservoir. Like a match — tiny object, but strike it and the energy stored inside can light a whole room on fire. Small actions carry enormous consequences, but the source never runs dry.

#### 4. Coherence-Only Coupling

True grace builds order (coherence, or C). If it creates chaos (increases S), it's not grace. Like a tuning fork — when you strike it near other objects, they start vibrating at the same frequency. One source of perfect pitch that brings everything else into harmony.

#### 5. Conjugate to Faith [G,Q] = iℏeff

Grace and Faith are Fourier pairs. You cannot know both precisely at the same time. Like standing in front of two doors — until you choose one, both futures are possible. The moment you choose, reality locks in. Faith is the act of choosing before you can see what's behind the door.

## Conjugate Pair: G ↔ Q

G (Grace) ⟷ Q (Faith)

"By grace *through* faith" — this is a multiplicative coupling: G × Q, not additive. You need both, and they multiply each other's effect.
