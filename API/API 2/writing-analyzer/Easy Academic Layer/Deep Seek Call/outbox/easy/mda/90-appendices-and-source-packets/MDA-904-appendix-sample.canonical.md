# Critical Thresholds

## 1962 — Engel v. Vitale

The Supreme Court removed prayer from public schools. This was the first domino to fall. Within one generation (about 20-25 years), every single FACTS metric started going downhill.

## 1973 — Roe v. Wade

The Supreme Court redefined when life is sacred. They did this through a court ruling, not through a vote by the people. This made it easier to separate what is morally right from what people choose to do. The consequences of your choices became less connected to the choices themselves.

## 1995 — Internet Mass Adoption

The internet became widely available. Information was no longer controlled by a few sources. Within 15 years, people's attention spans (how long they can focus on one thing) got cut in half. Also, computer programs (algorithms) started pushing people toward more extreme views.

## 2015 — Social Media Saturation

Social media became everywhere. This caused **semantic collapse** — words lost their clear, shared meanings. Words like "violence," "safety," and "justice" no longer meant the same thing to everyone. Public conversation broke into pieces. People could no longer agree on basic facts.
