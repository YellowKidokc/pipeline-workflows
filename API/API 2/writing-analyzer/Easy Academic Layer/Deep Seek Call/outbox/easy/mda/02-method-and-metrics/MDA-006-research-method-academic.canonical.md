# The Five-Page System

Every unit of research in this project uses **five documents**. Each one does a different job. You have to use all five for every question you investigate.

## Inquiry Seed

**Question & Orientation**

This document writes down the research question and what the researcher already believes *before* using AI. This step is critical: the researcher's own thinking, before any AI help, is the foundation that holds everything up. If you let AI frame the question for you, you stop being the researcher and become just someone checking AI's work.

**File:** Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md

## AI Prompt Engine

**How AI Is Used**

This document records the exact prompts you gave the AI, which AI models you used, and the raw outputs — without judging them yet. At this stage, AI is like a map-making tool: it shows you different ways scholars interpret things, names the key researchers and disagreements, and points out areas where you need to dig deeper. You deliberately put off deciding what's true.

**File:** AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md

## Research Workspace

**Evidence & Links**

This is where verified evidence piles up. You take what the AI suggested and check it against real primary sources (original research, historical documents, etc.). How many of the AI's claims survive this check is itself useful information — it tells you where scholarly agreement might be fake, or where the evidence is genuinely disputed.

**File:** Research_Workspace_[Question].md

## Contradictions & Resolution

**Sense-Making**

This is the heart of the whole system. You write down every disagreement you find: sources that contradict each other, places where AI and primary sources don't match, times when your gut says one thing but the data says another. You are *not allowed* to resolve these disagreements too quickly. You have to sit in the confusion long enough to understand its shape, instead of just picking evidence that supports what you already think.

Truth is most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily.

**File:** Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md

## Quotes, Domains, & Links

**Shared Library**

This is a growing collection that stays the same across all your research units. It holds important quotes, lists of topic areas you've explored, and links to sources with notes. This document keeps growing and becomes the raw material you use to write your final publications.

**File:** Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md

# What AI Does — and Does Not Do

## AI Is Used To

- Map out the intellectual landscape quickly across many different fields
- Bring contradictions between different interpretations to the surface
- Put competing frameworks side by side so you can compare them
- Suggest where you need to do deeper research in primary sources
- Test your early conclusions against counterarguments

## AI Is Not Used To

- Decide what is true
- Replace checking original sources
- Frame the initial question before the researcher does
- Choose which evidence matters
- Resolve contradictions that need human judgment

Incomplete and wrong AI outputs are expected in this method — they're not a bug. AI errors work like a warning light: they show you where the intellectual terrain is genuinely disputed, where scholarly agreement might have been manufactured instead of earned through evidence, and where you need to dig deeper into primary sources.

# How to Use This System

**Workflow Sequence**

1. **Start with the Inquiry Seed.** Write down your research question and what you already believe *before* you ask AI anything.

2. **Run the AI Prompt Engine phase.** Record everything the AI gives you without judging it. Collect first, decide later.

3. **Move to the Research Workspace.** Follow the leads the AI found back to original sources. Cite everything with full attribution. Record what the sources actually say.

4. **Build the Contradictions & Resolution document.** Write down every disagreement between sources. Sit in the uncertainty. Don't resolve it too fast.

5. **Keep the Quotes, Domains, & Links library going.** Each research unit adds to the growing archive.

You must repeat this five-document structure for every question you investigate. This isn't just paperwork — it's the framework that keeps AI-assisted research from turning into AI-generated opinion pretending to be independent scholarship.
