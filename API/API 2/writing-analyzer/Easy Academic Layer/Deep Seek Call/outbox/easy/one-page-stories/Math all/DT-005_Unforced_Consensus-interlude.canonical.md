# Unforced Consensus

## 01 - Name Your Ground

### What problem appears when you try to make things agree?

This chapter asks one big question: How does agreement happen when nobody is forcing anyone to agree?

Think about it. When you try to make people agree — by arguing, threatening, or bribing — you get fake agreement. People say what you want to hear, but they don't really believe it. Real agreement is different. It happens naturally, like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet.

So the question is: Can we build a system where people come to the same conclusion without anyone twisting their arm?

## 03 - The Method

### The short version

The method is the evidence.

That sounds weird, so here's what it means: If you want to know whether unforced consensus works, you don't just read about it. You watch it happen. The way we get to agreement — open discussion, shared evidence, no coercion — is itself the proof that it works.
