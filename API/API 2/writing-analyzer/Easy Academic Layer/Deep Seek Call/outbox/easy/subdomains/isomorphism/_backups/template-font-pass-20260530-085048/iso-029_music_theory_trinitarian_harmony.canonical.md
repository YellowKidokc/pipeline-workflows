## THE MAPPING

**Mathematical Form A:**

Harmonic series: f, 2f, 3f, 4f, 5f, ...

All musical pitch material comes from one starting frequency f by multiplying it by whole numbers. The first three unique pitch classes are:

- f (fundamental / root)
- 2f (octave — ratio 2:1)
- 3f (perfect fifth — ratio 3:2 when brought down to the same octave)

Consonance is measured by how simple the frequency ratio p:q is. Euler's "gradus suavitatis" (sweetness scale), Helmholtz's roughness function, and Plomp-Levelt dissonance curves all agree: simpler ratios = more consonant.

Lerdahl's Tonal Pitch Space measures tonal gravity as a distance function d(x, y) between pitches, chords, and keys. Attraction toward the tonic follows:

A(x → T) proportional to 1/d(x, T)^2

This means: the pull toward the home note gets stronger the closer you are to it, just like gravity.

The dominant seventh chord (V7) contains a tritone (the most dissonant interval in the standard scale). This tritone resolves to the tonic with the highest attraction value in the system.

A chord needs at least three different pitch classes (root, third, fifth) to have a harmonic identity. Two notes make an interval — a relationship but not a harmony. Four or more notes are either extensions or combinations of three-note structures.

**Mathematical Form B:**

Generation (not creation) of the Son from the Father:

Father → Son (eternal generation — Nicene Creed)
Father (and Son) → Spirit (eternal procession)

One source (the Father) generates the other two persons. The three persons are the smallest number needed for complete relationship in God — two persons would be a relationship but not a communion; four would be unnecessary given the relational structure.

Convergence (Law I): Everything created is pulled toward its source. The coherence field C(x) comes from the divine source, and all things are attracted toward coherence:

div(C) = 0 (conservation — the field does not degrade)
d/dt[coupling(system, C)] can increase or decrease depending on openness

This means: the field that holds things together doesn't wear out, but how connected you are to it can change.

**Shared Structure:**

1. **Single-source generation** — All harmonic structure comes from one fundamental by multiplying by whole numbers. All divine persons come from one Father by generation and procession. In both cases, the things that come from the source are not created separately but come from the source by a process that keeps the nature of the source.

2. **The first derivative is a perfect image** — The octave (2f) sounds the same as the fundamental; it's the same note in a higher range. The Son is "the image of the invisible God" (Colossians 1:15), "the exact imprint of his nature" (Hebrews 1:3). In both domains, the first thing generated is a perfect copy of the source — not a weaker version but a full reproduction.

3. **The second derivative creates motion** — The fifth (3f, brought down to ratio 3:2) is the interval that creates harmonic movement. The cycle of fifths produces all twelve tones. Without the fifth, music is static (octaves alone create no harmonic tension). The Spirit is the "mover" — the person of the Trinity who acts, who "hovers over the waters" (Genesis 1:2), who "blows where he wishes" (John 3:8). In both domains, the third entity introduces dynamic motion into the system.

4. **Three is the minimum for completeness** — A chord needs root, third, and fifth. Two notes give an interval (a relationship) but not a harmony (a communion). In music theory this is not just a convention but a mathematical fact: the triad is the smallest structure that establishes tonal identity (major vs. minor), harmonic function (tonic, dominant, subdominant), and the ability to move voices. The Trinity is the smallest structure that establishes relational completeness — love requires a lover, a beloved, and the love between them (Augustine, *De Trinitate*).

5. **Consonance as relational coherence** — Simple frequency ratios (2:1, 3:2, 4:3) produce consonance. Complex ratios (45:32, the tritone) produce dissonance. Consonance is not a property of individual notes but of the relationship between them. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet. Similarly, moral and spiritual coherence in theology is not a property of isolated agents but of their relational alignment with the divine source.

6. **Dissonance requires resolution** — The dominant seventh (V7) contains a tritone that MUST resolve to the tonic in tonal music. This is not just a stylistic choice but a result of tonal gravity (Lerdahl). Moral disorder similarly requires resolution — sin creates tension that cannot stay unresolved without the system breaking down.

7. **Resolution is costly** — Voice-leading rules require specific stepwise motion to resolve dissonance. The resolution is not free; it requires particular movements. Grace is costly — "you were bought with a price" (1 Corinthians 6:20). In both domains, the return to consonance/coherence requires specific, directed action, not random motion.

8. **Tonal gravity as convergence** — Lerdahl's attraction function mirrors Law I convergence. Every pitch in the key is pulled toward the tonic with force inversely proportional to distance in tonal pitch space. Every created thing is pulled toward its source with force related to coherence coupling.

9. **Modulation preserves structure** — A key change moves all relationships into a new frame while keeping their internal structure the same. A perfect cadence in C major has the same harmonic function as a perfect cadence in E-flat major. This maps to Law VII (Conservation under transformation) — the same truths expressed in different cultural/historical frames.

10. **Overtone generation is exhaustive** — The harmonic series eventually creates ALL possible pitch relationships from one fundamental, given enough overtones. Nothing in the pitch universe exists outside what the fundamental generates. "All things were made through him, and without him was not any thing made that was made" (John 1:3). The generative source is exhaustive.

- NOT claiming the Trinity IS a chord — the Trinity is not three frequencies vibrating at whole-number ratios. The isomorphism is structural (same shape of relationships), not material (same substance).
