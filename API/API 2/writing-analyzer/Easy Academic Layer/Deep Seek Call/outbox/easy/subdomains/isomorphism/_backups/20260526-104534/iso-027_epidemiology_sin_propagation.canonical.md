# ISO-027: How Sin Spreads Like a Disease

## ISOMORPHISM RECORD

**ID:** ISO-027
**Date:** 2026-03-10
**Status:** Testing

## DOMAINS

**Domain A:** Epidemiology (how infectious diseases spread: R₀, SIR model, herd immunity, super-spreaders, quarantine, contact tracing, vaccination, epidemic curves)

**Domain B:** Christian Theology — The Study of How Sin Spreads (sin's social spread: Romans 5:12 original sin, 1 Cor 5 church discipline, the leaven metaphor, Hebrews 12:15 "root of bitterness," Judges cycles)

**Concept A:** Infectious diseases spread through a population according to the SIR model. The equations are:
- dS/dt = -βSI (the number of susceptible people who get infected depends on the transmission rate β times contact between susceptible S and infected I)
- dI/dt = βSI - γI (the infected population grows by new infections minus people who recover)
- dR/dt = γI (recovered people increase at the recovery rate γ)

The basic reproduction number is R₀ = β/γ. When R₀ > 1, each infected person infects more than one other person, so the disease spreads exponentially. When R₀ < 1, the disease dies out. Containment strategies either reduce β (quarantine, masks, distancing), increase γ (treatment), or reduce S (vaccination/immunity).

**Concept B:** Sin spreads through communities in the same way. Each sinful behavior causes people to copy it, based on how many people are vulnerable and how much contact they have with the behavior. The Bible's authors used disease language on purpose: "leaven" (1 Cor 5:6, Gal 5:9), "root of bitterness springing up and defiling many" (Heb 12:15), "bad company ruins good morals" (1 Cor 15:33). Paul's instructions to kick people out of the church in 1 Corinthians 5 are structurally identical to how doctors quarantine patients.

## THE MAPPING

**Mathematical Form A:**

SIR Model:
- dS/dt = -βSI (susceptible people become infected at rate β times contact between S and I)
- dI/dt = βSI - γI (infected population grows by new infections minus recoveries)
- dR/dt = γI (recovered people increase at recovery rate γ)

R₀ = β/γ (basic reproduction number)
- R₀ > 1: epidemic (each infected person infects more than one other)
- R₀ < 1: containment (infection dies out)

Herd immunity threshold: H = 1 - 1/R₀ (the fraction of people that must be immune to protect the whole population)

**Mathematical Form B:**

Spiritual SIR Model:
- dV/dt = -βVF (vulnerable members become fallen at rate β times exposure to fallen behavior)
- dF/dt = βVF - γF (fallen population grows by new fallings minus restorations)
- dR/dt = γF (restored people increase at restoration/grace rate γ)

R₀_sin = β_sin/γ_grace
- R₀ > 1: apostasy epidemic (sin spreading faster than correction/restoration)
- R₀ < 1: community holiness maintained (correction outpaces spread)

Holiness threshold: H = 1 - 1/R₀ (the fraction of the community that must be "immune" — mature, discerning, resistant — to protect the vulnerable)

**Shared Structure:**

Both describe how things spread through populations with the same mathematical structure:

1. **R₀ as the epidemic threshold** — In disease science, R₀ decides whether a disease becomes an epidemic or dies out. In community morality, the same threshold decides whether a sinful behavior pattern spreads or gets contained. The leaven metaphor ("a little leaven leavens the whole lump" — 1 Cor 5:6, Gal 5:9) describes the R₀ > 1 condition: when the spread rate is faster than the containment rate, the whole community gets affected.

2. **β (transmission rate) = cultural exposure rate** — In disease science, β depends on how often people touch, how crowded they are, and how easily the germ spreads. In moral contagion, β depends on how often people interact, how much media they consume, and how attractive or easy-to-copy the behavior is. Both can be changed with the same kinds of fixes: reduce contact (quarantine/church discipline), reduce crowding (small groups), reduce how easy it is to catch (education/discipleship).

3. **γ (recovery rate) = grace/intervention rate** — In disease science, γ depends on how strong the immune system is, medical treatment, and natural recovery. In moral contagion, γ depends on spiritual maturity, pastoral care, community support, and grace. Both represent how fast infected/fallen people get restored. Increasing γ is the treatment strategy in both areas.

4. **Herd immunity = community holiness threshold** — When enough individuals are immune, the population is protected even if some people are still vulnerable. When enough community members are spiritually mature and resistant, the community is protected even if some members are weak. The OPPOSITE is the leaven principle: when immunity drops below the threshold, the contagion becomes an epidemic.

5. **Super-spreaders = disproportionate influence** — In disease science, about 20% of infected people cause about 80% of new infections (this is called the Pareto distribution of super-spreading). In biblical history, certain leaders spread sin way more than others: Jeroboam "who caused Israel to sin" is mentioned 21 times in the book of Kings because his R₀ was extremely high — he was a super-spreader. Jesus warned about the "leaven of the Pharisees" (Matt 16:6) — when leaders sin, their R₀ is much higher than normal.

6. **Quarantine = church discipline** — Paul's instructions in 1 Cor 5:11 ("not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother if he is guilty of sexual immorality or greed...not even to eat with such a one") are structurally identical to quarantine rules. The goal is not punishment but containment: reduce β by reducing contact between infected and susceptible people. Paul even uses the leaven metaphor in the same passage (1 Cor 5:6-7): "Cleanse out the old leaven."

7. **Contact tracing = pastoral care / accountability** — Disease contact tracing finds out who has been exposed and watches them for symptoms. Pastoral accountability finds out who has been exposed to harmful influence and gives them proactive support. Both are early-detection systems that lower the effective R₀ by catching infection before it can spread.

8. **Asymptomatic carriers = hidden sin** — Some infected people show no symptoms but still spread the disease. Some people have sin that is invisible to the community but still influences others. Psalm 19:12 ("Who can discern his errors? Declare me innocent from hidden faults") acknowledges asymptomatic carriage. Asymptomatic carriers increase effective R₀ because they escape quarantine/discipline.

9. **Vaccination = discipleship/education** — Vaccination introduces a controlled, weakened version of the germ to build immunity. Discipleship introduces controlled exposure to moral challenges to build discernment and resistance. Both create immunity ahead of time rather than waiting for full-blown infection. Both protect the individual AND lower community R₀ (contributing to herd immunity).

10. **Epidemic curve = revival/apostasy cycles** — The SIR model produces characteristic epidemic curves: exponential growth → peak → decline as the pool of susceptible people runs out. The Judges cycle (sin → oppression → cry for help → deliverance → sin again) produces a similar pattern. Revival is the γ-spike (sudden increase in restoration rate) that bends the epidemic curve downward. Apostasy is the β-spike (sudden increase in transmission) that starts a new epidemic.

**What Is NOT Claimed:**

- NOT claiming sin IS a virus or works through biological mechanisms — the similarity is in how things spread through populations (how behaviors spread through communities), not in how they transmit (viral replication vs. social imitation)
- NOT claiming disease models can predict specific moral outcomes in specific communities — the structural parallel is in the shape of the dynamics, not in exact numerical prediction
- NOT claiming church discipline is the same as medical quarantine in severity, method, or purpose — the structural parallel is in the function (reducing contact to lower R₀), not in how it's done
- NOT claiming all social influence is contagion — positive influence (virtue, faith, courage) also spreads through communities. This mapping is specifically about harmful contagion, though a parallel positive mapping exists (possible future study: virtue epidemiology)
- NOT claiming the SIR model is the only disease model — more complex models (SEIR, network models, agent-based models) may provide richer parallels. The SIR model is used here because it's clear and simple
- NOT claiming Paul knew calculus — claiming Paul observed the same structural dynamics and prescribed the same structural fixes, expressed in pastoral language rather than mathematical language

## TESTS

**Swap Test:** Can you swap epidemiology with another population dynamics model?

Population genetics (how gene frequencies change) shares population-level dynamics but lacks the contagion mechanism — genes spread through reproduction, not social contact. Predator-prey dynamics (Lotka-Volterra equations) have coupled equations but a different structure — the predator eats the prey rather than converting it. Economic contagion (financial panics) shares more structural features but lacks the vaccination/immunity parallel.

The SIR model is specifically about contagion + recovery + immunity, and all three are present in the theological domain (sin spreading + restoration + discipleship-based resistance). Models that lack any of these three components produce weaker mappings.

**Swap test result: PASSED.** The mapping is specific to contagion-recovery-immunity dynamics, not generic to all population models.

**Prediction in Domain A (Epidemiology):**

- **Super-spreader events dominate transmission.** The Pareto distribution of spreading (few individuals cause most transmission) is a core epidemiological finding. Confirmed repeatedly: SARS (2003), MERS, COVID-19 all showed super-spreading dynamics.
- **Quarantine reduces R₀.** Reducing contact between infected and susceptible people reduces transmission. This is the foundational principle of infectious disease control, confirmed across centuries of practice.
- **Herd immunity thresholds exist.** When the fraction immune exceeds H = 1 - 1/R₀, epidemic spread stops even without universal immunity. Confirmed for measles (R₀ ≈ 12-18, H ≈ 92-95%), polio, smallpox (eradicated through vaccination achieving herd immunity).
- **Asymptomatic transmission complicates containment.** Confirmed: COVID-19's high asymptomatic transmission rate made containment dramatically harder than SARS-1 (which had low asymptomatic transmission).
- **Vaccination provides both individual and community protection.** Confirmed across all vaccination programs: herd immunity is a population-level property that emerges from individual immunity.

**Prediction in Domain B (Theology):**

- **Leadership sin should have disproportionate community impact.** The super-spreader prediction implies that when leaders sin, the community effect is orders of magnitude greater than when a marginal member sins. This is observable in church history: pastoral moral failure produces community-wide crisis far exceeding the individual act. James 3:1 ("Not many of you should become teachers, for you know that we who teach will be judged with greater strictness") reflects awareness of the super-spreader dynamic.
- **Church discipline (when practiced) should reduce the spread of the specific sin being addressed.** The quarantine prediction implies that 1 Cor 5-style discipline should lower the effective R₀ for the behavior being disciplined. Conversely, communities that never practice discipline should show higher rates of behavioral spread. This can be tested through long-term studies of church communities.
- **There should be a minimum threshold of spiritually mature members required for community health.** Below this threshold, sin spreads faster than it can be contained. This predicts that church plants and new communities are especially vulnerable to moral contagion (low initial immunity) and need external support during formation — matching the vulnerability of populations with no prior exposure to a disease.
- **Hidden/unconfessed sin should be more destructive to communities than visible sin.** The asymptomatic carrier prediction implies that sin which evades detection (and therefore evades discipline/quarantine) should have higher effective R₀ than visible sin. Proverbs 28:13 ("Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper") may reflect this epidemiological insight.
- **Revival should follow epidemic curve dynamics.** If revival is a γ-spike (sudden increase in restoration rate), it should produce a characteristic curve: rapid recovery → gradual return to baseline as the "recovered" population depletes the "infected" pool. Post-revival churches should show a period of sustained health followed by gradual vulnerability as new, unvaccinated/undiscipled members enter.

**Bidirectional:** Yes.

- Epidemiology → Theology: The SIR model limits which community health strategies are structurally possible. Any strategy that ignores transmission dynamics (R₀) and focuses only on individual treatment (γ) will fail when R₀ > 1 — new infections outpace cures. This predicts that purely individualistic sanctification models (focused only on personal holiness with no community strategy) will fail in high-exposure environments.
- Theology → Epidemiology: The theological framework's emphasis on "the leaven principle" (small amounts of contagion affecting the whole) predicts that epidemiological models should show threshold effects rather than linear relationships — and they do. The framework's emphasis on leadership sin as super-spreading predicts that hierarchical social structures should show different epidemic dynamics than flat structures — and network epidemiology confirms this.

**Falsification:**

1. **Break the R₀ parallel:** Show that sinful behaviors in communities do NOT spread through social contact in ways described by contagion models — that each individual's behavior is fully independent of others' behavior. If there is zero social transmission of behavioral patterns, the entire epidemiological parallel fails. (Note: social contagion research by Christakis & Fowler (2007) on obesity, smoking, and happiness spreading through social networks strongly supports the contagion model for behavioral spread.)

2. **Break the quarantine effectiveness:** Show that church discipline (contact reduction) has zero effect on the spread of the disciplined behavior. If reducing social contact between the sinning member and the community does not reduce the behavior's spread, the quarantine parallel fails.

3. **Break the herd immunity threshold:** Show that communities with very high proportions of spiritually mature members are equally susceptible to moral contagion as communities with very low proportions. If "spiritual herd immunity" does not exist, the threshold parallel fails.

4. **Break the super-spreader distribution:** Show that leadership sin has the same community impact as peripheral member sin — that influence is uniformly distributed regardless of social position. If all individuals have equal R₀ regardless of social position, the super-spreader mapping fails.

5. **Break the epidemic curve shape:** Show that revival/apostasy patterns do NOT follow S-curves (exponential growth → peak → decline) but instead follow some entirely different dynamic. If the temporal pattern is fundamentally different from epidemic curves, the dynamical parallel fails.

**Honest weakness:** The R₀ for moral contagion is not independently measurable the way R₀ for infectious disease is. We can estimate it from behavioral spread data (Christakis & Fowler's network studies), but it is not as rigorously quantified as biological R₀. The mathematical form is identical; the measurement precision is not. This makes the mapping stronger at the structural level than at the quantitative level.
