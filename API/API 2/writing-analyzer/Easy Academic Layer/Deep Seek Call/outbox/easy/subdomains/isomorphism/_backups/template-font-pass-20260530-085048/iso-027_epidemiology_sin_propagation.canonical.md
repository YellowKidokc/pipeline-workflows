# ISO-027: How Sin Spreads Like a Disease

## ISOMORPHISM RECORD

**ID:** ISO-027
**Date:** 2026-03-10
**Status:** Testing

---

## DOMAINS

**Domain A:** Epidemiology (how infectious diseases move through groups: R₀, SIR model, herd immunity, super-spreaders, quarantine, contact tracing, vaccination, epidemic curves)
**Domain B:** Christian Theology — the study of how sin spreads socially (Romans 5:12 original sin, 1 Cor 5 church discipline, the leaven metaphor, Hebrews 12:15 "root of bitterness," Judges cycles)

**Concept A:** Infectious diseases spread through a population using the SIR model. Here are the equations:

- dS/dt = -βSI (the number of susceptible people who get infected depends on how often they contact infected people, times the transmission rate β)
- dI/dt = βSI - γI (the infected group grows from new infections, minus people who recover at rate γ)
- dR/dt = γI (the recovered group grows at the recovery rate γ)

The key number is R₀ = β/γ. This is called the "basic reproduction number." When R₀ > 1, each infected person passes the disease to more than one other person, so it spreads fast. When R₀ < 1, the disease dies out because each infected person passes it to less than one person on average.

Containment strategies work by lowering β (quarantine, masks, distancing), raising γ (treatment), or lowering S (vaccination/immunity).

**Concept B:** Sin spreads through communities in the same way. Each sinful act causes others to copy it, based on how often people interact and how vulnerable they are. The Bible writers used disease language on purpose: "leaven" (1 Cor 5:6, Gal 5:9), "root of bitterness springing up and defiling many" (Heb 12:15), "bad company ruins good morals" (1 Cor 15:33). Paul's instructions in 1 Corinthians 5 to separate from a sinning brother are the same structure as quarantine.

## THE MAPPING

**Mathematical Form A:**

SIR Model:

- dS/dt = -βSI (susceptible people become infected at rate β times contact between S and I)
- dI/dt = βSI - γI (infected population grows by new infections minus recoveries)
- dR/dt = γI (recovered/removed people increase at recovery rate γ)

R₀ = β/γ (basic reproduction number)

- R₀ > 1: epidemic (each infected person infects more than one other)
- R₀ < 1: containment (infection dies out)

Herd immunity threshold: H = 1 - 1/R₀ (the fraction that must be immune to protect the whole group)

**Mathematical Form B:**

Spiritual SIR Model:

- dV/dt = -βVF (vulnerable/susceptible members become fallen at rate β times exposure to fallen behavior)
- dF/dt = βVF - γF (fallen population grows by new fallings minus restorations)
- dR/dt = γF (restored individuals increase at restoration/grace rate γ)

R₀_sin = β_sin/γ_grace

- R₀ > 1: apostasy epidemic (sin spreading faster than correction/restoration)
- R₀ < 1: community holiness maintained (correction outpaces spread)

Holiness threshold: H = 1 - 1/R₀ (fraction of community that must be "immune" — mature, discerning, resistant — to protect the vulnerable)

**Shared Structure:**

Both describe how things spread through groups with the same math. Here are the ten connections:

1. **R₀ as the epidemic threshold** — In disease science, R₀ decides if a disease becomes an epidemic or dies out. In community morality, the same number decides if a sinful behavior pattern spreads or gets stopped. The leaven metaphor ("a little leaven leavens the whole lump" — 1 Cor 5:6, Gal 5:9) describes the R₀ > 1 condition: when the spread rate is faster than containment, the whole community gets affected.

2. **β (transmission rate) = cultural exposure rate** — In disease science, β depends on how often people touch, how crowded they are, and how easily the germ spreads. In moral contagion, β depends on how often people interact, how much media exposure there is (social media, TV), and how attractive or easy-to-copy the behavior is. Both can be changed with the same methods: reduce contact (quarantine/church discipline), reduce crowding (small groups), reduce how easy it is to copy (education/discipleship).

3. **γ (recovery rate) = grace/intervention rate** — In disease science, γ depends on how strong the immune system is, medical treatment, and natural recovery. In moral contagion, γ depends on spiritual maturity, pastoral care, community support, and grace. Both represent how fast infected/fallen people get restored. Raising γ is the treatment strategy in both areas.

4. **Herd immunity = community holiness threshold** — When enough individuals are immune, the whole group is protected even if some people are still vulnerable. When enough community members are spiritually mature and resistant, the community is protected even if some members are weak. The OPPOSITE is the leaven principle: when immunity drops below the threshold, the contagion becomes an epidemic.

5. **Super-spreaders = disproportionate influence** — In disease science, about 20% of infected people cause about 80% of new infections (this is called Pareto distribution). In biblical history, certain leaders spread sin way more than others: Jeroboam "who caused Israel to sin" is mentioned 21 times in the book of Kings because his R₀ was super high — he was a super-spreader. Jesus warned about the "leaven of the Pharisees" (Matt 16:6) — leadership sin has a much higher R₀.

6. **Quarantine = church discipline** — Paul's instructions in 1 Cor 5:11 ("not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother if he is guilty of sexual immorality or greed...not even to eat with such a one") are the same structure as quarantine. The goal is not punishment but containment: lower β by reducing contact between infected and susceptible people. Paul even uses the leaven metaphor in the same passage (1 Cor 5:6-7): "Cleanse out the old leaven."

7. **Contact tracing = pastoral care / accountability** — Disease contact tracing finds who has been exposed and watches them for symptoms. Pastoral accountability finds who has been exposed to harmful influence and gives them proactive support. Both are early-detection systems that lower the effective R₀ by catching infection before it can spread.

8. **Asymptomatic carriers = hidden sin** — Some infected people show no symptoms but still pass the disease. Some people have sin that is invisible to the community but still influences others. Psalm 19:12 ("Who can discern his errors? Declare me innocent from hidden faults") acknowledges asymptomatic carriage. Asymptomatic carriers raise the effective R₀ because they escape quarantine/discipline.

9. **Vaccination = discipleship/education** — Vaccination gives a controlled, weakened version of the germ to build immunity. Discipleship gives controlled exposure to moral challenges to build discernment and resistance. Both create immunity ahead of time instead of waiting for full infection. Both protect the individual AND lower the community R₀ (adding to herd immunity).

10. **Epidemic curve = revival/apostasy cycles** — The SIR model produces characteristic epidemic curves: fast growth → peak → decline as the pool of susceptible people runs out. The Judges cycle (sin → oppression → cry for help → deliverance → sin again) produces the same pattern. Revival is the γ-spike (sudden increase in restoration rate) that bends the epidemic curve downward. Apostasy is the β-spike (sudden increase in transmission) that starts a new epidemic.

**What Is NOT Claimed:**

- NOT claiming sin IS a virus or works through biology — the parallel is in how things spread through groups (population dynamics), not in the mechanism (virus copying vs. social copying)
- NOT claiming disease models can predict exact moral outcomes in specific churches — the structural parallel is in the shape of the dynamics, not in exact numbers
- NOT claiming church discipline is the same as medical quarantine in how harsh it is, how it's done, or what it's for — the structural parallel is in the function (reducing contact to lower R₀), not in the method
- NOT claiming all social influence is contagion — good influence (virtue, faith, courage) also spreads through communities. This mapping is specifically about harmful contagion, though a positive mapping could exist too (future ISO: virtue epidemiology)
- NOT claiming the SIR model is the only disease model — more complex models (SEIR, network models, agent-based models) might give richer parallels. The SIR model is used here because it's clear and simple
- NOT claiming Paul knew calculus — claiming Paul observed the same structural dynamics and prescribed the same structural solutions, just using pastoral language instead of math

## TESTS

**Swap Test:** Can you swap epidemiology with another population dynamics model?

Population genetics (how gene frequencies change) shares group-level dynamics but lacks the contagion mechanism — genes spread through reproduction, not social contact. Predator-prey dynamics (Lotka-Volterra) have coupled equations but a different structure — the predator eats the prey instead of converting it. Economic contagion (financial panics) shares more structural features but lacks the vaccination/immunity parallel.

The SIR model is specifically about contagion + recovery + immunity, and all three are present in the theological domain (sin spreading + restoration + discipleship-based resistance). Models that lack any of these three components produce weaker mappings.

**Swap test result: PASSED.** The mapping is specific to contagion-recovery-immunity dynamics, not generic to all population models.

**Prediction in Domain A (Epidemiology):**

- **Super-spreader events dominate transmission.** The Pareto distribution of spreading (few people cause most transmission) is a core epidemiological finding. Confirmed repeatedly: SARS (2003), MERS, COVID-19 all showed super-spreading dynamics.
- **Quarantine reduces R₀.** Reducing contact between infected and susceptible people reduces transmission. This is the foundational principle of infectious disease control, confirmed across centuries of practice.
- **Herd immunity thresholds exist.** When the fraction immune exceeds H = 1 - 1/R₀, epidemic spread stops even without universal immunity. Confirmed for measles (R₀ ≈ 12-18, H ≈ 92-95%), polio, smallpox (wiped out through vaccination achieving herd immunity).
- **Asymptomatic transmission complicates containment.** Confirmed: COVID-19's high asymptomatic transmission rate made containment much harder than SARS-1 (which had low asymptomatic transmission).
- **Vaccination provides both individual and community protection.** Confirmed across all vaccination programs: herd immunity is a group-level property that emerges from individual immunity.

**Prediction in Domain B (Theology):**

- **Leadership sin should have a much bigger community impact.** The super-spreader prediction says that when leaders sin, the community effect is way bigger than when a regular member sins. This is easy to see in church history: pastoral moral failure causes community-wide crisis far beyond the individual act. James 3:1 ("Not many of you should become teachers, for you know that we who teach will be judged with greater strictness") shows awareness of the super-spreader dynamic.
- **Church discipline (when practiced) should reduce the spread of the specific sin being addressed.** The quarantine prediction says that 1 Cor 5-style discipline should lower the effective R₀ for the behavior being disciplined. On the flip side, communities that never practice discipline should show higher rates of behavioral spread. This can be tested through long-term studies of church communities.
- **There should be a minimum threshold of spiritually mature members needed for community health.** Below this threshold, sin spreads faster than it can be contained. This predicts that church plants and new communities are especially vulnerable to moral contagion (low initial immunity) and need outside support during formation — like how immunologically naive populations are vulnerable.
- **Hidden/unconfessed sin should be more destructive to communities than visible sin.** The asymptomatic carrier prediction says that sin which escapes detection (and therefore escapes discipline/quarantine) should have higher effective R₀ than visible sin. Proverbs 28:13 ("Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper") may reflect this epidemiological insight.
- **Revival should follow epidemic curve dynamics.** If revival is a γ-spike (sudden increase in restoration rate), it should produce a characteristic curve: rapid recovery → gradual return to baseline as the "recovered" population uses up the "infected" pool. Post-revival churches should show a period of sustained health followed by gradual vulnerability as new, unvaccinated/undiscipled members come in.

**Bidirectional:** Yes.

- Epidemiology → Theology: The SIR model limits which community health strategies can work. Any strategy that ignores transmission dynamics (R₀) and focuses only on individual treatment (γ) will fail when R₀ > 1 — new infections outpace cures. This predicts that purely individualistic sanctification models (focused only on personal holiness with no community strategy) will fail in high-exposure environments.
- Theology → Epidemiology: The theological framework's emphasis on "the leaven principle" (small amounts of contagion affecting the whole) predicts that epidemiological models should show threshold effects rather than straight-line relationships — and they do. The framework's emphasis on leadership sin as super-spreading predicts that hierarchical social structures should show different epidemic dynamics than flat structures — and network epidemiology confirms this.

**Falsification:**

1. **Break the R₀ parallel:** Show that sinful behaviors in communities do NOT spread through social contact in ways described by contagion models — that each person's behavior is completely independent of others' behavior. If there is zero social transmission of behavioral patterns, the entire epidemiological parallel fails. (Note: social contagion research by Christakis & Fowler (2007) on obesity, smoking, and happiness spreading through social networks strongly supports the contagion model for behavioral spread.)
2. **Break the quarantine effectiveness:** Show that church discipline (contact reduction) has zero effect on the spread of the disciplined behavior. If reducing social contact between the sinning member and the community does not reduce the behavior's spread, the quarantine parallel fails.
3. **Break the herd immunity threshold:** Show that communities with very high proportions of spiritually mature members are equally susceptible to moral contagion as communities with very low proportions. If "spiritual herd immunity" does not exist, the threshold parallel fails.
4. **Break the super-spreader distribution:** Show that leadership sin has the same community impact as peripheral member sin — that influence is evenly distributed regardless of social position. If all individuals have equal R₀ regardless of social position, the super-spreader mapping fails.
5. **Break the epidemic curve shape:** Show that revival/apostasy patterns do NOT follow S-curves (fast growth → peak → decline) but instead follow some completely different dynamic. If the time pattern is fundamentally different from epidemic curves, the dynamical parallel fails.

**Honest weakness:** The R₀ for moral contagion cannot be measured independently the way R₀ for infectious disease can. We can estimate it from behavioral spread data (Christakis & Fowler's network studies), but it is not as rigorously quantified as biological R₀. The math form is identical; the measurement precision is not. This makes the mapping stronger at the structural level than at the quantitative level.
