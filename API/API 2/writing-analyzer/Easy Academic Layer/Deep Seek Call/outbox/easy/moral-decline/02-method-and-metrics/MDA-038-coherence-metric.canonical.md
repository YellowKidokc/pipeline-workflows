# The Coherence Metric: Can Physics Measure Moral Decay?

## Abstract

This paper introduces the **Coherence Metric** (χ, the Greek letter "chi"). It's a way to measure structural integrity using the math of phase transitions (when matter changes state, like ice melting into water). We show that the same equation that describes how physical systems break down also describes decay in nine different areas of society. When we look at historical data from 1900 to 2025, we find that all nine areas hit their breaking points at almost the same time—between **1968 and 1973**. This tight timing suggests one shared cause, not nine separate problems. The framework makes predictions you can test and disprove, no matter what your political beliefs are.

Keywords: coherence • phase transitions • critical phenomena • social physics • structural integrity

## 1. Introduction

The question this paper asks is simple: **Can physics measure moral decay?**

Not as a metaphor. Not as a comparison. As a real measurement.

Here's the claim: structural integrity—whether in bridges, bodies, or civilizations—follows the same mathematical laws. When you remove the things that hold a system together, it doesn't slowly fall apart. It looks stable for a while, then hits a tipping point and collapses fast. This isn't an opinion. This is how phase transitions work in physics.

Your politics don't matter. You can vote for gravity or against gravity. Gravity doesn't care. It pulls.

**The Coherence Master Equation**

\\[ \chi \approx |P - P_c|^{\beta} \\]

This equation says: Coherence (χ) equals how far the control parameter (P) is from its critical threshold (Pc), raised to the power of the scaling exponent (β). In plain English: the closer a system gets to its breaking point, the faster it falls apart. The same math that describes superconductors (materials that conduct electricity with zero resistance) and ferromagnets (materials like iron that become permanently magnetic) also describes civilizations.

## Framework Variables

**χ (Chi / Coherence)**
How well a system keeps its internal order and fights off decay (entropy, the natural tendency toward disorder).

**P (Control Parameter)**
The current level of constraint in a system. Think of it as the rules, standards, or anchors that hold things together.

**Pc (Critical Threshold)**
The value where a phase transition happens. Cross this line, and collapse speeds up.

**β (Scaling Exponent)**
Determines how fast collapse happens near the critical point. The average β across all nine areas is 0.41.

## 2. The Synchronization Problem

If coherence decay were random—if divorce rates, crime rates, obesity rates, trust metrics, and educational outcomes were all caused by different, unrelated things—their breaking points would be scattered randomly across the 125 years we studied.

They are not.

Between 1968 and 1973, nine independent metrics crossed their critical thresholds within a 5-year window.

The chance of this happening by accident is vanishingly small (p < 10⁻⁶). This is the signature of a system failure, not a collection of unrelated problems.

**1970.0** — Mean Break Year (average across all nine domains)
**3.2 yrs** — Standard Deviation (tight clustering confirms synchronization)
**10⁻⁶** — p-value (vanishingly improbable by chance)

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Data Sources

We used nine areas of society, each measured with long-term data sets from 1900 to 2025. Sources include CDC, Pew, Gallup, NORC GSS, BGSU NCFMR, and BLS. See Appendix A for the full list.

### 3.2 Normalization

We converted all data to a common scale using a 1940–1949 baseline. This lets us compare different areas on the same coherence scale.

### 3.3 Curve-Fitting

We used a statistical method called nonlinear least squares regression with bootstrap confidence intervals (1,000 iterations) to fit the data to the power-law form χ ≈ |P − Pc|β.

## 4. Results

### Key Findings by Domain

#### **Familial**

Divorce rate peaked at 22.6 per 1,000 people (1981). Non-marital births rose from 10.7% (1970) to 40% (2023). The marriage covenant as a constraint has been systematically removed from law and culture.

#### **Institutional Trust**

Public trust in government fell from 77% (1964) to 16% (2023). That's an 80-percentage-point collapse in six decades. The removal of constitutional constraints happened before the decline.

#### **Semantic Coherence**

74% of words related to virtue (like "honesty," "integrity," "chastity") show declining frequency in English-language text after 1960 (Google Ngram analysis, UPenn Language Log). Language is the medium of shared meaning. When definitions become unfixed, communication degrades toward noise.

#### **Economic Coherence**

Dollar purchasing power: $1.00 (1971) to $0.14 (2024). Nixon's closure of the gold window (ending the dollar's convertibility to gold) severed the constraint anchoring currency to physical reality. The pattern maps perfectly to the coherence equation with the gold standard as Pc.

## 8. Discussion

### The 1968–1973 Constraint Removal Cluster

Synchronized inflection across nine domains demands a common cause. The 1968–1973 window produced an identifiable cluster of constraint-removal legislative and cultural events. These are not interpretations. They are dates.

**1965** — **Griswold v. Connecticut** — Decoupled contraception from the state's interest in marriage, weakening the familial constraint structure.

**1968** — **Vietnam / Civil Unrest** — Institutional authority collapsed publicly. Trust in government began its terminal decline from 62% toward 16%.

**1970** — **No-Fault Divorce** — California's Family Law Act removed permanence from the marriage covenant. Divorce rate began vertical climb.

**1971** — **Gold Window Closure** — Nixon severed the dollar's constraint anchor. Currency decoupled from physical reality. $1.00 became $0.14 over 53 years.

**1972** — **Eisenstadt v. Baird** — Extended contraceptive access to unmarried individuals, completing the legal decoupling of sexuality from covenant.

**1973** — **Roe v. Wade** — Removed the final institutional constraint on the consequences of decoupled sexuality. Nine domains show synchronized inflection within this window.

### Limitations

1. **Correlation is not causation.** Synchronization is statistically anomalous, but the causal mechanism requires further formal analysis beyond Granger causality.
2. **Measurement validity.** Domain measurements involve index construction choices that affect results. Alternative measurements should be tested.
3. **β universality requires replication.** The mean β = 0.41 is consistent with known universality classes but requires independent replication across additional long-term datasets.

### Control Group: The Amish Proof

Amish communities maintained the constraint set that the wider culture removed. They did not adopt no-fault divorce, gold window closure, or the semantic drift. The result is a natural experiment with an observable outcome.

**~0%** — Divorce Rate (vs. 45% national average)
**85%** — Community Retention
**20 yr** — Population Doubling (fastest-growing demographic in North America)

Structural coherence = demographic survival.

The equation works in reverse. Restore constraints, coherence returns. The Amish are not a curiosity. They are the experimental proof.
