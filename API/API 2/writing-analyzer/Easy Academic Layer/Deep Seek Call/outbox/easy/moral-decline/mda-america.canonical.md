### Critical Thresholds

**1962 — Engel v. Vitale**  
The Supreme Court removed prayer from public schools. Within one generation (about 20-30 years), every FACTS metric started to decline. FACTS stands for the five pillars this series tracks: Family, Authority, Culture, Truth, and Stability.

**1968 — Phase Transition Begins**  
SAT scores hit their highest point ever. Then they started dropping. Social trust — how much people believe they can trust strangers and institutions — began to fall. The divorce rate started speeding up. This was the turning point (called an "inflection point" in math) across many separate sets of data that all agree with each other.

**1971 — Nixon Shock**  
The United States stopped trading dollars for gold. Money was no longer backed by something real. This broke the honesty of the money system. After this, the average worker's pay stopped keeping up with how much they produced. Economic morality — the idea that money should mean something real — became a ghost.

**1973 — Roe v. Wade**  
The Supreme Court redefined when human life is legally protected. A court decision separated the moral weight of an action from the personal choice to take it. An institution declared that consequence and choice could be split apart.

**2008 — Financial Crisis & Smartphone Saturation**  
Trust in institutions — banks, government, media, churches — fell below the point where it can come back on its own. Smartphones and social media algorithms started capturing human attention in a new way. The person watching (the observer) began to disappear as a stable self.

### A Note on Methodology

This series uses the math of thermodynamics (the physics of heat, energy, and disorder) to model social data. This is not a comparison or a metaphor. It is a structural match — the same shape fits both sets of facts.

When disorder (called "entropy") increases inside a closed system — a system with no new energy coming in from outside — it does not reverse on its own. Ice melts into water. Water boils into steam. You cannot un-melt the ice without adding energy from outside.

The same math that governs these physical phase transitions maps exactly onto the social data we observe: slow decline at first, then faster decline past a critical point, and then no way back without energy from outside the system.

Every claim in this series has a source. Every measurement is tracked over many years (this is called "longitudinal data"). The FACTS framework was built to be testable and provably wrong — if any of the five pillars showed signs of recovery, the whole argument would get weaker. None of them has.
