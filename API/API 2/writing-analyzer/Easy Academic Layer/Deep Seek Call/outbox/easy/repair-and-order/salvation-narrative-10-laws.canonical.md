**You stopped resisting the curvature. The draw was always there.**

This is the Gibbs free energy equation. When ΔG is negative, the reaction happens spontaneously. The system releases more energy than it absorbs. The phase transition is thermodynamically favorable. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system.

**Spiritual:**
Justice demands the entropy debt. Mercy provides E > TS. **The Cross = the work term.**

**The books balance. The transition is thermodynamically spontaneous.**
