# The Ontological Decision Tree

This is a tree of all possible answers to the biggest questions. At every fork, we list every possible answer. Some branches die. Some keep going. Secular physics (science without God) and Theophysics (science with God) climb the same tree. They only split apart at certain points.

## Methodology: Four Question Types × Four Death Conditions

**Question Type 1 — "Does X hold?"**
Yes / No / Meaningless / Illusory. Max 4 branches.

**Question Type 2 — "What IS X?"**
List every possible category: material / mental / informational / relational / mathematical / brute / composite.

**Question Type 3 — "What grounds X?"**
Where does it come from? Branches: nothing (brute fact) / external (risk of infinite chain) / itself (self-grounding) / illusory / necessary.

**Question Type 4 — "What must X be?"**
Not branching — deduction. Given what survived, figure out what properties it must have.

**Death Condition — Self-Refutation**
The claim destroys itself when you say it.

**Death Condition — Infinite Regress**
The answer just pushes the question back without solving it.

**Death Condition — Empirical Contradiction**
The claim contradicts what we actually observe.

**Death Condition — Logical Incoherence**
The claim contradicts something already proven in the tree.

## Level 0 — Bedrock

**Q0: Does anything exist?**

This question proves itself. The act of asking it requires a questioner.

- **Q0-A: No, nothing exists.** DEAD. SELF-REFUTATION. To say "nothing exists" requires someone to say it. The statement itself is something. The claim destroys itself when spoken.

- **Q0-B: Yes, something exists.** CONTINUES. This is undeniable. Even the most extreme skeptic (like Descartes with his "I think, therefore I am") ends up here. The act of doubting is itself something. This forces the next question.

- **Q0-C: The question is meaningless.** DEAD. SELF-REFUTATION. Declaring something meaningless requires a meaning-bearing thing to declare it. The statement assumes what it denies.

- **Q0-D: Existence is an illusion.** COLLAPSES into Q0-B. An illusion requires something to be illusory *for*. The illusion itself exists as an illusion. It routes back to "something exists."

**SYS-B (Theophysics):** A1.1 — "Something exists rather than nothing."
**SYS-A (Secular Physics):** Agrees completely. No split here.

## Level 1 — Distinction

**Q1: Can that which exists be told apart from that which does not?**

Forced by Q0-B. If "something" exists, the word implies it is not "nothing." But is that difference real?

- **Q1-A: No — being and non-being are the same.** DEAD. LOGICAL INCOHERENCE. Contradicts Q0-B. If existence and non-existence are the same, "something exists" has no meaning. You've destroyed your own starting point.

- **Q1-B: Yes — the difference between being and non-being is real.** CONTINUES. This is what "existence" MEANS. To exist is to be different from not-existing. Forces Q2.

- **Q1-C: The difference is real at one level but an illusion at another.** COSTLY. Some Eastern philosophies live here: everyday difference is real, but ultimate difference is an illusion. But stating THIS requires telling the two levels apart — which creates a loop. Carries a cost forward.

**SYS-B:** A1.2 — "Existence requires distinguishability."
**SYS-A:** Agrees. Science defines observables by how you tell them apart.

## Level 2 — The Information Fork

**Q2: What IS a distinction?**

Forced by Q1-B. Distinction is real. What is it, at its most basic level? This is the first major kill zone — six worldviews die here at once.

- **Q2-A: A property of matter — physical objects differ.** CIRCULAR. This assumes matter exists. We haven't established matter yet. We only have "something exists" and "distinction is real." What IS matter, if not a pattern of distinctions?

- **Q2-B: A property of mind — observers create difference.** CIRCULAR. This assumes observers exist. We haven't established consciousness yet. What tells the observer apart from the non-observer?

- **Q2-C: A distinction IS information — the smallest unit of difference is the bit.** CONTINUES. This is the definition. Claude Shannon (1948) formalized exactly this: information = reduction of uncertainty between states you can tell apart. One distinction = one bit. No extra building blocks needed. Forces Q3.

- **Q2-D: A brute, unanalyzable primitive.** DEAD. A "brute distinction" that carries no information is the same as no distinction (violates Q1-B). If it DOES carry content, you've just restated Q2-C.

- **Q2-E: A mathematical relation.** MERGES into Q2-C. Mathematical relations ARE informational structures. This is Q2-C in different language. Not wrong — not independent.

- **Q2-F: A structural relation.** COLLAPSES into Q2-C. "Relations all the way down." But relations between what? If the things being related are eliminated → relations without content → Q2-D (dead). If the things carry content → "structure" = "pattern of distinctions" → Q2-C.

**SYS-B:** A1.3 — "Distinguishability IS information; information is the most basic building block."
**SYS-A:** Operates in Q2-A territory. It works but is logically circular at this level.

## Level 3 — Order

**Q3: Does information need structure, or can it exist as pure randomness?**

- **Q3-C: Structure is imposed by the observer, not built-in.** DEFERRED. This assumes observers again. And for the information itself, it's random until observed → Q3-A. Kicks the can down the road.

## Level 4 — The Great Fork

**Q4: What grounds the structure?**

THE primary split. Secular physics and Theophysics take different branches here. Both are logically valid — but one stops and one keeps going.

- **Q4-A: Nothing — the structure is a brute fact.** TERMINAL. This is where secular physics lives. Honestly. "The laws are what they are." Logically coherent. No more questions can be answered. Not a contradiction — a boundary.

- **Q4-B: Another structure grounds it — turtles all the way down.** DEAD. INFINITE REGRESS. Every "ground" needs a deeper ground. No explanation is ever complete. No foundation is ever reached.

- **Q4-C: The structure grounds itself — self-consistent and self-referencing.** CONTINUES. Self-grounding. The "why" and the "what" are the same at the deepest level. The system couldn't NOT exist — it's a mathematical necessity. Forces Q5 (property derivation).

- **Q4-E: The question is illegitimate — "why" has no answer.** COLLAPSES into Q4-A. Saying "no answer exists" IS accepting brute fact. Same position, different hat.

## Level 6 — Observer Requirement

**Q6: Does the ground need actualization — does information become real on its own?**

The measurement problem. Potential vs actual. Quantum particles exist in multiple states at once until observed — why?

- **Q6-A: Information makes itself real — no observer needed.** PROBLEMATIC. CONTRADICTS QUANTUM MECHANICS. If information makes itself real, why does the multiple-state condition exist at all? The measurement problem IS the question of how potential becomes actual.

- **Q6-B: Information needs observation to become actual.** CONTINUES. Participatory universe. John Wheeler's delayed-choice experiment. The Born rule. Potential needs actualization, actualization needs observer. Forces Q7.

- **Q6-C: Decoherence handles it — spontaneous collapse.** INCOMPLETE. Explains the *appearance* of collapse, not the thing itself. Wigner's Friend thought experiment: where does the observer chain end?

- **Q6-D: No potential/actual difference — everything is always actual.** DEAD. EMPIRICAL CONTRADICTION. The multiple-state condition is experimentally verified. Interference patterns require it.

**SYS-B:** A5.1 — Observer required for actualization. A5.2 — Observers participate.
**SYS-A:** Split — Many-Worlds (Q6-A), Copenhagen (Q6-B), Decoherence (Q6-C). No agreement.
