The study advances a methodological bridge between systematic theology and computational psychometrics, proposing that spiritual formation may be understood as the cultivation of specific emotional-cognitive states through disciplined practice.

### 6.2 Methodological Limitations

Several limitations of the present study should be acknowledged. First, the L6 lexical scores for seven of the nine fruits are zero, which may reflect limitations in the embedding algorithm rather than genuine semantic absence. Second, the GoEmotions taxonomy was developed for modern English text, and its application to Koine Greek may introduce translational artifacts. Third, the sample size (n = 9 fruits) precludes robust statistical inference; the confidence intervals reported in Section 2.3 should be interpreted as indicative rather than definitive.
