# Theophysics: A Formal Framework for Cross-Domain Analysis of Moral Decline in America (1900–2025)

## Abstract

This article presents a methodological framework for investigating the measurable decline in moral indicators across American society from the early twentieth century to the present day. The investigation employs a structured, interdisciplinary approach that integrates sociological, theological, economic, and philosophical perspectives within a unified analytical architecture. The central thesis posits that the observed moral decline constitutes a phenomenon requiring simultaneous analysis across multiple domains of inquiry, and that conventional single-discipline methodologies prove insufficient for capturing the complexity of this transformation. A five-page system is introduced as a formal research protocol designed to preserve intellectual rigor while leveraging artificial intelligence as a collaborative analytical tool rather than an authoritative conclusion-generator. The framework emphasizes the critical importance of contradiction identification and resolution as primary loci of truth discovery, rather than consensus-based validation.

## 1. Introduction: The Research Imperative

### 1.1 Statement of the Research Question

The foundational inquiry driving this investigation is: *Why is there a measurable, widespread change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to today?* This question, while ostensibly sociological in its surface formulation, implicates multiple intersecting domains of human experience and knowledge production. The question is not merely descriptive but explanatory, requiring the identification of causal mechanisms that operate across theological, economic, institutional, and cultural registers simultaneously.

### 1.2 Epistemological Positioning

The author acknowledges a personal investment in this research question that extends beyond conventional academic detachment. This investment is explicitly stated not as a methodological weakness but as a motivational substrate that drives the inquiry forward. The recognition of personal bias is incorporated into the research design as a load-bearing element: the researcher's pre-AI intuitions are preserved and documented before any computational assistance is engaged, thereby maintaining authorial sovereignty over the framing of the question itself. This approach aligns with established practices in reflexive sociology and phenomenological research, wherein the researcher's situatedness is treated as data rather than noise.

### 1.3 The Interdisciplinary Imperative

The research question, by its nature, resists disciplinary containment. It spans:

- **Sociology**: Shifts in collective moral norms, institutional trust, and social cohesion
- **Theology**: Transformations in religious belief, moral ontology, and teleological frameworks
- **Economics**: Changes in material conditions, labor structures, and wealth distribution
- **Family Structure**: Alterations in kinship patterns, child-rearing practices, and intergenerational transmission of values
- **Philosophy**: Evolving conceptions of the good, the right, and the nature of moral truth

No single disciplinary lens can adequately capture the phenomenon under investigation. The methodology must therefore be designed to hold complexity without collapsing it into a single explanatory framework.

## 2. The Five-Page System: A Formal Research Protocol

### 2.1 Architectural Overview

The investigation employs a structured five-page system, each component of which serves a distinct and non-redundant function within the research workflow. This architecture is not decorative but constitutive of the method itself. The five pages are:

1. **Inquiry Seed**: Question and Orientation
2. **AI Prompt Engine**: Documentation of AI Utilization
3. **Research Workspace**: Evidence and Primary Sources
4. **Contradictions and Resolution**: Sense-Making Through Disagreement
5. **Quotes, Domains, and Links**: Cumulative Reference Library

### 2.2 Page One: Inquiry Seed

**Function**: To state the research question with precision and to capture the researcher's initial intuition or belief prior to any AI input.

**Methodological Rationale**: The pre-AI framing of the question is identified as "load-bearing" within the research architecture. If the researcher permits AI to frame the question, authorial sovereignty over the inquiry is compromised at its inception. The Inquiry Seed therefore functions as a baseline against which all subsequent AI-assisted exploration is measured.

**Formal Specification**: Each Inquiry Seed shall include:
- The precise research question
- The researcher's initial hypothesis or intuition
- A timestamp documenting the moment of articulation
- A declaration of the researcher's epistemic stance (e.g., certainty, uncertainty, provisional commitment)

### 2.3 Page Two: AI Prompt Engine

**Function**: To document the exact prompts used, the AI models consulted, and the outputs received—without premature judgment.

**Methodological Rationale**: The AI output is treated as raw data at this stage. No evaluation is performed. The engine maps the intellectual terrain: competing views, key scholars, major fault lines, and suggested areas for deeper investigation. The AI does not decide what is true; it surfaces possibilities for human evaluation.

**Formal Specification**: Each AI Prompt Engine entry shall include:
- The exact text of the prompt
- The AI model and version used
- The date and time of the query
- The raw output, unedited and unannotated
- A notation of any observed inconsistencies or apparent errors (to be addressed in Page Four)

### 2.4 Page Three: Research Workspace

**Function**: The living document where evidence accumulates through engagement with primary sources.

**Methodological Rationale**: AI suggestions are tested against primary literature. Not every AI claim survives contact with the source material. The Research Workspace is where this collision occurs. Every claim is traced to a verifiable source with appropriate citation data.

**Formal Specification**: Each Research Workspace entry shall include:
- The claim under investigation
- The primary source(s) consulted
- Full citation data (author, title, publication, date, page numbers where applicable)
- The researcher's assessment of the claim's validity
- Confidence level (high, moderate, low) with justification

### 2.5 Page Four: Contradictions and Resolution

**Function**: To identify, document, and engage with points of disagreement within the evidence base.

**Methodological Rationale**: This page is identified as the most critical component of the system. The researcher is required to identify:
- Where sources disagree with one another
- Where AI output contradicts primary sources
- Where the researcher's intuition conflicts with the data

The protocol explicitly prohibits the selective deployment of confirming evidence. Truth, according to the methodological axiom adopted here, is "most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily."

**Formal Specification**: Each Contradictions entry shall include:
- The two (or more) competing claims
- The sources supporting each claim
- The nature of the disagreement (factual, interpretive, methodological)
- The researcher's attempt at resolution, or a declaration that resolution is not yet possible
- A timestamp documenting the duration of engagement with the contradiction

### 2.6 Page Five: Quotes, Domains, and Links

**Function**: A cumulative reference library built across all inquiries.

**Methodological Rationale**: This page grows organically across the research process. It serves as the raw material from which publications are drawn. Key quotes are preserved with attribution and date. Domain categories are identified and refined. External links are contextualized.

**Formal Specification**: Each entry shall include:
- The quoted text (with quotation marks)
- Full attribution (author, work, publication date, page)
- The domain category to which the quote belongs
- A brief context note explaining the quote's relevance
- The date the entry was added to the library

## 3. The Role of Artificial Intelligence in the Research Process

### 3.1 Permitted Uses

Artificial intelligence is deployed within this framework for the following specific purposes:

1. **Terrain Mapping**: Rapid exploration of intellectual landscapes across multiple disciplines
2. **Contradiction Surfacing**: Identification of points of disagreement between competing interpretations
3. **Framework Organization**: Side-by-side presentation of competing theoretical frameworks
4. **Research Direction Suggestion**: Identification of areas requiring deeper primary-source investigation
5. **Stress-Testing**: Evaluation of preliminary conclusions against counterarguments

### 3.2 Prohibited Uses

Artificial intelligence is explicitly prohibited from performing the following functions:

1. **Truth Determination**: AI does not decide what is true
2. **Primary Source Replacement**: AI does not substitute for direct engagement with primary literature
3. **Question Framing**: AI does not frame the initial research question
4. **Evidence Selection**: AI does not determine which evidence counts as significant
5. **Contradiction Resolution**: AI does not resolve contradictions that require human judgment

### 3.3 Error as Signal

The protocol anticipates that some AI outputs will be incomplete or incorrect. This is not treated as a failure of the method but as a signal. AI error indicates:
- Where experts genuinely disagree
- Where consensus was forced rather than earned
- Where deeper investigation is required

## 4. Workflow Sequence

The research proceeds through the following sequential stages:

1. **Inquiry Seed**: The researcher writes the question and initial intuition before any AI prompt is run.
2. **AI Prompt Engine**: The AI is queried, and the output is collected without judgment.
3. **Research Workspace**: The AI's leads are followed into primary sources. Everything is cited.
4. **Contradictions Page**: Disagreements are forced into the open and engaged with directly.
5. **Quotes, Domains, and Links**: The cumulative library is maintained and expanded with each session.

This sequence is repeated for each new research question. The structure is not procedural red tape but the framework that prevents AI-assisted research from devolving into AI-generated opinion.

## 5. Conclusion: The Epistemological Commitment

This research exists because the investigator felt the weight of the question and determined that feeling alone was insufficient. The method requires:
- Active looking rather than passive reception
- Engagement with uncomfortable data
- Sustained attention to contradiction before resolution is attempted

The framework presented here constitutes a formal protocol for interdisciplinary research that preserves human judgment while leveraging computational assistance. It is designed to produce knowledge that is rigorous, transparent, and accountable to the complexity of the phenomena under investigation.
