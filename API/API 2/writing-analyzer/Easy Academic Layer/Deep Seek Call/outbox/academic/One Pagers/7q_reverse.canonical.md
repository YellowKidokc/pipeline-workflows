# The 7Q Framework: A Reverse-Direction Methodology for Theory Evaluation in Theophysics

## Abstract

This article presents the 7Q Framework, a methodological structure for theory evaluation that inverts the conventional scientific method. Rather than commencing with a hypothesis and seeking confirmatory evidence, the 7Q approach begins with systematic destruction—identifying all conditions under which a proposed theoretical construct would fail—and proceeds through seven sequential stages to arrive at a named conclusion only after survival has been demonstrated. The framework is explicated through application to General Relativity (GR), demonstrating how each stage operates within a well-established physical theory. The 7Q Framework constitutes a formalized procedure for epistemic warranting, wherein theoretical identity is earned through survival rather than assumed through initial postulation.

---

## 1. Introduction: The Problem of Assumed Identity in Conventional Methodology

The conventional scientific method proceeds through a forward-directed sequence: hypothesis formation, evidence gathering, confirmatory testing, and conclusion announcement. This trajectory, while historically productive, embeds a fundamental epistemic vulnerability: the hypothesis carries its identity—and thus its claim to explanatory legitimacy—from the outset. Confirmation bias is structurally privileged, as the investigator seeks to validate a pre-existing belief rather than to test its capacity for survival under adversarial scrutiny (Kahneman, 2011; Nickerson, 1998).

The 7Q Framework proposes an inversion of this sequence. Rather than beginning with a named hypothesis and seeking confirmation, the method commences with exhaustive destruction—the systematic identification of all conditions under which the candidate claim would be falsified—and proceeds through forced consequences, foundational tracing, evidential confirmation, precise statement, cross-domain generalization, and terminal naming. The conclusion is not proven; it is observed to survive.

This methodological inversion was identified through structural comparison of adversarial testing protocols (Popper, 1959), Bayesian confirmation theory (Earman, 1992), and the epistemology of survival in evolutionary epistemology (Campbell, 1974). The framework is presented as a formal procedure applicable across disciplinary boundaries, with particular relevance to interdisciplinary domains such as theophysics, where claims must satisfy criteria from multiple epistemic communities simultaneously.

## 2. The Seven Stages of the Reverse-Direction Framework

### 2.1 Stage Q7: Destruction — Defining Failure Conditions First

**Function:** Prior to any constructive argumentation, enumerate all conditions under which the candidate claim would be falsified, refuted, or rendered incoherent.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Unknown → Vulnerable

The investigator does not commence by stating what is believed. Rather, the investigator explicitly catalogues every identifiable mode of failure. Five canonical death conditions are specified:

1. **Self-refutation:** The claim contradicts its own presuppositions.
2. **Infinite regress:** The claim requires an unending chain of justification.
3. **Empirical contradiction:** The claim conflicts with observed phenomena.
4. **Logical incoherence:** The claim violates principles of formal logic.
5. **Explanatory failure:** The claim fails to account for phenomena within its purported domain.

**Formal Principle:** If a claim's death conditions cannot be specified, its epistemic status cannot be determined. The capacity to articulate falsification conditions is a prerequisite for meaningful theoretical discourse (Popper, 1959, pp. 40–41).

#### Application: General Relativity

Prior to any exposition of GR's content, the following death conditions are specified:

- GR dies if light does not bend in the presence of gravitational fields.
- GR dies if gravitational waves are not physically realizable.
- GR dies if Global Positioning System (GPS) satellites do not require relativistic time-dilation corrections.
- GR dies if spacetime singularities are mathematically or physically impossible.
- GR dies if the equivalence principle is empirically falsified.

These five conditions constitute the kill zone. The reader is invited to observe whether the theory survives each condition as the argument proceeds.

### 2.2 Stage Q6: Force — Extracting Necessary Consequences

**Function:** If the candidate claim survives Stage Q7, determine what it necessarily generates—predictions, constraints, and consequences that must obtain if the claim is true.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Vulnerable → Constrained

The investigator asks: What must follow from the survival of this claim? Not what is hoped to follow, but what is logically or physically compelled. A claim that survives destruction but generates no downstream consequences is epistemically inert—it explains nothing (Hempel, 1965, pp. 245–295).

**Formal Principle:** A surviving claim \( C \) forces a set of consequences \( \mathcal{F}(C) = \{f_1, f_2, \ldots, f_n\} \) such that if any \( f_i \) is false, \( C \) is falsified. The cardinality and specificity of \( \mathcal{F}(C) \) are measures of the claim's explanatory power.

#### Application: General Relativity

If spacetime curvature is determined by mass-energy distribution, then the following are forced consequences:

- **Black holes must exist:** Extreme curvature produces event horizons.
- **Gravitational waves must propagate:** Ripples in spacetime curvature are a necessary consequence of the field equations.
- **Time dilation near massive bodies:** Metric distortion produces gravitational time dilation.
- **Gravitational lensing:** Geodesic paths through curved spacetime produce light deflection.

These consequences were not all predicted by Einstein (1915) prior to the theory's formulation; rather, they were forced by the mathematical structure of the theory itself.

### 2.3 Stage Q5: Ground — Tracing Foundational Assumptions

**Function:** Identify the foundational assumptions upon which the surviving claim depends. Trace the chain of dependency to its terminus.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Constrained → Grounded

The investigator asks: What must already be true for this claim to hold? The chain of dependency terminates at one of three possible endpoints:

1. **Axiom:** A foundational proposition that is assumed without proof.
2. **Brute fact:** A contingent state of affairs that cannot be further explained.
3. **Circularity:** The chain loops back upon itself, indicating a closed system.

**Formal Principle:** A claim \( C \) stands on a foundation set \( \mathcal{G}(C) = \{g_1, g_2, \ldots, g_n\} \), where each \( g_i \) is either axiomatic, brute, or circular. The epistemic status of \( C \) is contingent upon the epistemic status of \( \mathcal{G}(C) \).

#### Application: General Relativity

GR stands on the following foundations:

- **Equivalence principle:** Gravitational acceleration is locally indistinguishable from inertial acceleration (axiom).
- **Riemannian geometry:** Spacetime is modeled as a smooth, four-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifold (axiom).
- **Constancy of the speed of light \( c \):** A fundamental postulate of special relativity (axiom).
- **Conservation of energy-momentum:** A principle derived from Noether's theorem (derived, but foundational within the theory).

The chain terminates at the equivalence principle—an axiom, not a derived result. The smooth manifold assumption constitutes a hidden root that may be invalid at quantum scales, where spacetime is hypothesized to be discrete or non-commutative (Rovelli, 2004).

### 2.4 Stage Q4: Support — Evidential Confirmation After Survival

**Function:** Present empirical evidence that confirms the surviving claim. Evidence arrives after the structure has already survived destruction, not as a constructive element.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Grounded → Supported

In the forward direction, evidence is used to build the case for a hypothesis. In the reverse direction, evidence confirms a structure that has already demonstrated survival capacity. The evidential role shifts from persuasion to confirmation (Mayo, 1996, pp. 175–220).

**Formal Principle:** Evidence \( E \) supports claim \( C \) if and only if \( P(E|C) > P(E|\neg C) \), where the prior probability of \( C \) has been established through survival in Stages Q7–Q5.

#### Application: General Relativity

The following empirical confirmations are presented after the structural argument:

- **Mercury's perihelion precession:** Predicted value of 43 arcseconds per century (Einstein, 1915).
- **Light deflection:** Confirmed during the 1919 solar eclipse expedition (Dyson, Eddington, & Davidson, 1920).
- **Gravitational waves:** Direct detection by LIGO in 2015 (Abbott et al., 2016).
- **GPS relativistic corrections:** Operational necessity for satellite navigation systems (Ashby, 2003).

The reader has already observed: what kills the theory, what it forces, and what it stands on. The evidence confirms a structure that was already standing.

### 2.5 Stage Q3: State — Precise Formulation of the Surviving Claim

**Function:** State the claim precisely and formally. This is no longer a hypothesis; it is a conclusion that has survived systematic adversarial testing.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Supported → Stated

Only at this stage is the claim articulated in its final form. The reader has observed the entire process of destruction, consequence extraction, foundation tracing, and evidential confirmation. The claim does not require belief; it requires acknowledgment as that which has survived.

#### Application: General Relativity

**Formal Statement:** The distribution of mass-energy determines the curvature of spacetime, and free-falling objects follow geodesics through that curvature. Mathematically, this is expressed by the Einstein field equations:

\[
G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}
\]

where:
- \( G_{\mu\nu} \) is the Einstein tensor, representing spacetime curvature,
- \( \Lambda \) is the cosmological constant (dimension \( L^{-2} \)),
- \( g_{\mu\nu} \) is the metric tensor (dimensionless),
- \( G \) is Newton's gravitational constant (dimension \( M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2} \)),
- \( c \) is the speed of light in vacuum (dimension \( L T^{-1} \)),
- \( T_{\mu\nu} \) is the stress-energy tensor (dimension \( M L^{-1} T^{-2} \)).

This statement appears at Stage Q3, not at Stage Q7. The reader already knows what kills it, what it forces, what it stands on, and what data confirms it.

### 2.6 Stage Q2: Generalize — Cross-Domain Structural Isomorphism

**Function:** Demonstrate that the surviving claim's structure is not domain-specific but appears across multiple fields of inquiry.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Stated → Located

The investigator asks: Does the same formal structure appear elsewhere? If the architecture holds when variables are relabeled, this indicates structural isomorphism—a strong indicator that the claim captures something real rather than merely local (Hofstadter, 1979, pp. 49–68).

**Formal Principle:** A claim \( C \) exhibits cross-domain validity if there exists an isomorphism \( \phi: \mathcal{D}_1 \to \mathcal{D}_2 \) such that the formal structure of \( C \) in domain \( \mathcal{D}_1 \) maps to a valid structure in domain \( \mathcal{D}_2 \).

#### Application: General Relativity

The Riemannian geometry underlying GR appears in multiple domains:

- **Machine learning:** Manifold learning algorithms (Tenenbaum, de Silva, & Langford, 2000).
- **Economics:** Curved utility surfaces in consumer choice theory (Debreu, 1972).
- **Information theory:** Fisher information geometry (Amari & Nagaoka, 2000).

This is not analogy; it is structural isomorphism. The mathematical architecture holds when the variable labels are changed, indicating that GR's formal structure is not physics-specific but reflects a deeper mathematical reality.

### 2.7 Stage Q1: Name — Identity Earned Through Survival

**Function:** Assign a name to the claim only after all preceding stages have been completed. Identity is earned, not assumed.

**Epistemic Status Transition:** Located → Defined (Earned)

The full structure is now visible: death conditions, forced consequences, foundational dependencies, evidential confirmation, precise formulation, and cross-domain location. Only now is the claim named. Everything that could have died is dead. What remains has been tested, traced, supported, stated, and generalized.

#### Application: General Relativity

After all preceding stages, the theory is named:

**General Relativity: A foundational causal theory of spacetime curvature, derived from the equivalence principle, confirmed across 100+ years of empirical testing, with one known open question at quantum scales.**

This name carries meaning because it was earned through survival.

## 3. The Complete Sequence

The 7Q Framework proceeds through seven stages in reverse order:

| Stage | Designation | Function | Epistemic Transition |
|-------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 (Q7) | Destroy | Define failure conditions | Unknown → Vulnerable |
| 2 (Q6) | Force | Extract necessary consequences | Vulnerable → Constrained |
| 3 (Q5) | Ground | Trace foundational dependencies | Constrained → Grounded |
| 4 (Q4) | Support | Present confirming evidence | Grounded → Supported |
| 5 (Q3) | State | Formulate precise claim | Supported → Stated |
| 6 (Q2) | Generalize | Demonstrate cross-domain validity | Stated → Located |
| 7 (Q1) | Name | Assign earned identity | Located → Defined |

The sequence proceeds: death condition → consequence → dependency → evidence → assertion → location → identity.

## 4. Conclusion

The 7Q Framework constitutes a formal methodology for theory evaluation that inverts the conventional scientific method. By commencing with systematic destruction rather than hypothesis formation, the framework ensures that theoretical identity is earned through survival rather than assumed through initial postulation. The reader does not need to believe the conclusion; the reader has observed everything else fail.
