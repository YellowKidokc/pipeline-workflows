# Free Will and Divine Sovereignty: A Theophysics Analysis of the Calvinist–Arminian Debate Through Coherence Dynamics

## Abstract

This article applies the Theophysics coherence framework to the longstanding theological dispute between Calvinist and Arminian soteriological models. We propose that the debate, which has persisted for approximately five centuries without resolution, may be predicated upon a category error: both traditions have identified genuine variables within a dynamical system while treating those variables as exhaustive descriptions of the system itself. The coherence evolution equation, \(dC/dt = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C\), is introduced as a formal model that accommodates both divine grace and human openness as multiplicatively coupled terms. Analysis of the steady-state solution \(C^* = OG/(OG + S)\) reveals that neither term can be eliminated without collapsing the system to zero coherence. The surrender parameterization \(s \in [-1, +1]\) is developed to map the three attitudinal postures—autonomous resistance, self-righteous performance, and full surrender—onto distinct dynamical regimes with corresponding neurological correlates. Four falsifiable predictions are derived, and the framework's limitations, including its inability to adjudicate double predestination, are explicitly acknowledged.

## 1. Introduction: The Problem of Underspecification

The Pauline injunction in Philippians 2:12–13 presents what appears to be a paradoxical conjunction: "Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you, both to will and to work for his good pleasure" (Philippians 2:12–13, ESV). The text simultaneously asserts human agency ("work out") and divine sovereignty ("God who works in you") without resolving the apparent tension between them. This conjunction has generated a theological dispute—Calvinist versus Arminian, monergist versus synergist—that has persisted without resolution for approximately sixteen centuries in various formulations.

The present investigation proposes that this debate fails to achieve resolution not because either position is demonstrably false, but because both positions operate within an underspecified conceptual framework. The question "does God choose or do we choose?" presupposes a binary that the underlying dynamics may not support. We argue that the coherence equation—a dynamical model of spiritual alignment—provides a more complete description within which both variables are structurally necessary.

Section 3 maps the Calvinist–Arminian debate onto the equation's variables and demonstrates the structural dissolution of the binary. Section 4 develops the surrender parameterization and its three-pathway architecture. Section 5 examines the pre-Fall and post-Fall will in equation terms. Section 9 presents the neurological and behavioral correlates of the three pathways. Section 10 concludes with implications for future research.

## 2. The Coherence Evolution Equation

### 2.1 Formal Definition

The Theophysics framework models spiritual coherence—defined as the degree to which a human system is aligned with its source specification—as a dynamical variable \(C\) that evolves over time according to:

\[
\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C
\]

where:

- \(C \in [0,1]\) represents **coherence**, with 0 indicating total decoherence (complete separation from source) and 1 indicating perfect alignment.
- \(O \in [0,1]\) represents **openness**, the human agent's receptivity parameter.
- \(G > 0\) represents **grace input**, the rate at which divine grace is available to the system. This term is externally supplied and not generated by the agent.
- \(S \geq 0\) represents the **entropy/sin parameter**, the rate at which coherence decays due to misalignment, resistance, or moral failure.
- \((1-C)\) represents the **coherence gap**, the available space for grace to occupy.

The equation belongs to the family of logistic growth models with external drive and decay terms, a structure that appears throughout population biology, epidemiology, and information theory. No exotic mathematics is being introduced; rather, a known dynamical structure is being applied to a new domain.

### 2.2 Steady-State Analysis

At equilibrium, where \(dC/dt = 0\), the steady-state coherence is given by:

\[
C^* = \frac{O \cdot G}{O \cdot G + S}
\]

This solution reveals three structurally significant properties:

1. **Multiplicative coupling**: The terms \(O\) and \(G\) appear as a product. If either term is zero, the numerator vanishes and \(C^* = 0\) regardless of the magnitude of the other term.
2. **Grace dependence**: If \(G = 0\), \(C^* = 0\) for any finite \(O\). Grace is necessary for nonzero coherence.
3. **Openness dependence**: If \(O = 0\), \(C^* = 0\) for any finite \(G\). Openness is necessary for grace to produce coherence.

The equation is structurally synergistic—not by authorial choice, but as a consequence of the coupled dynamics.

## 3. The Calvinist–Arminian Dissolution

### 3.1 Variable Mapping

The historic debate maps onto the equation's variables as follows:

| Camp | Core Claim | Equation Variable | Valid Insight | Omission |
|------|------------|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Calvinist | Salvation is entirely God's work; human will is enslaved post-Fall; election precedes human response | \(G\) (Grace) | \(G\) is non-zero and externally sourced; the agent cannot initiate it | \(O\) is also a real variable; setting \(O \equiv 1\) (irresistible grace) assumes the answer |
| Arminian | God extends prevenient grace to all; humans genuinely choose or refuse; faith is a real human act | \(O\) (Openness) | \(O\) is a real variable; grace does not automatically override it | \(O\) is not self-generated *ex nihilo*; the capacity for openness is itself graced |

### 3.2 Structural Analysis of the Dispute

The equation reveals that both camps have identified one term of a product while treating that term as the whole equation. Calvinism correctly identifies grace as the driving force but fails to account for openness as a modulating factor. Arminianism correctly identifies openness as real but struggles to account for the origin of that openness in a post-Fall agent.

The debate is not wrong; it is underspecified. Both camps have identified real variables. Neither has written down the equation that contains both variables. Once the equation is written, the binary opposition dissolves—not into compromise, but into a more complete description.

### 3.3 The Pauline Conjunction

Philippians 2:12–13 structurally requires both terms. "Work out your salvation" corresponds to the \(O\) term; "God who works in you" corresponds to the \(G\) term. Paul does not choose between them because they are not in competition; they are in multiplication. The comma separating the clauses is not a hedge but a structural necessity.

## 4. The Surrender Parameterization

### 4.1 Definition

To capture the qualitative attitudinal postures available to human agents, we introduce a signed surrender parameter \(s \in [-1, +1]\) that subsumes the openness variable \(O\) through the coupling function:

\[
\alpha(s) = \frac{1+s}{2}
\]

The extended coherence equation becomes:

\[
\frac{dC}{dt} = \frac{(1+s)}{2} \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C
\]

### 4.2 Three Attitudinal Postures

| Value of \(s\) | Label | Description | Equation Effect |
|----------------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| \(s = -1\) | Autonomous Resistance | Active opposition to grace; the agent treats coherence as a threat to self-definition | Grace term vanishes; coherence decays exponentially toward zero |
| \(s \approx 0\) | Self-Righteous Performance | The agent attempts to generate coherence through effort; grace is accepted only as assistance | Grace term is reduced by half; equilibrium is low and unstable |
| \(s = +1\) | Full Surrender | The agent orients completely toward the grace source; self-generated effort yields to receptivity | Grace term maximized; highest achievable \(C^*\) given fixed \(G\) and \(S\) |

### 4.3 Steady-State Analysis by Posture

For \(s = -1\): \(\alpha(s) = 0\), yielding \(dC/dt = -S \cdot C\). The only equilibrium is \(C^* = 0\), and coherence decays exponentially toward this value.

For \(s \approx 0\): \(\alpha(s) \approx 1/2\), yielding:

\[
C^*_{\text{perf}} = \frac{G/2}{G/2 + S} = \frac{G}{G + 2S}
\]

For a system with \(G = S\), this gives \(C^*_{\text{perf}} = 0.33\).

For \(s = +1\): \(\alpha(s) = 1\), yielding:

\[
C^* = \frac{G}{G + S}
\]

For a system with \(G = S\), this gives \(C^* = 0.50\).

The performance posture thus achieves real but reduced coherence relative to surrender, and the equilibrium is inherently less stable due to the reduced coupling.

## 5. The Will Before and After the Fall

### 5.1 Pre-Fall Will

Before the collapse event (described in Article 01 of this series), the human agent operates at \(C \approx 1\)—near-maximal coherence with the source specification. In this state, the will is free in the strongest possible sense: it aligns with its own deepest nature, which is itself aligned with the Logos field.

This is not freedom *from* constraint but freedom *as* the fullest expression of what the agent is. The pre-Fall will experiences no gap between desire and specification. What it wants, it wants rightly, because its wanting-mechanism is coherent with its source.

The capacity to choose wrongly existed—represented by the tree in the garden—but it required active movement away from the coherence equilibrium. The agent had to *work* to decohere. Temptation in Eden is not an overwhelming force but a subtle proposal to reframe the measurement basis.

### 5.2 Post-Fall Will

After the collapse event, the human agent defaults to \(C \ll 1\)—low coherence, high entropy, dominant \(S\) term. The will is now systematically biased. It can still choose, but the choice-generating mechanism is itself distorted. The problem is not that the will has been removed but that it now *wants the wrong things at the first order of desire*.

This preserves the Calvinist insight: total depravity does not mean maximum possible evil in every choice but that the will's operating system has been corrupted at the root. The Arminian insight is also preserved: the will still exists, still makes real choices, and still bears real moral weight.

What changes the situation is the injection of a grace source term \(G\) into the system—a negentropic input that the agent did not generate and cannot earn. The grace term does not override the will; it creates a gradient toward which the will can orient if it chooses to do so. Prevenient grace, in equation terms, is simply \(G > 0\) being nonzero for all human agents regardless of their prior state.

## 6. Falsifiable Predictions

Four predictions emerge from the coherence equation applied to human agents:

**Prediction 1 — Path 3 outperforms Path 2.** Agents in sustained surrender posture (\(s \approx +1\)) should show measurably higher coherence-proxy measures (psychological integration, behavioral consistency, physiological markers of reduced chronic stress) than agents in high-effort religious performance (\(s \approx 0\)), controlling for religious activity level.

**Prediction 2 — Grace is not correlated with prior moral performance.** If \(G\) is genuinely externally sourced and not proportional to prior \(C\), then recipients of high-grace experiences should be randomly distributed across the coherence spectrum at time of receipt. Conversion data should show significant representation of low-\(C\) agents (high-entropy life histories) receiving strong grace encounters.

**Prediction 3 — The \(s\) parameter transitions are abrupt, not gradual.** The phase diagram of the coherence equation predicts that transitions between attractor basins (e.g., Path 1 → Path 3) should resemble threshold crossings rather than smooth ramps. Testimonies of conversion should cluster around abrupt threshold events rather than gradual linear improvement curves.

**Prediction 4 — Low \(O\) blocks grace regardless of grace magnitude.** Subjects in high autonomy mode (\(s = -1\)) exposed to high-grace environments should show minimal coherence increase. The grace is present; the coupling is absent. This predicts that grace-rich environments (retreats, liturgy, prayer communities) produce minimal change in participants with closed attitudinal posture.

## 7. Audit: Load-Bearing and Suggestive Claims

### 7.1 Load-Bearing Claims

1. **The equation form is motivated.** The structure \(dC/dt = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C\) is a standard logistic growth model with external drive and decay term, appearing throughout biology, epidemiology, and information theory.

2. **Philippians 2:12–13 structurally requires both terms.** Paul's language is not hedging but simultaneous assertion. Any framework that yields one term at the expense of the other contradicts the text.

3. **The three-pathway structure maps onto documented neurological and psychological states.** The distinction between autonomy, performance, and surrender maps onto established categories in psychology of religion (Granqvist, 2014; Piedmont, 1999; Exline, 2002).

4. **The steady-state analysis is algebraically trivial.** The claim that \(C^* = OG/(OG + S)\) is a direct algebraic result requiring no assumptions beyond the equation form.

### 7.2 Suggestive Claims (Requiring Further Work)

1. **The mapping of theological concepts to equation variables is interpretive.** The argument that \(G\) maps to grace and \(O\) maps to openness is motivated but not uniquely forced. Alternative parameterizations are possible.

2. **The surrender parameter \(s\) is not directly measurable.** Proxies (self-report scales, behavioral indicators, physiological markers) can be operationalized, but \(s\) itself remains a theoretical construct until measurement protocols are established.

3. **The phase transition prediction (Prediction 3) requires careful operationalization.** "Abrupt" versus "gradual" is a testable claim, but defining the threshold requires calibration work.

### 7.3 Acknowledged Limitations

1. **The equation does not prove Christian theology.** It shows that a particular dynamical model is consistent with a particular reading of Pauline text. Consistency is not proof.

2. **The "dissolution" of the Calvinist–Arminian debate is a reframing, not a refutation.** Both camps can respond that we have redefined their terms. The reframing is useful if it generates testable predictions; its theological authority is a separate question.

3. **Double predestination is not addressed.** The strong Calvinist claim that God actively decrees some to damnation is not touched by the coherence equation. The equation predicts outcomes given parameters; it does not specify who sets the parameters or whether \(G\) is truly universal.

## 8. Case Study: John MacArthur's Calvinist Framework

### 8.1 Mapping MacArthur's Claims

John MacArthur's five-point Calvinist framework (TULIP) maps onto the coherence equation as follows:

| MacArthur Claim | Equation Mapping | What Survives | What Requires Qualification |
|-----------------|------------------|---------------|----------------------------|
| Total Depravity — will is enslaved | \(S\) term large, \(O\) severely reduced post-Fall | Correct: default \(O\) is near zero post-Fall; agent cannot self-generate coherence | \(O\) is not identically zero. Even a small nonzero \(O\) under sufficient \(G\) can produce salvation |
| Irresistible Grace — the elect will come | God sets \(G\) sufficiently large that even small \(O\) produces \(C^* >\) threshold | Correct: if \(G\) is large enough, even a small \(O\) crosses the threshold | This does not require \(O \equiv 1\); it requires \(G\) to be large enough to overcome typical \(O\) values in the elect |
| Unconditional Election — not based on foreseen faith | God selects \(G\) without reference to prior \(C\) or prior \(O\) | Consistent with equation: \(G\) is externally sourced | Does not entail that \(O\) is irrelevant to the dynamics |
| Limited Atonement — designed for the elect | Grace \(G\) is nonzero for a defined subset of agents | Consistent with equation if \(G = 0\) for non-elect | Disputed by passages suggesting universal provision (1 John 2:2; Hebrews 2:9) |

### 8.2 The Two-Phase Resolution

MacArthur's most distinctive claim—that God regenerates the elect first, enabling them to believe—maps cleanly onto the equation. In equation terms: God injects \(G\) into the system before the agent's \(O\) has shifted. The grace injection raises \(C\) sufficiently that the agent's attractor basin shifts from \(s \approx -1\) (autonomy/resistance) to \(s \approx +1\) (openness/surrender). The agent then "chooses" willingly, but the conditions that made the willing possible were set externally.

This yields a two-phase model:

- **Phase 1** (\(s: -1 \to 0\)): God acts on the closed system to open it. The agent at \(s = -1\) did not choose this. This is monergism—salvation initiated by God alone.
- **Phase 2** (\(s: 0 \to +1\)): The channel is open; grace is flowing. The growth term requires both God's input and human openness. This is synergism—response and growth.

Both camps are correct about different phases of the same process.

### 8.3 The Double Predestination Question

MacArthur rejects strong double predestination in favor of passive reprobation: God passes over the non-elect, leaving them in their deserved state. The equation models this as \(G = 0\) for the non-elect, yielding \(C^* = 0\) through the entropy term alone. No active harm; simply no grace injection.

Active reprobation (\(G < 0\)) is mathematically permitted by the equation but represents a separate theological question the equation cannot adjudicate.

## 9. The Three Pathways: Neurological and Behavioral Correlates

### 9.1 Path 1: Autonomy (\(s = -1\))

**Posture**: The autonomous agent treats the self as the primary reference frame. External grace is experienced as a threat to self-definition.

**Neurological correlates**:
- Default Mode Network (DMN) hyperactivation, associated with self-referential processing and rumination
- Elevated cortisol and sympathetic baseline, consistent with chronic hypervigilance
- Reduced oxytocin receptor sensitivity, diminishing capacity for bonding and receptivity

**Equation dynamics**: With \(s = -1\), the grace coupling term vanishes entirely. The only active term is \(-SC\), driving \(C\) exponentially toward zero.

### 9.2 Path 2: Performance (\(s \approx 0\))

**Posture**: The performance agent accepts the existence of a grace source but frames the relationship transactionally. Grace is available but must be accessed through correct performance.

**Neurological correlates**:
- Prefrontal cortex (PFC) overactivation, consistent with active self-evaluation and rule-following
- Shame-cycling: oscillation between achievement states and shame states
- Conditional oxytocin response, creating attachment anxiety

**Equation dynamics**: With \(s \approx 0\), partial grace coupling yields \(C^* = G/(G + 2S)\), significantly lower than the Path 3 equilibrium. The equilibrium is inherently less stable, with small perturbations in \(S\) producing larger swings in \(C\).

### 9.3 Path 3: Surrender (\(s = +1\))

**Posture**: The surrendered agent reorients the self as a receiver rather than a generator. Effort continues but is downstream of reception rather than the precondition for it.

**Neurological correlates**:
- Reduced DMN activity, consistent with decreased self-referential baseline processing
- Increased parasympathetic tone, supporting social engagement and receptive processing
- D2 receptor restoration in preliminary data, suggesting measurable neurological substrate
- Elevated baseline oxytocin, associated with trust and bonding capacity

**Equation dynamics**: With \(s = +1\), full grace coupling yields \(C^* = G/(G + S)\), the maximum achievable equilibrium given fixed \(G\) and \(S\).

### 9.4 Transition Architecture

Transitions between pathways are predicted to be threshold crossings rather than smooth ramps. The surrender parameter \(s\) is more accurately modeled as a system with discrete attractor basins separated by energy barriers. This prediction is consistent with:

- William James's analysis of religious experience (James, 1902)
- Clinical data on addiction recovery (the "bottom" as threshold-crossing event)
- Psychological studies of value restructuring (Park, 2005; Tedeschi & Calhoun, 2004)

## 10. Conclusion

The coherence equation \(dC/dt = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C\) does not resolve the Calvinist–Arminian debate by selecting one position over the other. It dissolves the debate by providing a more complete description that requires both terms. Grace is real, externally sourced, and universally available. Openness is real, under some form of agent influence, and genuinely modulates the coupling. Neither term is optional.

The fight between the two camps is a fight about which term is more important—a question the equation renders meaningless because the two terms multiply. Paul knew this. He did not choose between "work out your salvation" and "God who works in you." He put them in the same sentence, separated by a comma, and left the tension standing. The equation is the comma.
