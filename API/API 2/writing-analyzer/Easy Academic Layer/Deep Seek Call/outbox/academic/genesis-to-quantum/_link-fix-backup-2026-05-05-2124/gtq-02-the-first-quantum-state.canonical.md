## Abstract

This paper proposes that the pre-Fall condition described in Genesis 2–3 constitutes not a metaphorical approximation of quantum superposition but a structural isomorphism with it—a mapping in which every defining property of a quantum superposition state corresponds precisely to an attested property of Edenic reality as described in the Genesis narrative. The Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil is analyzed as a measurement apparatus in the technical quantum mechanical sense, satisfying the three conditions of operator interaction, irreversibility, and eigenstate actualization. Two distinct physical regimes are identified corresponding to the Tree of Life (coherence stabilizer) and the Tree of Knowledge (measurement operator). The paper then addresses the central anomaly: the Eve-Adam measurement asymmetry, in which an apparently complete measurement interaction by the first observer produces no systemic collapse while an identical interaction by the second produces immediate, non-local, total collapse. This asymmetry is proposed to require a new variable—Informational Fidelity, designated \(I_f\)—representing the cleanliness of an observer's coupling to the system's original specification. Four candidate frameworks (weak measurement, Wigner's Friend, entanglement theory, decoherence threshold) are evaluated and found insufficient. The paper concludes that the Eve-Adam asymmetry constitutes a genuine open problem in quantum theory, pointing toward undiscovered principles of observer authority for which the Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR) Laboratory data (6.35σ) provides empirical anchoring.

## §1 Introduction: The Structural Question

The relationship between modern physics and ancient scriptural narrative is most productively approached neither through the lens of concordism—which seeks direct correspondence between scientific and scriptural claims—nor through the two-magisteria framework, which posits non-overlapping domains of inquiry. Both approaches presuppose what requires demonstration: namely, whether the structural logic underlying a given physical description and a given narrative description are identical, merely similar, or unrelated.

This paper advances a structural claim. Structural claims are stronger than analogical claims. An analogy asserts resemblance; a structural isomorphism asserts shared logical architecture—the same relationships between the same categories of elements, governed by the same operational constraints. Structural isomorphism constrains predictions in both domains simultaneously and is falsifiable in a way that analogical claims are not.

The argument proceeds through three layers of increasing strength:

1. **Direct mapping:** The five operationally definable properties of quantum superposition are mapped onto the pre/post-Fall state transition in Genesis with formal precision.

2. **Universality confirmation:** The same five properties are shown to appear in phase transitions (symmetry breaking)—an independent physical system with no obvious connection to either quantum mechanics or Genesis—constituting a universality claim across three independent domains.

3. **Open problem identification:** The Eve-Adam measurement asymmetry is identified as a genuine gap in current quantum theory, pointing toward undiscovered principles of observer authority.

## §2 Quantum Superposition: Formal Properties

Before the mapping can be evaluated, the formalism must be stated without evasion. A quantum system in superposition occupies multiple eigenstates simultaneously. This is not a statement about observer ignorance—it is not that the particle has a definite position unknown to the observer. Rather, the particle genuinely has no definite position until measurement. The mathematical object representing the system is a wavefunction:

**Quantum Superposition State**

\[
|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle
\]

where \(|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1\) and both eigenstates are simultaneously real until measurement forces resolution. The wavefunction evolves according to the Schrödinger equation—a perfectly smooth, deterministic, reversible evolution. Then measurement occurs, and everything that was smooth and reversible becomes discontinuous and irreversible. The wavefunction "collapses" to a single eigenstate. The probability amplitudes vanish. One outcome becomes actual; the others are gone.

This event has five properties that every serious interpretation of quantum mechanics must accommodate:

1. **Multiple states coexist.** Before measurement, all eigenstates are simultaneously real. The system is not in one state with unknown probability—it is in all states with weighted amplitude.

2. **No definite outcome exists until measurement.** The system evolves deterministically in superposition. Measurement, not passage of time, forces resolution. Without a measurement event, superposition persists indefinitely.

3. **Measurement is irreversible.** Once the wavefunction collapses, it does not spontaneously restore itself. There is no quantum un-measurement. The direction of time imposed by measurement is strict.

4. **Observer choice determines outcome.** The measurement basis—what property one chooses to measure—determines which eigenstates are available as outcomes. Choosing to measure spin-z yields different results than choosing to measure spin-x, even on the same particle.

5. **Post-collapse evolution differs.** The system after collapse evolves from a definite eigenstate, not from a superposition. The subsequent dynamics are qualitatively different from pre-measurement evolution. The system has entered a different regime.

These five properties are not arbitrary. They constitute the minimum description of superposition that any interpretation of quantum mechanics must preserve. Any mapping that captures all five is not an analogy; it is an isomorphism.

## §3 The Five Properties in Eden

Each property is mapped against the pre-Fall state as Genesis describes it. The mapping either holds with precision or fails; there is no credit for vague resemblance.

### 3.1 Property 1: Multiple States Coexist

Eden contains both trees at the center of the garden (Genesis 2:9, English Standard Version). The Tree of Life and the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil occupy the same space simultaneously. The moral state of humanity—obedience and disobedience, life and death, good and evil—coexists as real possibility. Neither has been actualized. The system holds both options as simultaneously present, weighted, real. Adam and Eve move through this garden with both eigenstates available. This is not metaphor for uncertainty; it is the formal structure of superposition: all eigenstates real, none collapsed.

### 3.2 Property 2: No Definite Outcome Until Measurement

The moral superposition persists for an indeterminate period. Genesis does not specify how long Adam and Eve inhabit Eden before the measurement event. Days, years, centuries—the text is silent because the duration is irrelevant to the physics. What matters is that the system does not self-collapse. God does not force the resolution. Time does not force the resolution. The superposition persists until a measurement event occurs. The system remains in the quantum ground state of Creation—coherent, undetermined, full of potential—indefinitely, waiting on the free action of the observers embedded within it.

**Pre-Fall Moral Superposition**

\[
|\Psi_{\text{Eden}}\rangle = \alpha|\text{Obedience}\rangle + \beta|\text{Transgression}\rangle
\]

Both moral eigenstates simultaneously real. No collapse until the federal observer engages the measurement apparatus.

### 3.3 Property 3: Measurement Is Irreversible

After the Fall, there is no return to Eden. This is stated explicitly (Genesis 3:24: cherubim and a flaming sword guard the way to the Tree of Life) and structurally implied throughout the remainder of Scripture. No prophet, no patriarch, no human effort reverses the collapse. The entire redemptive arc of Scripture—from Genesis 3:15 through Revelation 22—is not a reversal of the collapse but a forward trajectory toward a *new* coherent state, built on top of the collapsed one. This is precisely how quantum mechanics operates: one does not un-measure a system; one works with the new eigenstate it occupies.

### 3.4 Property 4: Observer Choice Determines Outcome

The measurement basis is set by Adam's choice of which system to engage. God installs the measurement apparatus (the Tree of Knowledge) and gives explicit instructions concerning it (Genesis 2:16–17). Adam can choose not to engage it. He can choose to eat from every other tree. He can choose to eat from the Tree of Life, which offers a different eigenstate entirely. The eigenstates available as outcomes depend on which apparatus the federal observer chooses to engage. This is not a general statement about free will; it is a specific claim: different measurement operators produce different eigenstates. Adam's choice of apparatus determined the post-collapse configuration of all of reality.

### 3.5 Property 5: Post-Collapse Evolution Differs

Before the Fall: entropy suppressed, direct relational coherence with God, negentropic dynamics, no death. After the Fall: entropy active, relational separation from God, thermodynamic decay, death as a new eigenstate of the human system. The post-collapse universe evolves from a definite, entropically active eigenstate, not from coherent superposition. The entire structure of post-Fall existence—suffering, toil, thorns, death, the arrow of time as experienced—is the evolution of a collapsed eigenstate. Physics as known empirically is post-Fall physics.

### 3.6 Verdict on the Mapping

All five properties map with structural precision. This is a five-for-five formal correspondence between the logical structure of quantum superposition and the logical structure of the pre/post-Fall state transition as Genesis describes it. The question is not whether the mapping holds; the question is what it signifies that it holds.

## §4 Universality Confirmation: Phase Transitions

One cross-domain mapping proves nothing; a clever researcher can identify analogies in any domain. The test of a real structural isomorphism is whether the same five properties appear in an entirely independent physical system that has no obvious connection to either quantum mechanics or Genesis.

Phase transitions provide that test. A system undergoing a phase transition passes from a symmetric, high-potential state to a definite, lower-symmetry state. The canonical example is a ferromagnet cooling through its Curie temperature, but the mathematics applies universally: liquid to solid, paramagnetic to ferromagnetic, unified gauge symmetry to broken symmetry in the early universe. The relevant framework is symmetry breaking:

**Symmetry Breaking Potential**

\[
V(\phi) = -\mu^2\phi^2 + \lambda\phi^4
\]

Above the critical temperature, the system sits at \(\phi = 0\) with full symmetry. Below it, the system must choose one of the degenerate minima at \(\phi = \pm\sqrt{\mu^2/2\lambda}\). The choice is irreversible.

The five superposition properties map onto phase transitions as follows:

| Property | Quantum Superposition | Phase Transition | Eden / Fall |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Multiple states coexist | All eigenstates simultaneously real in \(|\psi\rangle\) | All symmetry-equivalent ground states equally accessible above \(T_c\) | Both trees present; both moral eigenstates available |
| 2. No outcome until event | Superposition persists without measurement | Symmetric state persists above \(T_c\); no ground state selected | Coherence persists until federal observer engages measurement apparatus |
| 3. Irreversibility | Post-collapse state does not spontaneously restore superposition | Once symmetry broken below \(T_c\), system does not spontaneously restore full symmetry | No return to Eden; cherubim guard the way back |
| 4. Observer/environment choice | Measurement basis selects available eigenstates | External field, boundary condition, or fluctuation selects which degenerate minimum is occupied | Adam's choice of apparatus determines which collapse trajectory reality follows |
| 5. Post-event evolution differs | Collapsed system evolves from definite eigenstate, not from superposition | Below \(T_c\), system evolves within broken-symmetry phase with qualitatively different excitations (Goldstone bosons, domain walls) | Post-Fall physics is qualitatively different: entropy, decay, death |

Five for five, across an independent physical system. Phase transitions have no intrinsic connection to Genesis. Symmetry breaking was not designed to describe Eden. Yet the same logical structure appears, with the same five properties, in the same sequential order.

**Universality Claim:** When the same five-property structure appears in quantum superposition, phase transitions, and the Genesis Fall narrative—three independent domains—the structure is not coincidental. It is pointing at something real about how state-transitions operate in any system that transitions from high-symmetry coherence to broken-symmetry definiteness. This is what physicists call *universality*: the phenomenon by which systems with entirely different microscopic constituents exhibit identical macroscopic behavior near phase transitions. The Eden narrative is exhibiting universality with quantum mechanics. That is not a theological claim; it is a physical observation with theological implications.

## §5 Two Trees, Two Physics Regimes

Genesis places two trees at the center of the garden (Genesis 2:9). Most readings treat the Tree of Life as background scenery—a prize forfeited when the Tree of Knowledge is engaged. The physics reading makes a stronger claim: the two trees represent two distinct physical operators acting on the same system simultaneously.

### 5.1 The Tree of Life: Coherence Stabilizer

The Tree of Life does not appear to confer immortality by magic; it appears to maintain the negentropic conditions of the pre-Fall state. Revelation 22:2 describes it in the New Jerusalem as bearing twelve kinds of fruit, with leaves for the healing of the nations—ongoing, renewable, biological coherence maintenance. The tree is not a one-time gift but a continuous process. Eating from it regularly sustains the system against entropic degradation.

In physical terms, the Tree of Life functions as a *coherence stabilizer*: a mechanism by which the system actively counteracts the natural tendency toward disorder. One may conceptualize it as the biological analogue of a laser cavity, which sustains coherent light output by continuously pumping energy against entropy. The pre-Fall system was actively maintained in its coherent state by access to this operator.

God's statement after the Fall reveals the physics: "Now, lest he reach out his hand and take also of the tree of life and eat, and live forever" (Genesis 3:22). The tree was still functional. It could still have sustained the human system in the collapsed eigenstate indefinitely—not restoring pre-Fall coherence, but preventing physical death within the post-Fall regime. God blocks access to it not as punishment but as prevention: a system locked in a fallen eigenstate with indefinitely sustained life would constitute a universe with no exit from collapse. The Redemption arc requires mortality as a structural feature.

### 5.2 The Tree of Knowledge: Measurement Apparatus

The Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil functions as a measurement operator. Engaging it forces the moral superposition into a definite eigenstate. The formal relationship between the two operators may be expressed as:

**Two-Operator System**

\[
\hat{L}|\Psi_{\text{coherent}}\rangle \to |\Psi_{\text{sustained}}\rangle \quad \text{(Tree of Life: coherence maintenance)}
\]
\[
\hat{K}|\Psi_{\text{superposition}}\rangle \to |\Psi_{\text{collapsed}}\rangle \quad \text{(Tree of Knowledge: measurement / collapse)}
\]

The two operators act on different aspects of the same system. \(\hat{L}\) maintains the energy budget against entropic loss. \(\hat{K}\) forces state resolution. Blocking access to \(\hat{L}\) after \(\hat{K}\) has acted is not arbitrary—it is the minimum intervention required to prevent an infinite stable collapsed state.

This two-operator structure has a direct physical analogue: in quantum error correction, one requires both a stabilizer (to maintain coherence) and a syndrome measurement (which when triggered produces irreversible state information). The stabilizer and the syndrome measurement do not commute. Triggering the syndrome measurement while the stabilizer is active changes the system in ways the stabilizer cannot correct. This is precisely what occurred in Genesis: the measurement operator was triggered inside a system actively maintained by a coherence stabilizer, and the coherence stabilizer could not restore what the measurement destroyed.

## §6 The Eve-Adam Measurement Asymmetry

The most technically interesting feature of the Genesis account is the one that receives the least attention: Eve engages the measurement apparatus first, and nothing occurs. Adam engages it second, and everything collapses.

Standard quantum mechanics has no framework for this. There is no hierarchy of observers in current physics. Measurement is measurement. Any physical interaction that extracts which-state information collapses the superposition. There is no such thing as an unauthorized measurement, a partial collapse, or a collapse that awaits confirmation from a second observer.

Yet Genesis records precisely this phenomenon.

### 6.1 The Theological Framework

The New Testament is explicit about the asymmetry. Paul writes with clinical precision across three texts:

> "Therefore, just as sin came into the world through one man, and death through sin, and so death spread to all men because all sinned." (Romans 5:12)

> "For as by the one man's disobedience the many were made sinners, so by the one man's obedience the many will be made righteous." (Romans 5:19)

> "For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ shall all be made alive." (1 Corinthians 15:22)

> "Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and became a transgressor." (1 Timothy 2:14)

The doctrine of *federal headship* holds that Adam served as the representative head of humanity. His choices carry systemic consequences that propagate to all entangled descendants. Eve's transgression is real, morally culpable, and consequential—but it did not trigger the cosmic collapse. Adam's did. Paul does not write this as a cultural artifact; he writes it as structural physics.

### 6.2 Four Candidate Frameworks and Their Insufficiency

This is the open problem at the heart of the paper. The theology is clear. The phenomenon is documented. But the physical mechanism is not yet formalized. Four candidate frameworks are evaluated below:

| Framework | What It Captures | What It Misses | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weak Measurement** | Not all interactions with a system produce full collapse. Weak measurements extract partial information without forcing full eigenstate resolution. | Weak measurement still produces *some* collapse. It does not permit a complete, physically realized interaction (Eve ate) with zero state change. The text demands zero collapse, not weak collapse. | Insufficient |
| **Wigner's Friend** | An internal observer's measurement may appear collapsed from her reference frame while the external observer sees continued superposition. Measurement is relative to observer hierarchy. | Wigner's Friend requires the external observer to be in a position to re-coherently superpose the internal observer's system. It does not establish *why* Adam's measurement, specifically, is the external reference. The asymmetry is asserted, not derived. | Suggestive |
| **Entanglement / Non-locality** | Entangled observers share a joint quantum state. Measurement of one determines the state of both simultaneously. This explains why "the eyes of BOTH of them were opened" after Adam ate. | Explains propagation of collapse after it occurs. Does not explain why Eve's prior interaction failed to trigger the collapse. The asymmetry is in the *triggering*, not the *propagation*. | Partially applicable |
| **Decoherence Threshold** | Perhaps Eve's interaction did not meet some minimum threshold for decoherence to cascade through the system. A system requires sufficient environmental entanglement to fully collapse. | No existing decoherence theory includes an "authority threshold." Decoherence depends on physical coupling strength, not on the identity or role of the observer. The biblical asymmetry is role-based, not energy-based. | Category error |

### 6.3 Informational Fidelity as a Proposed Variable

The gap between all four frameworks points at the same missing concept: *observer authority*. The Eve-Adam asymmetry implies that the universe does not treat all observers equivalently. Some observers are authorized to collapse certain systems; others are not. This is not a feature of any current interpretation of quantum mechanics. It is a prediction that a more complete theory of consciousness-measurement coupling would need to accommodate.

**Definition — Informational Fidelity (\(I_f\)):** A scalar quantity ranging from 0 to 1 that measures the cleanliness of an observer's coupling to the system's original specification. Adam received the prohibition directly from God (\(I_f = 1\)). Eve received it secondhand, then had it actively corrupted by the serpent's noise-injection strategy (\(I_f < 1\)). The proposed mechanism: there exists a threshold \(I_c\) below which a measurement interaction fails to trigger systemic collapse.

The serpent's strategy may be analyzed as a three-step signal degradation process: (1) misquote the specification, (2) contradict the consequences, (3) substitute an alternative measurement basis. Eve was decohered before she touched the fruit.

### 6.4 Empirical Anchoring: The PEAR Laboratory Data

The Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research Laboratory documented 6.35σ deviations in random event generators correlated with operator intent over more than a decade of trials (Jahn et al., 1997; Dunne & Jahn, 2005). The effect is small—approximately one part in ten thousand—but it is real, replicated, and implies that consciousness is not a passive observer of quantum systems. Some minds exert greater measurable influence than others. Observer authority is not a theological fantasy; it is an experimentally anchored anomaly that current physics has no mechanism to explain.

The Eve-Adam asymmetry is the sharpest version of this open problem. Not "does consciousness influence quantum systems?"—that is already established at 6.35σ—but rather: "does the universe recognize a hierarchy of observer authority, and does that hierarchy determine which measurements are physically effective?" If yes, federal headship is not a theological doctrine imposed on physics; it is a theological description of a physical principle whose mechanism has not yet been derived.

## §7 Six Quantum Patterns in Genesis

The superposition mapping and the Eve-Adam asymmetry constitute the load-bearing arguments. However, Genesis exhibits six additional quantum-structural patterns that, taken together, build a convergent case. None of these alone is decisive; all six together constitute a body of evidence.

### 7.1 Pattern 1: Light Before Luminaries (Superposition of Source and Signal)

God creates light on Day One (Genesis 1:3). He creates the sun, moon, and stars on Day Four (Genesis 1:14–19). In current physics, light requires a source. In quantum field theory, photons are excitations of the electromagnetic field—the field is primary, the particles are secondary. Genesis describes light as a field phenomenon prior to any particle-level source, which is precisely the quantum field-theoretic structure. The Logos field (John 1:1–5: "In him was life, and the life was the light of men") is the pre-particle substrate from which photonic excitations arise. Genesis 1 preserves this ordering correctly.

### 7.2 Pattern 2: Naming as State Assignment (Measurement Creates Eigenstates)

Adam names the animals (Genesis 2:19–20), and "whatever the man called every living creature, that was its name." In quantum mechanics, measurement does not merely reveal a pre-existing property—it creates the eigenvalue. The particle does not have a definite spin until one measures it; measurement constitutes the definiteness. Adam's naming is a measurement operation: the act of naming assigns a definite identity-state to each creature. The language is precise: whatever he called it, that *was* its name—not "that described its name" but that constituted it.

### 7.3 Pattern 3: "And There Was"—Collapse Language in the Creation Account

The recurring formula of Genesis 1 is: "God said... and there was." This is not poetry for gradual natural development; it is collapse language—the instantaneous actualization of a potential state by a word that functions as an operator. The Logos speaks, and what was potential becomes actual. This is not Platonic idealism; it is a description of the observer-measurement-collapse structure applied to cosmological creation events. The Logos is the primordial measuring operator whose actions constitute reality.

### 7.4 Pattern 4: Eve from Adam (Non-Local State Derivation)

Eve is derived from Adam's rib (Genesis 2:21–22). Theologically, this establishes the "one flesh" bond (Genesis 2:24). Physically, it establishes quantum entanglement: Eve's state is derived from Adam's state, and they share a joint wavefunction from inception. This is why the collapse propagates non-locally to both observers when Adam measures: they are not two separate systems but one entangled system that appears as two. "The eyes of BOTH of them were opened" is not sequential—it is simultaneous. Non-local state projection across an entangled system.

### 7.5 Pattern 5: The Prohibition as Boundary Condition (Not Moral Preference)

God does not say "I prefer you not eat from the tree." He says "in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die" (Genesis 2:17). This is a boundary condition statement, not a preference expression. In physics, boundary conditions determine which solutions to a differential equation are physically realizable. The prohibition establishes which region of the system's phase space is accessible without triggering collapse. The serpent attacks the boundary condition directly—"you will not surely die"—because destabilizing the boundary condition is the mechanism by which the system is pushed toward the measurement event.

### 7.6 Pattern 6: The Protoevangelium as Built-in Correction Protocol (Genesis 3:15)

"I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring; he shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise his heel." God's first response to the collapse is not the declaration of consequences; it is the announcement of a correction protocol. This appears in Genesis 3:15, *before* the curses. The correction protocol is embedded in the system's response to collapse as a constitutive feature, not an afterthought. In quantum error correction, the error correction code is designed into the system before any errors occur. The protoevangelium is the error correction code; the cross is its execution.

## §8 Falsification Criteria

The following conditions would falsify the central claims of this paper. They are listed in order of difficulty to test.

1. **Any of the five superposition properties fails to map.** If even one of the five properties—coexistence, persistence without measurement, irreversibility, observer-basis dependence, qualitative post-collapse difference—cannot be identified in the Genesis text without interpretive stretching, the superposition isomorphism fails. Partial credit is not awarded.

2. **The phase transition mapping fails on any property.** If phase transitions fail to exhibit any of the five properties in the same structural relation, the universality claim collapses. One counter-example in either direction breaks the universality argument.

3. **A single coherent physical mechanism explains the Eve-Adam asymmetry without appeal to observer hierarchy.** If current quantum mechanics—without modification—can account for a complete physical interaction producing zero wavefunction collapse, followed by a second identical interaction producing total collapse, then the "new physics" claim is unnecessary. The mechanism must account for: (a) Eve ate; (b) no collapse; (c) Adam ate; (d) total simultaneous collapse for both observers.

4. **PEAR-LAB data found to be methodologically compromised.** The 6.35σ result anchors the observer-authority claim. If replication studies with pre-registered protocols and independent auditing fail to reproduce effects distinguishable from chance, the empirical anchor weakens. The paper is not falsified by this alone, but the strongest physical evidence for observer authority is removed.

5. **An alternative cross-domain mapping with equal or greater precision.** If a non-theological framework maps the same five properties onto the Genesis text with the same precision while better accounting for the Eve-Adam asymmetry, parsimony favors the alternative. The mapping should be compared on predictive precision, not on prior commitments.

## §9 Conclusion: The Research Frontier

The argument in this paper has three layers of increasing strength.

**Layer One** is the direct mapping: the five properties of quantum superposition map onto the pre/post-Fall state transition in Genesis with structural precision. This is either a remarkable coincidence or it is pointing at something real. The mapping is not loose; it is five-for-five, each property matching its Genesis analogue without requiring interpretive gymnastics.

**Layer Two** is the universality confirmation: the same five properties appear in phase transitions—an independent physical system with no obvious connection to either quantum mechanics or Genesis. When the same logical structure appears in three independent domains, the universality claim becomes serious. This is not three analogies; it is one structure appearing in three different physical languages.

**Layer Three** is the open problem: the Eve-Adam measurement asymmetry is a genuine physics gap. It is not explained by current theory. It is not a theological assertion; it is an empirical claim about observer hierarchy that, if true, requires new physics. The PEAR-LAB data provides a 6.35σ anchor suggesting the direction this new physics needs to go. The question "does the universe recognize observer authority?" is now a physics question, not a theology question. Genesis forced it to become one.

The Eve-Adam asymmetry demands a physical theory of observer authority—a formal account of why some observers are authorized to collapse certain quantum states while others engaging identical physical interactions are not. This is not a theological question dressed in physics language; it is a prediction about how a more complete theory of consciousness-measurement coupling must behave. PEAR-LAB data provides the empirical anchor; Genesis provides the sharpest known test case. The formalism does not yet exist. Building it is the research frontier this paper opens.

Eden is not a myth decorated with scientific vocabulary. Eden is the coherent quantum ground state of Creation—the state from which post-Fall physics is the shadow. The equations written today are approximations of a prior reality. And the narrative of Scripture is, among other things, the most precise description available of what that reality looked like before the first irreversible measurement event collapsed it.
