## 4. Pair I: Gravity ↔ Grace · Resurrection ↔ Expansion (G, R)

### 4.1 Physics Frame

**Newtonian Gravitation.** The gravitational force between two masses is given by:

$$F = G\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

where $G$ is the gravitational constant ($6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N·m}^2/\text{kg}^2$), $m_1$ and $m_2$ are masses (kg), and $r$ is the separation distance (m). The force is attractive, follows an inverse-square law, and defines an escape velocity:

$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}$$

representing the threshold kinetic energy required to overcome gravitational binding.

**General Relativity.** Einstein's field equations describe gravitation as spacetime curvature:

$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$

where $G_{\mu\nu}$ is the Einstein tensor encoding spacetime curvature, $\Lambda$ is the cosmological constant, $g_{\mu\nu}$ is the metric tensor, $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the stress-energy tensor, $G$ is the gravitational constant, and $c$ is the speed of light. In this formulation, gravity is not a force but a geometry: mass-energy tells spacetime how to curve, and spacetime tells mass-energy how to move along geodesics.

$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2G_s M_{\text{sin}}}{r}}$$

representing the threshold faith-energy required to break free from habitual sin.

**Grace as Moral Landscape Curvature.** Grace is modeled as geometric curvature of the moral landscape:

$$\mathcal{G}_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda_{\text{grace}}\, g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G_s}{c_s^4}\, \mathcal{T}_{\mu\nu}$$

where $\mathcal{G}_{\mu\nu}$ is the moral curvature tensor, $\Lambda_{\text{grace}}$ is the grace constant (analogous to negentropy), $\mathcal{T}_{\mu\nu}$ is the moral-energy tensor, $G_s$ is the spiritual coupling constant, and $c_s$ is the Logos propagation speed. Grace reshapes the moral landscape such that geodesic paths lead toward redemption without requiring force-based resistance.
