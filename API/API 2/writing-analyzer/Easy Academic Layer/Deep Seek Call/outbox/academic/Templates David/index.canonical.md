The central thesis posits that the Fall narrative may be formally represented as a wavefunction collapse, the Edenic state as quantum superposition, and the Crucifixion as a retrocausal repair mechanism. The framework identifies fifty-six structural isomorphisms between quantum mechanics and scriptural narrative, classified by confidence level and accompanied by specified falsification conditions.

## 4. Epistemological Caveats

The authors wish to emphasize that **structural parallel is not identity**. Isomorphism between quantum mechanical and theological structures does not constitute proof of mechanism. Where a mapping is demonstrated, this is explicitly stated. Where identity is proposed, this is also stated and marked. Where the nature of the correspondence—whether identity, causation, or coincidence—remains undetermined, this tension is maintained rather than collapsed for rhetorical convenience.

The framework includes five concrete falsification conditions—observations that would, if made, invalidate the proposed isomorphisms. These conditions are documented in the Full Registry Bridge Registry, which catalogues all fifty-six structural isomorphisms with classification levels, kill conditions, and documentation standards.

The Genesis to Quantum series presents a systematic framework for theophysics that maps quantum mechanical formalisms onto scriptural narrative through a master coherence equation. While the structural isomorphisms identified are substantial, the authors maintain epistemic humility regarding the ontological status of these correspondences.
