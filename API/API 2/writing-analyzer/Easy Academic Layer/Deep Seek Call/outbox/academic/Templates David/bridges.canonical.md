# A Formal Theophysics Analysis: Isomorphic Structures Between Quantum Mechanics, Thermodynamics, and the Genesis Narrative

## Abstract

This article presents a systematic interdisciplinary analysis identifying structural isomorphisms between established principles in quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and information theory, and theological constructs derived from the Genesis narrative and subsequent soteriological frameworks. Fifteen Level-3 isomorphisms (defined as five-property structural mappings with one-to-one correspondence between defining characteristics) are identified and examined. The analysis employs formal mathematical notation, dimensional analysis where applicable, and standard academic citation protocols for both physics and theological sources. The framework posits that the incompatibility between general relativity and quantum mechanics may represent a substrate-level fracture rather than a mathematical incompleteness, and that probability as formalized by the Born rule may constitute a post-Fall epistemic condition rather than a fundamental ontological feature. All claims are presented with explicit kill conditions and falsification criteria.

---

## 1. Introduction: Methodological Framework

The present analysis proceeds from the premise that structural isomorphisms—defined as mappings between two domains preserving relational structure across five or more defining properties—may obtain between physical and theological constructs. This methodology differs from analogical reasoning (which requires fewer than five property matches) and from mere correspondence (which lacks formal mapping criteria). The classification system employed herein designates Level-3 isomorphisms as those satisfying five-property structural mapping, Level-2.5 as strong correspondences (four properties), Level-2 as correspondences (three properties), and Level-1 as analogies (fewer than three properties).

Each isomorphism is presented with explicit kill conditions—empirical or logical criteria whose failure would falsify the claimed mapping—and, where available, statistical confidence measures derived from empirical testing.

### 2.2 Quantum Superposition and the Edenic Moral State (ISO-002)

**Claim:** The formal structure of quantum superposition maps one-to-one onto attested properties of the pre-Fall world as described in Genesis 1-2.

**Formalization:** A quantum superposition state is expressed as \( |\psi\rangle = \sum_i c_i |\phi_i\rangle \), where \( c_i \) are complex probability amplitudes satisfying \( \sum_i |c_i|^2 = 1 \). **Empirical support:** The Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR) laboratory reported consciousness-related effects on physical systems at 6.35σ confidence (Jahn et al., 2007), providing empirical context for the claim that observer-state relationships may differ fundamentally from classical expectations.

**Kill condition GTQ-02:** Demonstration that any of the five properties fails to map would reduce this to analogy (Level 2 or below).
