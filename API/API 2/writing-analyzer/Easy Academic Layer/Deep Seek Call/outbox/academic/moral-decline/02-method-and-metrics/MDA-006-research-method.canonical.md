# Research Methodology for Interdisciplinary Theophysics Inquiry: A Formal Framework

## Abstract

This article delineates a structured research methodology designed for interdisciplinary investigations spanning physics, theology, sociology, and moral philosophy. The methodology, designated the Five-Page System, constitutes a cognitive architecture intended to prevent artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted research from devolving into AI-generated opinion while preserving the epistemic rigor required for cross-domain inquiry. The system operationalizes a deterministic gate—comprising an axiomatic foundation and a seven-question (7Q) evaluative framework—through which all claims must pass prior to acceptance into the proof layer. This article constitutes the methodological foundation for a broader research program investigating the observed decline in moral indicators within American society from the early twentieth century to the present day.

## 1. Introduction and Research Orientation

### 1.1 The Research Question

The central research question driving this investigative program is as follows: *Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to present day?* This question, by its nature, resists disciplinary confinement. It implicates sociology, theology, economics, family structure, institutional trust, and philosophy. A methodological framework capable of sustaining such complexity without collapsing it into disciplinary reductionism is therefore required.

### 1.2 Epistemological Positioning

The present author (D. Lowe) acknowledges a personal conviction that contemporary Western societies—particularly the United States—are approaching economic, social, and institutional collapse. This conviction is not presented as evidence but as an orienting intuition that motivates the inquiry. The methodology described herein is designed to subject such intuitions to rigorous falsification rather than confirmation. As the author states: "This is not a commitment to having the answers. It is a starting point for asking the right questions."

## 2. Methodological Architecture: The Five-Page System

Each inquiry within this research program employs five distinct documents, each serving a specific cognitive function. These documents are not optional embellishments but constitute the structural integrity of the method.

### 2.1 Inquiry Seed

**File:** `Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md`

**Function:** The Inquiry Seed captures the research question with precision and records the researcher's initial intuition or belief prior to any AI-mediated input. This pre-AI framing is epistemologically load-bearing: allowing AI to frame the question constitutes a surrender of authorial and analytical control. The seed thus functions as a fixed reference point against which subsequent AI contributions can be evaluated for bias, distortion, or framing shifts.

### 2.2 AI Prompt Engine

**File:** `AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md`

**Function:** This document records the exact prompts submitted to AI models, the models consulted, and the raw outputs received—without evaluative judgment at this stage. The AI output is treated as terrain mapping: it surfaces competing interpretations, key authors, major disciplinary fault lines, and suggested search domains. The Prompt Engine does not adjudicate truth claims; it maps the intellectual landscape.

### 2.3 Research Workspace

**File:** `Research_Workspace_[Question].md`

**Function:** The Research Workspace is the living document where primary-source evidence accumulates. It contains data links, study citations, statistics with dates and sourcing, and annotations from primary literature. This is the site where AI-generated suggestions undergo stress-testing against primary sources. Not all AI claims survive contact with the primary literature; the Workspace documents this collision.

### 2.4 Contradictions and Resolution

**File:** `Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md`

**Function:** This is the most epistemically critical component of the system. It forces the researcher to identify and sit with points of disagreement: where sources contradict one another, where AI output diverges from primary literature, and where the researcher's intuition conflicts with the data. The method explicitly prohibits premature resolution. As the author states: "Truth is most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily."

### 2.5 Quotes, Domains, and Links

**File:** `Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md`

**Function:** This cumulative reference library grows across all inquiries. It contains key quotes with attribution and date, domain categories surfaced during research, and external links with contextual annotations. This document constitutes the raw material from which publications are subsequently drawn.

## 3. The Role of Artificial Intelligence in the Research Process

### 3.1 Permitted Functions

AI is employed within this methodology for the following purposes:

1. **Rapid intellectual terrain mapping** across multiple disciplines simultaneously
2. **Surface identification of contradictions** between competing interpretive frameworks
3. **Side-by-side organization** of competing frameworks for comparative analysis
4. **Suggestion generation** for deeper primary-source investigation
5. **Stress-testing** of preliminary conclusions against counterarguments

### 3.2 Prohibited Functions

AI is explicitly not employed for the following purposes:

1. **Truth adjudication**—AI does not decide what is true
2. **Primary-source verification**—AI does not replace direct engagement with primary literature
3. **Question framing**—the researcher frames the initial question before AI input
4. **Evidence selection**—AI does not determine which evidence counts as significant
5. **Contradiction resolution**—human judgment resolves contradictions

### 3.3 Error as Signal

The methodology treats AI error not as failure but as signal. Incomplete or incorrect AI outputs indicate where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested, where consensus may have been manufactured rather than earned, and where deeper primary-source investigation is required.

## 4. The Proof Pressure Layer and Deterministic Gate

### 4.1 Gate Structure

Claims within this research program must pass through a deterministic gate comprising two components:

1. **The Axiomatic Foundation:** A set of axioms derived from the foundational principles of theophysics, against which all claims are evaluated for internal consistency
2. **The 7Q Evaluative Framework:** A seven-question protocol designed to assess claim strength across multiple epistemic dimensions

### 4.2 Current Status

As of the present article (MDA-006), no claim from this article has survived the deterministic Axiom + 7Q gate with sufficient strength for inclusion in the proof layer. This is not a failure of the method but an expected outcome of rigorous gatekeeping. Claims that fail to survive the gate are retained for further investigation rather than discarded, as their failure provides information about the structure of the contested intellectual terrain.

## 5. Workflow Sequence

The prescribed workflow for each inquiry is as follows:

1. **Initiate the Inquiry Seed:** Write the research question and initial intuition before running any AI prompt
2. **Execute the AI Prompt Engine:** Run prompts, collect outputs without judgment
3. **Populate the Research Workspace:** Follow AI leads into primary sources, cite everything, record what the data actually states
4. **Construct the Contradictions Page:** Force disagreements into the open, resist premature resolution
5. **Maintain the Quotes, Domains, and Links Library:** Add to the cumulative reference library with each session

This five-page structure is replicated for each new research question. It is not bureaucratic overhead but the cognitive architecture that prevents AI-assisted research from collapsing into AI-generated opinion.

## 6. Methodological Justification

The method described herein is predicated on the observation that truth is rarely found where consensus is easiest. As the author states: "Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees. It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable." The Five-Page System operationalizes this principle by forcing the researcher to encounter, document, and sit with contradiction before reaching for resolution.

The method further recognizes that interdisciplinary inquiry—particularly at the intersection of physics and theology—requires a cognitive architecture capable of holding complexity without collapsing it. The Five-Page System provides this architecture by distributing cognitive functions across distinct documents, each with a clearly defined epistemic role.

## 7. Conclusion

This article has presented the methodological framework for a research program investigating the observed decline in moral indicators within American society. The Five-Page System, combined with the deterministic Axiom + 7Q gate, constitutes a rigorous approach to AI-assisted interdisciplinary research that preserves human judgment as the final arbiter of truth claims. The method treats AI as a collaborator in intellectual terrain mapping rather than an authority or conclusion machine, and it treats error as signal rather than failure.

The research exists because the investigator felt the weight of the question and determined that feeling alone was insufficient. The method compels the investigator to actually look, to go where the data is uncomfortable, and to let contradictions speak before reaching for resolution.
