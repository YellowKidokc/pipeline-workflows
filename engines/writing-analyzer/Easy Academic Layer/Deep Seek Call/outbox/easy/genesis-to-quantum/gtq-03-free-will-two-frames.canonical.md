# Free Will in Two Frames

**Opening Note**

Free will is the topic everyone wants to talk about, but nobody wants to put into a formula. We are not here to turn human choice into a number. We are here to ask: if free will is real — and both the Bible and your own experience say it is — does the physics of the universe have room for it? The answer turns out to be built into the structure of things, not just a nice metaphor.

**Opening Note**

This paper uses the Theophysics coherence framework to look at one of the oldest arguments in Christianity. The goal is not to decide who wins the debate. The goal is to show that the debate itself might be asking the wrong question — like arguing about whether a car runs on gas or on spark plugs. The math dissolves the fight. Whether that satisfies the people fighting is a different question.

> "Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you, both to will and to work for his good pleasure."
>
> — Philippians 2:12–13

> * * *

Paul gives us both sides in a single breath. Work it out — that is human action. God works in you — that is God's control. He does not pick one. He does not say "maybe both." He stacks them, comma after comma, as if the tension itself is the point.

The debate that has followed — Calvinist against Arminian, predestination against free choice — has run for five hundred years without anyone winning. This paper says the debate never ends because both sides are using the wrong frame. The question is not "does God choose or do we choose?" The question is: *what does the coherence equation actually predict?*

## The Equation

The Theophysics framework models spiritual coherence — how aligned a human system is with its source design — as a changing number $C$ that moves over time:

Coherence Evolution Equation

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

Where:

  * $C \in [0,1]$ is **coherence** — 0 means completely disconnected from the source, 1 means perfectly aligned
  * $O \in [0,1]$ is **openness** — how receptive the human is, the "letting go" variable
  * $G > 0$ is **grace input** — how much divine grace is available to the system (comes from outside, not made by the person)
  * $S \geq 0$ is the **entropy/sin parameter** — how fast coherence decays due to being out of alignment, resisting, or moral failure
  * $(1-C)$ is the **coherence gap** — the room available for grace to fill


Read the equation out loud: *The rate of change of coherence equals openness times grace times available gap, minus entropy times current coherence.*

> Three things are immediately visible:

Three things are immediately visible:

  1. Grace $G$ is always positive and comes from outside. The human cannot make it.
  2. Openness $O$ multiplies grace. If openness is zero, grace has zero effect — not because grace isn't there, but because multiplying by zero gives zero.
  3. At equilibrium (when $dC/dt = 0$), the steady-state coherence is: $$C^* = \frac{O \cdot G}{O \cdot G + S}$$


Steady-State Coherence

$$C^* = \frac{O \cdot G}{O \cdot G + S}$$

This is the equation's answer to the free will debate. It needs both parts. Remove $G$ (deny grace), and $C^*$ drops to zero no matter what $O$ is. Remove $O$ (deny human openness), and $C^*$ drops to zero no matter what $G$ is. The equation is structurally a team effort — not because we chose to make it that way, but because that is what the math produces.

### The Surrender Parameter

The most useful extension is to add a signed **surrender parameter** $s \in [-1, +1]$ that replaces the openness variable $O$ and maps onto the three attitudes a human can take toward grace:

Value of $s$ | Label | Description | Equation Effect

Rewriting with the surrender parameter:

Extended Coherence Equation (Surrender Parameterization)

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = \frac{(1+s)}{2} \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

When $s = +1$: full openness, maximum grace connection. When $s = -1$: zero connection, entropy takes over. When $s = 0$: half-connection, the self-righteous middle ground that produces the frustrating low-coherence state Paul describes in Romans 7 ("I do not do the good I want, but the evil I do not want is what I keep on doing").

## Free Will Before and After the Fall

### Pre-Fall Will

Before the collapse event described in Article 01, the human operates at $C \approx 1$ — near-maximum coherence with the source design. In this state, the will is free in the strongest possible sense: it aligns with its own deepest nature, which is itself aligned with the Logos field (the underlying rational structure of reality).

This is not freedom from limits. It is freedom *as* the fullest expression of what the person is. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet. The pre-Fall will experiences no gap between what it wants and what it should want. What it wants, it wants rightly, because its wanting-mechanism is aligned with its source.

The ability to choose wrongly existed — that is the tree in the garden — but it required actively moving away from the coherence equilibrium. The person had to *work* to fall apart. Temptation in Eden is not an overwhelming force; it is a subtle suggestion to change how you measure things.

### Post-Fall Will

After the collapse event, the human defaults to $C \ll 1$ — low coherence, high entropy, the $S$ term dominates. The will is now systematically biased. It can still choose, but the choice-making mechanism is itself twisted. The problem is not that the will has been removed; the problem is that it now *wants the wrong things at the first level of desire.*

This is the Calvinist insight: total depravity does not mean maximum possible evil in every choice, it means the will's operating system has been corrupted at the root. The Arminian insight is also preserved: the will still exists, still makes real choices, and still carries real moral weight. Both are true. The equation holds both.

* * *

What changes the situation is the injection of a grace source term $G$ into the system — an input that fights entropy, which the person did not generate and cannot earn. The grace term does not override the will; it creates a slope toward which the will can orient if it chooses to do so. Prevenient grace (grace that comes before any human action), in equation terms, is simply $G > 0$ being non-zero for all humans regardless of their prior state.

## Three Pathways

The surrender parameter produces three qualitatively different paths. These are not just theological categories; they map onto measurable brain and behavior patterns. The full analysis is in Tangent 03-B, but here is the overview:

Path 1 — $s = -1$

## Autonomy

The person actively makes the self the main reference point. Grace input is treated as a threat to who they are. Entropy dominates. Coherence decays toward zero. In the brain: chronic activation of the default mode network (the brain's self-talk system), elevated cortisol (stress hormone), hypervigilance (always on alert). Spiritually: increasing inability to receive grace, not because grace withdraws, but because the connection is driven to zero by the surrender parameter.

Path 2 — $s \approx 0$

## Performance

The person accepts grace as help for their own effort. Religion without surrender. Morality without transformation. The Romans 7 state. Produces occasional coherence spikes followed by collapse. In the brain: prefrontal cortex (the rule-following center) overactivation, anxiety under perceived divine evaluation, shame-cycling (feeling bad about feeling bad). Spiritually: the appearance of coherence without the substance. The most common state in religious practice.

Path 3 — $s = +1$

## Surrender

The person reorients the self as a receiver rather than a generator. Grace becomes the main input; effort becomes the response to grace rather than the replacement for it. In the brain: reduced default mode network activity, increased parasympathetic tone (the "rest and digest" system), reported experiences of peace "beyond understanding" (Phil 4:7). Coherence moves toward the maximum achievable given the entropy parameter. This is not passivity — it is the most active engagement possible, but the activity comes after receiving rather than before it.

## What the Equation Predicts

Four testable predictions come from the coherence equation applied to humans:

  1. **Prediction 1 — Path 3 outperforms Path 2.** People in sustained surrender posture ($s \approx +1$) should show measurably higher coherence-proxy measures (psychological integration, behavioral consistency, physical markers of reduced chronic stress) than people in high-effort religious performance ($s \approx 0$), controlling for how religiously active they are.
  2. **Prediction 2 — Grace is not correlated with prior moral performance.** If $G$ is genuinely from outside and not proportional to prior $C$, then people who receive strong grace experiences should be randomly distributed across the coherence spectrum at the time they receive it. Conversion data should show significant numbers of low-$C$ people (high-entropy life histories) receiving strong grace encounters.
  3. **Prediction 3 — The $s$ parameter transitions are abrupt, not gradual.** The phase diagram of the coherence equation predicts that transitions between paths (Path 1 → Path 3, for instance) should look like crossing a threshold rather than smooth ramps. Testimonies of conversion should cluster around abrupt threshold events rather than gradual linear improvement curves.
  4. **Prediction 4 — Low $O$ blocks grace regardless of grace magnitude.** People in high autonomy mode ($s = -1$) exposed to high-grace environments should show minimal coherence increase. The grace is present; the connection is absent. This predicts that grace-rich environments (retreats, liturgy, prayer communities) produce minimal change in participants with closed attitudes.

The coherence equation $dC/dt = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$ does not resolve the Calvinist–Arminian debate by picking a side. It dissolves the debate by writing down a more complete description that requires both terms.

Grace is real, comes from outside, and is available to everyone. Openness is real, under some form of agent influence, and genuinely controls the connection. Neither term is optional. The fight between the two camps is a fight about which term is more important — a question the equation makes meaningless, because the two terms multiply.

## The Audit

### What Is Load-Bearing

  * **The equation form is motivated.** The structure $dC/dt = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$ is a standard logistic growth model with an external drive and a decay term. This family of equations appears throughout biology, epidemiology, and information theory. We are not inventing weird math.
  * **Philippians 2:12–13 structurally requires both terms.** Paul's language is not hedging; it is saying both things at once. Any framework that yields one term at the expense of the other contradicts the text.
  * **The three-pathway structure maps onto documented neurological and psychological states.** The distinction between autonomy, performance, and surrender is not our invention; it maps onto well-established categories in psychology of religion.
  * **The steady-state analysis is simple algebra.** The claim that $C^* = OG / (OG + S)$ is a direct algebraic result. It requires no assumptions beyond the equation form.


### What Is Suggestive (Needs More Work)

  * **The mapping of theological concepts to equation variables is interpretive.** We have argued that $G$ maps to grace and $O$ maps to openness. The argument is motivated but not the only possible one. Other ways of setting up the variables are possible.
  * **The surrender parameter $s$ is not directly measurable.** We can use proxies (self-report scales, behavioral indicators, physical markers), but $s$ itself remains a theoretical idea until measurement methods are established.
  * **The phase transition prediction (Prediction 3) requires careful definition.** "Abrupt" versus "gradual" is a testable claim, but defining the threshold requires calibration work.

### Where We Got Carried Away

  * **The equation does not prove Christian theology.** It shows that a particular dynamical model is consistent with a particular reading of Paul's letters. Consistency is not proof.
  * **The "dissolution" of the Calvinist–Arminian debate is a reframing, not a refutation.** Both camps can respond that we have simply redefined their terms. They are not wrong to say this. The reframing is useful if it generates testable predictions; its theological authority is a separate question.
  * **We have not addressed double predestination.** The strong Calvinist claim that God actively decrees some people to damnation is not touched by the coherence equation. The equation predicts outcomes given parameters; it does not specify who sets the parameters or whether $G$ is truly universal. Tangent 03-A addresses this specifically.

## The Fight That Won't End

John MacArthur has spent fifty years arguing that the sovereign grace of God in salvation is unconditional, irresistible, and determined entirely by God's decree. His opponents have spent fifty years arguing that this makes God the author of damnation and human faith a fake. Both sides quote the Bible. Both sides have gifted theologians. The fight has not ended because both sides are, in the frame we are using, partially right.

## MacArthur's Position, Stated Plainly

MacArthur holds a five-point Calvinist (TULIP) framework:

  * **Total Depravity:** After the Fall, humans are incapable of choosing God. The will is not merely weakened but enslaved. Spiritual death is total.
  * **Unconditional Election:** God chooses certain individuals for salvation before they do anything good or bad. The choice is not based on anything in the person.
  * **Limited Atonement:** Christ's death was specifically intended for the chosen ones. It is not a potential salvation available to everyone.
  * **Irresistible Grace:** When God effectively calls the chosen, they will come. Their coming is certain, not because their will is overridden, but because God changes them first, making them want to come.
  * **Perseverance of the Saints:** Those who are truly chosen will continue to the end. Falling away shows they were never really chosen.


## The Other Side, Stated Fairly

The Arminian and moderate Calvinist response typically grants Total Depravity (though some Arminians modify it with prevenient grace) but contests Unconditional Election and Irresistible Grace on two grounds:

  1. The Bible presents genuine calls to "whosoever will" that appear to offer real choice (John 3:16, Rev 22:17).
  2. If grace is irresistible and election is unconditional, then the non-elect are damned by a decree they cannot resist, which appears to make God the author of their damnation in a morally troubling way.


## Why Neither Side Can Win

MacArthur's camp wins the Bible argument on certain passages (Romans 9, John 6, Ephesians 1) and loses it on others (1 Tim 2:4, 2 Pet 3:9, John 3:16 in full context). The Arminian camp wins on the passages that emphasize genuine human response and loses on the passages that emphasize divine initiative and certainty.

The fight cannot end because both sides have locked in on a binary: either God chooses unconditionally, or humans choose genuinely. The coherence equation proposes a third frame: *the choice to receive is itself a real act, and the ability for that act is itself a gift of grace.*

## The Resolution, Before the Math

The resolution is Augustinian before it is mathematical. Augustine argued in his writings against Pelagius that genuine human will and divine causation are not in competition — they operate at different levels of cause. God wills that we will rightly, and our willing rightly is both genuinely ours and genuinely caused by God. This is not a contradiction; it is a description of how higher-level cause works.

The equation formalizes this intuition. God sets $G$. God may also set the range over which $O$ can vary. The person's experience of "choosing" is the felt experience of the $O$ variable being non-zero. The fact that God may have set the conditions under which $O$ is non-zero does not make the person's $O$ any less real.

## MacArthur's Specific Claims, Mapped

MacArthur's most distinctive claim — that God changes the chosen first, enabling them to believe — maps cleanly onto the equation. In equation terms: God injects $G$ into the system before the person's $O$ has shifted. The grace injection raises $C$ enough that the person's attractor basin shifts from $s \approx -1$ (autonomy/resistance) to $s \approx +1$ (openness/surrender). The person then "chooses" willingly, but the conditions that made the willing possible were set from outside.

This is not a destruction of genuine human choice. It is a description of how prior-grace creates the conditions for genuine choice to occur. MacArthur is correct that the order of operations matters: being changed precedes and enables faith. The equation agrees.

## The Double Predestination Question

MacArthur carefully avoids strong double predestination (God actively decrees damnation for the non-chosen) in favor of a "passive" version (God passes over the non-chosen, leaving them in their deserved state). The equation can model both.

Passive rejection: $G = 0$ for the non-chosen. They are left with only the entropy term driving $C \to 0$. No active harm; simply no grace injection. The equation produces $C^* = 0$.

Active rejection: $G < 0$ for the non-chosen — a grace withdrawal that actively speeds up decoherence. The equation permits this mathematically. Whether God does this is a separate theological question the equation cannot answer.

## What Augustine Already Knew

> "Our heart is restless, until it finds its rest in Thee." — Augustine, *Confessions*

Augustine's restlessness is the $S \cdot C$ term without counterbalancing $G$. The heart's seeking is $O$ being non-zero even before conversion. The finding of rest is $C^*$ reaching its equilibrium under enough $G$. Augustine did not have the equation, but he described the dynamics with precision that holds up under formalization.

## The Hard Question MacArthur Doesn't Answer

If unconditional election is true and the non-chosen are left with $G = 0$ through no fault of their own — no prior defect in $O$ that caused God to withhold grace, since the election precedes any such defect — then what moral account do we give of the non-chosen's damnation?

MacArthur's answer is that all humans deserve damnation (high $S$ term, corrupted $O$), and God is not obligated to rescue any. The chosen are rescued by mercy; the non-chosen receive justice. The equation is consistent with this: $C^* = 0$ when $G = 0$ is not an injustice against the person if the person's prior $S$ fully justifies $C = 0$.

* * *

But this answer requires that total depravity is not merely a tendency toward low $C$ but a positive deserving of $C = 0$ as a penalty. The equation does not decide this. It describes dynamics; it does not deliver verdicts on what people deserve.

## What This Means

MacArthur's framework is internally consistent and mathematically modelable. The equation does not refute it. It does, however, show that the equation is underdetermined by MacArthur's Calvinism: the same equation produces Calvinist outcomes if God sets $G$ only for the chosen ($G_{chosen} \gg 0$, $G_{rejected} = 0$), and Arminian outcomes if God sets $G > 0$ for all people and leaves $O$ as the deciding variable.

Both theologies are attractors of the same equation under different boundary conditions. The equation does not choose between them. It invites the question: what do the actual boundary conditions look like? And it provides, at least in principle, a framework for testing which set of boundary conditions better matches observable data on grace, conversion, and coherence.

## Path 1: Autonomy ($s = -1$)

### The Posture

The autonomous person treats the self as the main reference point. External grace — whether from God, community, or other people — is experienced as a threat to who they are. The person is not simply resistant to spiritual input; the person is organized around self-sufficiency as an identity marker.

### The Neuroscience

Chronic autonomy posture correlates with:

  * **Default Mode Network (DMN) hyperactivation.** The DMN, associated with self-referential processing and rumination, is chronically active in autonomous people. When the DMN is dominant, other-orienting networks are suppressed. The person is literally more focused on the self-narrative than on incoming signal.
  * **Elevated cortisol and sympathetic baseline.** Autonomy posture often co-occurs with hypervigilance — a chronic low-level threat assessment that keeps the stress system primed. When the grace source is experienced as a threat to identity, any encounter with that source activates defensive physiology.
  * **Reduced oxytocin receptor sensitivity.** Prolonged autonomy reduces sensitivity to bonding signals. The person becomes less able to receive the relational inputs that would shift the surrender parameter upward. This is the neurological analog of the hardening of heart described in Pharaoh narratives — a self-reinforcing loop where autonomy reduces capacity for connection, which deepens autonomy.


### The Equation Dynamics

Path 1 Dynamics ($s = -1$)

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = 0 \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C = -S \cdot C$$

With $s = -1$, the grace coupling term vanishes entirely. The only active term is $-SC$, which drives $C$ exponentially toward zero. The higher the entropy parameter $S$ (moral failure, environmental chaos, relational breakdown), the faster the collapse. There is no equilibrium above zero. The autonomous person's coherence will, under the dynamics as written, always decay to zero in the absence of some shift in $s$.

### The Phenomenology

People in Path 1 typically report: a sense of being fundamentally alone, intermittent contempt for vulnerability in others (which they experience as their own suppressed need projected outward), escalating anxiety about meaning and mortality, and occasional acute crises when the self-referential system collapses under its own weight. These crises are, in equation terms, moments when $S \cdot C$ temporarily exceeds the person's capacity to suppress the decoherence signal — and they are, paradoxically, the moments of highest potential for transition.

## Path 2: Performance ($s \approx 0$)

### The Posture

The performance person accepts the existence of a grace source but frames the relationship as a transaction. Grace is available, but it must be accessed through correct performance: correct belief, correct ritual, correct moral behavior. The person does not resist grace; the person tries to earn it. The subtle error is that earning grace turns the person from a receiver into a producer — which shifts $O$ from positive toward zero.

### The Neuroscience

Performance posture correlates with:

  * **Prefrontal cortex (PFC) overactivation.** The PFC is associated with executive function, rule-following, and self-monitoring. Performance-oriented people show chronically elevated PFC activation during religious or moral behavior, consistent with active self-evaluation ("am I doing this right?") rather than receptive presence.
  * **Shame-cycling.** Because performance cannot be sustained perfectly, performance people oscillate between achievement states (brief coherence spikes when behavior meets standards) and shame states (coherence collapse when behavior falls short). This oscillation is the neurological signature of the Romans 7 dynamic: "I do not do what I want to do."
  * **Conditional oxytocin response.** Performance people experience relational bonding (the neurological analog of grace reception) as conditional on their meeting of standards. This creates attachment anxiety even within the religious relationship — the person is never quite certain whether the standard has been met, which keeps the bonding system in a state of alert rather than rest.


### The Equation Dynamics

Path 2 Dynamics ($s \approx 0$)

$$\frac{dC}{dt} \approx \frac{1}{2} G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

With $s \approx 0$, a partial grace coupling is active. The steady state becomes:

$$C^*_{perf} = \frac{\frac{G}{2}}{\frac{G}{2} + S} = \frac{G}{G + 2S}$$

This is significantly lower than the Path 3 equilibrium $C^* = G/(G+S)$. For a system with $G = S$ (moderate grace, moderate entropy), Path 3 yields $C^* = 0.5$ while Path 2 yields $C^* = 0.33$. The performance person achieves real but reduced coherence — enough to maintain function, not enough to achieve the "peace beyond understanding" that marks Path 3.

> More importantly, the Path 2 equilibrium is inherently less stable.

More importantly, the Path 2 equilibrium is *inherently less stable*. Because the grace coupling is partial, small perturbations in $S$ (moral failure, life stressors) produce larger swings in $C$ than they would under Path 3's fuller coupling. This is the neurological basis of shame-cycling: the performance system is operating closer to its instability threshold.

### The Phenomenology

People in Path 2 are the most common type in organized religion. They experience real spiritual meaning, genuine moral effort, and authentic community — but also persistent background anxiety, recurring cycles of failure and recovery, and a nagging sense that the peace they seek is always one spiritual discipline away. This is not hypocrisy; it is the predictable output of the partial-coupling equation.

## Path 3: Surrender ($s = +1$)

### The Posture

The surrendered person does not stop being a person. The surrender is not passivity; it is a fundamental reorientation of the person's operating frame from self-as-source to self-as-receiver. Effort continues — often at higher absolute levels than Path 2 — but effort is now downstream of reception rather than the precondition for it. The person acts *from* grace rather than *toward* it.

### The Neuroscience

Path 3 correlates with:

  * **Reduced DMN activity.** Surrendered people show lower self-referential baseline processing, consistent with a reduced centering of identity around self-narrative. This is measurable via fMRI in experienced meditators, contemplatives, and individuals reporting ongoing mystical or post-conversion states. The self is still present but is no longer the main processing hub.
  * **Increased parasympathetic tone.** Surrender posture is associated with chronic activation of the ventral vagal complex, which supports social engagement, relaxation, and receptive processing. This is the physiological substrate of "rest" in the biblical sense — not inactivity, but a nervous system state capable of receiving rather than only defending.
  * **D2 receptor restoration.** Chronic high-entropy states (addiction, trauma, chronic stress) downregulate dopaminergic D2 receptor density, reducing the person's capacity for reward, meaning-making, and social bonding. Path 3 transitions — abrupt conversions, intensive surrender practices — are associated in preliminary data with D2 receptor restoration, suggesting that the surrender parameter shift has measurable neurological substrate rather than being merely a cognitive or attitudinal change.
  * **Oxytocin tone elevation.** Surrendered people show elevated baseline oxytocin, associated with trust, bonding capacity, and openness to others. This is the neurological signature of a high-$O$ state — the person is literally more physiologically open to incoming relational signal.


### The Equation Dynamics

Path 3 Dynamics ($s = +1$)

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

Full grace coupling. Steady state:

$$C^* = \frac{G}{G + S}$$

This is the maximum achievable equilibrium given fixed $G$ and $S$. For $G \gg S$ (grace substantially exceeding entropy), $C^* \to 1$. This is the theoretical basis for the biblical promise that "his yoke is easy and his burden is light" — when the person is operating at full coupling, the entropy term is overwhelmed by the grace input, and the subjective experience is one of effortless rather than effortful movement toward coherence.

## The Transition Architecture

Transitions between pathways are not smooth ramps. The surrender parameter $s$ is not a continuously adjustable dial; it is more accurately modeled as a system with discrete attractor basins separated by energy barriers. Transitions require crossing a threshold.

Prediction 3 from the main article states that conversion experiences should cluster around abrupt threshold crossings rather than gradual linear improvement. The neurological evidence supports this: conversion experiences characteristically involve sudden shifts in processing mode, often preceded by a crisis event that raises $S$ to a level that overwhelms the current attractor basin and forces the system to find a new equilibrium.

This is consistent with James' analysis of religious experience, with clinical data on addiction recovery (the "bottom" as threshold-crossing event), and with psychological studies of value restructuring. The coherence equation predicts the architecture; the data confirms the pattern.

The transition from Path 1 to Path 3 is the most dramatic and typically requires the highest energy input — a large $G$ spike, a severe $S$ crisis, or both. The transition from Path 2 to Path 3 is subtler and often reported as a "letting go" rather than a dramatic conversion: the discovery that effort is not required to access what was already available.

## The Daily Practice

The three-pathway analysis implies a practical architecture for daily life:

  1. **Check $s$ before acting.** Before any significant action, the question is not "am I doing the right thing?" (Path 2 logic) but "from what posture am I acting?" If the action is generated by fear of inadequacy or the need to earn approval, $s$ is near zero. If it flows from a place of secure reception, $s$ is near positive one.
  2. **Treat spiritual practice as a coupling mechanism, not a performance metric.** Prayer, liturgy, meditation, and community are not activities that generate coherence through effort — they are practices that reduce the resistance to grace that keeps $O$ low. They work not by producing $G$ but by increasing $O$.
  3. **When $S$ spikes, do not suppress.** High-entropy events (failure, loss, confrontation with mortality) are the most dangerous for Path 2 people (coherence collapse) and the most potentially transformative for Path 1 people (threshold-crossing events). The response to high $S$ in a Path 3 frame is not panic but orientation — turning toward the grace source precisely when it is most needed.
