## I. The Equation

The coherence dynamics of any conscious agent coupled to the Logos Field (the divine information structure that orders reality) are governed by:

**The Coherence Equation**

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

- **$C$** = coherence (alignment with Logos, range $0 \to 1$)
- **$O$** = openness (the human variable — reception, surrender, willingness)
- **$G$** = grace (the divine variable — negentropic input from God)
- **$S$** = entropy/sin (the decay pressure, pulling toward disorder)
- **$(1-C)$** = room to grow (the closer to perfect coherence, the less room remains)

This equation means: The rate of change in coherence over time equals openness times grace times the remaining room to grow, minus entropy times current coherence.

Look at the structure. Two terms fighting.

The first term, $O \cdot G(1-C)$, is the _growth_ term. It increases coherence. It requires _both_ $O$ and $G$ multiplied together. Not added — multiplied. This is why the most faithful people face the fiercest resistance. The drag is proportional to what you've built.

Now watch what happens when you remove variables.
