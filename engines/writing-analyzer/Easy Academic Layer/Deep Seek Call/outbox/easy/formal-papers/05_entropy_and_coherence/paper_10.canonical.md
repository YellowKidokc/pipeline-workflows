## 2. The Physics of Synchronization

In physics, synchronization happens when separate oscillators (things that swing back and forth, like pendulums or clocks) start moving together because of a coupling force (a force that links them). The **Kuramoto Model** describes this phase transition (a sudden change from one state to another): when the coupling strength passes a critical threshold (K_c), the system snaps from chaos into collective coherence (everyone moving as one). The order parameter (r) measures how synchronized the system is. It ranges from 0 (total chaos) to 1 (full coherence, everything in perfect unison).

## 3. The One-Directional Mapping: Christ

As stated in _Colossians 1:17_ : _"He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together."_ Christ is the Master Integral—the product of all variables in their aligned state.

Think of it like a tuning fork. When you strike it near other objects, they start vibrating at the same frequency. Christ is one source of perfect pitch that brings everything else into harmony.

The framework ends by recognizing two absolute attractors:
