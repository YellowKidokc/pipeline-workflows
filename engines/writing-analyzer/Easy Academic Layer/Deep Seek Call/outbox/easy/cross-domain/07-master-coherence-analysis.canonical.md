# Section V: Recommendations

#### Strengthen Joy Across All Projects

Joy is negative or zero in 7 out of 8 projects. This means the corpus is good at finding problems but bad at amplifying what works.

**Action:** Add explicit celebration of what works, success stories, and regenerative patterns.

#### Develop Hope Architecture

Hope averages near-zero, meaning failure states are often presented as the end of the road.

**Action:** Every problem diagnosis should include at least one recovery pathway, even if that pathway requires outside help (Grace).

#### Increase Patience in Applied Content

Theological Engineering and Layer 4 Applications show negative Patience—forced conclusions.

**Action:** Allow conclusions to emerge step by step. Add stepping stones between premises and conclusions.

#### Expand Theological Engineering

The lowest-scoring project (49.4) needs the most work. The Grace score is good (0.667), but the framework needs more step-by-step development and non-terminal failure states.

**Action:** Develop intermediate proof stages and allow theological conclusions to build incrementally rather than arriving fully formed.
