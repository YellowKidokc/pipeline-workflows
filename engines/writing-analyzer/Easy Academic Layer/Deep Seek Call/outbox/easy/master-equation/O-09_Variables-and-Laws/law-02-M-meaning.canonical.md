## The Story

"In the beginning was the Word..."

— John 1:1

Mass-energy says: **matter IS energy.** Meaning says: **events ARE interpretation.** Just like E=mc² united matter and energy (which seemed like separate things), meaning unites what happens and what it means.

Nihilism is the belief that M=0 — that events happen but carry no built-in significance. This framework rejects that. M is a real variable in the Master Equation. Without it, χ collapses toward zero.

The **conservation of meaning**: meaning cannot be destroyed, only transformed. Trauma doesn't erase meaning — it squeezes it into denser forms. Redemption releases it back out.

## Five Axioms

#### 1. Meaning is Conserved

Cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed. Suffering compresses meaning; redemption expands it.

#### 2. Meaning Bends Narrative

Like mass curves spacetime, meaning curves the path of a life toward purpose.

#### 3. Meaning Emerges from Relationship

Isolated events have no M. Meaning requires a connection between observer and what is observed (the R variable).

#### 4. False Meaning Decays

Meaning that is forced on something (like ideology) starts with high M but falls apart quickly. Authentic meaning stays stable.

#### 5. Conjugate to Relationship [M,R]

Meaning without relationship is just a projection. Relationship without meaning is empty.
