# The Coherence Factor (χ)

**The Coherence Factor (χ)** measures how well a civilization's parts—families, schools, churches, government, trust between people, and shared beliefs—fit together and work as a team.

χ = 1.0 means everything is perfectly aligned. χ = 0.0 means everything is shattered.

Current U.S. χ: **≈ 0.27**

Critical Threshold: **0.35**

Status: **Threshold Crossed**

## Five Years

Between 1968 and 1973, something broke.

Not slowly. Not secretly.

**Everything. All at once.**

In five years, coherence dropped from 0.72 to 0.55—a 24% collapse.

More decline in five years than in the previous sixty-five.

This wasn't slow wear and tear. This was **phase transition** (a sudden shift from one stable state to another, like water turning to ice).

## The Physics of Phase Transition

Water doesn't slowly become ice.

At 33°F, it's water. At 32°F, it's ice. The change happens at one specific point, all at once, completely.

This is called **phase transition**—when a system jumps from one stable state to another.

Human societies work the same way.

You can add pressure slowly, invisibly, for decades. The system absorbs it. Adapts. Holds.

Then one day, you add a little more pressure—and everything collapses at once.

**1968-1973 was America's phase transition.**

## The Trigger Events

### Assassinations (1963, 1968)
JFK. Malcolm X. MLK. RFK. Four leaders shot in five years.

This didn't just kill people. It killed the idea that you could change the future through normal, legal means.

**Coherence impact**: Trust in institutions. Faith in the system. Hope for reform.

### Vietnam
The government lied. Everyone knew it. The Pentagon Papers proved it.

A whole generation learned that their leaders would send them to die based on lies.

**Coherence impact**: Trust collapsed. Never fully recovered.

### Watergate
The President was a criminal. The system was corrupt. The whole thing was rotten.

"I am not a crook" became a joke.

**Coherence impact**: Trust in institutions hit a low it has never recovered from.

### No-Fault Divorce
California 1969. Every state followed.

Marriage went from "binding promise forever" to "contract you can cancel."

Divorce rate doubled within a decade.

**Coherence impact**: Family structure, the smallest unit of coherence, became optional.

### Sexual Revolution
Birth control pills. Available 1960. Widespread by 1968.

For the first time in human history, sex could be completely separated from having babies.

Traditional morality—built on the idea that sex has consequences—became optional.

**Coherence impact**: The shared meaning framework shattered.

### Church Collapse
Mainline Protestant attendance dropped 25-40% between 1965 and 1975.

Not because people moved to other churches. Because they stopped going.

The institution that had been the main coherence generator for three centuries... stopped generating coherence.

**Coherence impact**: Shared meaning, trust, civic participation, passing values to the next generation—all tied to church—all collapsed together.

## Why Everything Broke At Once

The areas aren't separate. They support each other.

When one breaks, it pulls on the others.

- **Trust** breaks → **Civic participation** drops → **Shared meaning** fragments
- **Shared meaning** fragments → **Family structure** weakens → **Passing values to kids** fails
- **Family** weakens → **Self-control** drops → **Mental health** declines
- **Mental health** declines → **Trust** erodes → **Safety** gets worse

It's a cascade (a chain reaction). Once it starts, it speeds up.

By 1973, the cascade was unstoppable.

## The Point of No Return

χ = 0.55 was still above the critical threshold (0.35).

Recovery was still possible in 1973.

But the path was set. The institutions that could have fixed things were themselves damaged. The generation that could have rebuilt was itself broken.

From 1973 on, every attempt at recovery would be fighting against the current.
