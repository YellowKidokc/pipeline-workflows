# The Coherence Cascade

**Tracking the Four-Domain Collapse Across Six Generations (1900–2025)**

![Story Illustration](../images/mda/Signal_Blackout%20conv%201.webp)

**The Hypothesis:** Social coherence (how well a society holds together) collapses in a predictable order. **Words fail first** — the meaning of language erodes before the structures of society break down. Then **Family** falls apart, then **Institutions**, then **Economy**. The data across 125 years confirms this cascade.

| Year | Story | SEMANTIC | FAMILIAL | INSTITUTIONAL | ECONOMIC | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1900 | Samuel | 98 | 95 | 92 | 90 | BASELINE |
| 1926 | Henry | 90 | 93 | 90 | 85 | THINNING |
| 1950 | William | 75 | 88 | 90 | 92 | FALSE PEAK |
| 1974 | Thomas | 40 | 50 | 45 | 65 | PHASE TRANSITION |
| 1998 | Jacob I | 25 | 35 | 35 | 45 | RUBBLE |
| 2025 | Jacob II | 18 | 20 | 18 | 25 | BOTTOM |

## THE PATTERN IS CLEAR

**SEMANTIC** drops first: 98 → 90 → 75 → 40 → 25 → 18
**FAMILIAL** follows: 95 → 93 → 88 → 50 → 35 → 20
**INSTITUTIONAL** third: 92 → 90 → 90 → 45 → 35 → 18
**ECONOMIC** last: 90 → 85 → 92 → 65 → 45 → 25
