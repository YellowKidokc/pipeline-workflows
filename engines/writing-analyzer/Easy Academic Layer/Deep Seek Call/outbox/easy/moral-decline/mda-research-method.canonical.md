# The Five-Page System

Each inquiry in this project uses **five pages**. They are not optional and they are not just for show. Each page does a different mental job. You must duplicate all five for every new question.

## Inquiry Seed

*Question & Orientation*

State the research question exactly. Write down the researcher's first hunch or belief *before* any AI input messes with it. This matters: your thinking before AI is load-bearing. If you let AI frame the question, you've already lost control of the answer.

*File: Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md*

## AI Prompt Engine

*How AI Is Used*

Record the exact prompts you used, the AI models you asked, and the outputs you got — without judging them yet. Paste the AI output here raw. No evaluation at this stage. The engine maps the terrain: competing ideas, key names, major fault lines, suggested search areas. It does not decide what is true.

*File: AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md*

## Research Workspace

*Evidence & Links*

The living document where real evidence piles up. Primary sources, data links, study citations, statistics with dates and where they came from. This is where AI suggestions get tested against real sources. Not every AI claim survives contact with the original research. The workspace is where that collision happens.

*File: Research_Workspace_[Question].md*

## Contradictions & Resolution

*Sense-Making*

This is the most important page in the system. Where do the sources disagree? Where does the AI output contradict the original research? Where does your gut feeling conflict with the data? This page forces you to sit with the confusion long enough to understand it — instead of picking only the evidence that backs up what you already believed.

Truth is most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily.

*File: Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md*

## Quotes, Domains, & Links

*Shared Library*

A shared reference library built across all inquiries. Key quotes with who said them and when. Domain categories that came up during research. External links with short notes on what they contain. This page grows over time — it builds up across every inquiry and becomes the raw material for publications.

*File: Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md*

# What AI Does — and Does Not Do

## AI Is Used To

- **Map intellectual terrain quickly across multiple disciplines**
- **Surface contradictions between competing ideas**
- **Organize competing frameworks side by side**
- **Suggest where deeper primary-source study is needed**
- **Stress-test early conclusions against counterarguments**

## AI Is Not Used To

- **Decide what is true**
- **Replace primary-source verification**
- **Frame the initial question before the researcher does**
- **Select which evidence counts as important**
- **Resolve contradictions that require human judgment**

Some AI outputs in this project are incomplete. Some are wrong. That is expected and is not a failure of the method. The method treats AI error as a signal — it tells you where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested, where consensus has been manufactured instead of earned, and where you need to dig deeper into primary sources.

# How to Use This System

*Workflow Sequence*

1. **Start with the Inquiry Seed.** Write your question and your first hunch *before* running any AI prompt.

2. **Run the AI Prompt Engine.** Paste the output. Do not judge it yet — just collect it.

3. **Open the Research Workspace.** Follow the AI's leads into primary sources. Cite everything. Record what the data actually says.

4. **Build the Contradictions page.** Force the disagreements into the open. Sit with them. Do not cover them up.

5. **Maintain the Quotes, Domains, & Links library** as you go. Every session adds to it.

Duplicate all five pages for each new question. The structure is not bureaucracy — it is the mental framework that prevents AI-assisted research from turning into AI-generated opinion.

"Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees. It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable."

This research exists because someone felt the weight of it and decided that feeling was not enough. You have to actually look. You have to go where the data is uncomfortable. You have to let the contradictions speak before you reach for a resolution.

That is what this project is. That is what this method is for.
