# The Four Tests That Confirmed Social Coherence Is Real

If social coherence (χ — the Greek letter "chi," used here as a symbol for how well a society holds together) is a real, measurable thing controlled by constraint pressure (P — the amount of rules and limits that keep society stable), then it should make specific predictions that can be proven wrong.

Not vague guesses. Precise, structural predictions that can be checked against 125 years of American data. We tested four of them. Here is what the data showed.

## The Four Predictions

1. **Cross-domain correlation** — All parts of society should move together, not separately.
2. **Structural synchronization** — The big collapse should happen at the same time across all parts of society.
3. **Threshold behavior** — The collapse should be sudden, not gradual.
4. **Control group divergence** — Groups that kept their rules should keep their coherence.

## Test 1: Cross-Domain Correlation

**Null hypothesis (the "nothing special" idea):** The seven areas of society (family, religion, institutions, education, media, economy, social life) are all independent. Expected correlation (how much they move together): R ≈ 0.

**Our hypothesis:** The seven areas are all measuring the same hidden thing (χ). Expected correlation: R >> 0 (much greater than zero).

**Method:** Calculate pairwise Pearson correlations (a standard way to measure how two things move together) across all seven areas for the full measurement period (1940–2024, with 11 measurement points).

## Test 4: The Control Group

If the collapse of coherence is caused by removing constraints (rules and limits), then populations that *rejected* removing constraints should keep their coherence. The Amish provide the cleanest natural experiment in modern social science.

### Constraints the Amish Rejected

- Did not adopt no-fault divorce
- Did not adopt fiat currency dependence (money not backed by gold)
- Did not adopt mass media saturation
- Did not adopt the contraceptive revolution
- Maintained religious authority structures

**Prediction:** χ_Amish(t) ≈ constant (stays the same over time) while χ_America(t) → 0 (drops to near zero)

Same genetics. Same geography. Same century. Different constraints. Different outcome. Like the slope of a hill — you don't get pushed, the ground itself tilts so you naturally move toward the bottom. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet. ## The Constraint Removal Events

What caused constraint pressure (P) to drop below the critical threshold in 1968–1973? No single event caused the collapse. The cumulative removal of constraints dropped P below the critical point.

| Year | Event | Constraint Removed | Domain Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1968 | Hays Code collapse | Media censorship | Media: −25 pts |
| 1968 | MLK / RFK assassinations | Authority legitimacy | Institutional: −15 pts |
| 1969 | Woodstock / counterculture | Cultural norms | Social |
| 1970 | No-fault divorce (CA) | Marital permanence | Family: −20 pts |
| 1971 | Nixon closes gold window | Monetary discipline | Economic (delayed) |
| 1972 | *Eisenstadt v. Baird* | Reproductive constraint | Family |
| 1973 | *Roe v. Wade* | Reproductive constraint | Family |
| 1973–74 | Watergate / Nixon resignation | Political trust | Institutional |

Each event alone was survivable. Together they represented a coordinated removal of the constraint architecture that held P above the critical threshold. The system crossed the threshold and underwent a state change it has not recovered from. Like a shattered glass — you can't unshatter it, and you can't hide the pieces. Every shard is still there, accounted for. What broke stays broken until someone comes with glue from outside.
