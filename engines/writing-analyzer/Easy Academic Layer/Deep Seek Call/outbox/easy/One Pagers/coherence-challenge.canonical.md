## What the Calculator Measures

The Coherence Score is a combined measure of six stress factors that any book must survive. Each factor adds entropy — noise, damage, information loss. A higher score means a book kept more of its original message while facing more destructive pressure.

**The six factors:** age (time = entropy), number of independent authors (harder to keep the story straight), languages translated into (translation loss), composition timespan (harder to stay consistent over centuries), manuscript gap (more copies = more degradation), and textual agreement (how much the copies actually match each other).

**The Bible's numbers:** 3,400 years old, 40+ authors, 700+ languages, 1,500-year composition span, 25,000+ surviving manuscripts, only 25-year gap between the New Testament being written and the earliest copy we have, 99.5% textual agreement.

By the rules of information theory, a book with those transmission numbers should be a mess of noise. The Bible isn't. That gap between what we expect and what we actually measure is the key finding.
