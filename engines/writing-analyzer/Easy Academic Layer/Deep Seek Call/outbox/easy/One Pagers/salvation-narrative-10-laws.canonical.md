# Theophysics: The Physics of Salvation

## Act 1: The Setup (Before Salvation)

### Law 9 — Entropy / The Adversary
**You are dying.**

**Physics**
The system is running down. Everything moves from order to disorder. This is the second law of thermodynamics — entropy (a measure of disorder) always increases in a closed system. You can't stop it from the inside.

**Spiritual**
Sin is the entropy of the soul. Left to yourself, you decay. You don't need to *try* to fall apart — it happens naturally.

**You are a closed system running out of energy.**

### Law 1 — Grace / Gravitation
**You feel the pull.**

**Physics**
General relativity says mass curves spacetime (the fabric of the universe), and objects follow geodesics — the straightest possible path in curved space. You don't need a force pushing you. Grace changes the shape of the ground under your feet.

**Spiritual**

**You stopped resisting the curvature. The draw was always there.**

This is the Gibbs free energy equation. When ΔG is negative, the reaction happens spontaneously — it releases more energy than it absorbs. The phase transition is thermodynamically favorable. Like heat flowing from hot to cold — it only moves one direction, and you can't make it go backwards without spending energy from outside the system.

**The books balance. The transition is thermodynamically spontaneous.**
