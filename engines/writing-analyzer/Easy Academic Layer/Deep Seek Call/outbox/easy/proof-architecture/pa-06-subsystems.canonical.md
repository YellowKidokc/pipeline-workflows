# Section 2

## Five Paired Subsystems

This is the middle layer of the architecture. This is where the proof has to work across multiple areas of knowledge and pass all its closure checks.

Think of it like a self-contained website with interlinked pages. Each pair is a test. If you remove one side of the pair, the equation or the whole system of interpretation stops working the way the framework requires.

### Pair (G,R) — Gravity / Resurrection

Gravity is paired with restoration and reordering logic. The claim is simple: if you remove either side, the system breaks. This isn't just symbolism. It's a closure test.

Remove G or R → equation does not close

### Pair (M,S) — Matter / Entropy

Matter is tracked together with the direction of entropy (the tendency of things to move from order to disorder). Sin is treated as entropy here. Grace is later treated as negentropic intervention — meaning it pushes against the natural slide into disorder.

dS > 0 ; matter without entropy accounting becomes incomplete

This equation says the change in entropy is always greater than zero in a closed system. Matter without accounting for entropy is incomplete.

### Pair (E,F) — Energy / Faith

Faith is not treated as a feeling or sentiment here. It's treated as a way of coupling with the system — like how an observer has to connect to what they're observing.

Faith coupling → observation linkage

### Pair (Q,C) — Quantum / Coherence

Quantum behavior is paired with coherence conservation (keeping things vibrating in sync). A closed system cannot just generate more and more coherence on its own. Like the tuning fork analogy — one source of perfect pitch brings everything else into harmony, but the system can't do that for itself.

Closed subsystem: coherence conservation constraint

### Pair (T,K) — Time / Knowledge

Time is paired with knowledge and pathing (the route information takes). The framework treats time as something that carries real weight for what we can know. It's not just a neutral stage where things happen.

Remove T or K → path and closure conditions deform

### Why these matter

These five pairs are the middle architecture. The foundation alone doesn't give you a working worldview. This subsystem band shows how the basic rules have to operate across many different areas — not just in abstract philosophy.
