Every choice you make at Q2 — Matter, Mind, Information, Mathematical, Relational, or Brute Primitive — gets carried through all 12 levels of the tree. **Key:**
- ✓ PASS = The substrate answers the question
- ⚠ STRAIN = The substrate answers but has to borrow from another substrate

### The Propagation Principle

Whatever you choose at Q2 becomes your foundation. That foundation must answer **every question after it** — Q3 through Q12 — without contradicting itself. Where it can't answer, that's a **blank**. Where it contradicts itself, that's a **death**. Where it answers only by stealing words from another foundation, that's **strain**. The strain builds up and becomes too much to handle — usually by Q6 to Q8.

At each node, **published secular theories** are embedded. They show what physics already proved and what questions it left wide open. Those open questions all point to the same shape: the form of the boundary conditions (BC1 through BC8).
