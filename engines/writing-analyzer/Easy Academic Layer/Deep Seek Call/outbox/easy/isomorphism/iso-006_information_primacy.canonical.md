## DOMAINS

**Domain A:** Physics / Information Theory
**Domain B:** Christian Theology — John's Gospel Opening / Creation Theology
**Concept A:** Information is the most basic building block of reality. Physicist John Wheeler called this "It from Bit" — every physical thing gets its meaning from yes/no choices (bits of information). Matter and energy come second. The ability to tell things apart (information) comes first.
**Concept B:** "In the beginning was the Word (Logos)" (John 1:1) — a rational, structuring message comes before and creates all physical reality. "All things were made through him" (John 1:3). Creation happens by speaking (Genesis 1: "And God said...").

---

## THE MAPPING

**Mathematical Form A:**

Wheeler (1990): "It from Bit" — every physical measurement gets its meaning from binary yes/no distinctions (bits of information).

- **Landauer's Principle:** Erasing one bit of information gives off at least kT ln(2) joules of energy. This means information has a real physical cost. It's not just a side effect — it has weight in the real world.
- **Zeilinger's Foundational Principle:** Each basic quantum system (qubit) carries exactly one bit of information. The information content IS the system itself.
- **Quantum information theory:** The quantum state |ψ⟩ is the most complete description of a system. There is nothing "underneath" the information — no hidden gears or particles. The wavefunction IS what's really there (on the Everett/information-theoretic reading).

Formal expression: Physical reality R comes from an information base I:

R = f(I), where I cannot be broken down further and R depends on it.

**Mathematical Form B:**

John 1:1-3: "In the beginning was the Logos (λόγος), and the Logos was with God, and the Logos was God. He was in the beginning with God. All things were made through him, and without him was not any thing made that was made."

Genesis 1: Creation happens by divine speech: "And God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light." The speech act (sending information) comes before and causes the physical result.

Hebrews 11:3: "The universe was created by the word of God, so that what is seen was not made out of things that are visible." — visible (material) reality comes from invisible (informational/verbal) cause.

Formal parallel: Material reality M comes from Logos L:

M = f(L), where L comes first and M depends on it.

**Shared Structure:**

1. **Information comes before matter** — In both domains, the informational/verbal base is FIRST and matter/energy is DERIVED. The order is not: matter exists, then we describe it with information. The order is: information exists, then matter shows up from it.
2. **Generative function** — Information doesn't just describe reality; it MAKES reality. Wheeler's bit makes the "it." God's word makes creation. Both produce things, not just describe them.
3. **Cannot be reduced further** — In both domains, the information base cannot be broken down. You cannot ask "what is the bit made of?" in Wheeler's framework. You cannot ask "what came before the Logos?" in John's framework. Both are the bottom layer.
4. **Physical cost of information** — Landauer's principle shows information has real physical effects. Biblical speech acts have real physical effects ("Let there be light" — and there WAS light). Information is not cheap talk in either domain.

**What Is NOT Claimed:**

- NOT claiming Shannon entropy IS the Logos — Shannon entropy measures uncertainty in a probability distribution. The Logos is a self-aware, personal, creative agent. These are completely different categories.
- NOT claiming John was doing information theory — John's Gospel opening draws on Greek philosophy (Stoic logos, Philo's mediating Logos), not on math formulas. The structural parallel exists even though the contexts are totally different.
- NOT claiming physics proves John 1:1 — the "It from Bit" idea is a philosophical interpretation of quantum mechanics, not a proven experiment. Many physicists reject it.
- NOT claiming "Word" and "information" mean the same thing — logos in Greek carries meanings of reason, proportion, speech, and cosmic order that "information" in the Shannon sense does not. See the Strain Analysis below.
- NOT claiming this is the strongest isomorphism in the framework — it may be an analogy with structural features rather than a full structural match. This record is honest about that.

## TESTS

### The Four-Test Protocol

**Test 1 — Prediction Constraint:** Does the mapping limit what can happen in both domains?

In Domain A: If information is truly the most basic thing, then:

- No physical process can destroy information (unitarity). This is testable and is exactly what the Black Hole Information Paradox is about. The resolution (Hawking's 2004 concession, Page curve calculations, recent replica wormhole results) supports information conservation.
- Every physical law must be expressible as an information-processing operation. This limits what kinds of physical theories are possible.

In Domain B: If the Logos truly creates all reality, then:

- No created thing exists independently of the Logos. "Without him was not any thing made that was made" (John 1:3). This is a total-scope claim: no exceptions.
- Destruction of the Logos would mean destruction of all reality. This matches Colossians 1:17: "in him all things hold together."

Verdict: The mapping does limit predictions in both domains, but the limits are not equal. The physics limits are quantitative and experimentally testable (unitarity, Landauer bound). The theological limits are qualitative and not experimentally testable the same way. **Partial pass.**

**Test 2 — Symmetry Breaking:** Can you swap the elements?

Can matter come before information? In Wheeler's framework: no. In John's framework: no. Both domains agree on the order.

Can information be derived from something else? In Wheeler: that would break the framework — you'd be back to classical materialism. In John: that would mean the Logos was created, which is the Arian heresy directly contradicted by "the Logos WAS God."

**Symmetry breaking result: PASSED.** The order is not random in both domains.

**Test 3 — Connection Density:** How many other ISOs does this touch?

Connection count: 4+ direct connections. **Passed.**

**Test 4 — Falsifiability Invitation:** What would break this mapping?

1. Show that "It from Bit" is wrong — that information comes from some sub-informational physical base (hidden variables, superdeterminism, or a future theory that derives quantum information from something more basic). If information is NOT the most basic thing in physics, Domain A collapses and the mapping fails.
2. Show that John's logos is not about information being first but purely about divine personhood with no claim about creating the universe. This is hard to support given John 1:3 ("all things were made through him"), but a purely existentialist reading of the Prologue would weaken Domain B.
3. Find a physical system whose behavior cannot be described informationally. If there exists a physical process that is real but carries zero information content, Wheeler's thesis fails.

### STRAIN ANALYSIS — The Honest Part

**The core objection:** "Word" (logos) in John 1 and "information" (bit) in Wheeler mean DIFFERENT THINGS.

This objection is serious and must be addressed head-on:

- **Logos** (λόγος) in Greek philosophy means: rational principle, cosmic order, the intelligible structure of reality, speech, proportion, reason. In Philo of Alexandria (a writer near John's time), logos is the mediating principle between the distant God and the material world. In the Stoics, logos is the active rational principle that fills the cosmos. John takes this loaded term and identifies it with a specific person (Jesus Christ). The logos is therefore PERSONAL, CONSCIOUS, INTENTIONAL, and DIVINE.

- **Information** in Shannon's sense means: reduction of uncertainty, measured in bits, defined by the probability distribution over a message space. Shannon information is IMPERSONAL, UNCONSCIOUS, UNINTENTIONAL, and MATHEMATICAL.

These are not the same concept. The mapping holds at the structural level (both come before matter, both generate physical reality) but breaks at the personal level (logos is a who; information is a what).

**Honest assessment:** This isomorphism is STRUCTURAL, not semantic. The word "logos" and the word "information" do not mean the same thing. What they share is a structural role: the most basic base from which physical reality comes. The mapping is real at that level but should not be pushed to identity. Logos includes information but is not reducible to it. Information may be a shadow or aspect of logos but is not the whole thing.

**Where it holds:** The claim that reality is information-first (not matter-first) maps cleanly onto the claim that reality is logos-first (not matter-first). The generative direction, the irreducibility, and the ontological priority all match.

**Where it strains:** When you ask "is information conscious?" physics says no (or is silent). When you ask "is the logos impersonal?" theology says absolutely not. The personal dimension of logos has no counterpart in Shannon information or Wheeler's bit. This is a significant gap.

**Possible resolution within the framework:** The Theophysics framework suggests that consciousness/personhood is itself an informational phenomenon (integrated information, per Tononi's IIT). If consciousness IS sufficiently integrated information, then the gap narrows: logos is maximally integrated information (infinite Φ), and impersonal information is logos at low integration (low Φ). This is speculative but structurally consistent.
