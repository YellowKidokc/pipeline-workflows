### What this does not prove

Lean verifies logical structure, not empirical truth. It proves: "Given these definitions and axioms, these theorems follow by logical necessity." It does not prove that the ten laws are true descriptions of reality, that each physics/theology pair is isomorphic (has the same structure) to the physical world, or that the framework's theological claims are correct.

What it does prove: the internal architecture is formally consistent, the structural claims hold under any algebra satisfying the axioms, and no one can dismiss this as "just analogies." The algebra is real. The proofs are machine-checked.
