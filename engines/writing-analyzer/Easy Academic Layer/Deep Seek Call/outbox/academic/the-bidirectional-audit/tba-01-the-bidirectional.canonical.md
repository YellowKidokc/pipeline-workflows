## Abstract

This paper presents a systematic, bidirectional epistemological audit of Biblical theism and scientific naturalism, applying identical standards of evidential evaluation to both frameworks. Through structural comparison of 16 prescient Biblical claims against their subsequent scientific confirmation—yielding a mean temporal priority gap of approximately 2,800 years—and through the application of seven epistemic criteria derived from the Biblical text to the scientific enterprise as a totalizing worldview, we demonstrate that scientific naturalism fails five of seven standards while the Bible satisfies or exceeds scientific expectations on prescient claims. Additionally, ten structural gaps in naturalistic explanation are identified, and five historical cases of civilizational coherence collapse following the formal removal of divine grounding are examined. The analysis yields a Theophysics Master Equation formalizing the interaction of grace, moral law, entropy, sin, time, knowledge, revelation, quantum state, faith, and consciousness within a unified coherence field. The authors acknowledge the Christian worldview presupposition underlying this investigation and provide explicit kill conditions for falsification.

## Part 2: The Mirror Test — Holding Science to the Bible's Standards

The Bible supplies seven explicit epistemic standards for evaluating truth-claims. When those same standards are applied symmetrically to the scientific enterprise as a totalizing worldview, the following results obtain.

### Standard 1 — Multiple Witnesses (Deut 19:15) — **Fail**

The replication crisis constitutes the empirical verdict. Psychology replicates at 36–40% (Open Science Collaboration, 2015). Social Psychology replicates at approximately 25% (Nosek et al., 2022). Biomedical science replicates at 11–25% (Begley & Ellis, 2012; Ioannidis, 2005). An estimated $28 billion is spent annually on irreproducible research (Freedman et al., 2015). Science's core claim to authority rests on independent witness—and that standard is failing by its own metrics.

### Standard 2 — Fruit Inspection (Matt 7:16–20) — **Partial**

Good fruit: vaccines, surgery, clean water, structural engineering. Bad fruit: nuclear weapons, eugenics, ecological destruction, the industrialization of death. Science as a method is not morally valenced; however, Science as a worldview (scientism) produces fruit that is deeply mixed. A partial pass constitutes the honest assessment.

### Standard 3 — Consistency with Prior Revelation (Isa 8:20) — **Fail**

Three hundred ninety-six documented medical reversals have been identified (Prasad et al., 2013). Dietary science reverses every decade. The "self-correction" that science celebrates is, by the Biblical standard, precisely the instability that disqualifies it as a foundation for truth. A corrected system is a previously wrong system. The track record is disqualifying under this criterion.

### Standard 4 — Humility Before the Source (Prov 1:7) — **Fail**

A majority of 2,000 surveyed psychologists admitted to Questionable Research Practices—p-hacking, HARKing (Hypothesizing After Results are Known), selectively reporting outcomes (John et al., 2012). The structural incentive of "publish or perish" systematically rewards overconfidence. The posture of the practitioner affects the data. The institution's default posture is not humility.

### Standard 5 — Moral Transformation (2 Cor 5:17) — **Fail**

There is no demonstrated correlation between scientific literacy and moral behavior. The most scientifically advanced nation in Europe in 1933 was Germany. The Soviet Union's scientific apparatus was world-class. Science does not produce moral fruit in its adherents; it produces technical capability—which is morally neutral at best.

### Standard 6 — Testimony Under Persecution (Rev 2:10) — **Partial**

"Publish or perish" constitutes a structural incentive toward conformity. Careers have been ended for heterodox findings. Wegener's continental drift was suppressed for forty years (Oreskes, 1999). Barry Marshall ingested *H. pylori* to be believed (Marshall & Warren, 1984). Science eventually self-corrects, but testimony is demonstrably fragile under institutional and social pressure. A partial pass is warranted, given the self-correction mechanism.

### Standard 7 — Authority Chain (Matt 28:18) — **Fail**

Scientific consensus is not authority—it is majority opinion. Wegener had the right answer while the consensus was wrong. No unchanging source of authority grounds scientific claims. The authority chain terminates in "current best available data," which is by definition provisional. A totalizing worldview built on provisional authority has no authority chain at all.

## Self-Assessment

### What We Got Right

The bidirectional framing is genuinely original—applying the Bible's own epistemic standards back to science constitutes a methodological move not previously executed in this form. The replication crisis data is devastating by any measure; those numbers are not cherry-picked. The civilizational collapse dataset is empirical, not theological. The asymmetry of containment argument (Theism contains Science; Science cannot contain Theism) is logically sound.

### What We Got Wrong

The E-score values (0.61 Science, 0.40 Theophysics) require recomputation with a fully specified scoring rubric—they are directionally correct but not precisely derived in this draft. Some prescience claims in Table 1 vary substantially in specificity; entries 8 and 14 are weaker than the others and should be marked accordingly. The scoring is directionally valid but should not be treated as precise measurement.

### What We Overstated

"Science fails 5 of 7" is true *by the Bible's criteria*—but the Bible's criteria presuppose its own authority. A committed naturalist will not grant those criteria as applicable. The rhetorical force of the Mirror Test depends on accepting the symmetry argument. The point is symmetry, not a knockout blow. That distinction should be foregrounded more explicitly.

### Where We're Vulnerable

The prescience table requires philological verification in the original Hebrew and Greek. Some "claims" may reflect later editorial harmonization rather than original authorial intent. The table requires a Hebrew/Greek scholar's review before it can be cited as definitive. This constitutes the paper's single most important verification gap.
