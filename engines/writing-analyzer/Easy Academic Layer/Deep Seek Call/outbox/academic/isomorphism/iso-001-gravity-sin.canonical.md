## 3. Domain Specifications

### 3.1 Domain A — Physics: Classical Mechanics (Gravitation)

In classical Newtonian mechanics, gravitational force is governed by the inverse-square law (Newton, 1687, *Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica*, Book III, Proposition VII). Every mass attracts every other mass with a force proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centers. Objects within a gravitational well accelerate toward the center of mass; escape from such a well requires the input of kinetic energy exceeding the gravitational binding energy. The force is universal in scope—it acts on all mass-energy without exception, irrespective of composition or prior state.

Key formal properties include:
- **Inverse-square dependence:** \(F \propto 1/r^2\)
- **Binding energy:** \(E_{\text{bind}} = G m_1 m_2 / r\)
- **Escape velocity:** \(v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{2GM/r}\)
- **Universal attraction:** No known mass exhibits gravitational repulsion

### 3.2 Domain B — Theology: Hamartiology (Study of Sin)

Within Christian theological tradition, sin is understood as a condition of moral separation from God (the Source) that exerts a downward pull on moral agents. Distance from the Source increases the attractive force of sin, such that moral agents at greater relational distance experience stronger pull toward further separation. Escape from the gravitational well of sin requires external energy input—grace—exceeding the binding energy of habit, nature, and environmental context. The pull is universal in scope—it acts on all moral agents without exception, irrespective of prior moral state (Romans 3:23: "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God").

Key formal properties include:
- **Inverse-square dependence:** Doubling relational distance from God quarters the moral resistance to sin
- **Binding energy:** The cumulative moral debt and habitual entrenchment that must be overcome for liberation
- **Escape velocity (grace):** The threshold quantity of divine enablement required for genuine conversion
- **Universal attraction:** No known moral agent exhibits innate repulsion from sin
