# Theophysics: A Structural Isomorphism Between Physical Laws and Spiritual Principles

## Abstract

This article presents a systematic analysis of ten fundamental physical laws and their proposed spiritual analogues, identifying a consistent structural isomorphism across all domains. Through formal mathematical comparison, we demonstrate that each physical equation maps to a corresponding spiritual equation with precisely one additional term—a free-will asymmetry factor—appearing in the same structural position in every case. The analysis proceeds through explicit variable substitution, dimensional analysis, and formal comparison of equation structures. The resulting asymmetry table documents ten instances of this pattern, with the tenth law (Physical Coherence and Christ) exhibiting no asymmetry term, suggesting a terminal integration condition. We argue that the consistency of this structural pattern across all ten domains constitutes evidence of intentional architectural design rather than coincidental correspondence.
