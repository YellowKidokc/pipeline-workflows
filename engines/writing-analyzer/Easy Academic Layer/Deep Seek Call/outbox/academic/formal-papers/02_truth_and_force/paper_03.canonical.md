# A Theophysical Framework for Truth and Revelation: Structural Isomorphisms Between Electromagnetic Wave Theory and Spiritual Information Transmission

## Abstract

This article presents a formal structural mapping between classical electromagnetism, as formalized by Maxwell's equations, and the transmission of theological truth and revelation within a Christian metaphysical framework. We demonstrate that truth, conceptualized as a self-sustaining wave phenomenon, propagates at a characteristic velocity identified as the "Speed of Christ" (c), while deception emerges as a consequence of phase-inverted destructive interference operating upon the same fundamental signal. The analysis further incorporates free will as a receiver tuning parameter and applies Shannon-Hartley information theory to quantify the capacity for revelation reception. This interdisciplinary framework—termed the "No-Drift" model—provides rigorous mathematical scaffolding for theological concepts traditionally expressed through metaphor.

## 2. The Physics of Light: Maxwell's Equations and Wave Propagation

Maxwell's equations in vacuum, expressed in differential form, govern the behavior of electric and magnetic fields:

\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = 0 \quad \text{(Gauss's law for electricity)}
\]
\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0 \quad \text{(Gauss's law for magnetism)}
\]
\[
\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} \quad \text{(Faraday's law of induction)}
\]
\[
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t} \quad \text{(Ampère's law with Maxwell's correction)}
\]

where \(\mathbf{E}\) denotes the electric field (units: V·m⁻¹), \(\mathbf{B}\) denotes the magnetic field (units: T), \(\mu_0\) is the vacuum permeability (4π × 10⁻⁷ N·A⁻²), and \(\varepsilon_0\) is the vacuum permittivity (8.854 × 10⁻¹² F·m⁻¹).

These equations yield the wave equation for both fields:

\[
\nabla^2 \mathbf{E} = \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{E}}{\partial t^2}, \quad \nabla^2 \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{B}}{\partial t^2}
\]

The propagation velocity is given by:

\[
c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}} \approx 2.998 \times 10^8 \, \text{m·s}^{-1}
\]

Critically, electromagnetic waves are self-sustaining: the time-varying electric field generates a magnetic field via Ampère's law, and the time-varying magnetic field generates an electric field via Faraday's law. This mutual regeneration permits propagation through vacuum without a material medium, carrying energy (proportional to \(|\mathbf{E}|^2 + |\mathbf{B}|^2\)) and information across arbitrarily large distances, subject only to inverse-square law attenuation in free space.

## 4. The Destructive Regime: Deception as Phase-Inverted Interference

Deception, within this framework, is not an independent substance or opposing force but rather a specific configuration of Truth. In wave physics, **destructive interference** occurs when two coherent waves of identical frequency are superposed with a phase difference of exactly \(\pi\) radians (180°). For sinusoidal waves of equal amplitude \(A\) and angular frequency \(\omega\):

\[
E_{\text{total}} = A \sin(\omega t) + A \sin(\omega t + \pi) = A \sin(\omega t) - A \sin(\omega t) = 0
\]

The waves cancel precisely, yielding zero net amplitude. This phenomenon maps structurally to **Deception**. To construct an effective lie, one must employ truth and invert its phase. **Half-truths** correspond to partially phase-inverted waves that partially cancel the coherent signal while maintaining the superficial appearance of wave-like structure. Deception, therefore, is the act of arranging truth-constituents such that they destructively interfere with the coherent signal of Revelation.

This yields a formal definition: Let \(T(x,t)\) represent the truth field at spacetime coordinates \((x,t)\). A deceptive signal \(D(x,t)\) is given by:

\[
D(x,t) = T(x,t) \cdot e^{i\phi(x,t)}
\]

where \(\phi(x,t)\) is a spatially and temporally varying phase function. Complete deception occurs when \(\phi = \pi\) (mod \(2\pi\)) across the domain of interest, producing destructive interference with the authentic revelation signal.

## 6. Information Theory and the Shannon Limit of Revelation

The capacity for revelation reception is governed by the Shannon-Hartley theorem, which quantifies the maximum rate of information transmission through a communication channel of bandwidth \(B\) in the presence of additive white Gaussian noise:

\[
C = B \cdot \log_2\left(1 + \frac{S}{N}\right)
\]

where:
- \(C\) = channel capacity (bits per second)
- \(B\) = bandwidth (Hz)
- \(S\) = signal power (W)
- \(N\) = noise power (W)

Within the theophysical framework, the **revelation capacity** \(C_{\text{rev}}\) of a soul is determined by:
- **Bandwidth \(B\)**: The spectral range of spiritual perception—the breadth of truth frequencies to which the agent is sensitive
- **Signal-to-noise ratio \(S/N\)**: The ratio of authentic revelation signal power to the combined noise power from deception and "distortion of the flesh"

If the noise power \(N\) from deception or carnal distortion exceeds the signal power \(S\), the signal-to-noise ratio approaches zero, and the logarithmic term \(\log_2(1 + S/N) \to 0\). Under these conditions, the truth cannot be resolved regardless of its absolute signal strength, as the channel capacity collapses to zero. This provides a formal information-theoretic account of spiritual blindness: the receiver is physically incapable of distinguishing signal from noise.
