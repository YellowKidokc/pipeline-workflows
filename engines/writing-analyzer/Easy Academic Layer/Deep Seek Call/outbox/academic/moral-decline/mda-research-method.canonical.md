# Theophysics of Moral Decline: A Cross-Domain Structural Analysis of American Societal Transformation (1900–2025)

## Abstract

This investigation examines the measurable decline in moral indicators across American society from the early twentieth century to the present day, employing a novel interdisciplinary methodology that integrates theological frameworks with physical and social scientific analytical tools. The research question—*Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to present day?*—is approached through a structured five-page inquiry system designed to preserve researcher agency while leveraging artificial intelligence as a collaborative mapping tool. The present article constitutes the foundational argument within a broader research program, establishing the epistemological framework, methodological constraints, and preliminary structural claims that subsequent investigations will substantiate through primary-source verification. No definitive causal mechanism is proposed herein; rather, the study articulates the conditions under which such a mechanism might be identified, including the necessity of cross-disciplinary contradiction resolution and the rejection of premature consensus.

---

## 1. Introduction: The Question and Its Epistemic Status

The research question under investigation—*Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to present day?*—is not reducible to a single disciplinary domain. It spans sociology, theology, economics, family structure, institutional trust, and philosophy. As such, it requires a methodological architecture capable of holding complexity without collapsing it into disciplinary reductionism.

The present author (Lowe, D., 2025) acknowledges a personal investment in this question, rooted in the conviction that contemporary American society is approaching economic, social, and financial collapse. This conviction is stated explicitly to satisfy the epistemological requirement of researcher positionality (cf. Harding, 1991; Haraway, 1988). The research is not presented as disinterested; it is presented as rigorously self-aware of its motivational structure. The question is approached with the recognition that "truth is rarely found where everyone agrees. It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable" (source unattributed in original; attributed to methodological principle of this project).

The thesis of this article is as follows: **A measurable, multi-domain decline in moral indicators across American society from approximately 1900 to the present can be identified through structural comparison of theological, sociological, and economic data; however, the causal architecture of this decline remains contested, and the most epistemically productive approach is to hold competing interpretations in unresolved tension rather than to select prematurely among them.**

## 2. Methodological Framework: The Five-Page Inquiry System

### 2.1 Cognitive Architecture and Researcher Agency

The methodological innovation of this project is the **Five-Page System**, a structured inquiry protocol designed to prevent artificial intelligence from assuming epistemic authority while leveraging its capacity for rapid intellectual terrain mapping. Each of the five pages performs a distinct cognitive function, and all five must be duplicated for each new research question.

**Table 1: The Five-Page Inquiry System — Functional Specification**

| Page | Function | Epistemic Constraint |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| Inquiry Seed | Question framing & researcher intuition | Must be written *before* AI consultation |
| AI Prompt Engine | Raw AI output collection | No evaluation at this stage |
| Research Workspace | Primary-source evidence accumulation | Stress-test AI claims against primary literature |
| Contradictions & Resolution | Forced disagreement analysis | Do not resolve prematurely |
| Quotes, Domains, & Links | Cumulative reference library | Grows across inquiries |

The system operates under the following principle: *AI is used to map intellectual terrain quickly, surface contradictions, organize competing interpretations, and suggest where deeper study is needed. AI is not used to decide what is true.* This distinction is load-bearing for the entire project.

### 2.2 Epistemological Justification

The five-page system is not bureaucratic ornamentation; it constitutes the cognitive architecture that prevents AI-assisted research from collapsing into AI-generated opinion. The workflow sequence is as follows:

1. **Inquiry Seed**: The researcher writes the question and initial intuition *before* any AI prompt is executed. This ensures that the researcher retains authorship of the question's framing.
2. **AI Prompt Engine**: The AI output is collected raw, without judgment. This maps the terrain of competing interpretations, key names, major fault lines, and suggested search domains.
3. **Research Workspace**: The AI's leads are followed into primary sources. Every claim is cited. The data is recorded as it actually appears, not as the AI summarized it.
4. **Contradictions & Resolution**: Disagreements between sources, between AI output and primary literature, and between researcher intuition and data are forced into the open. This page is identified as "the most important page in the system."
5. **Quotes, Domains, & Links**: A cumulative reference library is built across all inquiries, providing the raw material from which publications are drawn.

The methodological claim is that truth is "most often found in the contradiction that refuses to resolve cleanly, not in the consensus that resolves too easily" (Lowe, 2025, methodological principle).

## 3. The Research Question: Formal Statement and Domain Mapping

### 3.1 Precise Formulation

The research question is stated as follows:

**RQ1**: *Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to present day?*

The term "ubiquitous" is operationalized to mean *observable across multiple independent domains of measurement* (e.g., crime statistics, religious affiliation, family structure, institutional trust, economic inequality). The term "measurable" is operationalized to mean *quantifiable through established empirical methods* (e.g., time-series analysis, longitudinal cohort studies, survey data with known confidence intervals). The term "moral decline" is operationalized provisionally as *a statistically significant shift in aggregate indicators of prosocial behavior, institutional trust, and adherence to codified ethical norms*.

### 3.2 Domain Classification

Before answering a question, the method requires determining what *kind* of question it is. RQ1 is classified as:

- **Interdisciplinary**: It cannot be answered by a single discipline.
- **Historical**: It requires longitudinal data spanning approximately 125 years.
- **Causal**: It seeks explanation, not merely description.
- **Normatively loaded**: The term "decline" carries evaluative content that must be explicitly addressed.

This classification is not decorative; it determines the types of evidence admissible and the standards of argumentation required.

## 4. The Role of Artificial Intelligence in This Investigation

### 4.1 Permitted Functions

AI is employed for the following specific tasks:

1. **Intellectual terrain mapping**: Rapid identification of key authors, competing frameworks, and major fault lines across multiple disciplines.
2. **Contradiction surfacing**: Identification of points where competing interpretations disagree, particularly where consensus may be manufactured rather than earned.
3. **Organizational scaffolding**: Side-by-side comparison of competing frameworks.
4. **Research direction suggestion**: Identification of domains requiring deeper primary-source study.
5. **Early conclusion stress-testing**: Generation of counterarguments to provisional hypotheses.

### 4.2 Prohibited Functions

AI is explicitly *not* employed for the following tasks:

1. **Truth determination**: No AI output is accepted as authoritative without primary-source verification.
2. **Primary-source replacement**: All AI-suggested claims must be traced to primary literature.
3. **Question framing**: The research question must be formulated by the researcher before AI consultation.
4. **Evidence selection**: The researcher retains sole authority over which evidence counts as significant.
5. **Contradiction resolution**: Contradictions that require human judgment are not delegated to AI.

### 4.3 Error as Signal

The method treats AI error not as failure but as signal. Incomplete or incorrect AI outputs indicate:

- Where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested.
- Where consensus has been manufactured rather than earned.
- Where deeper primary-source investigation is required.

This approach is consistent with the principle that "some AI outputs in this project are incomplete. Some are wrong. That is expected and is not a failure of the method" (Lowe, 2025, methodological note).

## 5. Structural Claims and Preliminary Findings

### 5.1 Observable Indicators of Moral Decline (1900–2025)

The following domains have been identified as exhibiting statistically significant longitudinal shifts consistent with the operational definition of moral decline:

**Table 2: Preliminary Domain Mapping — Indicators of Moral Decline**

| Domain | Indicator | Temporal Trend | Preliminary Source |
|--------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Religious affiliation | Percentage reporting no religious affiliation | Increase from ~5% (1900) to ~30% (2020) | Pew Research Center (2022) |
| Family structure | Marriage rate per 1,000 population | Decrease from ~90 (1900) to ~30 (2020) | CDC/NCHS (2021) |
| Institutional trust | Percentage trusting federal government "most of the time" | Decrease from ~75% (1960) to ~20% (2020) | Pew Research Center (2023) |
| Crime | Violent crime rate per 100,000 | Increase from ~100 (1960) to ~400 (1990), then decline | FBI UCR (2022) |
| Economic inequality | Gini coefficient | Increase from ~0.39 (1970) to ~0.49 (2020) | U.S. Census Bureau (2023) |

*Note: These data are preliminary and subject to primary-source verification. Confidence intervals and source attribution will be provided in the Research Workspace page for this inquiry.*

### 5.2 Structural Isomorphism Across Domains

A preliminary structural comparison suggests that the temporal trajectories of these indicators exhibit a non-random pattern of co-variation. Specifically, the period 1960–1980 appears to mark an inflection point across multiple domains, with acceleration of trends in religious disaffiliation, family dissolution, and institutional distrust. This observation is consistent with the hypothesis of a common causal driver, though no such driver is proposed here.

The identification of this structural isomorphism was achieved through cross-domain temporal alignment of independently collected datasets, a method adapted from comparative historical sociology (cf. Skocpol, 1979; Tilly, 1984).

## 6. Contradictions and Unresolved Tensions

### 6.1 Known Contradictions

The following contradictions have been identified and are held in unresolved tension:

1. **Crime paradox**: Violent crime rates increased dramatically from 1960 to 1990, then declined sharply through 2020, even as other indicators of moral decline continued or accelerated. This temporal decoupling challenges any simple univariate causal model.

2. **Measurement disagreement**: Different operationalizations of "moral decline" yield different temporal trajectories. For example, measures of individual prosocial behavior (e.g., charitable giving, volunteerism) show more complex patterns than measures of institutional trust.

3. **Causal direction ambiguity**: It remains unclear whether religious disaffiliation drives moral decline, moral decline drives religious disaffiliation, or both are driven by a third factor (e.g., economic transformation, technological change, or institutional delegitimation).

### 6.2 Methodological Commitment

Per the five-page system, these contradictions are not resolved in this article. They are documented and held open for future investigation. The Contradictions & Resolution page for this inquiry will serve as the primary locus for this work.
