# The Lean 4 Corpus: A Formal Verification of Theophysics Structural Claims

## Abstract

This article presents the formal verification architecture of the Theophysics Research Initiative, comprising 287 machine-verified theorems encoded in the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover. The corpus establishes internal structural consistency across seven independent lines of inquiry—logical necessity, moral reasoning, information theory, consciousness, creation theology, soteriology, and convergent epistemology—each converging upon an isomorphic structural configuration designated the "God-shape" and its consequent "Cross-shape." The formal system defines dual-substrate coupling states, polarity disciplines, sign conversion operators, bridge matrices with ten-factor signature validation, and uniqueness proofs for the justice-mercy convergence configuration. Sixteen Lean source files implement an eight-layer dependency architecture, with two standalone modules—MaxwellTrinity.lean and JusticeMercyOperator.lean—constituting the strongest formally verified results. The corpus explicitly delineates its verification boundaries: 287 theorems are formally proved, 70+ adversarial rejections are documented, and specification gaps requiring domain judgment, empirical validation, or historical evidence are named. The framework does not claim to prove metaphysical or historical truth; rather, it demonstrates that the structural architecture under investigation is internally consistent and that alternative configurations fail under specified formal constraints.

## 1. Introduction: Formal Verification Methodology

The Theophysics Research Initiative employs Lean 4, an interactive theorem prover implementing dependent type theory, to verify the internal structural consistency of a cross-domain framework bridging physics and theology. A theorem compiles in Lean 4 when every logical step is formally verified, with zero `sorry` or `admit` placeholders remaining. The present corpus achieves this standard across all 287 theorems.

The formal verification establishes *internal structural consistency*—that the definitions, gate conditions, isomorphisms, sign disciplines, and uniqueness results cohere within the axiomatic framework. Whether these formal models faithfully represent the physical and theological domains they reference constitutes a separate question of domain judgment, which the framework addresses through empirical evidence, adversarial review, and named kill conditions. The "overclaim guard" is executed on every theorem, specifying precisely what each proof establishes and what it does not.

## 3. Formal Architecture: The Sixteen Lean Files

The corpus comprises sixteen Lean source files organized in an eight-layer dependency architecture. Three branches extend from the trunk; six files are standalone, proving their claims without importing from the trunk.

### 3.1 Layer 0: Core.lean (Proved)

**Dual-Substrate Foundation**

Five theorems establish the foundational architecture: coupling states, irreversibility gate, and the χ product. Theorem C0_ne_C1 proves that the two coupling states are structurally distinct. Theorem coupling_modification_irreversible establishes that the C0→C1 transition is one-way with no backward constructor. Theorem Q_zero_collapses_chi demonstrates that any single zero factor collapses the entire χ product. Theorem Q_nonzero_not_sufficient_for_positive_chi (Rejection) establishes that no single factor is sufficient for positive χ.

### 3.2 Layer 1: StageMachine.lean (Demoted)

**Stage Ordering**

Five theorems establish strictly ordered, irreversible operation sequences. This file is demoted to teaching layer because the real dependency object is a lattice, not a line; DependencyLattice.lean supersedes this.

**Physics↔Theology Sequence Map**

Three theorems establish a 1-to-1 order-preserving map (localization→incarnation; confirmation→resurrection). This file is demoted because it proves a hand-assigned mapping, which is circular if used as a major proof. The lattice supersedes this.

### 3.6 Layer 5: FieldBridgeControls.lean (Proved)

**SPEC-03:** Empirical Validation. The 5.7–6.35σ experimental correlations are not formalized in Lean. Statistical claims reference external data not encoded in the proof system.

## 7. The Compression: Four Theses

### 7.1 The God-Shape

Every worldview depends on coherence. Coherence requires a reference. The reference cannot be the broken system being measured. Therefore the source of coherence must be prior to the system. ### 7.3 The Challenge

Can any worldview walk through all seven lanes and avoid that shape? Not because the questions are tricks, but because reality is coherent, and coherence is identified with the source.

### 7.4 The Honest Boundary

Lean proves the framework is internally consistent: 287 theorems, zero axioms, zero `sorry`, zero `admit`. Whether the model is the right model for the real world is a question Lean cannot answer. That question belongs to theology, physics, history, and honest argument. The honest boundary is public. The gaps are named. The kill conditions are stated.
