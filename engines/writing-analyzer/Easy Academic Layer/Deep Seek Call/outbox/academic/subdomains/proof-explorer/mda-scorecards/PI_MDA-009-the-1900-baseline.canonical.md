Several limitations constrain the interpretability of these results. First, the lexical density analysis (L6) is based on a single textual source (Galatians 5:22–23) and may not capture the full semantic range of each fruit attribute across the Pauline corpus. Second, the emotional activation scores (L8) are derived from computational models trained on contemporary English-language corpora, which may not accurately map onto first-century Hellenistic emotional categories. Third, the anti-fruit mapping relies on modern antonymic relations that may not correspond to Pauline conceptual oppositions.
