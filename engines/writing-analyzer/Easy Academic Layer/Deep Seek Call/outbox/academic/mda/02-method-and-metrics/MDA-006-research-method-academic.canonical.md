# A Methodological Framework for Interdisciplinary Theophysics Research: Epistemic Protocols for AI-Augmented Inquiry into Moral Decline

## Abstract

This article presents a formalized methodological protocol for conducting interdisciplinary research at the intersection of physics, theology, and moral philosophy, with particular application to the empirical investigation of moral decline in the United States from the early twentieth century to the present day. The protocol, designated the Five-Page System, establishes an epistemological scaffolding that preserves human-epistemic primacy while leveraging artificial intelligence as an instrumental collaborator for rapid intellectual terrain-mapping. The methodology explicitly prohibits AI from adjudicating truth claims, instead employing AI to surface interpretive contradictions, organize competing theoretical frameworks, and identify domains requiring deeper primary-source investigation. This article articulates the theoretical foundations, procedural architecture, and epistemic constraints of the methodology, situating it within the broader context of interdisciplinary theophysics research. The protocol is presented as a replicable framework applicable to any inquiry unit within theophysics, with particular relevance to questions that span sociology, theology, economics, family structure, institutional trust, and moral philosophy.

**Keywords:** theophysics, interdisciplinary methodology, epistemic protocols, AI-augmented research, moral decline, human-epistemic primacy

## 1. Introduction: The Epistemological Problem of Cross-Domain Moral Inquiry

The research question that motivates this methodological framework is as follows: *Why is there a ubiquitous, measurable change in moral decline in America from the early 1900s to the present day?* Prior to any attempt at answering this question, however, one must interrogate the epistemological category of the question itself. This question spans sociology, theology, economics, family structure, institutional trust, and moral philosophy. It cannot be resolved within the scope of a single discipline. It requires a methodology capable of holding that cross-domain complexity without collapsing it into a single explanatory register.

The present article does not purport to answer this question. Rather, it establishes the methodological architecture within which such an answer might be pursued with epistemic rigor. The protocol described herein was developed in response to a perceived trajectory of systemic economic, social, and institutional decline in the United States—a perception that the author acknowledges may carry epistemic risk. In accordance with the Biaxiosum disclosure standard, this motivating sense of moral urgency is documented here as a matter of methodological transparency. It is not presented as evidence for any particular conclusion, but rather as the affective and ethical context within which the inquiry was initiated.

The central thesis of this methodological framework is that **truth is rarely found where everyone agrees; it is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable**. This principle informs every structural element of the Five-Page System, which is designed to force disagreement, structural tension, and coherence failures to surface early in the inquiry cycle, thereby achieving temporal efficiency without sacrificing epistemological depth.

## 2. Theoretical Foundations: Human-Epistemic Primacy in AI-Augmented Research

### 2.1 The Principle of Epistemic Delegation

The methodology presented here is structured around the principle of **human-epistemic primacy**. This principle holds that the researcher's uncontaminated prior reasoning constitutes the load-bearing epistemological foundation of any inquiry. Ceding question-framing to AI renders the researcher an auditor of AI outputs rather than the author of an inquiry. Consequently, the protocol mandates that the research question be formalized and the researcher's pre-AI epistemic commitments be documented *prior to* any AI consultation.

AI is employed here as an instrumental collaborator that accelerates terrain-mapping without substituting for the researcher's synthetic judgment. Specifically, AI is used to: (a) map intellectual terrain quickly across multiple disciplines; (b) surface contradictions between competing interpretations; (c) organize competing frameworks side by side; (d) suggest where deeper primary-source study is needed; and (e) stress-test early conclusions against counterarguments.

Conversely, AI is explicitly prohibited from: (a) deciding what is true; (b) replacing primary-source verification; (c) framing the initial question before the researcher does; (d) selecting which evidence counts as significant; and (e) resolving contradictions that require human judgment.

### 2.2 The Diagnostic Function of AI Error

Incomplete and erroneous AI outputs are an anticipated feature of this methodology, not a defect. AI error functions as a diagnostic signal: it identifies domains where the intellectual terrain is genuinely contested, where scholarly consensus has been manufactured rather than earned through evidence, and where deeper primary-source investigation is warranted. The attrition rate of AI claims upon contact with primary sources is itself methodologically informative, indicating where consensus has been manufactured or where the empirical record is genuinely contested.

This approach draws on the epistemological tradition of **error-elimination** (Popper, 1963), wherein the falsification of hypotheses—including those generated by AI—constitutes the primary mechanism of knowledge growth. By treating AI outputs as provisional hypotheses rather than authoritative claims, the methodology maintains the critical distance necessary for rigorous inquiry.

## 3. The Five-Page System: Procedural Architecture

Each unit of inquiry within this project is structured around five documents, each performing a distinct and non-redundant cognitive function. The five-document structure must be replicated for each inquiry unit. This architecture is not procedural bureaucracy; it is the epistemological scaffolding that prevents AI-augmented research from collapsing into AI-generated opinion presented as independent scholarly judgment.

### 3.1 Inquiry Seed: Question and Orientation

**File:** `Inquiry_Seed_[Question].md`

The Inquiry Seed formalizes the research question and documents the researcher's pre-AI epistemic commitments. This step is methodologically critical: the researcher's uncontaminated prior reasoning constitutes the load-bearing epistemological foundation. The document should include:

- The precise formulation of the research question
- The researcher's initial epistemic commitments and prior beliefs
- The scope and boundaries of the inquiry
- Any relevant personal or ethical motivations (in accordance with disclosure standards)

### 3.2 AI Prompt Engine: How AI Is Used

**File:** `AI_Prompt_Engine_[Question].md`

The AI Prompt Engine documents the exact prompts, AI models consulted, and raw outputs without preliminary evaluation. At this stage, the AI functions as a terrain-mapping instrument: it surfaces competing interpretive frameworks, names relevant scholars and fault lines, and identifies domains requiring deeper investigation. Epistemic adjudication is explicitly deferred. The document should include:

- The complete text of all prompts submitted to AI systems
- The model(s) consulted and version information
- Raw AI outputs without editorial modification
- Timestamps and session metadata

### 3.3 Research Workspace: Evidence and Links

**File:** `Research_Workspace_[Question].md`

The Research Workspace serves as the primary evidentiary repository where verified sources accumulate. AI-generated claims are stress-tested against primary literature at this stage. The document should include:

- Full bibliographic citations for all sources consulted
- Direct quotations from primary sources with page numbers
- Empirical findings as stated in the source literature
- Annotations indicating the researcher's assessment of source reliability
- Links to digital repositories or archival materials where applicable

### 3.4 Contradictions and Resolution: Sense-Making

**File:** `Contradictions_Resolution_[Question].md`

This document constitutes the methodological core of the system. Source disagreements, AI-primary literature divergences, and intuition-data conflicts are surfaced and documented here. Premature resolution is proscribed; the researcher must inhabit irresolution long enough to comprehend it structurally rather than selecting confirmatory evidence. The document should include:

- Explicit documentation of all source disagreements
- Identification of points where AI claims diverge from primary sources
- Documentation of conflicts between the researcher's intuitions and empirical data
- Analysis of structural tensions that resist clean resolution
- Provisional syntheses only after sustained engagement with disconfirming evidence

### 3.5 Quotes, Domains, and Links: Shared Library

**File:** `Quotes_Domains_Links_[Question].md`

The Shared Library is a cumulative reference library that persists across all inquiry units, consolidating attributed quotations, domain taxonomies surfaced during research, and annotated external links. This document grows continuously and constitutes the raw material from which publications are subsequently derived. The document should include:

- Attributed quotations with full source information
- Domain taxonomies and classification schemes
- Annotated external links with brief summaries
- Cross-references to related inquiry units

## 4. Workflow Sequence and Implementation

The Five-Page System follows a prescribed workflow sequence that must be executed in order:

1. **Initiate with the Inquiry Seed.** Formalize the research question and document initial epistemic commitments *prior to* any AI consultation.

2. **Execute the AI Prompt Engine phase.** Record outputs without evaluation; collection precedes adjudication.

3. **Proceed to the Research Workspace.** Trace AI-surfaced leads to primary sources. Cite all evidence with full attribution and record empirical findings as stated in the source literature.

4. **Construct the Contradictions and Resolution document.** Surface all source disagreements explicitly. Inhabit irresolution; do not collapse it prematurely.

5. **Maintain the Quotes, Domains, and Links library continuously.** Each inquiry unit contributes to the cumulative reference archive.

The five-document structure must be replicated for each inquiry unit. This architecture ensures that the methodology remains scalable across multiple research questions while maintaining consistent epistemic standards.

## 5. Methodological Constraints and Limitations

### 5.1 Epistemic Limitations

The methodology explicitly acknowledges that the researcher's motivating sense of moral urgency may introduce epistemic bias. The Biaxiosum disclosure standard requires documentation of such motivations as a matter of methodological transparency. This disclosure does not eliminate bias but renders it visible and therefore subject to critical scrutiny.

### 5.2 Temporal Constraints

The methodology is designed to achieve temporal efficiency without sacrificing epistemological depth. However, the requirement to inhabit irresolution before synthesis may extend the inquiry timeline beyond what is typical in conventional research protocols. This trade-off is accepted as necessary for maintaining epistemic rigor.

### 5.3 Domain-Specific Constraints

The methodology is designed for questions that span multiple disciplines and cannot be resolved within a single explanatory register. For questions that fall entirely within a single disciplinary framework, more conventional methodologies may be more appropriate.

## 6. Conclusion: The Function of Methodological Discipline

This research exists because the perceived weight of civilizational decline demands more than affective response—it demands empirical investigation conducted under disciplined epistemic constraints. The method requires sustained engagement with disconfirming evidence before synthesis is attempted. That is its function.

The Five-Page System provides a replicable framework for interdisciplinary theophysics research that preserves human-epistemic primacy while leveraging AI as an instrumental collaborator. By forcing disagreement, structural tension, and coherence failures to surface early in the inquiry cycle, the methodology achieves temporal efficiency without sacrificing epistemological depth. It is the researcher conducting the research, with greater speed and greater methodological honesty, using AI instrumentally at the boundaries of established knowledge.

## Appendix B: Disclosure Standards

The Biaxiosum disclosure standard requires the researcher to document:

1. Any personal, ethical, or affective motivations that may influence the inquiry
2. Any prior beliefs or commitments that may constitute epistemic risk
3. Any financial, institutional, or relational conflicts of interest
4. The specific AI models and versions consulted in the inquiry

This disclosure is not intended to eliminate bias but to render it visible and subject to critical scrutiny.
