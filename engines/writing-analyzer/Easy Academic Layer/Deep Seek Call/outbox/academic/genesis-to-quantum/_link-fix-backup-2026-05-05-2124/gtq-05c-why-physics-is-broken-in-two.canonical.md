### Section 2: Comparative Analysis of Physical Regimes

#### 2.1 General Relativity: The Logos-Imprint

General Relativity is characterized by smoothness, continuity, exactness, and determinism. Given complete initial conditions, the future state of a system is calculable with perfect precision. These properties—structure, law, coherence, determinism—are identified within the framework as properties of the Logos. If the physical world was created through the Logos (John 1:3), then the physical world should bear the signature of its source. #### 2.2 Quantum Mechanics: The Fracture Signature

Quantum Mechanics is characterized by noise, discontinuity, probability, and observer dependence. The act of measurement changes the system. The observer is not external and neutral but is entangled with the observed system.

These properties—noise, probability, observer dependence—are not properties of the Logos. They are, within the framework, what a receiver produces when decoupled from a perfectly ordered source. Quantum indeterminacy is interpreted as the signature of a decoupled receiver attempting to read a Logos-structured signal through a broken instrument.
