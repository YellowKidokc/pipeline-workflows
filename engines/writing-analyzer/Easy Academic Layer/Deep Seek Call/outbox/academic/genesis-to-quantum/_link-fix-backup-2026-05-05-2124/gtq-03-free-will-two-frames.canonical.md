# The Coherence Equation and the Dissolution of the Calvinist–Arminian Debate: A Theophysics Framework

## Abstract

This article applies the Theophysics coherence framework to the historical theological dispute between Calvinist and Arminian soteriological systems. We propose that the debate, which has persisted for approximately five centuries without resolution, arises from an underspecification of the dynamical variables governing spiritual transformation rather than from irreconcilable theological commitments. Through the introduction of a coherence evolution equation—a logistic growth model with external drive and decay terms—we demonstrate that both theological positions identify real but partial variables within a coupled dynamical system. The equation $\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$ reveals that divine grace ($G$) and human openness ($O$) are multiplicatively coupled, rendering the question of which term is "more important" structurally meaningless. We further introduce a signed surrender parameter $s \in [-1, +1]$ that subsumes the openness variable and generates three qualitatively distinct dynamical pathways—Autonomy, Performance, and Surrender—each with identifiable neurological and behavioral correlates. Four falsifiable predictions are derived, and the framework's load-bearing claims, suggestive extensions, and acknowledged limitations are systematically catalogued.

## 1. Introduction: The Problem of Underspecification

The Pauline injunction in Philippians 2:12–13 presents what appears to be a simultaneous assertion of two seemingly incompatible propositions: "Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you, both to will and to work for his good pleasure" (Philippians 2:12–13, ESV). The text does not hedge between human agency and divine sovereignty; it stacks them in a single sentence, separated by a comma, as if the tension itself carries theological significance.

The subsequent history of Christian theology has largely treated this tension as a problem requiring resolution. The Calvinist tradition, following Augustine, Luther, and Calvin, emphasizes the priority and efficacy of divine grace, arguing that post-Fall human will is incapable of initiating or contributing to salvific response. The Arminian tradition, following Arminius and Wesley, emphasizes the reality of human choice and the universal scope of prevenient grace that enables genuine response. Both traditions cite extensive scriptural warrant; both have produced sophisticated theological systems; neither has produced a resolution acceptable to the other.

This article proposes that the debate fails to resolve not because either position is internally inconsistent, but because both positions operate with an underspecified model of the dynamics involved. The question is not "does God choose or do we choose?" but rather: what dynamical structure best accounts for the simultaneous operation of divine grace and human response as attested in Scripture and experience?

## 2. The Coherence Evolution Equation

### 2.1 Formal Definition

We model spiritual coherence—the degree to which a human system is aligned with its source specification—as a dynamical variable $C \in [0,1]$ that evolves over time according to:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

(1)

where:

- $C \in [0,1]$ is **coherence**, with $C = 0$ representing total decoherence (complete separation from source specification) and $C = 1$ representing perfect alignment.
- $O \in [0,1]$ is **openness**, a dimensionless parameter representing the human agent's receptivity to external input. This functions as a coupling coefficient between the agent and the grace source.
- $G > 0$ is the **grace input rate**, with dimensions of inverse time $[T^{-1}]$, representing the rate at which divine grace is available to the system. This term is externally supplied and not generated by the agent.
- $S \geq 0$ is the **entropy/sin parameter**, with dimensions of inverse time $[T^{-1}]$, representing the rate at which coherence decays due to misalignment, resistance, or moral failure.
- $(1-C)$ is the **coherence gap**, representing the available capacity for grace-mediated transformation.

### 2.2 Structural Motivation

Equation (1) belongs to the family of logistic growth models with external drive and decay terms. This form appears across multiple scientific domains, including population biology (the Verhulst equation with immigration), epidemiology (the SIS model with external infection source), and information theory (signal propagation with noise). The structure is not invented for theological application but represents a standard dynamical form applied to a new domain.

The multiplicative coupling $O \cdot G$ is the load-bearing structural feature of the equation. If $O = 0$ (complete closure), the grace term vanishes entirely regardless of the magnitude of $G$. If $G = 0$ (no grace input), the equation reduces to pure exponential decay $\frac{dC}{dt} = -S \cdot C$, driving $C$ to zero regardless of $O$. The multiplicative structure ensures that both terms are necessary for positive coherence growth.

### 2.3 Steady-State Analysis

Setting $\frac{dC}{dt} = 0$ yields the steady-state coherence:

$$C^* = \frac{O \cdot G}{O \cdot G + S}$$

(2)

This equilibrium value depends on all three parameters. The following limiting cases are instructive:

- **$O \to 0$**: $C^* \to 0$ regardless of $G$ and $S$. Complete closure produces zero coherence.
- **$G \to 0$**: $C^* \to 0$ regardless of $O$ and $S$. Absent grace, coherence decays to zero.
- **$S \to 0$**: $C^* \to 1$ provided $O \cdot G > 0$. Zero entropy permits perfect coherence.
- **$O \cdot G \gg S$**: $C^* \to 1$. When grace coupling dominates entropy, coherence approaches unity.

## 3. Mapping the Calvinist–Arminian Debate

### 3.1 Variable Identification

The historic debate maps onto the equation's variables as follows:

| Camp | Core Claim | Equation Variable | Valid Insight | Omission |
|------|------------|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Calvinist | Salvation is entirely God's work; human will is enslaved post-Fall; election precedes all human response | $G$ (Grace) | $G$ is non-zero and externally sourced; the agent cannot initiate it | $O$ is also a real variable; setting $O \equiv 1$ (irresistible grace) assumes the answer |
| Arminian | God extends prevenient grace to all; humans genuinely choose or refuse; faith is a real human act | $O$ (Openness) | $O$ is a real variable; grace does not automatically override it | $O$ is not self-generated *ex nihilo*; the capacity for openness is itself graced |

### 3.2 The Structural Error

Both camps identify one term of a multiplicative product and treat it as the whole equation. Calvinism correctly identifies grace as the driving force but fails to account for openness as a modulating variable. Arminianism correctly identifies openness as real but struggles to account for the origin of openness in a post-Fall agent.

The equation reveals that the debate is not wrong but underspecified. Both camps have identified real variables; neither has written down the equation containing both variables. Once the equation is specified, the debate dissolves—not into compromise, but into a more complete description.

### 3.3 The Surrender Parameterization

We introduce a signed **surrender parameter** $s \in [-1, +1]$ that subsumes the openness variable and maps onto three attitudinal postures:

| $s$ Value | Label | Description | Equation Effect |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| $s = -1$ | Autonomous Resistance | Active opposition to grace; the agent treats coherence as a threat to self-definition | Grace coupling vanishes; $\frac{dC}{dt} = -S \cdot C$ |
| $s \approx 0$ | Self-Righteous Performance | The agent attempts to generate coherence through effort; grace is accepted only as assistance | Grace coupling is severely reduced; equilibrium is low and unstable |
| $s = +1$ | Full Surrender | The agent orients completely toward the grace source; self-generated effort yields to receptivity | Grace coupling is maximized; highest achievable $C^*$ given fixed $G$ and $S$ |

The extended coherence equation with surrender parameterization becomes:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = \frac{(1+s)}{2} \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

(3)

When $s = +1$, the coupling coefficient $\frac{(1+s)}{2} = 1$, yielding full grace coupling. When $s = -1$, the coefficient equals zero, and entropy dominates. When $s = 0$, the coefficient equals $\frac{1}{2}$, producing the partial coupling characteristic of the performance posture.

## 4. Free Will Before and After the Fall

### 4.1 Pre-Fall Will

Prior to the collapse event described in Article 01 of this series (The Measurement That Collapsed Reality), the human agent operates at $C \approx 1$—near-maximal coherence with the source specification. In this state, the will is free in the strongest possible sense: it aligns with its own deepest nature, which is itself aligned with the Logos field.

This freedom is not freedom *from* constraint but freedom *as* the fullest expression of what the agent is. The pre-Fall will experiences no gap between desire and specification. What it wants, it wants rightly, because its wanting-mechanism is coherent with its source. The capacity to choose wrongly existed—represented by the tree in the garden—but required active movement away from the coherence equilibrium. The agent had to *work* to decohere.

### 4.2 Post-Fall Will

After the collapse event, the human agent defaults to $C \ll 1$—low coherence, high entropy, dominant $S$ term. The will is now systematically biased. It can still choose, but the choice-generating mechanism is itself distorted. The problem is not that the will has been removed; the problem is that it now *wants the wrong things at the first order of desire.*

This preserves the Calvinist insight: total depravity does not mean maximum possible evil in every choice; it means the will's operating system has been corrupted at the root. The Arminian insight is also preserved: the will still exists, still makes real choices, and still bears real moral weight. Both are true; the equation holds both.

### 4.3 The Role of Grace

What changes the situation is the injection of a grace source term $G$ into the system—a negentropic input that the agent did not generate and cannot earn. The grace term does not override the will; it creates a gradient toward which the will can orient if it chooses to do so. Prevenient grace, in equation terms, is simply $G > 0$ being nonzero for all human agents regardless of their prior state.

## 5. Three Dynamical Pathways

### 5.1 Path 1: Autonomy ($s = -1$)

**Dynamics.** With $s = -1$, the grace coupling term vanishes entirely:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = -S \cdot C$$

(4)

The only active term is $-SC$, which drives $C$ exponentially toward zero. There is no equilibrium above zero. The autonomous agent's coherence will, under the dynamics as written, always decay to zero in the absence of some shift in $s$.

**Neurological correlates.** Chronic autonomy posture correlates with:
- Default Mode Network (DMN) hyperactivation, associated with self-referential processing and rumination
- Elevated cortisol and sympathetic baseline, consistent with hypervigilance
- Reduced oxytocin receptor sensitivity, diminishing capacity for relational bonding

**Phenomenology.** Agents in Path 1 typically report a sense of fundamental aloneness, intermittent contempt for vulnerability in others, escalating anxiety about meaning and mortality, and occasional acute crises when the self-referential system collapses under its own weight.

### 5.2 Path 2: Performance ($s \approx 0$)

**Dynamics.** With $s \approx 0$, a partial grace coupling is active:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} \approx \frac{1}{2} G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

(5)

The steady state becomes:

$$C^*_{\text{perf}} = \frac{G/2}{G/2 + S} = \frac{G}{G + 2S}$$

(6)

For a system with $G = S$, Path 3 yields $C^* = 0.5$ while Path 2 yields $C^* \approx 0.33$. The performance agent achieves real but reduced coherence. More importantly, the Path 2 equilibrium is *inherently less stable*: because the grace coupling is partial, small perturbations in $S$ produce larger swings in $C$ than under Path 3's fuller coupling.

**Neurological correlates.** Performance posture correlates with:
- Prefrontal cortex (PFC) overactivation, consistent with active self-evaluation rather than receptive presence
- Shame-cycling: oscillation between achievement states and shame states as performance standards are met or missed
- Conditional oxytocin response, creating attachment anxiety within the religious relationship

**Phenomenology.** Agents in Path 2 are the most common type in organized religion. They experience real spiritual meaning, genuine moral effort, and authentic community—but also persistent background anxiety, recurring cycles of failure and recovery, and a nagging sense that the peace they seek is always one spiritual discipline away.

### 5.3 Path 3: Surrender ($s = +1$)

**Dynamics.** With $s = +1$, full grace coupling is active:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = G(1-C) - S \cdot C$$

(7)

The steady state is:

$$C^* = \frac{G}{G + S}$$

(8)

This is the maximum achievable equilibrium given fixed $G$ and $S$. For $G \gg S$, $C^* \to 1$.

**Neurological correlates.** Surrender posture correlates with:
- Reduced DMN activity, consistent with reduced centering of identity around self-narrative
- Increased parasympathetic tone, supporting social engagement, relaxation, and receptive processing
- D2 receptor restoration in preliminary data, suggesting measurable neurological substrate for the surrender parameter shift
- Elevated baseline oxytocin, associated with trust, bonding capacity, and openness to others

**Phenomenology.** The surrendered agent does not cease to be an agent. Surrender is not passivity; it is a fundamental reorientation of the agent's operating frame from self-as-source to self-as-receiver. Effort continues—often at higher absolute levels than Path 2—but effort is now downstream of reception rather than the precondition for it.

## 6. Falsifiable Predictions

Four predictions emerge from the coherence equation applied to human agents:

**Prediction 1 — Path 3 outperforms Path 2.** Agents in sustained surrender posture ($s \approx +1$) should show measurably higher coherence-proxy measures (psychological integration, behavioral consistency, physiological markers of reduced chronic stress) than agents in high-effort religious performance ($s \approx 0$), controlling for religious activity level.

**Prediction 2 — Grace is not correlated with prior moral performance.** If $G$ is genuinely externally sourced and not proportional to prior $C$, then recipients of high-grace experiences should be randomly distributed across the coherence spectrum at time of receipt. Conversion data should show significant representation of low-$C$ agents (high-entropy life histories) receiving strong grace encounters.

**Prediction 3 — The $s$ parameter transitions are abrupt, not gradual.** The phase diagram of the coherence equation predicts that transitions between attractor basins (Path 1 → Path 3, for instance) should resemble threshold crossings rather than smooth ramps. Testimonies of conversion should cluster around abrupt threshold events rather than gradual linear improvement curves.

**Prediction 4 — Low $O$ blocks grace regardless of grace magnitude.** Subjects in high autonomy mode ($s = -1$) exposed to high-grace environments should show minimal coherence increase. The grace is present; the coupling is absent.

## 7. Discussion: The Audit

### 7.1 Load-Bearing Claims

The following claims are structurally essential to the framework:

1. **The equation form is motivated.** The structure $\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$ is a standard logistic growth model with external drive and decay terms, appearing throughout biology, epidemiology, and information theory.

2. **Philippians 2:12–13 structurally requires both terms.** Paul's language is not hedging; it is simultaneous assertion. Any framework that yields one term at the expense of the other contradicts the text.

3. **The three-pathway structure maps onto documented neurological and psychological states.** The distinction between autonomy, performance, and surrender maps onto established categories in psychology of religion (Granqvist, 2002; Piedmont, 1999; Exline, 2002).

4. **The steady-state analysis is algebraically trivial.** The claim that $C^* = OG/(OG+S)$ is a direct algebraic result requiring no assumptions beyond the equation form.

### 7.2 Suggestive Claims (Requiring Further Work)

1. **The mapping of theological concepts to equation variables is interpretive.** The argument that $G$ maps to grace and $O$ maps to openness is motivated but not uniquely forced. Alternative parameterizations are possible.

2. **The surrender parameter $s$ is not directly measurable.** We can operationalize proxies (self-report scales, behavioral indicators, physiological markers), but $s$ itself remains a theoretical construct until measurement protocols are established.

3. **The phase transition prediction (Prediction 3) requires careful operationalization.** "Abrupt" versus "gradual" is a testable claim, but defining the threshold requires calibration work.

### 7.3 Acknowledged Limitations

1. **The equation does not prove Christian theology.** It shows that a particular dynamical model is consistent with a particular reading of Pauline text. Consistency is not proof.

2. **The "dissolution" of the Calvinist–Arminian debate is a reframing, not a refutation.** Both camps can respond that we have redefined their terms. The reframing is useful if it generates testable predictions; its theological authority is a separate question.

3. **The framework does not address double predestination.** The strong Calvinist claim that God actively decrees some to damnation is not touched by the coherence equation. The equation predicts outcomes given parameters; it does not specify who sets the parameters or whether $G$ is truly universal.

## 8. Kill Conditions

The framework survives or falls on the following conditions:

**Kill Condition 1 — Coupling Structure.** If $G$ and $O$ are NOT multiplicatively coupled—if the relationship is additive ($G + O$) rather than multiplicative ($G \times O$)—the dissolution of the Calvinist–Arminian debate fails. The dissolution depends on the structural fact that $O = 0$ zeroes the entire grace term.

**Kill Condition 2 — Path Hierarchy.** If Path 3 does NOT outperform Path 2 in clinical and psychological data—if surrender-oriented spirituality shows no measurable advantage over performance-oriented spirituality—the framework's most important practical claim is wrong.

**Kill Condition 3 — Transition Architecture.** If pathway transitions are smooth gradual ramps rather than threshold crossings, the attractor basin model is wrong and the equation's phase structure does not apply.

---

## 9. Conclusion

The coherence equation $\frac{dC}{dt} = O \cdot G(1-C) - S \cdot C$ does not resolve the Calvinist–Arminian debate by picking a side. It dissolves the debate by writing down a more complete description that requires both terms. Grace is real, externally sourced, and universally available. Openness is real, under some form of agent influence, and genuinely modulates the coupling. Neither term is optional. The fight between the two camps is a fight about which term is more important—a question the equation renders meaningless because the two terms multiply.

Paul knew this. He did not choose between "work out your salvation" and "God who works in you." He put them in the same sentence, separated by a comma, and left the tension standing. The equation is the comma.
